A reimagining of Cinderella built around a simple idea: what happens the morning after the fairy tale?

Instead of retelling the familiar story, the film takes its ridiculous logic completely seriously — a mysterious woman appears at a royal ball, captivates the Prince, vanishes without identification and leaves behind a single glass shoe.

Naturally, the authorities investigate.

The project is an experiment in using AI filmmaking for character-driven narrative comedy, not spectacle for its own sake.
