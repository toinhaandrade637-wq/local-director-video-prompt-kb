Death has come, but nothing goes as planned.

A story about anger and the things we never took the time to saying, based on my personal story.

✨ CONTEXT

This film is based on my personal journey since my father passed away a few years ago: navigating grief, forgiveness, and now connecting with him in the afterlife.




STORY

On her very first day, a clumsy rookie Reaper can't pry loose the soul of an absent father, because his seven-year-old son's heart is too heavy to let him go. To free him, she dares the boy to finally tell his dad what he's never said.

CHARACTERS

FATHER and SON are mirrors, with REAPER between them. 
FATHER: Blue, well-groomed, square face, tired of having followed the rules all his life.
SON: Orange, round. Unlike his father's neat look, his hair represents his free and creative spirit.
REAPER: Purple, a mix of blue and orange.




LOCATIONS

I wanted the story to be an emotional ride and built an audio environnement for each.
1. Room: Cold tone. Heart monitor sound.
2. Woundland: Grayish. The wound from SON's consciousness. Lot of echo
3 Farewell: A multicolored universe from SON's consciousness. I'm using a subtle reversed delay to show that time doesn't exist here: past, present and future coexist.




FIRST & LAST FRAME

The film opens on a father's closed eyes and ends on his son's, opening.
Grief taught me that the story doesn't stop with the one who leaves. It goes on with the one who wakes up the next morning, carrying a little of their light.




MUSIC

Je suis là ("I'm here") is a song I wrote while I was grieving. It's about a soul who comes back to tell a loved one that they're okay. For this film, I used SUNO to turn it into an instrumental movie soundtrack, with no lyrics. The original version isn't in the film, but the song is where the film comes from.

AUDIO SFX

If you ask me, I believe sound is 51% of the moviegoing experience. That's why I generate SFX in a separate pass and layer it on top of the video generations. Sometimes I blend 20% of the audio from Seedance, sometimes the audio is entirely rebuilt from scratch.

For SFX generations I use Seed Audio, Elevenlabs or Sonilo. Because SFX generation is really cheap, I can generate, try and iterate fast.
