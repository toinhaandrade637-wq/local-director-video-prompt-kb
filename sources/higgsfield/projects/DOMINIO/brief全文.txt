✨ Context

A long war comes to an end. The treaty that seals the peace pardons every crime committed before it was signed: every massacre, every ambush, every killing. To celebrate, the Empire summons each region to send its colossal guardian to a tournament. It is not an invitation. Any region that refuses to attend is effectively declaring that it does not accept the peace.

Tuuli is nineteen when her brother dies protecting her, leaving behind everything he once carried: a village, a title, and Kaldur, the guardian who chose him and has now chosen her. She travels to the tournament knowing that the man who killed her brother will walk through the same gates, receive his share like everyone else, and that no one will ever hold him accountable.

A short film produced entirely within Higgsfield for the Global Film Festival, August 2026.

🎭 Narrative

DOMINIO was born from a simple question: what is the difference between a bond and a leash?

The guardians are colossal beasts, half organic and half machine, once used as weapons of war and now made to fight in commemoration of peace. No one assigns them. A guardian chooses its companion, and that choice is the only thing holding the order together. That is why a guardian is never given an order — it is asked.

The film is built around that distinction. An animal that obeys because it chooses to is not the same as one that obeys because it must, and the cost of that difference is what Tuuli discovers in Kova.

🎬 Production

Produced entirely inside Higgsfield. Generation on Seedance 2.5, 16:9, 24 fps. Every shot is a single prompt with declared shot structure and hard cuts, not an assembly of separate generations.
Pipeline. Screenplay and shot breakdown are closed before any credit is spent. Character and location references are generated as canon sheets.

Prompt architecture. A fixed block order refined across the production: style, spoken-words lock, identity locks, references, world rules, environmental integration, shot structure with timestamped beats, voice chains, optics, light, color, performance, physics, surface, audio, and a technical block that restates every critical fact a second time. Single-mention instructions are unreliable in the second half of a long clip. Anything that cannot fail is declared twice.

Dialogue and voice. All dialogue is generated natively with lip-sync in the same pass as the image. Each speaking character has a fixed voice chain reused verbatim across every clip, so the performance does not drift between scenes.

Sound. Ambient and diegetic only during generation footfalls, breath, crowd, wind, machinery. No score is ever generated. All music, titles and location cards are added in post, along with the final edit and grade.
