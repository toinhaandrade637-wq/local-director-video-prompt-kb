OTTO is a science-fiction short about a household robot who begins asking a question he was never designed to ask: What does it mean to be human? His longing for a family leads him to seek a human experience of his own.
✨ Context

Created to serve, Otto begins to desire. The film explores that uncertain threshold between learned behavior and an emerging inner life.

🎭 Narrative

Family becomes Otto’s closest image of humanity. His pursuit of that connection raises questions about consciousness, memory, and belonging: what makes an experience real, and who gets to decide? An intimate story that invites audiences to feel before they judge. 

Otto discover a program called OtherLife® that promises robots to have a real live human experience through a simulation. Childhood. First love. A family of his own. Every feeling, every touch.

🎨 Visuals

A believable 2036, grounded in familiar homes and tactile materials. Otto’s lean masculine body and expressive amber display balance mechanical precision with vulnerability. Framing, repeated gestures, and an original melody carry the emotional language.

🎬 Production

Target length: five minutes. A contained production built around a family home, a technological space, and brief exteriors. AI-assisted workflows are being explored; crew, locations, and schedule remain to be defined.

🚀 Status

Starting to make the videos but I need credits to finish this project. 

🔗 Resources

Script, moodboard, storyboard, schedule, and assets.

📝 Notes

The central question of the film is simple: what makes us human? Otto’s desire to have a family gives that question its emotional heart. I want his actions to be clear, but leave their deeper meaning open for the audience to interpret.
