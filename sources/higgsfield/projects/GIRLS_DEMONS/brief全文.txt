GIRLS / DEMONS is a psychological anime short about love, guilt, memory and the monsters we create inside ourselves.

After a traumatic first love, a young man slowly becomes colder, more distant and disconnected from the people around him. Years later, his life turns increasingly surreal as desire, fear and relationships begin to take the form of literal demons.

Visually, the film feels like a lost late-90s Japanese anime: hand-drawn 2D, imperfect movement, neon Tokyo nights, green and amber light, rain, bokeh, drifting cars, underground spaces and melancholic silence.

At its core, it asks one question:

How much of the darkness in our lives comes from what happened to us… and how much comes from what we became because of it?
