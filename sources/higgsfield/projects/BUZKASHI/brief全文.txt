A short film about a boy who plays a brutal, ancient game to be claimed as a son, and the uncle beside him who is fighting the same grief in silence.

 Context

A boy raised to copy his uncle's every gesture is left off the roster on game day. Watching from the boundary of a horseman's brutal sport, he finally understands which part of the man he actually inherited.

Synopsis

A boy raised on imitation is left off the roster on game day. Watching from the boundary, he sees — or believes he sees — his dead father's horse cross through the dust. In that instant he makes the one gesture of his uncle's he never understood: the turn toward the hills. He finally knows what it means. BUZKASHI is a black-and-white study of a boy and the silent man raising him — of what passes between two people who never say the true thing out loud.
