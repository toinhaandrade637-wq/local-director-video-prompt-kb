A short film about how the very things that make us human are devalued and lose their worth in the endless race for efficiency.

Context

Technology is taking over more and more of our daily lives. This film is about of what people are willing to sacrifice for success - and how that pursuit can take away the most precious thing of all: our human memories.

🎭 Narrative

Logline: A man comes to a job interview at a major corporation, but to get the position, he must sacrifice a part of himself.

Message: Success isn't what matters most in life if achieving it means you stop being yourself. 

For all audience, 0+.

🎨 Visuals

Minimalism runs through everything in this world - if all the worlds exist in the mind, there's no need to spend anything on shaping the reality around you. Everything is built for functionality and the most basic tasks. Most shots use a static camera.

The system squeezes out those who don't want to lose themselves and conform to it. In a world governed entirely by wireless commands, there's almost no place left for the "old-model" human. He can't understand the others - he can't even open a door on his own.

Teal and cyan tones - soulless and cold, like the consciousness of most of this world's inhabitants.
