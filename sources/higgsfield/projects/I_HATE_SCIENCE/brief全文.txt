A short film about an eccentric physics professor whose hypothetical lecture scenarios manifest physically in his classroom, subjecting his students to catastrophic "what-if" experiments while he remains completely oblivious.

Context:

This project exists to explore the intersection of academic theory and physical reality. It satirizes the disconnect between ivory-tower intellectualism and the practical consequences of scientific "concepts," using dark humor and slapstick to emphasize the comedic frustration of a classroom where the professor's musings have literal, destructive power.

Narrative:
Story: A professor poses hypothetical physics scenarios that wreak havoc on his students, who are the unwilling test subjects. The film captures the absurdity of the professor's obliviousness in the face of immense danger.

Message: Science is powerful and unpredictable; true wisdom requires awareness of one's surroundings, not just theoretical knowledge.

Audience: Fans of high-concept comedy, dark humor, and short films with strong visual gags.
