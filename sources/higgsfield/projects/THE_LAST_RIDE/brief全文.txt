A cinematic animated short set in a vibrant future where humans and dinosaurs share a culture built around racing, survival, and trust.
✨ Context

An original post-collapse world reclaimed by nature, where ancient ruins have become part of a new civilization and dinosaurs live alongside humans.

🎭 Narrative

A former champion returns to the world he left behind, confronting the bond that once defined him. A story about ambition, trust, regret, and learning when to let go.

🎨 Visuals

Stylized cinematic 3D with a hand-painted illustration aesthetic, bold brushwork, angular silhouettes, exaggerated proportions, vibrant colors, matte surfaces, and no outlines.

🎬 Production

AI-driven filmmaking using Higgsfield for visual generation, with external editing, sound design, music, and post-production within festival guidelines.

🚀 Status

Currently in production. Character development and world-building are established, with final sequences now being generated and assembled.

🔗 Resources

Script, visual development, character references, environment concepts, storyboard, music, and production assets.

📝 Notes

The film is designed to communicate primarily through image, movement, music, and sound, keeping dialogue to a minimum.
