# K11 质感与后期通道（生图轨）

成片感不是一次生成出来的——官方把**质感与调色作为独立生成通道**，在底图之上单独立法。

## 1. TEXTURE AND GRADE PASS ONLY [案例 ANERNEQ 16×]
> `TEXTURE AND GRADE PASS ONLY`——只做质感与调色的独立通道提示词：不改构图、不改内容，只在既有帧上做材质与调色。家族代表 3,113 字符。

## 2. 分区质感立法 [案例 ANERNEQ]
> `SKIN TONE` / `MATERIALS` / `LIGHTING & GRADE` 三块独立立法（12×）——皮肤、材质、光调分账户管理，不混写。

## 3. 材质真实立法 [案例 ANERNEQ 16×]
> `MATERIAL TRUTH — ICE, NOT ROCK (KEY)`——材质身份单独立法：这是冰不是岩石。材质的物理身份先于外观。

## 4. 胶片链在后期通道的延续 [案例 ANERNEQ]
质感通道内延续胶片语言：grain alive in them / halation / gate weave / photochemical——底图与质感通道共用同一套胶片事实。

## 5. EXTREME REALISM 前置块 [案例 ANERNEQ]
> `EXTREME REALISM — a real...` + `CAMERA AND LENS` + `FILM CHARACTER...`——写实声明作为质感通道的开场块。

## 2026-11-04 增补：品牌等级参照与黑位保留 [案例 S27 side-b 31,820c 超长样本]

- **品牌广告参照法**：`the level of controlled, moody lighting used in a Cartier, Chanel, or Rolex campaign film`——用奢侈品牌广告片定光线"等级"（可执行的高级感锚点，替代 expensive/cinematic 空词）
- **黑位保留立法**：`Deep, rich blacks with genuine shadow detail retained (not crushed to flat black), controlled specular highlights on metal, fabric, and skin rather than blown-out glow`——黑位三态立法：黑要深（deep blacks）+ 细节要留（genuine shadow detail）+ 高光要控（controlled speculars, not blown-out）
- **质感收尾**：`Fine, consistent, barely-there film grain — the texture of a large-format campaign`——颗粒度用媒介参照（large-format 相片）而非数值

## 2026-11-05 增补：T3 #33-#40 批次（S36）
- **18% 灰无影棚生图全套** [案例 FOOL_S_GOLD 中位 4,841c 逐字]：背景 `single flat 18% neutral gray field — one uniform value at every pixel corner to corner…no seam line, no gradient, no hotspot, no vignette`；布光 `Relight from scratch overriding any reference lighting`（完全平光：`No key-and-fill ratio, no modelling, no shadow side, no cheek triangle, no nose shadow, no rim light, no hair light, no kicker`）；投影否定 `Absolutely zero shadow anywhere outside the subject…Shading exists only on the subject and stops cleanly at the silhouette`；串色否定 `zero light bleed outside the subject`；收尾宣言 `Photographed not generated`——证照棚拍级 flat-light 立法全集。

## 短摘录·好在哪·迁移法（2026-11-05 范本对齐）

### 材质排除清单 [案例 DST #87 逐字]

> HE IS EXPLICITLY NOT, AND MUST NEVER RESEMBLE: cardboard, a cardboard cutout, a standee, a foam board, a printed sign, a paper doll, a sticker, a decal, a vinyl figure, a plastic toy, a costume, a mascot suit, a person in a mask, a puppet, a marionette, a paper craft, a papercut animation, a felt or fabric figure, a 3D model with cel-shading, or a flat object physically present in the room.

**好在哪**：负面清单定义"不是什么"（约 20 项）+属性负表（NO thickness/edge/cut border/paper grain/card texture/fold/warp/seam/drop shadow/bevel）+正句收口（"THE SEAM MUST READ. He should look impossible, not built"）——三件套防材质漂移。
**迁移**：易漂移材质=负面清单+属性负表+一句"必须读作什么"；清单按项目材质族定制。

### 光学豁免立法 [案例 DST #87 逐字]

> His outline does not blur when the camera moves and does not blur when HE moves. He gains NO film grain, NO lens distortion, NO depth-of-field falloff, NO atmospheric haze.

**好在哪**：把画中角色从镜头光学体系整体豁免——噪点/景深/雾/畸变四不吃；与 cel 隔离（不吃光/不投影/无声/尘埃不交互 DST #80）合成完整豁免面。
**迁移**：合成元素逐项声明光学豁免；豁免项与隔离项分开列（光学面/物理面/声学面）。
