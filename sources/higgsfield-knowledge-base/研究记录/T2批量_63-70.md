## 核心发现（全部原文 verbatim）

### 1. AUDIO RESTRICTIONS：11 连音乐禁令 [案例 petya，块名带冒号]

```
AUDIO RESTRICTIONS:
No music.
Do not add any soundtrack.
No orchestral score.
No ambient pads.
No emotional chords.
No drones.
No melody.
No rhythm.
No humming.
No singing.
No cinematic musical accents.

Only natural environmental sound.
```

全库最极端音频否定清单——把"不要音乐"拆成 **11 个具体形态**逐一禁止（soundtrack/score/pads/chords/drones/melody/rhythm/humming/singing/accents），再以白名单收尾（Only natural environmental sound）。与 a-life 四重否定（NO MUSIC/SCORE/DIALOGUE）同族但细 7 倍。**否定清单的粒度=失控面的粒度**：音乐失控有 11 种形态，就禁 11 项。

### 2. SOUND / AUDIO 分工双块 [案例 sitara 10,298c 全文逐字]

同一提示词两块并存、分工明确：
- **SOUND×128 = 空间声学立法**：`The maintained quiet of a premium compound — the acoustic opposite of the naka: far pressure washer, one crow, low traffic wash beyond the wall, the fan's small whir inside the cabin.`——环境声学画像（"the acoustic opposite of X" 对比定义法）
- **AUDIO×83 = 声音透视随剪辑立法**：`In the wide, the guard's voice thin through the gap and Bittu's in open air; after the cut, the guard near-ear dry and close, Bittu's voice arriving through the gap with the room of the driveway behind it.`——**每个剪辑点重立法声音距离**（远景=隔缝细声 / 切后=贴耳干声+房间混响）。声画空间连续性。

### 3. 表演预算制（total budget）+ Living-face law [案例 sitara PERFORMANCE]

```
PERFORMANCE
FILED, NOT FELT — administered in real time. Bittu's total budget: the eye-drop,
one breath, the single consonant catch, the flattened melody, one slow blink on
the guard's repetition of the word, the millimeter recovery on the number.
Nothing else — no jaw clench for camera, no wet eyes, no trembling, no swallow
performed, eyebrows never rising. Living-face law throughout: natural blinks,
micro-saccades, breath — stillness alive, never frozen, the face never holding
perfectly still beyond one second.
```

**表演动作给定量预算**（total budget: 6 个动作逐项列出）+ "Nothing else" 封闭 + 否定排除 5 项表演过火（jaw clench/wet eyes/trembling/swallow/eyebrows）+ **Living-face law**（自然眨眼/微扫视/呼吸，面部完全静止不超一秒）。克制表演的合同化写法——与 VENGEANCE POSITIVE LOCKS 族、SEED_AUDIO 活体微动律同源但更系统。

### 4. 声源白名单立法 [案例 bash SOUND]

```
SOUND
No music.
Use only:
heavy rain
Bash wet breathing
2 distressed barks
faint continued barking as he leaves
barking fading in distance
wind
one harsh lightning crack
thunder
```

**Use only: 白名单**——声源逐项列举（连"2 distressed barks"的数量都立法）。与 petya AUDIO RESTRICTIONS 的"禁音乐"互补：一个禁什么、一个只许什么。同项目 IMPORTANT 块（画面要点清单 6 项）含终态锁：`End on empty stormy sky with lightning and thunder` + 悬念锁 `Do not reveal location / Do not show other characters`。

### 5. 编辑对偶块 [案例 the-golden-tree PRESERVE EXACTLY×39 + ONLY CHANGE×39]

PRESERVE EXACTLY（保什么）与 ONLY CHANGE（改什么）**成对出现、次数相等**——编辑模式的块化对偶（ANERNEQ ONLY CHANGE 92× 的配对块首次现身）。配套 POSITIVE LOCKS 量化锁：`Bat count stays small, 6 to 8, never a large swarm.` + `Lightning stays as diffuse sky glow only — no bolt shape ever appears.` + `Aaliya's fear reads through her scanning gaze and flinch, never through dialogue.`（情绪禁对白外化）+ 光源唯一性（Torch remains the only character-level light source）。

### 6. OPTICS 超密度与精确光学立法 [案例 ruby OPTICS×614]

```
OPTICS
47° diagonal field of view, standard normal lens character, camera 4 meters,
natural perspective, zero distortion. Moderate depth of field, the window softly
blown out, the whole room readable.
```

OPTICS×614 > CAMERA×505——光学块超密度（a-life AUDIO 超 CAMERA 同族现象：**单维度立法超密度 = 该项目核心难点维度**，ruby 的难点在光学）。配套：`Camera locked off on sticks at 80 centimeters. Static: no move, no zoom, no pan for the whole take.`（三重静止锁）。

### 7. ACTION TIMING 秒区间格式 [案例 ruby]

```
0:00 to 0:03 — both wipe calmly, ... at a steady pace, his unblinking eyes on his work.
0:03 to 0:04 — voice calls from off-frame camera-left. His head lifts, alert.
0:04 to 0:08 — reacting, he speeds up, his scrubbing arm accelerating into a real motion-blurred blur...
0:08 to 0:09 — a brief gritty catch; ...
```

秒区间第四种格式（`0:00 to 0:03 —`，此前有 SEED_AUDIO `[0s]`、new-girl `0.0s–2.0s`、VENGEANCE TIMING）+ 身体遮挡立法（`his body screens his working spot so the wall he scrubs is not yet legible`）+ 无空拍（`No empty establishing frame`，a-life 同族）。
