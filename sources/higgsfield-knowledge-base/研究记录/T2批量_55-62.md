### 2. SEED_AUDIO 发布片六项新方法（全文 5,426c 逐字读）
1. **180 度轴线立法**：`180-degree line held: the panther is downstage on the camera side, the two broadcasters face toward it.`——对话场景空间连续性（轴线不越）首见成文。
2. **唇同步正面律**：`framed frontally, never in profile` + 否定栈 `lip-sync on profile faces, lip-sync drift, leaf occluding the mouth during dialogue`——正面构图=唇同步质量前提，转场叶片也禁遮嘴。
3. **呼吸微动活体律**：`never fully locks off — a faint breathing micro-motion stays alive on the face so it never freezes into a still.`——静态相机的活体感保留。
4. **遮挡转场**：`a broad banana leaf sweeps full-frame across the lens, covering the move`——用前景遮挡隐藏 reframe 运镜。
5. **TEXT-DEFINED CREATURE (no reference)**：无参考件生物的显式文字定义声明（与 @image 锚点相对）。
6. **三种锚点类型命名**：`<<<image_4>>> — character identity lock` / `<<<image_2>>> — prop anchor` / `<<<image_1>>> — environment anchor` + `count fixed`（人数锁：exactly two humans and one panther, count fixed）。
7. **TIMELINE 秒级时间轴**：`[0s]…[2s]…[2s–5s]…[5s]…[5s–9s]` 每段镜头+台词+动作；与 new-girl `0.0s–2.0s`、VENGEANCE TIMING 三种秒级格式并存。
