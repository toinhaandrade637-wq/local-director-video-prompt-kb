### 3. 极简完整主义 [案例 THE CLIMB 2,495c 全文，均长 603c]

```
THE PLACE. A rise of moraine above the tents at first light. Prayer flags stream sideways in a steady wind that blows TOWARD the mountain.
THE LIGHT. One source: the first morning sun, pale gold, low from the side. It lights the summit fully and the two figures gently. Nothing else gives off light.
THE CAMERA. It stands to the side of Arjun at chest height... It moves only once, following the ashes when they go. It never shakes and it never cuts.

HOLD THROUGHOUT: the same two people first frame to last. One single speed. The wind blows in one constant direction the whole shot: from behind them toward the mountain, always. The ashes never blow back. Neither ever looks into the lens.
DO NOT SHOW: subtitles, captions, watermarks, text of any kind, logos, any third person, tears streaming, the ashes blowing backward, slow motion, flicker.
```

均长 603c 的极简项目仍保完整立法密度：**THE PLACE./THE LIGHT./THE CAMERA.** 三段式（各一段）+ 恒定立法五项（人数/速度/风向/灰烬方向/视线）+ 一行否定清单 + 光源唯一性（Nothing else gives off light）+ 相机单次运动（moves only once）。**立法密度与提示词长度无关——603c 也能全维立法**。
