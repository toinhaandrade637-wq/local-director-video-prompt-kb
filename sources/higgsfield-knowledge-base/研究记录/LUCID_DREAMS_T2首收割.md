## 特色方法

### 1. 多镜头散文时间戳 [案例 345×]
> `[Shot 1: 0-7s] Wide tracking shot from behind JONATHAN <<<uuid>>> and ALORA <<<uuid>>>...`
> `[Shot 2: 7-13s] Cut to a close-up shot of JONATHAN's face in flight...`

**每镜独立时间戳 + 镜间转场动词（Cut to）**——散文体也能做到结构化模板的秒级精度。

### 2. 情感逐镜导演 [案例]
> `His face is deeply sad and thoughtful, but his eyes are filled with profound love.`
> `Upon hearing his words, she stops laughing and a gentle, soft smile stays on her lips.`
> `At 21.0s, JONATHAN's sad expression melts away, and he bursts into a genuine, loud, cheerful laugh.`

每镜的情绪转变精确描述——不是"happy"或"sad"而是**情感变化的因果链**。
