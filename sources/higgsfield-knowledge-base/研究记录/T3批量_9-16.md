### 1. GEOGRAPHY LOCK 地理锁定与背景防循环 [案例 tyanpa 5,624c 逐字，960 全唯一]

```
GEOGRAPHY LOCK — the grass bank beside the racing river: she runs LEFT TO RIGHT, the world behind
her — glittering rapids, misted meadow, backlit trees — smeared into horizontal streaks of light
and colour by the speed and the shallow focus. New background streaks every second; never cycling,
never repeating. The thing she searches for stays OFF-SCREEN ahead and is never revealed.
She never looks at the lens.
```

地理锁四件：①空间方向（LEFT TO RIGHT）②背景物理行为（streaks smeared by speed + shallow focus）③**背景防循环立法**（New background streaks every second; never cycling, never repeating——AI 视频背景循环是高频失控面）④悬念物 Off-screen 立法 + 视线禁令。配套 CONSTRAINTS 超密度块（2.39:1/5s/ONE continuous shot/no cuts/Real time no speed ramps/EXTREME CLOSE-UP FOR EVERY FRAME/no wide reveals...）——**该项目 CONSTRAINTS×568 > STYLE×505 > CAMERA×482：约束=难点维度时约束块超密度**（a-life AUDIO 超 CAMERA 同律）。ACTING TASK 动物表演立法：`Hollywood animal-feature performance carried entirely in facial micro-mechanics... Nothing cartooned, nothing anthropomorphic. Nobody plays the emotion.`

### 2. HIGH/MID GUARD 武术架势阶梯 [案例 the-last-cure 11,160c 逐字，Arcane 风格]

```
HIGH GUARD — hard block above his head.
Immediately lower into MID GUARD — redirect the horizontal naginata.
Drop his center of gravity — retract his leg and narrowly avoid the LOW sweep.
Sharp pivot — intercept the SIDE thrust.
Keep the entire sequence fast, tense, and continuously animated.
No pose holds between defenses.
The apparitions continue attacking in overlapping combinations.
```

**架势阶梯防御链**（HIGH GUARD→MID GUARD→LOW sweep→SIDE thrust 四段，每段=架势名+物理动作+对应攻击）+ **No pose holds between defenses**（防御间禁定格）+ 攻击方重叠组合（overlapping combinations）。K14 打戏页防御性编舞的新形态（此前 P1-P6 为进攻编舞）。

### 3. IMPORTANT MOTION STYLE 帧率动画立法 [案例 the-last-cure]

```
IMPORTANT MOTION STYLE:
This is stylized 3D CG animation, NOT 2D animation.
Animate every movement continuously at smooth 24 FPS.
Full-frame interpolation between poses, fluid 3D body mechanics, continuous camera movement,
smooth cloth physics, smooth hair physics and coherent weapon motion.
NO stepped animation, NO animation-on-twos, NO held poses during action, NO choppy frame cadence,
NO stop-motion feeling, NO 2D cel-animation timing.
Keep the painterly Arcane-inspired rendering and textures, but movement must feel like
high-end fluid 3D cinematic animation.
```

**帧率动画专项正解**（覆盖面审判"动画帧率专项"空洞）：风格与运动分离立法——渲染保持 painterly（Arcane 手绘风）但运动必须 24 FPS 全帧插值 3D 流体；六重否定（stepped/on-twos/held poses/choppy cadence/stop-motion/2D cel timing）。**风格本体≠运动时序**——手绘画风可以配 3D 流体运动。配套 CLANG. 拟声镜头节拍块（块头=金属撞击声，块体=该撞击瞬间的镜头——**声音驱动剪辑的块化形态**）+ 虚体分身物理（one real performer manifests 3 extra translucent smoky apparitions... volumetric smoke and fading particles——一真三虚+被击碎成体积烟雾）。
