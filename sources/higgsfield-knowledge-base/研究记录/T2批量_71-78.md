## 核心发现（全部原文 verbatim）

### 1. 编辑法律块族（LAW 后缀命名）[案例 i-can-carry-us-both 9,942c 逐字]

- **RELIGHT LAW**：`both boys are lit ENTIRELY by the night of <<<video_1>>> and by nothing else`——光源唯一性 + 每件服装在夜光下的具体显色立法（`brick-red sweatshirt reads as a deep desaturated dark red barely warmer than the night`）+ 画外光源禁令（`No studio key, no fill, no rim from any lamp outside the picture`）
- **REPLACEMENT LAW**：`this is an edit of <<<video_1>>> and it is a change of AGE, not a change of cast. Nothing but the two people is replaced, added or removed.`——替换范围立法（只换人+年龄）+ 换掉物逐项列举 + 防混淆（`The only hood in the picture is the boy's own charcoal hood — never a sweater, never fabric worn on the body`）+ `KEEP EVERYTHING ELSE OF <<<video_1>>> UNCHANGED`（连 same bokeh 都锁）

**LAW 后缀 = 编辑操作的成文法典命名**（RELIGHT LAW/REPLACEMENT LAW/CAMERA LAW/CUT LAW/MANIFESTATION LAW）——比 LOCK 更法典化：LOCK 锁状态，LAW 立行为规则。

### 2. 双角色身份交换防护极端完整版 [案例 beyond-the-rift-a 9,085c 全文逐字]

```
# SCENE 13 — FINAL_BEAST_01 ARRIVES
# STRICT CHARACTER + WARDROBE CONTINUITY VERSION
EXACT DURATION: 20.00 SECONDS.
==================================================
CHARACTER_A — ABSOLUTE IDENTITY LOCK
==================================================
Preserve EXACTLY:
same adult male identity, same facial structure, same dense full dark beard,
same beard length and density, same short dark hair, same hairline,
same eyebrows, same eyes, same nose, same skin tone, same body proportions.

CHARACTER_A WARDROBE IS PERMANENT:
- black hooded field jacket / jacket OPEN at the front / hood DOWN, never covering his head...

CHARACTER_A MUST NEVER WEAR:
a button-up shirt, a dress shirt, CHARACTER_B's clothing, armor, cloak...

Battle continuity:
A's existing black jacket may contain subtle accumulated dust, mud and minor
battle wear ONLY. Do NOT change its cut, shape, material or design.
==================================================
NEVER SWAP A AND B
==================================================
A = DENSE BEARD + HOODED BLACK JACKET + BLACK T-SHIRT.
B = SHORTER/LIGHTER BEARD + BLACK BUTTON-UP COLLARED SHIRT + NO JACKET.
These combinations are PERMANENT.
Never create: A's face on B's body, B's face on A's body, mixed facial features...
```

结构五件套：①场景标题+版本声明（# 前缀）②EXACT DURATION 精确到百分秒 ③每人 ABSOLUTE IDENTITY LOCK（11 项 Preserve EXACTLY 逐项）④服装 PERMANENT 清单+MUST NEVER WEAR 清单 ⑤NEVER SWAP 区（组合公式+交换禁令）。外加 **ABSOLUTE NEGATIVE / ERROR PREVENTION 大区**（30+ 条 NO 分组禁令：服装组/面部组/…）+ 磨损许可立法（battle wear ONLY + 不得改剪裁材质）+ 物理撞击立法（`fracture originates EXACTLY from the physical impact point. Only the damaged section collapses.`）+ 终态立法（`END EXACTLY AT 20.00 SECONDS.` + `CUT TO BLACK.`）。

**完整句块头风格**：块头=完整祈使句（END EXACTLY AT 20.00 SECONDS. / REAL ACTORS. / NO JACKET. / CUT TO BLACK.）——39 条提示词全唯一 1.0× 手写项目。

### 3. 品牌广告参照法 + 缩略图优化 [案例 side-b 31,820c 超长样本]

- **品牌等级参照**：`the level of controlled, moody lighting used in a Cartier, Chanel, or Rolex campaign film`——用奢侈品牌广告片定光线等级（可执行的"高级感"锚点）
- **黑位保留立法**：`Deep, rich blacks with genuine shadow detail retained (not crushed to flat black), controlled specular highlights on metal, fabric, and skin rather than blown-out glow`
- **thumbnail-optimized**：`reads as one calm, cohesive, expensive-looking photograph... even at small size`——缩略图可读性立法（社媒分发场景）
- **负空间构图**：`noticeably smaller than your first instinct` + `Do not fill the frame edge-to-edge under any circumstances` + photo booth strip 比喻
- **CHANGE n 编号编辑块**：CHANGE 1×35/CHANGE 2…——多条编辑指令编号立法
- STRICT EXCLUSIONS：排除块的更严格命名（NEGATIVE/NEGATIVES/AVOID/FORBIDDEN/STRICT EXCLUSIONS 五名并存）

### 4. 低多边形风格的身份立法 [案例 the-coat 6,189c 逐字，840 全唯一 1.0×]

```
FORBIDDEN: no realistic hair strands, no fine individual hairs, no rendered hair
fibres or groomed strand geometry — the hair is painted with big visible
brushstrokes, and those brushstrokes must be large and countable, never fine or hair-like
```
```
the polygonal planes of the face are part of his identity and must be kept
unchanged: never smoothed away, never rounded off, never softened into a
continuous surface, and never covered over with added texture
```

**反真实+正面替代成对立法**（禁写实发丝→笔画必须大且可数）+ **几何面=身份**（low-poly 项目的身份不是皮肤纹理而是多边形面——身份立法随风格本体变化）。FORBIDDEN 否定块第六名。WARDROBE 逐件+尺寸关系立法（`clearly one full size too big for him — the shoulders drop past his own, the sleeves reach past his wrists`）。

### 5. 叙事哲学立法 [案例 pick-me 17,997c markdown 散文]

- **镜头顺序=叙事**：`★★ THE PAN GOES OLDEST → NEWEST... THE ORDER IS THE STORY. It is the only thing telling the audience this [story]`
- **隐喻法条**：`The drawing is the promise; she is the fact.`（画中女孩→真女孩切换的叙事承诺结构）
- **不可见事物两元素替代**：`**3 · NO ADULT IS EVER SEEN.** The argument is a sound behind a door and a hard strip of warm light underneath it.`——不拍吵架，拍门后声音+门缝光条（暗示立法）
- **场景元素必要性**：`A shot of this room without them is a different room.`——贴画必须出现在每个房间镜头（"缺了就不是这个房间"立法）
- **markdown 法条标记**：★/⚠️/编号加粗（`**3 · NO ADULT IS EVER SEEN.**`）——散文体内嵌分级标记法
