## → 视频/05-多主体调度（换人微指令句式族 · 已成熟可集成）

### 换人微指令句式族五变体+三形态 [案例 博客池 B1/B2 · 池内 3-1.8K 赞实证]

> A：Change the person in the video to the person in @Image 1（aiartistlab）
> B：replace the person in @Video 1 with @image（wypd）
> C：replace the person in the video with attached image of a person（sadiesfaction）
> D：replace the person in the video with identity from attached image（oblivion）
> E（全规格）：Replace the only person in the source video with the person from the uploaded reference photo, regardless of gender, ethnicity, skin tone, height or body type. Match their individual facial features, natural skin tone, visible age, hairstyle, hair texture, hair color and facial hair. Preserve their recognizable appearance throughout every turn, including profile and rear views. Do not retain the source person's face or impose their physique on the replacement.（TWILIGHT）

**好在哪**：五个省力档位——一句话换人（A-D）到全规格合同（E：六属性无视+八要素匹配+全转身保持+否定保留双保险）；E 是 1.8K-289 赞顶流复用的范本。
**迁移**：快改用 A-D；正式项目用 E 全规格；多主体分区（主角/群演分参考）加防串用令（见下）。

### 配套微规则族 [案例 B1/B2]

- **replacement not transformation 定性声明**：This is a character replacement, not an on-screen transformation + 须首帧在场（must already be present in the first frame）[kaye BOB & CHATGPT]
- **身高推断禁令**：If the photo shows only a face, use natural, anatomically plausible proportions; do not assume an exact height from a face photo [EVERYTHING-I-AM 1.8K]
- **动物解剖清除六残余**：no remaining fur, muzzle, animal ears, paws, or tail [kaye BOB & CHATGPT]
- **参考权限 identity only**：Use the selfie for identity only; do not copy its background, pose, lighting, or framing [kaye]
- **服装继承+合身适配**：Keep the source outfit's design and colors: red sleeveless top, plaid skirt…Fit these clothes naturally to the replacement person's body [EVERYTHING-I-AM]
- **接触点物理三件**：realistic hand-to-chair, foot-to-ground and seated contact [EVERYTHING-I-AM]
- **分区替换+防串用**：MAIN CHARACTER→Ref1 / SURROUNDING→Ref2 + Do not apply Reference 2 to the main character or Reference 1 to background characters [mrusama 六段大写块]
- **动作迁移措辞对**：use @Video 1 as the strict reference video, and copy the same motion [rishabh]
- **手绘风格保护版**：TEXTURE AND HATCHING ARE PROTECTED（排线六保护：direction/spacing/density/thickness/contrast/irregularity）+ 反摄影渗入（Do not transfer photographic skin…No beauty retouching, smoothing, denoising, airbrushing）+ **保真优先于相似度**（Prioritize preservation of the original drawing and texture over stronger facial resemblance）+ 最小改动域 [kaye GOTHAM]

### 句柄时间戳语法 [案例 kaye HOTEL LOBBY 1.4K/kentdhani 494]

句柄后加 · Ns 锚定参考视频特定时长；另一变体：use specifically its first 10 seconds [mrusama]。Keep/Replace 分账（Keep the man, his face…exactly as in this clip. Replace the studio, the mannequin…）[kentdhani]。

### 台词过度表演病与静默立法互证 [案例 kyulak777]

写台词给模型→音乐剧演员病（两take都瞪眼张嘴）；规则=** 입은 닫고（闭嘴），眼令+慢眨眼만，대사는 나중에 목소리로 얹는다（台词后期配音）**——第三take出完美 deadpan。与视频/07 静默立法、表情克制条互证。

### 成片必须亲耳听 [案例 kyulak777 NaN 事故]
