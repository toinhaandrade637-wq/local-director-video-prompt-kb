## 核心发现（全部原文 verbatim）

### 1. 时间线性立法 + 打击乐四层配比 [案例 no-room-for-two 18,101c 逐字，8,589c 均长]

```
SHOT HEADER — FINAL 20-SECOND SEQUENCE: EQUAL CONFRONTATION, MID-AIR PUNCH,
IMPACT-TRIGGERED PAINTERLY TRANSFORMATION AND MULTI-ANGLE 2D LANDING
...
The action must always move forward in time.
Never rewind.
Never replay the punch.
Never reset to an earlier pose.
Never show the same impact twice.
```
- **SHOT HEADER=序列级块头**（镜头序+全段四阶段枚举）——多镜头动作序列的总标题块
- **时间线性五重禁令**（move forward/never rewind/never replay/never reset/never show same impact twice）——多角度打斗的高频失控面（模型会重放冲击）

```
Punch: Synchronize one extremely heavy central punch sound exactly with face contact.
Combine a compact strike transient, dense body-impact layer, short low-frequency shock
and restrained high-frequency crack.
```
- **一拳四层声学配比**（strike transient + body-impact layer + low-frequency shock + high-frequency crack）——单个打击音效的分层配方
- **NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS 动作否定枚举**：攻击形态逐项禁（No slap/No open-paw swat/No double-paw attack/No two-paw push/No chest shove）+ 行为否定组（No submissive Kekik/No stepping backward/No lowering head in fear）+ 时间否定组（No action replay/No rewind/No temporal reset）——猫打架动作空间的全枚举否定
- **三音频块并存**：SOUND×48（配乐+音效）/SOUND DESIGN×32（设计层）/AUDIO×44（通用）——同项目三块名并存、按内容分工
- ELEMENT LOCK：动物角色锁定（`Preserve her exact adult Turkish Van identity: white fur, exact ginger forehead markings...`）

### 2. 三面板硬锁与装备参考分工 [案例 you-had-to 5,754c 全文]

```
CRITICAL:
EXACTLY 3 PANELS.
NO MORE.
NO LESS.
The SAME MAYA must appear in all three panels.
```
```
PRESENTATION
Professional AAA cinematic character equipment reference sheet.
Exactly three panels:
1. FRONT FULL BODY
2. REAR 3/4 FULL BODY
3. CLOSE-UP PORTRAIT
```
- **面板数完整句三连硬锁**（EXACTLY 3 PANELS./NO MORE./NO LESS.）+ 跨面板同一性锁（The SAME MAYA in all three panels）
- **PRESENTATION 块**=参考表规格声明（面板枚举+背景+质量等级 "AAA cinematic"+比例）
- **双参考分工**：REFERENCE 1=角色身份（EXACT MAYA character identity）/ REFERENCE 2=MASTER diving equipment design——身份与装备分参考（K16 分参考路线新形态）
- **装备替换立法**：`Replace the standard open-face scuba mask... with a FULL-FACE PROFESSIONAL DIVING COMMUNICATION MASK` + 15 件装备全清单 + 风格锚定否定（`NOT futuristic technology`）

### 3. NO BGM 22 连禁令与画面内节拍源 [案例 roraima 19,620c 逐字，585 全唯一]

```
NO BGM — CRITICAL, ABSOLUTE: this sequence has NO background music of any kind at any point...
No score, no soundtrack, no instrumental, no underscore, no ambient musical pad, no drone,
no tone bed, no swell, no sting, no rise, no hit, no percussion, no strings, no flute, no drums,
no chanting, no vocals, no humming, no singing, no whistling, no lyrics, no melodic element of
any kind, no tonal atmosphere...
```
- **22 连音乐形态否定**（petya 11 连的翻倍，音乐失控面全枚举）+ CRITICAL, ABSOLUTE 双标记
- **画面内节拍源立法**：`THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. Dust kicks up beneath their feet. The rhythm builds naturally from the crowd itself — still NO MUSIC.`——节奏来自画面内人群脚步（THUMP. 拟声×3 重复）而非配乐——**无配乐场景的节奏解决方案**

### 4. TOP PRIORITY 距离优先权立法 [案例 ego 3,797c 逐字]

```
TOP PRIORITY — THE CAMERA NEVER APPROACHES: the camera stays far away from the couple for the
ENTIRE shot and NEVER gets closer — no forward movement, no push-in, no dolly-in, no zoom,
no travelling toward them at any moment, not even during the burst or the reaction.
The couple remains small, distant figures the whole time. The ONLY camera motion permitted is
a slow vertical crane down (a gentle descending move). NO close-up, NO medium shot, ever.
This distance rule overrides everything else.
```
- **距离优先权五重运镜禁令**（no forward/push-in/dolly-in/zoom/travelling）+ 唯一许可运动（slow vertical crane down）+ 景别全禁（NO close-up, NO medium shot, ever）+ **overrides everything else 优先权声明**（与其他规则冲突时距离规则胜出——规则权重立法首见）
- **首帧绝对优先**：`the shot must begin on the very first frame already as an extreme macro close-up of the red balloon completely filling the screen, with the word "EGO" — stencilled in cracked, peeling white paint — spanning the frame... No establishing wide shot, no other subject, no lead-in`——首帧即满屏特写+片名字样入画+无引入
- **参考图用途反转**：`Use <<<image_5>>> for the position of the 2 characters and the size of the 2 balloons. Do not use <<<image_5>>> in the final image.`——构图板用但不入终图（ANERNEQ DO NOT START FROM THE ATTACHED FRAME 同族细化）
- DO NOT 完整句祈使族（DO NOT ZOOM / DO NOT MAKE THE BALLOONS BIG 独立块头）
