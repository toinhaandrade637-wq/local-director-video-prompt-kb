## 核心发现（全部原文 verbatim）

### 1. 手绘动画三法 [案例 a-long-lunch 13,861c 逐字]

```
BOIL. The drawing is re-made every frame — lines, watercolour edges and marks wandering slightly
frame to frame. Backgrounds boil at the same rate as the characters so they read as part of the
same drawing rather than a held plate. Design, proportions, palette and composition stay locked;
only the marks are re-made.
```
```
AMBIENT LIFE. The environment is continuously alive, especially through the motionless passages.
A steady breeze moves across the ground. Grass tufts bend and spring back, shrub leaves tick and
turn, loose seed heads travel across frame...
STILLNESS IS NEVER FROZEN. Even in the statue he keeps continuous small surface [motion]...
```
- **BOIL 手绘沸腾律**：逐帧重绘笔触（lines wandering slightly frame to frame）+ **背景与角色同速沸腾**（read as part of the same drawing rather than a held plate——防"贴图感"）+ 设计锁定只重绘笔触（Design/proportions/palette/composition stay locked; only the marks are re-made）
- **AMBIENT LIFE + 静止不冻结**：环境持续活体（breeze/grass/seed heads）"especially through the motionless passages"——**静止段落反而是环境生命立法重点**；雕像角色也保持 continuous small surface motion
- **CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE**：`Three shots, hard cuts only. The first frame already contains both animals in full.` + 命名分镜 `[SHOT 1 — 0:00–0:08 — the low tracking wide]` + **⚠️跟拍锁定**（`THE CAMERA TRACKS SIDEWAYS TO SCREEN-RIGHT ALONGSIDE HER at exactly her pace, holding her PINNED IN THE SAME PLACE IN FRAME — the same position, the same size, never drifting toward an edge. The LANDSCAPE STREAMS PAST BEHIND HER`）+ **THE STAGING 双机位朝向对照**（She in SIDE PROFILE / He FRONT-ON, NOT in profile）
- HARD CUT. 独立块 + SHOT 2 头部完全锁定（`His head is COMPLETELY LOCKED — it does not move, turn, tilt or track for the whole shot. Only the ruff stirs in the breeze.`）

### 2. 一镜到底延展链与标题入画 [案例 not-a-monster-movie 15,872c 逐字，video_extension×80 主力]

```
Continue exactly and seamlessly from the previous shot.
This new 15-second extension is also ONE CONTINUOUS UNBROKEN TAKE WITH NO CUTS.
NO CUTS. NO HIDDEN CUTS. NO DISSOLVES. NO MORPHS. NO TRANSITIONS.
NO TELEPORTATION. NO IMPOSSIBLE CAMERA REPOSITIONING.
```
```
ABSOLUTELY NO MUSIC OF ANY KIND FROM THE FIRST FRAME TO THE LAST FRAME.
RAW PRODUCTION AUDIO ONLY.
NO SCORE. / NO BACKGROUND MUSIC. / NO CINEMATIC MUSIC. / NO HORROR MUSIC. / NO EMOTIONAL MUSIC.
NO DRONE. / NO PIANO. / NO STRINGS. / NO PERCUSSION. / NO RHYTHMIC PULSE. / NO RISER. / NO STINGER.
NO BASS HIT. / NO MUSICAL SOUND DESIGN.
THE AUDIO MUST SOUND LIKE RAW SOUND RECORDED LIVE ON LOCATION.
ONLY SOUNDS PHYSICALLY EXISTING INSIDE THE SCENE.
```
- **延展续接七连否定**（cuts/hidden cuts/dissolves/morphs/transitions/teleportation/impossible repositioning）——video_extension 一镜到底链的每段重申
- **RAW 现场声立法**：音乐按类型逐类禁（cinematic/horror/emotional 分型 + riser/stinger/bass hit 音效库术语禁 15 连）+ `RAW SOUND RECORDED LIVE ON LOCATION` + `ONLY SOUNDS PHYSICALLY EXISTING INSIDE THE SCENE`
- **标题入画**：`Large real LED news panels in the street also clearly and stably display: NO MONSTER MOVIES. Correct spelling. The words are physically part of the environment. No subtitles. No graphic overlays. No floating text.`——**项目标语作为场景内 LED 屏内容**（世界内文字+拼写正确立法+物理在场+三重覆盖层禁令）
- **指向关系三行图**：`The attacker deliberately points it toward the MONSTER'S SHOULDER. NOT HIS FACE. NOT HIS HEAD. NOT HIS CHEST. The visual relationship must be unmistakable: ATTACKER → VISIBLE GUN → MONSTER'S SHOULDER.`——视觉关系箭头图式立法 + `The dramatic focus is NOT the weapon`（戏剧焦点转移）
- **人群自然律**：`Everybody remains in physical motion... Nobody behaves like a synchronized group of extras. Everything feels uncontrolled, spontaneous and completely real.`——群演去同步化立法
- 光源全实体：`Lighting comes entirely from believable practical sources inside the environment: streetlights, cinema marquees, LED screens, headlights... The monster naturally moves through different pools of illumination.`——移动穿行光池立法

### 3. 剧本 slugline 融入与帧率变化脚本 [案例 emberdant 5,562c 逐字，385 全唯一]

```
SCENE HEADING: EXT. THE RAVEN'S DUE — DECK @deck — CONTINUOUS — THE RELEASE
INTERNAL PACING:
• 30:00 – 30:08 — She shreds free of the net by pure will @lyra_netbreak — and is in the air...
• 30:08 – 30:14 — The burst of magic bows the hydra backward. Not a blast — a pressure, a visible
concussion of pale copper light that pushes the air and folds the creature's necks away from the ship.
```
```
CAMERA DIRECTION:
• THE BREAK: 96fps, three seconds (designated ramp) — the net shredding outward...
This is the film's Lyra hero-frame — the poster shot for her.
• SNAP TO 24fps for the note. Full speed, full violence.
• THE CONCUSSION: shoot the air, not the light — fog blown outward in a ring...
```
- **SCENE HEADING=剧本 slugline 融入**（EXT./场景名/@deck 场景锚/CONTINUOUS/段落名——电影剧本格式进提示词）
- **@标签语义化扩展**：@deck（场景）/ @lyra_netbreak（动作时刻）——标签不止标记资产还标记叙事时刻
- **INTERNAL PACING 秒级节奏脚本**+戏剧行为定性（`Not a blast — a pressure`——"不是什么，是什么"定性法）
- **CAMERA DIRECTION 帧率变化脚本**：96fps 三秒 designated ramp（hero-frame 海报镜头）→ SNAP TO 24fps（full violence）——**升格镜头有指定时长与回归点**
- **拍摄对象立法**：`shoot the air, not the light`——物理后果优先于视觉特效
- CREATURE REALISM：`true-to-nature scale and hide texture, wet and matte surfaces, anatomically plausible weight`

### 4. 引用权限双块与三重连续性 [案例 yin-yang-agency 4,389c 逐字]

```
REFERENCE PRIORITY:
IMAGE 1 defines the exact rainy-night alley continuity, Mr Lam's dark suit... IMAGE 2 defines
Mr Lam's exact facial identity... Do not reproduce the character sheet, text, studio background
or collage.
STORY MOMENT: The exchange has finished... a restrained, calculating smile of greed slowly appears...
This is not a friendly, joyful or comedic smile. His lips remain mostly closed, with only one
corner of his mouth lifting slightly...
STRICT CONTINUITY: Exactly one Mr Lam in the foreground. At most one distant, blurred mysterious
courier in the background. Exactly one black umbrella and one closed wooden box...
The yellow sealing talisman remains intact. Mr Lam is turning to leave, not turning back...
The expression is a subtle greedy smirk, not a broad smile, laughter, fear or sadness.
No exaggerated villain expressions.
```
- **REFERENCE PRIORITY/AUTHORITY 双块**：每图 defines 逐项声明 + **参考图使用边界**（`Do not reproduce the character sheet, text, studio background or collage`——参考图的非内容要素禁入）
- **STORY MOMENT 剧情时刻块**：剧情位置（The exchange has finished）+ 情绪定性（not a friendly, joyful or comedic smile——四态否定）+ 嘴部物理（mostly closed, one corner lifting）
- **STRICT CONTINUITY 三重连续性**：计数锁（Exactly one/At most one 量化）+ 状态锁（talisman remains intact）+ 表情边界（subtle smirk, not broad/laughter/fear/sadness）

### 5. 色彩豁免立法 [案例 and-still-life 10,436c 逐字]

```
NO FACE DRIFT.
NO DIFFERENT DRESS.
NO GROOM VISIBLE.
...
NO FULL-COLOR WORLD.
NO NATURAL SKIN COLOR.
...
ONLY BLUE/CORAL PAINT HAS COLOR.
NO GLOW. NO MAGIC. NO NEON. NO MUSIC.
```
```
CRITICAL: herself remains completely BLACK AND WHITE.
ONLY actual BLUE/CORAL paint physically adhering to: her shoes, the lower hem of her dress,
the flooded ground may appear in color.
```
- **色彩豁免清单**：黑白世界的唯一色彩例外=真实附着的颜料（ONLY BLUE/CORAL PAINT HAS COLOR）——**选择性色彩（辛德勒名单式）的否定清单形态**；CRITICAL 块把豁免点逐处枚举（鞋/裙摆/积水地面三处）
- NO FACE DRIFT.×66 脸部漂移否定块（一致性否定专项化）
- NO MUSIC.×197/NO SCORE.×112/ULTRA×162——否定与质量双超密度（每条近 1 NO MUSIC+0.5 NO SCORE+0.5 ULTRA）
