## 核心发现（全部原文 verbatim）

### 1. ELEMENT AUTHORITY：@标签权限声明 [案例 oops 3,945c 逐字]

```
ELEMENT AUTHORITY:
@loc_loc_floor4_work-room controls ONLY the room: cramped apartment work room, walls, desk, shelves, clutter.
@char_char_floor4_adult-worker controls ONLY the man — early thirties, tired face, build, clothing.
@prop_prop_floor4_sketchbook controls ONLY the sketchbook's design: open page of childlike doodles...
```

**引用分工的权限合同化**：每个 @标签后接 `controls ONLY <范围>`——引用件不得越权（参考图只管它该管的）。且标签自带**类型前缀系统**：`@loc_`（场地）/`@char_`（角色）/`@prop_`（道具）+ 项目内编号（floor4_work-room）——比 SEED_AUDIO 的锚点三类型（identity lock/prop anchor/environment anchor）更进一步的命名化引用体系。

### 2. DIRECTING LINE：导演题旨行 [案例 oops]

```
DIRECTING LINE:
There is nobody to fight. The thing he gave up was in the room the whole time, under the bills.
```

**一句话主题陈述独立成块**——给模型的戏剧意图（不是画面描述，是"这场戏在讲什么"）。与 ACTING TASK（导演动作意图）、FINAL FEELING（情绪终态）三足鼎立。

### 3. 情绪来源七要素立法 [案例 bill VOICE 块]

```
NO MUSIC, or at most the faintest almost imperceptible emotional swell consistent with the rest of the film.
The emotion comes entirely from CAMERA DISTANCE, CUT TIMING, SLOW MOTION, SCALE, BODY LANGUAGE, SOUND PERSPECTIVE and STILLNESS.
```

**把"感动"分解为七个可执行的电影手段**（机位距离/切点时机/慢动作/体量对比/肢体语言/声音透视/静止）——情绪不是写"感人"而是立法情绪的**来源清单**。配套音乐弹性许可（NO MUSIC, or at most the faintest...——禁令带例外档位）+ 表演克制否定（No exaggerated acting. No superhero pose. No celebratory gestures.）。

### 4. 多相机编号与续接立法 [案例 permanent 3,856c 逐字]

```
CONTINUATION — A SEAMLESS CUT. The first frame is IDENTICAL to the previous shot's last frame:
same LOW camera looking UP at the man, same framing and light. Eight seconds. TWO cameras, one declared CUT at 2.5s.

CAMERA A, 0.0-2.5s — THE LOW ANGLE UP AT HIM, head and face in frame, in the RIGHT THIRD.
MOUTH SHUT, no teeth ever. HIS EYES AIM DOWN INTO THE LOWER LEFT CORNER throughout Camera A — he never looks up or at the camera.

FRAME LEFT — THE FATHER'S LEGS ONLY, cut off above the knee. No torso, no head in Camera B.
MIDDLE — THE TABLE: an ORDINARY LOW TABLE KNOCKED ONTO ITS SIDE... NOT angled toward the back wall,
NOT turned toward the drafting board, NOT rotated at all. It changes only when his foot moves it.
```

- **CONTINUATION 续接块**：首帧=上一镜末帧 IDENTICAL（镜间无缝合同）+ 多相机总声明（TWO cameras, one declared CUT at 2.5s）
- **CAMERA A/B 编号+秒区间块头**：`CAMERA A, 0.0-2.5s`——单镜内多相机分段立法（video_extension×70 主力的延展项目）
- **画幅三分区**：FRAME LEFT/FRAME RIGHT/MIDDLE 各区内容+裁切立法（legs ONLY, cut off above the knee）
- **物体运动唯一驱动**：`It changes only when his foot moves it` + 四重朝向禁令（NOT angled/NOT turned/NOT rotated...）
- 视线与嘴部禁令：`HIS EYES AIM DOWN INTO THE LOWER LEFT CORNER throughout Camera A` / `MOUTH SHUT, no teeth ever`
