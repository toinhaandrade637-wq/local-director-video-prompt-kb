## 核心发现（全部原文 verbatim）

### 1. 音频立法两极：EXCLUSIVE AUDIO LOCK vs COMPLETE SOUNDTRACK LOCK [案例 where-letters-fall 同项目两块并存]

**独占音频锁**（无配乐封闭型）：
```
EXCLUSIVE AUDIO LOCK
The entire soundtrack consists solely of the specified door movement, porch contacts and creaks,
running footsteps, grit scuffs, breathing, clothing friction, Betsy's single spoken line, wind
through leaves, and sparse bird calls.
Every sound follows its physical source, timing, distance, and intensity. Use only these sounds,
with natural pauses between individual events. Maintain their spatial continuity and restrained
levels through the final frame.
```

**完整配乐锁**（重制型）：
```
COMPLETE SOUNDTRACK LOCK
Create every sound as a new synchronized recording for this scene. <<<video_2>>> guides Betsy's
vocal identity only. <<<video_3>>> guides forest appearance only. Neither reference contributes
recorded audio.
00.00–04.00
Begin with a brief door-latch click and a short wooden hinge creak as Will pushes the door open...
```

同一项目两块并存：**独占型**（entire soundtrack = 白名单声源 + Every sound follows its physical source + natural pauses + restrained levels）与**重制型**（Create every sound as a new synchronized recording + 引用件明确"不给音频"）——音频立法的完整的两极形态。K17 音频谱系补全：从"无音频块"→"一行否定"→"AUDIO 块"→"禁令清单"→"独占锁"→"完整重制锁"六层。

### 2. CAMERA CHARACTER 相机人格化 + LAYER SEPARATION 粒子景深 [案例 nadia-7-of-9 5,455c 逐字]

```
GLOBAL OPTICS / CAMERA CHARACTER
Use natural anamorphic cinema optics throughout... Favor 65mm–85mm anamorphic primes, shot close
to wide open... The camera is operated by a skilled human operator responding physically to Nadia
and Sophie in real time.
Do not use mechanically perfect subject tracking, stabiliz[ation]...
```
```
LAYER SEPARATION
Maintain a subtle atmospheric haze throughout the room to create depth and separation between
foreground, midground, and background planes.
The air should contain dry suspended particles, like fine dust, not smoke, fog, steam...
The distribution of particles should vary by depth:
Foreground: very few particles, small and only occasionally visible near lens
Midground: ...
```

- **相机人格化**：相机=熟练人类操作者（responding physically in real time），禁机械完美跟踪/稳定——相机行为有"人格"
- **层分离立法**：空气粒子作为景深工具（dry suspended particles, like fine dust, NOT smoke/fog/steam 三重排除）+ **粒子按景深分级分布**（前景少而小/中景…）——用大气颗粒做前景中景背景分离的可执行配方

### 3. 文字禁令全清单 20 项 [案例 everything-nice 19,259c 逐字]

```
NO ON-SCREEN TEXT — CRITICAL: no on-screen text of any kind anywhere in frame at any point.
No captions, no subtitles, no burned-in dialogue, no auto-captions, no karaoke text, no lower thirds,
no titles, no title cards, no credits, no watermarks, no logos, no timecode, no UI overlays,
no social-media overlays, no interface elements, no Chinese characters, no Korean characters.

全库最全的**文字禁令清单**（20 项：从 captions 到 karaoke text 到 lower thirds 到 timecode 到 UI overlays 到中韩文字符）+ 封闭句（clean of all overlay graphics from first frame to last）。配套 `THE BITE IS REAL — CRITICAL`（物理真实性 CRITICAL 块族）。ASSETS 块：角色资产卡（身高体型+永久物件 `A small silver hoop in each lobe, permanent and present in every frame`）。

```
Model: Seedance 2.5

**七段冒号分节散文**（Model/Aspect/Duration/Reference role/Style & Mood/Camera/Dynamic Description）+ **Ref 缩写锚点**（Ref CH/Ref LEG/Ref TB + anchors 动词——引用件的"角色"命名法，比 @image_N 更语义化）+ **笑点来源立法**（humor comes entirely from total lack of reaction + nature documentary 视角）+ **单镜喜剧法**（single held shot lets a deadpan joke land without any edit softening it）。
