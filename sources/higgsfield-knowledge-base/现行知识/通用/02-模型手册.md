## 1. 模型路由制（生图侧）

### 兼容序四路由 [官方 cineimage-soul v2.7 逐字]

> 1. Soul or Soul Cinema for new cinematic generation. 2. Seedream 4.5 Edit for compatible supplied-image edits. 3. Nano Banana Pro. 4. GPT Image.
**好在哪**：官方给出明确的模型兼容顺序——新生成走 Soul 系，供图编辑走 Seedream 4.5 Edit，再往后才是 Nano Banana Pro / GPT Image；edit-only 路由不用于新生成。
**迁移**：生图任务先判断「新生成还是改图」——新生成选 Soul/Soul Cinema，改图选 Seedream 4.5 Edit；用户点名不在兼容序的模型时不复述其名，选首兼容路由继续。

### 模型信任边界 [官方 cineimage-soul v2.7 逐字]

> CONTROL THE IMAGE'S LOGIC, CONTENT, AND CAMERA. DIRECT THE LIGHT. DO NOT SIMULATE THE RENDERER.
**好在哪**：对 Soul 系模型，曝光、材质响应、纹理、色彩微调、高光滚降、阴影、成片感都是模型自管——提示词只给场景逻辑与艺术指导，不给渲染配方（微对比图/降噪/高光保护层全禁）。
**迁移**：写生图提示词时问一句「这是场景逻辑还是渲染器操作？」——后者删掉；材质写物理现实（raw gray concrete with faint tire marks）不写渲染操作。

## 2. Seedance 2.0 平台硬约束

### 输入与时长上限 [官方 seedance-prompt-gen 逐字]

> Image input: ≤ 9 images. Video input: ≤ 3 videos, total ≤ 15 seconds. Audio input: ≤ 3 MP3 files, total ≤ 15 seconds. Total mixed files: ≤ 12. Generation duration: 4–15 seconds (user chooses).
**好在哪**：平台侧硬约束一次给全——参考图最多 9 张、视频参考最多 3 段共 15 秒、音频参考最多 3 段共 15 秒、混传总数 12、单代时长 4-15 秒。
**迁移**：多资产项目先按上限分桶——超 9 图拆代；超 15s 视频参考剪精華；时长规划落在 4-15s 区间内。

### 双入口与六大能力 [官方 seedance-prompt-gen 逐字]

> Entry points: "All-in-One Reference" (multimodal) or "First and Last Frames" (image + prompt only). … Video extension: "extend @video1 by X seconds" — generation duration = new part length. … Character replacement: "Replace the [character] in @Video1 with [new character from @Image1]".
**好在哪**：平台有两入口（全模态参考 / 首尾帧）；六大原生能力可直接在提示词里调用——视频延长、两视频间插场景、换角色、连续动作衔接、一镜长拍、改既有视频、音乐对拍。
**迁移**：延长用 extend @video1 by X seconds（时长=新段长度）；换角色用 Replace the X in @Video1 with Y from @Image1；插场用 add a scene between @Video1 and @Video2。

## 3. Seedance 2.5 代际增量

### 720p 渲染与 8K 语义 [官方 prompt-builder-2-5 逐字]

> 2.5 renders at 720p — words like "8K" set detail intent, not output size; if native 4K is the requirement, generate on Seedance 2.0.
**好在哪**：2.5 的「8K」是细节意图词不是输出尺寸——要原生 4K 得回 2.0 代。写 8K 在 2.5 上不亏（提细节意图），但别指望分辨率。
**迁移**：2.5 项目技术尾栈写 8K 当细节意图；交付规格要 4K 时切 2.0 生成。

### 30 秒单代预算 [官方 prompt-builder-2-5 逐字]

> Duration budget (2.5): one generation runs up to 30 seconds. Plan the shot to use it — a full emotional arc, a 14-segment beat-cut music sequence, or one unbroken oner all fit in a single take. A 2-minute piece is 4 generations.
**好在哪**：2.5 单代上限从 15s 翻倍到 30s——完整情绪弧、14 段卡点音乐序列、一个不切断的长镜头都装得下；2 分钟=4 代。
**迁移**：多切序列先算总秒数——≤30s 一代装下；超了按叙事节拍切代，切点放在 HARD CUT 处。

### Voice Lock 声音随板锁定 [官方 prompt-builder-2-5 逐字]

> Seedance 2.5 locks the voice together with the appearance in the character sheet. Reuse the same sheet reference across generations and the voice stays consistent — describe the voice once in the tag line, not in every prompt.
**好在哪**：2.5 把声音和外观一起锁进角色板——同一块板跨代复用=声音自动一致；声音只在 tag 行写一次，不逐条重复。
**迁移**：有声台词项目建「外观+声音」合一的角色板，跨代复用同板；声音描述（音色/语速/口音）写在板行不写进每条正文。

### 2.5 强项清单（放心写） [官方 prompt-builder-2-5 逐字]

> Lean on what 2.5 does well — write these confidently instead of avoiding them: true lens behavior (a real fisheye warps the near hand before the face), mirror and floor reflections, lit crowds where extras act, one light source keeping its rhythm across every cut, the camera as a physical object (a phone set on a rock wobbles then locks; a drone catches lens droplets and wake shake), and 30-second single takes that carry a full emotional arc.
**好在哪**：官方明示六项 2.5 强项可以大胆写——真实镜头畸变（鱼眼先弯近处的手）、镜面地板反射、群演有戏、单光源跨切守恒、相机作为实体（手机放石头会晃后稳、无人机有镜片水珠和尾流震）、30s 长镜。其中「反射」与 2.0 的「避反射镜头」引擎律形成代际反转。
**迁移**：2.5 项目敢写反射镜头与实体相机细节；2.0 项目反射仍是禁区（见 §4）。

### V2V 律与失败协议 [官方 prompt-builder-2-5 逐字]

> The source clip must be at least 4 seconds long. Shorter clip → freeze-frame the last shot to pad it. Set the generation duration to match the reference (usually 4s). …declare it master: "master for camera, framing, focus, motion, expression timing, background, lighting, grain and duration." …if the same defect shows up across all four batches, the problem is the prompt or the source — more batching only burns credits. Usual cause: the action you want has no anchor in the source… Switch to image-to-video: screenshot a frame of the location, use it as the starting frame, and write the action from scratch. Plan a deliberate empty-frame pause (1–2 seconds of just the location) where you intend to stitch takes in the edit.
**好在哪**：V2V 三律（源≥4s 不足垫尾帧/代时长=参考时长/master 八项声明+单一改动其余全锁）+失败协议（四批同 defect=停烧 credit 转策略；无锚动作转 I2V 从截图首帧重写；缝合点预埋 1-2s 空帧停顿）。
**迁移**：V2V 前先查源长度与动作锚点；失败两次以上不蛮干——转 I2V 或改源；剪辑缝合点在提示词里预埋空帧。

## 4. Seedance 引擎硬律（2.0 Director）

### 动作节拍=意图+招式名 [官方 seedance-director-skill 逐字]

> Action beats = intent + named technique, not biomechanics. ✅ "spinning back kick connects." ❌ "left forearm rotates 45° to deflect the incoming right hook at wrist level." …Describe force and direction, not destruction sequence.
**好在哪**：引擎不消化生物力学描述——动作写「意图+招式名」，破坏写「力与方向」不写破坏序列；用户点名的招式要保留。
**迁移**：打戏写 "spinning back kick connects" 不写关节角度；撞车写 driven into the car, metal buckling 不写玻璃碎裂三段论。

### 空间连续性七律 [官方 seedance-director-skill 逐字]

> Spatial continuity breaks on cuts. Re-anchor positions and facing direction after any cut. …≤ 3 characters tracked across cuts. …Exit-frame = implicit cut. Character leaves frame → gone for remainder of shot. Never choreograph exit + re-entry in same continuous shot. …Off-screen = nonexistent. State changes must be shown on camera before being referenced. …Avoid reflection shots (in blades, puddles, mirrors) — Seedance breaks scene geography when rendering reflections.
**好在哪**：五条空间律——切后重锚（位置+朝向）、跨切跟踪≤3 角色、出画=隐式切（禁同镜出回）、画外=不存在（变化必须先上镜）、反射镜头禁区（反射破坏空间地理——2.0 代）。
**迁移**：每切后重写谁在哪朝哪；同镜不出回；重要变化必须拍到；2.0 不写镜面/刀面/水洼反射镜头（2.5 反转见 §3）。

### 双对比切镜强制 [官方 seedance-director-skill 逐字]

> Every cut changes both shot size and camera character. Shot-size scale: extreme wide → wide → medium → medium close-up → close-up → ECU. Camera modes: Handheld | Static/locked-off | Stabilized tracking | Crane/vertical | Aerial/drone — never repeat across a cut.
**好在哪**：每切必须双变——景别变且相机模式变；五相机模式不跨切重复；inserts 0.3-0.5s 无节拍、因果驱动、必须具名主体（否则渲染错内容）。
**迁移**：排切镜表时两列并行检查（景别列+相机模式列）不许同行重复；insert 写 HIS hand gripping metal 不写一只手。

### 台词预算与年龄盲区 [官方 seedance-director-skill 逐字]

> Dialogue word limit: ~25–30 spoken words fit into 15 seconds of video. …Never describe characters by age — in either language. Trigger words to avoid: boy, girl, child, kid, young, teen, little, 男孩, 女孩, 孩子, 少年, 少女, 小孩, 年轻.
**好在哪**：两条硬约束——15 秒只装 25-30 个口语词（超了保主换线+前后各一，其余转动作）；年龄词双语全禁（安全侧），按 role+服装+action 标注人物。
**迁移**：台词先数词再定时长；人物一律写 a figure in a wool cloak 式功能标签。

## 5. 写作总律（跨代有效）

### WRITE THE VISIBLE [官方 higgsfield-seedance-prompt 逐字]

> The model reacts to what can be seen and measured, not to mood words. …❌ "tense scene" → ✅ "man freezes, slowly clenches his fist, light only from the side, half his face in shadow".
**好在哪**：总律中的总律——模型只反应可见可测，情绪词必须翻译成可观察行为；生成前默放一遍检查首帧非空、主体位置朝向、光源方向。
**迁移**：每写一句问「镜头能拍到吗？」——拍不到的删或翻译；情绪→肌肉（her eyes drop to the table, jaw tightens）。

### CONTEXT ISOLATION 白板律 [官方 higgsfield-seedance-prompt 逐字]

> Each generation is a blank slate — no memory of previous shots. A prompt is a sealed single-shot document. Do not carry in: scene numbers, script headings, summaries of prior scenes, unused tags/characters, "as above / continues" phrasing.
**好在哪**：每次生成都是白板——prompt 是密封单发文档；场号/前情梗概/as above 措辞/未用 tag 全禁入。
**迁移**：每条 prompt 自包含（这也是项目法头制度的根因——见 视频/01）；检查有没有引用本条之外的上下文。

### 量纲立法 [官方 higgsfield-seedance-prompt 逐字]

> Speeds in km/h. …Atmosphere in percent / meters. …Atmosphere builds in steps across shots. Shot 1: fog 20% → Shot 2: 40% → Shot 3: 60%. …Giant scale via human-height comparison. …Left/right is from the camera.
**好在哪**：四大量纲——速度 km/h 禁快慢、大气密度 %/米且跨镜阶梯递进、巨物尺寸用人高对比、左右永远是镜头视角。
**迁移**：写速度给数（40 km/h）、写雾给百分比并按镜递进、写巨物写 as tall as four humans stacked、方位词全部换成镜头视角。

### 资产标签制 [官方 higgsfield-seedance-prompt 逐字]

> @TAG: age + role/build + current state + unique visible features + action-critical details + voice (only if it has a line). 100% matches the reference. …State critical details (small text, logos, color) in words even if they appear on the reference — the model can drop them. …Never place an @tag in a shot where that object is not present.
**好在哪**：tag 行六要素公式+100% matches the reference 硬承诺+关键细节文字重述（模型会掉参考上的小字/logo）+tag 禁入不在场镜头（模型会硬塞入画）。
**迁移**：每块参考板按六要素写 tag 行；小字/logo/颜色正文重述一遍；逐镜检查 tag 是否在场。时码多切项目用文件名原样做句柄（见 ⑦）。

## 6. FOV 度数制与机位

### 九档锚点表 [官方 higgsfield-seedance-prompt 逐字]

> Use only the discrete steps from the table. Not "23°" — use 18° or 29°. In a multishot, set FOV per segment and add "no drift mid-segment".
**好在哪**：prompt 里写度数不写毫米——九档锚点（180°鱼眼/107°/84°/63°/47°/29°/18°/12°/8°）禁自造度数；多切每段设 FOV+no drift mid-segment。
**迁移**：把九档表贴在写作环境里；段落内 FOV 只从表里取值；多切逐段标注度数。

### CAMERA 第三位律 [官方 higgsfield-seedance-prompt 逐字]

> Place the CAMERA block in the 3rd position of the prompt's core layers (subject → action → camera → style → constraints). Moved to the end, FOV gets ignored; moved to the front, it conflicts with identity.
**好在哪**：机位块有唯一正确位置——第三层；放尾部 FOV 被无视，放头部与身份冲突。
**迁移**：核块序 subject→action→camera→style→constraints；CAMERA 永远第三。

### 极端 FOV 四机制一致性栈 [官方 higgsfield-seedance-prompt 逐字]

> 1. Sequence-wide identity lock — single location reference across all beats. 2. LENS LOCK opener — explicit FOV phrase at the start of each beat. 3. LENS CHECK closer — confirm FOV at the end of the beat. 4. Color via material + light, not as a list. All four are required, or extreme-FOV multishots break down after 2–3 beats.
**好在哪**：8°/107° 极端视角下四机制缺一不可——全序列身份锁+每拍 LENS LOCK 开头+每拍 LENS CHECK 结尾+色彩=材质+光。
**迁移**：极端 FOV 多切必配四件套；普通 FOV 可省略。

## 7. 时码多切生产法

### 时码三重校验 [官方 higgsfield-timed-prompts 逐字]

> validate that shot headings are contiguous (end_i == start_{i+1}), the last end equals the declared duration, and spoken lines and times agree with the SOUND list.
**好在哪**：时码切表三条校验——镜间无缝（前镜终点=后镜起点）、总长=声明时长、台词表与 SOUND 块互证。
**迁移**：交付前跑三查；改任何一镜时长后全表重排时码（单一 regex 通跑）再重验。

### 转场三法与漂移率 [官方 higgsfield-timed-prompts 逐字]

> MATCH CUT on an action…Same lens, same angle, same face size, same pose, same hands, same move continuing without a stop. …HIDDEN CUT under full occlusion…the world changes inside the occlusion. Prefer match cuts when the user wants reliability; occlusion cuts drift more often.
**好在哪**：三转场法（动作锚 MATCH CUT/全遮挡 HIDDEN CUT/前景擦除 FOREGROUND WIPE）+官方漂移率结论——遮挡切漂移高于匹配切，求稳优先 match cut。
**迁移**：默认 MATCH CUT（同镜头同角度同脸尺寸同姿势动作不停）；遮挡切给足遮挡物描述；物件随世界切换消失 never before the cut。

### 失败→修复对照 [官方 higgsfield-timed-prompts 逐字]

> Washed-out colour → Remove film-emulation words; write "clean modern digital cinema look, deep clean blacks, punchy contrast". …Eyes closed / asleep → "eyes OPEN for the entire shot, pupils clearly visible". …Band/crowd stands still → Exact placement paragraph + "playing every moment on screen".
**好在哪**：官方给出症状→修复直接映射表（9 症状）——颜色发灰是胶片模拟词的锅、闭眼写全程睁眼、群演呆立写 playing every moment。
**迁移**：出片后按症状查表改词，不全局禁令（保留已选的胶片风格只改相关措辞）。

### 出绘重构图 [官方 higgsfield-timed-prompts 逐字]

> HOW THE FRAME IS BUILT — TOP AND BOTTOM ONLY: fit the entire source uniformly to the output width…zero pixels generated on the sides…AUDIO — NOTHING IS GENERATED: source track only…silence is preferable to any new sound.
**好在哪**：16:9→9:16 官方出绘律——只扩上下带零侧生成（band 公式：1080×1920 上下各 656px）、带内只环境禁人手脸字、音频零生成（无声好过造假声）。
**迁移**：竖版交付用 GENERATIVE REFRAME 结构+band 公式；音频永不出绘。

## 8. 生图五道硬门（cineimage-soul）

### 空场门 [官方 cineimage-soul v2.7 逐字]

> occupancy = empty. people_presence = forbidden. …No people, silhouettes, faces, human figures, human-shaped reflections, mannequins, portraits that read as real occupants, or figures visible through windows, doors, mirrors, or screens. …Do not infer a mechanic in a garage, a cook in a kitchen…Human activity is communicated through maintained objects, wear, arrangement, and functional traces, not through an invented person.
**好在哪**：场景图默认无人是硬约束不是偏好——全句 no-people 清单连人形反射/模特/门窗透影都堵死；人类活动靠维护痕迹与功能遗留物传达；只有显式要人才能覆盖。
**迁移**：纯场景图必写全句 no-people（反射面少的场景可缩短但不可省 No people）；「有人在用」的感觉用磨损/摆放/工具痕迹写。

### 镜头门 [官方 cineimage-soul v2.7 逐字]

> Every new-generation prompt must state: camera position; camera height when spatially useful; viewing direction; shot scale; full-frame-equivalent focal length; approximate horizontal FOV; distortion behavior. …balanced interior: 32–35mm, approximately 54–59° horizontal FOV…Distortion is off. No anamorphic stretching or horizontal anamorphic flare streaks unless explicitly requested.
**好在哪**：七要素缺一不可+场景缺省焦段表（室内 32-35mm/紧凑 24-28mm/主体环境 35mm）+畸变默认 off（变形宽银幕除非点名）。
**迁移**：每张生图自查七要素；没头绪就抄缺省表；变形宽银幕要显式请求。

### 风格门与色调门 [官方 cineimage-soul v2.7 逐字]

> Every prompt must have exactly one primary style profile. If the user does not provide one, use: cinematic editorial realism. …Maintain a neutral photographic colour foundation with a broad, articulate middle register. …Do not use a global orange, cyan, teal, green, magenta, or blue wash as a shortcut for mood.
**好在哪**：恰好一个主风格（16 profile 谱系从 cinematic editorial realism 到 intimate domestic realism，永不混用）+中性中间调基底+源色空间限制+禁全幅色罩走捷径。
**迁移**：生图先选一个 profile 把它的特征写进构图/选物/光/色/状态；色彩锚材质与光源，不做全幅 tint。

### 文字门与长度带 [官方 cineimage-soul v2.7 逐字]

> If an object naturally carries visible text, specify its exact content. Include language, capitalization, carrier, placement, and line breaks when relevant. …simple subject: 250–400 words; controlled location: 500–750 words; complex subject-led environment: 600–850 words.
**好在哪**：文字只管天然载体且必须 exact（语言/大小写/载体/位置/断行五要素）；长度按复杂度三档（250-400/500-750/600-850 词）——短了丢控制长了稀释。
**迁移**：招牌/铭牌/屏幕逐字给内容；写完数字数落带；23 点 preflight 逐项过再交付。

## 9. 品牌安全与禁词

### 年龄盲区 CRITICAL [官方 seedance-director-skill 逐字]

> Never describe characters by age — in either language. Trigger words to avoid: boy, girl, child, kid, young, teen, little, 男孩, 女孩, 孩子, 少年, 少女, 小孩, 年轻.
**好在哪**：双语年龄触发词全禁——安全硬约束；图输入按 role+服装+action 标注，无图用功能标签（a figure in a wool cloak）。
