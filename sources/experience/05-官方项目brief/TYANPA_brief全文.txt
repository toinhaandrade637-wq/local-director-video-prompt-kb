✨ Context

TYANPA is an AI-animated short film about curiosity, growing up, and the unconditional bond between a mother and her child.

The story follows Tyanpa, a small and adventurous duckling who longs to explore the world beyond his mother’s protection. What begins as innocent curiosity takes him far from home and into a dangerous journey that forces him to confront fear, survival, and the consequences of his choices.

At its heart, TYANPA is a story about discovering that growing up isn't about being fearless—it’s about learning, understanding, and finding your way home.

🎭 Narrative
Story

Tyanpa is the most adventurous of four ducklings. Always distracted by butterflies, animals, and anything new, he dreams of seeing what lies beyond his mother’s watchful eyes.

His mother constantly warns him that the world is much bigger and more dangerous than he realizes. Tyanpa promises to stay close, but a chance encounter with a tiny snail and the discovery of a shiny packet reignite his curiosity.

One small decision takes Tyanpa far from home and into a terrifying chain of events involving an eagle, a raging river, and a devastating flood.

Separated from his family and fighting to survive, Tyanpa begins to understand the meaning behind his mother’s warnings—and the depth of her love.

His journey ultimately becomes more than an adventure. It becomes a journey of growing up, understanding love, and finding his way home.

Theme

Curiosity vs. protection.
The film explores how children naturally want to discover the world for themselves, while parents try to protect them from dangers they cannot yet understand.

The story does not portray curiosity as wrong. Instead, it explores how experience can teach us boundaries, responsibility, and the value of home.

Audience

A family-friendly animated adventure with emotional depth, designed to appeal to children and adults alike.

🎨 Visuals
Creative Direction

A cinematic animated world that combines wonder, warmth, scale, and danger.

The visual language begins with a sense of innocence and discovery—soft natural light, lush environments, playful details, and intimate character moments. As Tyanpa moves farther from home, the world gradually becomes darker, larger, and more threatening.

The film contrasts the small scale of a little duckling with the overwhelming scale of nature: rushing rivers, storms, enormous waves, towering trees, and birds of prey.

Visual References

The visual direction draws inspiration from the emotional storytelling and naturalistic cinematic worlds found in modern animated films such as The Good Dinosaur, Flow, and The Wild Robot, while maintaining its own character design and visual identity.

📝 Notes
Key Creative Decisions
Tyanpa’s curiosity is the central driving force of the story.
The mother is never portrayed as an antagonist; her restrictions come from love and protection.
The snail acts as a narrative mirror for Tyanpa’s desire to explore.
The eagle and flood escalate the story from innocent adventure into survival.
The visual scale of nature is intentionally contrasted with Tyanpa’s small size.
The emotional resolution focuses on understanding rather than punishment.
The journey changes Tyanpa emotionally and psychologically; he returns more mature and aware than the duckling who left.
The ending aims to leave the audience with a feeling of home, love, growth, and quiet emotional closure.
Core Statement

He set out to discover the world. Along the way, he discovered himself—and the meaning of home.
