Context

I've been chasing the Arcane look since the first time I saw it. But this film found its reason in a hospital.

My twins arrived early. I made almost all of this in a chair beside the NICU over ten days that I would not wish on anyone. And in that time I noticed what I was doing with my face. People kept coming to congratulate me, and I kept smiling. I meant it. I was a father. I was also more frightened than I have ever been in my life, and none of that reached my face, because what else do you do.

That gap is the whole film. A face saying one thing while everything underneath it is screaming.

So I built a city that made it law.

This one is for my two daughters, who came home <3
