A mockumentary about a seemingly superior robot who can't understand why his Neanderthal companion wants to build a boat.

✨ Context


I wanted to make a parable about humans and AI – one that asks the oldest questions: where do we come from, and where are we going? That's why it had to be a Neanderthal: someone we drove to extinction. And a robot: something that might one day do the same to us. The hat was my way of showing how we humanize technology to make it graspable. And at the core I wanted the contrast between a rather highly evolved being driven to explore and discover simply for the sake of it, and a machine that can't comprehend that urge. In the end, we're both sitting in the same boat – not knowing where we're going. 




All AI-generated creations, including all unused prompts, have been left as is and unsorted for display purposes, because this is how the film was created and it was not divided into specific scenes beforehand.
