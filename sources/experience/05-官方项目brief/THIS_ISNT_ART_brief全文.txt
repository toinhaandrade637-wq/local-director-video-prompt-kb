✨ The idea

Photography was called a mechanical trick. Cinema was called a fairground novelty. Every new medium has been told it wasn't art — usually by people who were told the same thing a generation earlier.




I didn't make this film to defend AI. I made it because the argument is older than any of us, and because the ones doing the mocking have almost always been mocked themselves.
