Premise

In the snow of a war torn province, a peasant woman kneels before the men who have sentenced her to death for theft.

Setting

The film is set in the Sengoku period, an age of near-constant civil war, when the countryside was lawless, the poor were expendable. 

Tone & Visual Style

Fallen leaves is shot primarily with naturalistic hand movement and lighting. Shots feel real rather than purely cinematic. Color grading based on a film emulation of fuji superia. Cool blue and green tones. One scene is shot deliberately warmer and brighter, in contrast to the cold feeling of the execution field.

Themes

The film asks us to hope, before taking that away from us. It reminds us that life is not always like a movie, there is not always a happy reunion. It asks us to question our pre-conceived notions of the honour of samurais. But most of all, its about the connection between a mother and daughter. 

Production

Fallen Leaves is a widescreen, color short film with a film-emulation finish, built entirely through AI generation, every image, and all of its audio, music, voice, and sound design, produced prompt by prompt with intention as a work of AI filmmaking. Written, edited, and envisioned by Jacob Everett.
