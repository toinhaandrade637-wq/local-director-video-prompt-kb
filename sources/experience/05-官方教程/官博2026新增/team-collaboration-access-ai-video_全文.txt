In Higgsfield, one Owner manages the workspace and billing. Each person you invite needs a paid team seat, but they do not need a separate personal subscription. Everyone generates from the workspace’s shared credit pool, while access and spending controls depend on their role and plan.

Set Up Your Team: Roles and Permissions

Higgsfield is an AI-native creative suite with 35+ models and tools, and its team plans, Team, Scale, and Enterprise, sit in the Business plans category. Members work in one shared workspace and draw on a single pool of credits across models. The Owner pays for the seats, so no member needs a personal subscription.

A workspace has three roles:

When someone leaves: remove them from the workspace so they lose access. Shared workspace assets remain, but removing the person does not automatically reduce the number of paid seats. Reducing seats is a separate billing action handled through Support for the next billing cycle.

Keep Team Assets in One Shared Library

Shared workspace assets are the materials the team collaborates on and reuses together, including shared folders, Soul ID characters, and Reference Elements. On Enterprise, the separate Member assets view lets authorized Owners and Admins review generations by individual member across the organization.

Track and Control Team Credit Spending

Everyone generates from the same pooled credit balance. On Team, members can draw from that shared pool without per-member spend controls. Scale adds the ability to pause an individual member’s credit usage, while Enterprise adds more granular controls such as per-user limits, groups, and usage reports.

The controls differ by plan, so check which one you have before relying on a feature:

Compare Team Plans and Pricing

Cost depends on the plan and the number of seats. Every seat is priced the same within a plan, and every seat adds the same number of credits to the shared pool. Five Team seats cost $395 a month and add 5,000 credits. Five Scale seats cost $1,075 a month and add 12,500 credits. Enterprise is priced individually.

Know When You Need Sub-Workspaces

For most 2–5 person teams working from the same projects and credit budget, one Team or Scale workspace is enough. Sub-workspaces become useful when separate clients, departments, or business lines need their own members, assets, or credit allocations.

One workspace is enough when a single team works with shared materials and one budget.
Sub-workspaces help when separate teams or business lines each need their own credit allocation.

To create one, go to Manage Account, then Sub-workspaces, then Add. You enter a name, the number of credits, the allocation period, and the email of the sub-workspace owner, who must already have a Higgsfield account. Credits can be allocated once, daily, weekly, or monthly. A sub-workspace is either Active or Paused, and you don't buy credits for it separately, since they come from the shared pool.

Enterprise also offers custom terms for credits, seats, and concurrent tasks, custom workflows and integrations, SSO, audit logs, and SLA support. Pricing and terms are arranged through Contact sales on the Enterprise card.
