What Is Adobe Firefly, and What Does It Include?

Adobe Firefly covers text-to-video and image-to-video generation. Prompt to Edit (beta) changes a clip with a text prompt, and the Firefly video editor (beta) provides a browser-based timeline for lightweight editing. It also includes Text to avatar, audio and video translation, 4K upscaling in Boards, and tools for generating speech, music, and sound effects.

For images, Firefly supports generation, editing, upscaling, object removal, and background expansion. Users can choose Adobe’s Firefly Image models or partner models such as Nano Banana 2, GPT Image, and FLUX.

Video partner models include Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Runway Gen-4.5, and Luma Ray3. Because Firefly brings these tools together, the alternatives below are compared on their overall video workflows, including supporting image and audio tools.

How We Compared These Adobe Firefly Alternatives

We chose six platforms that cover the Firefly video workflow in different ways: scene generation, editing, presenter video, script-to-video, training video, and cinematic motion. Using official product documentation and pricing pages, we compared each platform on five criteria:

Generation and editing: the models available and what you can do with an existing clip.
Control: camera, motion, and character tools.
Avatars, voice, and localization: presenters, voiceover, and translation.
From idea to finished video: how much of the workflow stays in one place.
Price, limits, and export: plans, credits, and watermark or resolution limits.
Higgsfield AI: Creative Suite With 35+ Models and Tools

Higgsfield is an AI-native creative suite with 35+ models and tools, including Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, and Veo 3.1 under one credit balance. Cinema Studio 4.0 sets genre, era, tempo, camera, lens, aperture, lighting, and color as controls, with clips up to 30 seconds, up to 50 references, Extend, Edit Video, and output up to 1080p. Soul ID trains a reusable character identity from 20+ reference images.

Marketing Studio builds video and image ads from a product link or photos, using 1,500+ templates across six categories, and Lipsync Studio turns a character image into a talking video or adds lip sync to existing footage. Starter is $15 a month for 200 credits, Plus is $49 for 1,000, and Ultra is $129 for 3,000. As of October 2026, a 10-second Cinema Studio 4.0 clip at 720p with native audio costs 70 credits, about $3.43 at the Plus rate.

Where Higgsfield falls short:

Credits reset monthly with no rollover on individual plans
Runway: Video Generation With Built-In Editing

Runway focuses on generating video and editing it afterward. Gen-4.5 is its own video model, at 12 credits per second. Aleph 2.0 applies edits from a modified frame across an existing video, at 28 credits per second, and Act-Two transfers a performance onto a character. Runway’s model catalog also includes Seedance 2.5 and Kling 3.0.

Music, voice, and sound effects can be generated within Runway, starting with the Standard plan. Pro and higher add 4K video upscaling and Custom Voices, while team plans add collaboration features such as Brand Kits. Standard is $15 a month for 625 credits, Pro is $35 for 2,250, and Max is $95 for 9,500.

Where Runway falls short:

Standard includes 625 monthly credits, enough for five 10-second Gen-4.5 generations if no credits are used elsewhere. Unused subscription credits do not roll over on Standard or Pro.
HeyGen: AI Presenters and Localization

HeyGen focuses on AI presenters, product walkthroughs, and localization. Avatar V powers HeyGen's video-based Digital Twins, letting a trained presenter deliver new scripts with their learned motion and expressions. The Creator plan also includes unlimited Photo Avatars and voice cloning.

Output is 1080p on Creator and 4K on Pro, with videos up to 30 minutes. Creator is $29 a month for 600 credits, and Pro starts at $49 for 1,000 credits, with a credit selector for larger monthly volumes. Business is $149 for the first seat plus $20 for each additional seat, with 1,500 shared credits. A free plan covers up to 3 videos a month, each up to 1 minute.

Where HeyGen falls short:

Creator is limited to 1080p export. Pro adds 4K, while the monthly credit allowance limits usage across generation and translation features.
Invideo AI: Video Creation and Editing With AI

Invideo AI combines video generation with an editor for assembling and refining your project. You can start with a brief, script, or uploaded footage, then use AI to help build scenes, add voiceover and subtitles, and make edits. A multitrack timeline also lets you adjust the result manually.

Monthly plans start at $9 per seat for Basic with 190 credits. Pro costs $30 per seat with 1,000 credits, and Ultra costs $80 per seat with 3,000 credits.

Where Invideo AI falls short:

AI agent tasks and media generation both consume credits, so usage depends on the work performed, models, and settings. Manual timeline edits do not use AI credits.
Synthesia: AI Avatars for Training and Presentations

Synthesia helps turn scripts, documents, and presentations into narrated videos with AI avatars. You can start with existing materials, build a sequence of scenes, and combine a presenter with text, visuals, and screen recordings. This makes it useful for tutorials, onboarding, internal updates, and educational content.

Its main appeal is the ability to update and adapt information without recording a presenter again. Teams can revise the script, reuse layouts, and create versions for different audiences or languages. Starter costs $29/month and Pro starts at $89/month with monthly billing. Enterprise pricing is available on request.

Where Synthesia falls short:

Its focus on presentations and avatar-led content makes it a less direct fit for projects centered on cinematic action or detailed camera choreography.
Kling AI: Cinematic Scenes and Native Audio

Kling AI supports text-to-video and image-to-video generation, multi-shot scenes, and character animation. Motion Control uses a reference video to guide a character’s movements. Audio, resolution, and other settings depend on the selected model and subscription.

Check the selected plan for resolution options, generation priority, watermark removal, and commercial-use terms. Standard is $10 a month for 660 credits, Pro is $37 for 3,000, Premier is $92 for 8,000, and Ultra is $180 for 26,000. Free access has usage limits.

Where Kling falls short:

Kling focuses on its own model family rather than offering a catalog of third-party video models.
Which Adobe Firefly Alternative Should You Choose?
Scene generation and camera control: Higgsfield, for explicit camera, lens, and lighting controls.
Editing existing footage: Runway, for Aleph 2.0 video-to-video editing.
Videos with AI presenters: HeyGen, for avatars and translation.
A video assembled from a script: Invideo AI, for brief-to-video with voiceover and subtitles.
Corporate training: Synthesia, for presenter-led learning content.
Character animation and motion: Kling AI, for multi-shot scenes and native audio.

Staying with Firefly makes sense when its tools already fit your workflow, for example if your team works in Premiere and Photoshop, or you want Adobe's own Firefly models and several partner models under one subscription.
