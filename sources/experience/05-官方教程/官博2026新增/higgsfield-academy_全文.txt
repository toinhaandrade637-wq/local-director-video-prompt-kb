Higgsfield Academy: AI Video Lessons, Homework & Certificate
Higgsfield
Oct 26, 2026
8 min

Higgsfield Academy teaches you how to create AI images and videos through video lessons and practical assignments. Its beginner course, AI Creation: First Steps, includes an introduction and seven lessons totaling 1 hour 42 minutes, supported by PDF guides. There is no separate course fee, but completing the required homework needs an active Higgsfield subscription and credits. Submit the required assignments to unlock later lessons and work toward a completion certificate.

What Is Higgsfield Academy?

Higgsfield Academy is the learning hub within Higgsfield, the generative platform for creative production. Courses connect creative decisions with hands-on workflows: developing an idea, writing a prompt, building reference assets, directing a shot, and refining the result.

Beginners can start with AI Creation: First Steps, created by Higgsfield Studio. The course introduces the tools through projects, from your first video to product advertising and your own scene. A separate catalog offers focused tutorials on filmmaking, social content, advertising, and workflows with AI agents.

Ready to get started? Open AI Creation: First Steps in Higgsfield Academy and begin with the introductory lesson.

How Do Homework and Lesson Access Work?

The course has required practice. A locked lesson opens after the required assignment is submitted.

The assignments:

Lesson 1: three videos, one from a simple prompt, one through Supercomputer, and one with a preset. The first two use the same idea, and the preset video uses the learner's own photo.
Lesson 2: a 15-second video from the learner's own idea through Supercomputer. The learner answers the assistant's questions and checks the prompt before generating.
Lesson 3: three images for a campaign and one vertical video ad for a chosen product, shot in a single location. The original product photo serves as the reference.

How submission works. Generations from the last three days qualify, and each generation can be submitted only once.

How lessons open. The introduction and the first three lessons sit in the opening part of the course. Lessons 4 to 7 open as the required assignments are completed. The first two assignments count as additional practice, and submitting the lesson 3 assignment opens lesson 4.

Homework isn't graded. Required submissions are used to unlock later lessons.

What Does Completing the Course Earn?

Finishing the starter course brings three things:

A Certificate of Completion.
The Foundation achievement.
A Creator Drop.

The certificate and rewards belong to the starter course. They don't carry over automatically to the other tutorials in the Academy.
