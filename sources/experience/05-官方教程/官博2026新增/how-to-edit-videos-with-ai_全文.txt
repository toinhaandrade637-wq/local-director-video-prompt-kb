How to Edit Videos with AI (Full Guide)
Higgsfield
Oct 24, 2026
10 min

To edit a video with AI, upload your footage, choose a tool, and guide the changes with a prompt, reference, or settings. On Higgsfield, you can transfer motion, change lighting and backgrounds, adjust framing, and increase resolution in your own footage or AI-generated clips. This guide explains which tool fits which task. It also walks through a complete workflow with the cost of each step.

TL;DR

Change an element, character, or product: Genjutsu or Seedance 2.5 Edit.
Transfer motion from one video to another: Genjutsu (Motion Transfer).
Change the light: Relight.
Remove a background: Remove Background.
Extend a clip: Extend Video in Cinema Studio 4.0.
Change the format for another channel: Reframe.
Raise the resolution or smooth the motion: FLUX.3 Upscale and FPS Boost.
What Can Be Changed in a Video with AI?

AI can edit a finished clip by swapping a character, product, or outfit, changing the lighting, or replacing or removing the background. It can also transfer motion from another video or extend a scene. Other tools adjust the aspect ratio, increase resolution, or boost the frame rate. Each of these is a separate operation, so one clip can go through several of them in sequence.

One operation is often enough when the problem is a single flaw, such as a distracting background or a clip that ends too early. Several operations are applied in order when a finished piece is being built, for example transferring motion, reframing the result, and upscaling it at the end. Upscaling comes last, once the content of the clip is settled, so credits aren't spent on enhancing a version that will change.

How Do You Edit a Video on Higgsfield?

Higgsfield is an AI-native creative suite with 35+ models and tools. Three options cover video transformation, targeted edits, and upscaling:

Higgsfield Genjutsu is a video-to-video tool for changing a clip that already exists, whether it is real footage or generated video. Motion Transfer uses a reference video between 4 and 30 seconds long to guide movement in a new scene. Full Workflow: Step-by-Step Guide

This example uses Higgsfield Genjutsu, the video-to-video tool for changing a clip that already exists.

Step 1: Upload your video. Open Higgsfield Genjutsu and select Motion Transfer to apply your video’s movement to a new character or scene, or Object Swap to replace a specific character, product, or outfit. Then upload your source video. 

Step 2: Add references. Upload images of the character, product, outfit, or setting you want to use. Choose clear references that show the details you want in the result. 

Step 3: Generate and review. Add a short description if needed, then generate the video. Compare it with your source clip and references, checking the requested changes, movement, and visual consistency.

Step 4: Make optional adjustments. If needed, use Upscale to improve video quality, Reframe to change the aspect ratio, Relight to adjust the lighting, or other editing tools to refine the result.

Step 5: Review and export. Watch the full clip to check for visual issues and confirm that the framing and edits look right. Download the finished video.

How Do You Get Better Results When Editing Video with AI?
Clear references. Sharp images of the character from several angles give the model more to work with.
Motion videos with distinct movement. A clip where the body is fully visible and the motion is easy to follow transfers better than a crowded or blurry one.
Precise descriptions of the change. Naming what should change and what should stay, for example "warm evening light, same character and background", gives clearer results.
One element at a time. If a result looks wrong, change only one input at a time: the prompt, a reference image, or a setting. This makes it easier to identify what improves the result.
A check after every stage. Problems are easier to fix in the stage where they appear than after more edits are built on top.
Upscaling last. The upscale runs once the content of the clip is settled.
Use footage you can edit and publish. Make sure you have the rights and any necessary permissions for your intended use.
