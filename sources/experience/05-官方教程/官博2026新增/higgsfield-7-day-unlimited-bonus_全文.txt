Higgsfield’s New 7-Day Unlimited Bonus: Models, Plans, and Limits
Higgsfield
Oct 30, 2026
10 min
Higgsfield has launched a 7-Day Unlimited Bonus offering unlimited generation across a selection of image and video models. The bonus is available with eligible Pro, Plus, Max, and Ultra subscriptions, with included models and supported settings varying by plan and billing cycle. The offer is available for a limited time.
What’s Inside the New Offer
The bonus includes Seedream 5.0 Pro, GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana Pro, and Seedance 2.5, with availability and supported settings depending on the plan and billing cycle.

All included models come with seven days of unlimited generation. GPT Image 2.5 includes Sunburst and Flare at Low and Medium quality.
Annual billing adds Nano Banana Pro and Seedance 2.5 to Pro and Plus. On Max, it raises the Seedance 2.5 resolution from 480p to 720p; on Ultra, from 720p to 1080p.
After the bonus expires, generations on these models use credits unless they’re covered by another active Unlimited offer.
How Monthly and Annual Bonuses Compare
Pro and Plus: Monthly vs. Annual.
 Monthly Pro and Plus include two image models: Seedream 5.0 Pro and GPT Image 2.5. Annual billing adds Nano Banana Pro and Seedance 2.5, bringing the total to four models. Image generation is available up to 2K, and Seedance 2.5 supports 720p videos up to 20 seconds.
Max: Monthly vs. Annual.
 Both monthly and annual Max include all four models. The difference is the Seedance 2.5 resolution: 480p with monthly billing and 720p with annual billing. Both options support videos up to 20 seconds.
Ultra: Monthly vs. Annual.
 Both monthly and annual Ultra include all four models. Seedance 2.5 supports 720p with monthly billing and 1080p with annual billing, with videos up to 20 seconds on either option.
How to Use the 7-Day Unlimited Bonus
Sign in to Higgsfield and select a model included in the plan’s active bonus.
Choose a resolution and quality setting covered by the offer.
Turn on the Unlimited toggle before generating.
Start the generation within the supported limits.
Eligible generations made with the Unlimited toggle enabled do not deduct credits. When the toggle is turned off, regular credit charges apply.
Unlimited applies to eligible generations on 
higgsfield.ai
. It does not apply to generations through MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer, or Marketing Studio.
Available Unlimited models and their expiry dates are listed under 
Manage Account → Subscription → Active unlimited models
.
What You Can Create
The included image models support product visuals, campaign concepts, and variations on the same creative brief. Plans with Seedance 2.5 Unlimited also support turning selected images into videos up to 20 seconds long, at the resolution covered by the plan.
How Generation Limits Work
Unlimited access still has limits on how many generations can run simultaneously.
How Generation Limits Work
Generation Type
Shared Unlimited Limit
Images
Two generations at a time, shared across all unlimited image models
Video
One generation at a time across unlimited video models
The image limit is shared across models. If two Seedream generations are already running, a new Nano Banana Pro generation joins the queue until a slot becomes available.
The video limit works the same way: additional unlimited video jobs wait while another is running.
These limits are separate from the paid-generation concurrency listed on subscription cards. A plan’s “Unlimited paid parallel generations” feature does not remove the concurrency limits for the Unlimited bonus.

Got any questions left?
Does Plus Monthly Include All Four Models?
No. Plus monthly includes Seedream 5.0 Pro and GPT Image 2.5. Plus annual adds Nano Banana Pro and Seedance 2.5.
Which Plans Include Seedance 2.5 Unlimited?
Seedance 2.5 Unlimited is included on annual Pro and Plus, and on both monthly and annual Max and Ultra. Monthly Max includes 480p; annual Pro, Plus, and Max and monthly Ultra include 720p; annual Ultra includes 1080p. All included options support videos up to 20 seconds. Monthly Pro and Plus do not include this video bonus.
Which GPT Image 2.5 Options Are Included?
The offer includes Sunburst and Flare at up to 2K, with Low and Medium quality settings.
Does Starter Include This Bonus?
No. Starter does not include the new seven-day unlimited access to these four models.
Does Each Image Model Allow Two Simultaneous Generations?
No. The two-generation limit is shared across the unlimited image models. Additional jobs enter the queue.
Does This Change the Existing 365-Day Unlimited Set?
No. The update applies to the seven-day bonus. The existing 365-day model set remains unchanged.
Do Business Plans Have the Same Video Bonus?
Business plans advertise a separate 30-day Unlimited Seedance 2.5 offer. Its specific conditions are listed in the business-plan details.
What happened to Wan 3.0 Unlimited?
Wan 3.0 is no longer part of the seven-day Unlimited offer. It has been replaced with Seedance 2.5 on eligible plans. If you purchased while Wan 3.0 Unlimited was included, your Unlimited access has been changed to Seedance 2.5 for the same seven-day period, at the resolution supported by your plan.
