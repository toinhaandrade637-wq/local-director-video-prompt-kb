Higgsfield Discord 200K Contest: How to Enter, Rules, and 200,000 Credits in Prizes
Higgsfield
Oct 8, 2026
8 min

Higgsfield is celebrating 200,000 Discord members with a contest and a 200,000-credit prize pool. To enter, recreate a viral video format with your own idea and make a video of up to 60 seconds in a contest project in Cinema Studio. Entries close October 14, 2026, at 5:00 PM UTC. After the Higgsfield team reviews entries for rule compliance, community voting decides the 10 winners, who share the 200,000-credit prize pool.

What Is the Higgsfield Discord 200K Contest?

The contest asks you to take a recognizable viral trend and make it your own. Pick a viral video format, recreate it on Higgsfield, and add something new: a character, a situation, a joke, or an unexpected twist. You can remake the idea, the setup, the camera move, and an everyday setting. Recreate the format, not the original footage. Do not use someone else’s characters, brands, logos, music, footage, or artwork unless you have the rights to use them. 

Who can enter:

Adults only: 18, or the age of majority in your country if it's higher
A member of the Higgsfield Discord community
A Higgsfield account in good standing, linked to your Discord account in the #verification channel
One entry per person, which you can replace with a new submission any time before the deadline

Format and video requirements:

One video of up to 60 seconds, in any aspect ratio
SFW and PG-13 content. Sexual content, violence against real individuals, political or religious statements and propaganda, misinformation, and illegal, defamatory, or rights-violating content are not allowed

Generation rules:

All AI images and video must be generated on Higgsfield, inside the contest project, during the contest period
Open community templates and presets are allowed
No third-party characters, brands, logos, music, footage, or artwork you don't have the rights to. An entry with unauthorized third-party IP is disqualified at any stage, prizes included
Real people can appear only with their permission, and you can appear yourself. That rules out celebrities, athletes, and creators unless they gave permission
Cloning or imitating a real person's voice is against the contest rules
How to Enter the Contest
Join the Higgsfield Discord and link your account. Link your Higgsfield account in the #verification channel.
Click Apply. Use the button under the contest announcement in Discord, or open the contest page. The application asks for a contact email, an optional profile (full name, headline, and bio), and a checkbox to accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. If your accounts aren't linked yet, you'll be asked to link Discord in #verification first.
Create a contest project in Cinema Studio. After you apply, Higgsfield creates your contest project in Cinema Studio. Make all AI image and video generations for your entry inside that project.
Make your video. Use the models available in the contest project. Add external edits only if you upload the final version back into the project.
Click Submit. Enter a title, the trend you recreated, and a short description. Mention any outside editing there.

Credits are added within 24 hours after the results are announced. They stay valid for 90 days from the day they're added, can't be transferred to another user, and can't be exchanged for money. One person can win one prize, and if an entry is disqualified, the next entry by votes moves up.

Winners also get the Contest Winner role in Discord and a mention in #hall-of-fame. Winners and standout entrants may be invited to the Higgsfield Creative Partner Program. Higgsfield passes your name to the program team, and by entering you agree it may contact you about the program. The invitation is separate from the prizes, optional, and isn't promised.

What to Know Before You Submit

Beyond the video itself, a few rules cover editing, audio, and what becomes public.

Outside editing, if you need it. You can use an external editor for cuts, titles, transitions, and color correction. Upload the final edit back into the contest project and mention the editing in your entry description.
Audio you have the right to use. You can use AI audio from any tool, your own original recording, music and sounds you have a license for, or no sound at all. The original track of a viral trend can't be used without the rights to it.

After you submit, your contest project becomes public, including the prompt, the settings, and the references you uploaded, and it stays public until the results. For that reason, don't upload someone else's original video as a reference. Study the trend and recreate the format yourself.

You keep the rights to your work. Higgsfield may show it in the contest gallery, the results, and its channels, with credit to you. Posting on social media is optional. If you share your video, post the same video under the same rules, use #HiggsfieldDiscord200K, and tag Higgsfield. The team may react or repost, but that isn't promised and doesn't affect the results.

5:00 PM UTC is 1:00 PM in New York and 10:00 AM in San Francisco on October 14. Results are announced in #updates and in a live stream on the same day.

Until the deadline, you can send a new version to replace your previous one. After the deadline, Submit and generation in contest projects close. The team reviews every entry after submissions close and again before prizes go out, and there's no window to fix an entry after the deadline, so submit your final version.

How Community Voting Works

Checking and choosing are two separate steps. First, the Higgsfield team reviews every entry against the rules. Entries that pass go into a gallery on the Higgsfield website, where the community votes for 48 hours.

Who can vote: Anyone with an active paid Higgsfield subscription. Linking Discord isn't required to vote.
How many votes: You can vote for as many entries as you like, once per entry, but you cannot vote for your own.
Inviting friends: You can ask friends to vote. Bots, fake accounts, buying votes, and vote manipulation are prohibited.
Final ranking: The final places are set by valid votes after review. Entries that break the rules are removed at any stage, prizes included.
