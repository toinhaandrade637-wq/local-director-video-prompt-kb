8 Best AI Image Upscalers in 2026 (Free and Paid)

Higgsfield Oct 31, 2026 13 min

Topaz Gigapixel is built for photos and print, with unlimited local rendering for a flat monthly fee. Magnific Upscaler adds new detail to AI images. Higgsfield lets creators generate, edit and upscale in one place, from 2 credits per upscale. Upscayl is free and runs offline. Let’s Enhance handles product files up to 512 MP.
TL;DR:
To enlarge photos, products or text without changes, use a fidelity mode: Topaz, Magnific Precision, Let’s Enhance Gentle.
To add detail to AI art, use a generative mode and check faces and labels afterward.
The same 4x upscale costs from $0 to about $0.67 across the eight tools.
An upscaler either enlarges the pixels you already have or invents new detail to fill the gaps. This guide compares eight tools on the same file. To create images from scratch, see Best AI Image Generators.

Fidelity vs Generative Upscaling: How Much Does the Image Change?
Fidelity upscalers enlarge the existing pixels and try to keep faces, text and product shapes as they are. Generative upscalers redraw the image with new texture and detail. Generative results often look sharper, but they can change a face, a logo or a label, so check them closely before you publish.
Vendors describe this split themselves. Adobe says Topaz Gigapixel “preserves existing detail,” while Topaz Bloom “adds new, creative detail.” Krea warns that its generative models can add undesired detail.

Settings control how far a generative model goes:
Creativity sliders (Higgsfield Topaz Generative, Magnific Creative, Krea, Firefly Bloom): lower values stay closer to the source.
Resemblance (Magnific Creative): higher values keep the original composition.
Face controls (OpenArt Face preservation, Krea face detection and face strength, Gigapixel Face Recovery): limit changes to faces.
Modes for text: Let’s Enhance recommends Gentle when an image has small text.
No upscaler restores detail that was never captured. A generative model can draw eyelashes or fabric weave that look real, but they are not in the original.
Topaz Gigapixel: Best for Photos and Print
Topaz Gigapixel is a desktop upscaler from Topaz Labs for Mac and Windows. It offers specialized models, from faithful enlargement to face recovery, with local or cloud rendering under one subscription.
Best for: photographers and print shops that upscale many images and want to keep files on their own computer.
Where it runs: desktop app; models include Standard, Standard Max, High Fidelity, Low Res, Text & Shapes, Art & CG, Recover, Redefine, Wonder and Face Recovery.
What you get:
Upscaling up to 6x
Unlimited local and cloud image rendering on paid plans
Dedicated models for text, CGI and low-resolution sources
Local rendering, so files do not leave your computer
Limits that remain:
No Linux version; generative models need 8 GB of VRAM to run locally
Personal plan allows commercial use only for organizations under $1M in annual revenue
No free plan or trial listed on the official site
Price: Personal plan from $29/mo billed monthly; full commercial use on Pro ($54/mo, billed monthly for 12 months).
Magnific Upscaler: Best for AI Images That Need New Detail
Magnific Upscaler belongs to Magnific, the platform formerly known as Freepik. Creative mode adds new detail with sliders and an optional prompt; Precision mode sharpens while staying close to the source.
Best for: illustrators and AI artists who want texture added to soft renders.
Where it runs: Magnific web app and API; Creative mode (Magnific or Classic model) and Precision mode (Magnific v2 or v1); Topaz upscalers on Premium+ and above.
What you get:
Creative sliders for Creativity, HDR and Resemblance, plus “Optimized for” presets
Precision presets (Balanced, Portraits, Grainy Analog) with Sharpness and Grain
Up to 16x in Precision mode
Bulk upload of up to 20 images
Limits that remain:
Credit cost grows with output size: a 4x upscale of our 1024×1024 file cost 360 credits in both modes
10K output is available only on Premium+ and above
Free plan allows personal use only, with attribution
Price: Premium from $20/mo for 20,000 credits.

Higgsfield is a generative platform for creative production, with 35+ frontier models for generating images and turning them into video scenes. Its Upscale tool works on uploads and past generations.
Best for: marketers who generate or edit images, then need a larger file for ads.
Where it runs: Higgsfield web app (Upscale); ByteDance Upscale (default), Topaz and Topaz Generative.
What you get:
ByteDance Upscale to 2K or 4K for a flat 2 credits
Topaz at x1, x2, x4, x8 or x16, from 2 credits
Topaz Generative with Prompt, Sharpness, Denoise, Creativity and Texture settings
Generation, editing and upscaling under one credit balance

Credit cost depends on the model and scale factor (Topaz Generative x4 was 8 credits on our file)
The highest factors depend on the source size
Price: from $15/mo for 200 credits; a 4x upscale cost 2 credits (≈$0.15) with Topaz or ByteDance.
Adobe Photoshop Generative Upscale: Best for Photoshop Users
Generative Upscale sits in Photoshop under Image > Generative Upscale. It offers Adobe’s Firefly Upscaler and two Topaz Labs models, and opens the result as a new document.
Best for: designers who already retouch and lay out images in Photoshop.
Where it runs: Photoshop desktop and Firefly web; Firefly Upscaler, Topaz Gigapixel and Topaz Bloom.
What you get:
2x or 4x scale
Firefly Upscaler up to 6144 × 6144 px
Topaz Gigapixel up to 56 MP, Topaz Bloom up to 9 MP
Bloom Creativity slider and Gigapixel Face recovery (Firefly web)
Limits that remain:
Each upscale uses generative credits: 10 credits for our 16.8 MP output with Firefly or Gigapixel
The Photoshop plan includes 25 credits a month
Topaz Bloom cannot reach 4096×4096 because of its 9 MP limit
Price: Photoshop plan $22.99/mo on an annual plan billed monthly. Credit costs come from Adobe Help (Partner models in Adobe products), last updated October 23, 2026.
Krea Enhancer: Best for Comparing Many Upscale Models
Krea Enhancer puts Krea’s own models, several Topaz models and Magnific models in one interface, so you can switch between them on the same image.
Best for: designers who want to test different upscalers without separate accounts.
Where it runs: Krea web app (Enhancer); Krea Upscale, Krea Enhance, Topaz (Standard, Low Resolution, High Fidelity, CGI), Topaz Image Upscale, Topaz Bloom and Bloom 2, Magnific Creative and Magnific Precise.
What you get:
Scale options of 1x, 2x, 4x and 8x
Up to 22K output with Topaz
Settings for strength, resemblance, sharpness, denoise and face detection
Listed prices from 8 units (Krea Upscale) to 250 units (Topaz Bloom)
Limits that remain:
Topaz and Magnific models require a paid plan
Generative models carry a minimum price, with the final cost shown before you enhance
Free upscaling stops at 2K with basic models
Price: Basic from $9/mo for 5,000 units.
Let’s Enhance: Best for Product Photos and Large Files
Let’s Enhance is a web upscaler for e-commerce and print. It charges one credit per image and supports outputs up to 512 MP on its largest personal plan.
Best for: online stores and print teams that need product images at a fixed cost per image.
Where it runs: Let’s Enhance web app and the Claid.ai API; modes include Prime (default), Gentle, Strong, Old Photo and Digital Art.
What you get:
Upscaling from 2x to 16x
Gentle mode for images with small text, such as product labels
Strong mode for very blurry inputs
One credit per image, regardless of mode
Limits that remain:
Maximum output varies by plan, from 256 MP (Starter) to 512 MP (Max)
Free credits produce watermarked images
Batch processing for businesses goes through sales
Price: Starter from $12/mo billed monthly for 100 credits (≈$0.12 per image).
OpenArt Image Upscaler: Best for Topaz Models on Trial Credits
OpenArt is a creative platform for images and video. Its Image Upscale tool runs on Topaz Labs models in three modes: Precise, Generative and Creative.
Best for: OpenArt users who want Topaz models without a Topaz subscription.
Where it runs: OpenArt web app; Precise (Topaz Standard v2), Generative (Topaz Redefine) and Creative (Topaz Bloom Creative).
What you get:
Upscale factor from 1x to 16x in Precise mode
Face preservation toggle in Creative mode
Cost and estimated time shown before you generate
40 one-time trial credits for new accounts
Limits that remain:
Generative modes cost more: 105 credits (Generative) and 190 credits (Creative) for a 4x upscale of our file
Commercial use starts on the Plus plan ($34/mo)
Free accounts keep creations for 7 days
Price: Starter from $14/mo for 4,000 credits; Precise 4x cost 20 credits (≈$0.07).
Upscayl: Best Free AI Image Upscaler
Upscayl is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. It runs open models such as Real-ESRGAN on your own graphics card, without credits.
Best for: anyone who needs free upscaling and has a compatible GPU.
Where it runs: desktop app (AGPL-3.0); built-in models include Real-ESRGAN, High Fidelity, Remacri and Ultrasharp, plus custom models.
What you get:
Upscaling up to 16x
Batch processing for whole folders
“Double Upscayl” to run an image through twice
Local processing on your own hardware
Limits that remain:
Needs a Vulkan-compatible GPU; most integrated graphics will not work
No prompt or creativity controls
Speed depends on your hardware
Price: free desktop app. Upscayl Cloud Pro costs $24.99 for 300 credits, with outputs up to 256 MP.

Free vs Paid: Watermarks and Commercial Use
Only Upscayl gives you unlimited free upscales. Krea’s free plan includes daily units but no commercial license, and it stops at 2K. Other free tiers are trials, limited to personal use, or watermarked.
Upscayl: free and open source; runs locally with no credits.
Krea: 100 units a day; upscales to 2K with basic models; commercial license from the Basic plan.
Magnific: free plan for personal use only, with attribution; commercial use on paid plans.
Let’s Enhance: 10 free credits; free results carry a watermark, removed after you buy a plan.
Higgsfield AI: upscaling needs a paid plan.
OpenArt: 40 one-time trial credits, enough for two Precise 4x upscales of our file but not one Generative upscale; commercial use from Plus.
Which AI Image Upscaler Should You Choose?
Choose by the task, not by the maximum scale factor. Fidelity tools suit real photos, products and print; generative tools suit AI art and soft renders. If you already create in Photoshop, Higgsfield, Krea or OpenArt, use the upscaler built into that platform.

Got any questions left?
Which AI image upscalers have an API? Magnific offers an Upscaler API, Let’s Enhance offers upscaling through the Claid.ai API from $59 for 1,000 credits, and Higgsfield’s ByteDance Upscale is available through Higgsfield MCP for use in AI assistants.

What resolution do I need to print an upscaled image? At 300 pixels per inch, an 8×10-inch print needs 2400×3000 pixels. A 4x upscale of a 1024×1024 image gives 4096×4096 pixels, enough for about a 13.6-inch square print at 300 PPI.

Can I upscale an image without uploading it to the cloud? Yes. Upscayl and Topaz Gigapixel both render on your own computer. Upscayl is free but needs a Vulkan-compatible GPU; Gigapixel needs a paid plan and, for generative models, 8 GB of VRAM.

Does upscaling make an AI image safe for commercial use? No. Commercial rights come from the plan, not the upscale. Magnific’s free plan is personal use only, OpenArt allows commercial use from Plus, and Higgsfield’s Free plan excludes commercial use.

Can AI upscalers fix old or damaged photos? They can sharpen and enlarge them, and some offer restoration modes, such as Let’s Enhance Old Photo and Gigapixel Face Recovery. For a full step-by-step restoration, see How to Restore Old Photos with AI.

Can I upscale video with the same tools? Some of them. Krea Enhancer upscales videos up to 8K, and Higgsfield Upscale accepts both images and videos. Magnific has a separate Video Upscaler. This guide covers image upscalers only. See AI Video Restoration for tools that enhance and upscale video.
