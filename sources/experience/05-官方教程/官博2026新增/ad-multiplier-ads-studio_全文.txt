Introducing Ad Multiplier: Turn One Video Ad Into Multiple Variations
Higgsfield
Oct 7, 2026
10 min

Higgsfield just launched Ad Multiplier in Ads Studio, a tool that creates variations from a reference ad with your characters, products, and locations. Upload one video, add your images, and describe the scene to generate several versions. You can also change the hook, script, or call to action per version. This guide covers how it works and what it costs.

What Is Ad Multiplier and How Does It Work?

Ad Multiplier turns one existing ad into new variations by replacing selected elements such as the character, product, outfit, or location while preserving the original motion, framing, cuts, timing, and other parts you do not ask it to change.

Here is an example with a made-up product, a matte lipstick in the shade Brick Rose from a fictional brand called Rosewood Cosmetics.

Test New Hooks, Scripts, and CTAs

Ad Multiplier changes the message as well as the visuals. Three parts of the ad can change from one concept:

Hook: The opening line or moment that earns the next few seconds of attention.
Script: The text of the ad, which you can rewrite.
CTA: The call to action, such as shop, book, or visit.

One concept can carry several messages, so you can compare them side by side:

Create Multiple Ad Variations From One Setup

How to Use Ad Multiplier in Ads Studio

Step 1: Open Ad Multiplier. Open Ads Studio in the Extension, go to Explore tools, and select Ad multiplier.

Step 2: Add the reference video. Upload the ad you want to recreate. It must be between 4 and 30 seconds, so trim a longer video first. Use a reference ad and character or product images that you own or have permission to use. If a real person appears in the reference material, make sure you have the necessary consent to use their likeness.

Step 3: Add your images. Add up to 20 images of your characters, products, or clothing.

Step 4: Describe the new scene. Write your prompt and mention the attached files with @. For example:

“Recreate the reference ad using @lipstick as the product. Replace the woman with @model and move the scene to a sunlit café window seat. Change the hook to "Looking for your everyday red?" and end with the CTA "Shop Brick Rose."”

Step 5: Choose the quality. Select 480p, 720p, or 1080p.

Step 6: Check the cost and generate. Review the credit cost on the Generate button, then start the generation.

Step 7: Review your versions in History. Open History to see the finished versions. Check the product's look, the text, and every claim before you use a version, and generate more from the ones that work.

Ad Multiplier Pricing

Ad Multiplier has no fixed price. The cost depends on your inputs, your prompt, whether you change the hook or the CTA, and the result you want. The total in credits shows on the Generate button before you start, and additional generations or alternatives may increase it.
