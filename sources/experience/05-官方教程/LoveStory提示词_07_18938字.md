# What it does: Animates the giant fight — the throw, the sword snatch and the tak

Assets: @Nate_arena, @giant, @arena, @sketch




TITLE — MASTER SEQUENCE · RIDE & THROW → THE SWORD → THE FIGHT: one combined prompt, thirteen shots in strict order, every shot written at the same level of detail. The boy rides the chain on the giant's back and is slung off (shots 1–5); he lands right beside his sword and takes it (shot 6); the fight, Fury-Road style, two calf cuts and the two-stage fall (shots 7–13). 30 seconds total, real time throughout.




— REFERENCE DEFINITIONS (shared by the whole sequence) —




@Nate_arena — the boy. Young Central Asian warrior, early 20s, ~175 cm / 70 kg, short dark side-parted hair soaked with sweat, clean-shaven face with dirt and bruise smudges, sculpted bronze muscled cuirass over a ragged dark-red short-sleeved tunic, wide leather belt with a round bronze sun-medallion buckle, bronze bracers, pleated dark-red skirt with a frayed hem, tall gladiator sandals laced to the knees. Lean, light, fast. Character appearance only. WEAPON STATE: BOTH HANDS EMPTY through shots 1–5 and the start of shot 6 — his gladius was knocked away earlier and lies in the sand; from the snatch inside SHOT 6 onward the short gladius-style sword is in his RIGHT hand for the rest of the sequence.




@giant — the giant. Colossal executioner gladiator, ~4.5 m tall, several tons, immensely strong and slow to turn. FRONT: head hidden under a crudely stitched dark-brown leather hood with a wide leather mantle over the shoulders, round stitched eye-holes and a sutured mouth seam ON THE FRONT of the hood only, small glinting eyes deep inside; bare hairy chest, heavy sagging belly, thick fat-padded arms, short thick neck. BACK: the blank leather crown and nape of the hood with its vertical stitched seam (no eye-holes on this side); the scalloped rear edge of the mantle; mottled sun-weathered skin, shoulder blades flanking the chain crossing, spine furrow, leather waistband and torn rear hem of the skirt. CHAIN: ONE massive rusted chain, and only one — crossing in a large X in the middle of the bare BACK below the mantle, its four runs travelling downward and outward around the sides of the torso and under the armpits; it lies flat against the skin, wrapped tight, never passes over the tops of the shoulders, has NO hanging ends, nothing dangles from it; this is the ONLY chain that exists anywhere in the sequence. Ragged dark-brown hide skirt, crude leather sandals. HUGE long-hafted bearded battle axe with a rusted iron head in one fist — in rear view the axe reads frame-right, fixing that hand as the axe hand and the other as the FREE hand. Character appearance only. NOT inherited from the reference: grey studio backdrop, flat light, static pose, framing.




@arena — the arena. Geometry, materials and atmosphere only: sun-bleached stone arena at midday, open sand, dust haze in the backlight, red-and-white perimeter wall, colonnade, roaring crowd behind the wall.




@sketch — scale lock only: the giant is roughly two and a half times the boy's height; the boy's head barely reaches the giant's hip. Hold in every frame.




— GLOBAL STYLE, OPTICS, AUDIO (all shots) —

8K IMAX-scale photorealism, live-action footage from a real physical camera. Extreme anamorphic look: horizontal squeeze and compression, oval elliptical bokeh with stretched highlights, curved barrel edge distortion, mild chromatic aberration toward the corners — all restrained. NO lens flares, NO streaks, NO floating bokeh circles. 2.39:1, 24fps, 180° shutter motion blur. Colour 60:30:10 — sand and pale stone / dark iron and leather / dark-red accent. Natural light only: harsh midday desert sun, contre-jour, camera on the shadow side; sky blown out white; dust haze with sun shafts; light direction identical across the whole sequence. Pore-level skin realism, sweat through dust, rust transferring onto palms. Hollywood acting texture: micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-lines, heavy breathing. NON-IP. True mass and real physics — every movement biomechanically real; dust raised in one shot still hangs in the next; correct contact shadows always. Consistent grade and grain across all thirteen shots.




Audio, environmental SFX only, pure diegetic: roaring crowd, footfalls through sand, chain links grinding, axe whoosh and heavy iron impacts, sword slice whoosh, the giant's muffled bellow and scream of pain, ragged breathing, deep body impacts, drifting dust hiss. No dialogue, no music, no narration, no subtitles.




— OCCLUSION AND GLOBAL LOCKS (shots 1–5) —

LOCATION LOCK: every shot is the SAME @arena — sand grade, wall, colonnade, crowd line, haze and light direction identical from shot 1 to shot 13. SCALE LOCK: the @giant / @Nate_arena height relationship holds exactly as in @sketch in every frame.

In shots 1, 2, 3 the camera stays BEHIND the action: layers near to far — the boy, then the giant's BACK, then the arena; back markers exactly as in @giant's BACK description (blank stitched crown and nape, scalloped mantle edge, shoulder blades flanking the chain crossing, spine furrow, mottled skin, rear waistband and rear hem, backs of the arms with elbow points at camera); front markers (chest, belly, groin, eye-holes, mouth seam, face of the hood) do NOT appear in shots 1–3. SHOT 4 is the one sanctioned shot showing the front of the hood, in three-quarter, with the boy invisible behind the bulk. POSTURE: the giant fully upright, vertical spine, never bent or staggered by 70 kg. GRIP POINT: the X-crossing of the chain between the SHOULDER BLADES, ~2.8 m up — fingers jammed under the crossed links; the only handhold. THE REACH FAILS: fat, thick, relatively short arms and a short thick neck — he physically cannot reach the middle of his own back; fingers close on air, short by a clear margin, every time; that is why he resorts to rotation. SCREEN DIRECTION: the boy travels LEFT TO RIGHT through the entire sequence. FIGHT RULES (shots 7–13): @giant fights like the HULK from the Avengers — a wrathful berserk colossus with explosive smash energy, yet a seasoned champion who almost NEVER misses: every axe blow and fist strike is precise, fully committed and devastating, cratering the sand exactly where it is aimed, so the boy survives by centimeters. @Nate_arena fights like JOHN WICK — a cold surgical professional: deft, attentive, economical, reading each attack a breath early, dodging with the minimum necessary movement; his sword answers only at the giant's legs — exactly TWO cuts across the BACKS of the calves, delivered from behind, each leaving a thin dark scar line SEAMED WITH BLOOD: the blood sits IN the cut, dark and clotted, beaded along the line and staining its edges — it stays inside the wound, never running, never dripping, never spattering. Each cut is followed by the giant's scream of pain. Strike and evasion always synchronized as one cause-and-effect pair: the giant aims at the exact spot where the boy IS, and the boy's move carries him away from THAT precise blow. Medium frames on the giant; only the boy's face may go closer; the skin everywhere else stays clean — the only blood in the sequence lives inside those two cuts.




— ACTION TIMING · 13 SHOTS —




SHOT 1 · 0.0–1.0s · THE TARGET — handheld, low (~0.9 m), running BEHIND AND JUST LEFT of @Nate_arena's shoulder, aimed forward at @giant across the @arena sand. The boy's back and right shoulder fill the near foreground-left, both hands empty, arms driving; ahead across the churned sand the giant stands upright, squarely FROM BEHIND as in @giant — blank crown of the hood, scalloped mantle, the chain's X catching the sun, axe hanging in his fist — hood turned away, scanning the far side of the arena. A SHORT shot: two-three driving strides, the camera jolting with each; the boy plants a sandal on the raised rim of an old axe crater and leaves the ground. Shot ends as his feet come off the rim. His eyes are locked on the chain crossing from the first stride.




SHOT 2 · 1.0–2.5s · THE LEAP IN PROFILE — wide, handheld, camera low (~1.0 m) to the SIDE and slightly behind @giant: the giant in three-quarter REAR view at frame-right per @giant, full 4.5 m in frame; @Nate_arena's flight arc crosses the frame in PROFILE from frame-left — quick and compact, body extended, arms reaching for the crossed links, legs trailing, skirt hem snapping, his shadow racing across the sand into the giant's, the flight in near-silhouette against the backlight. CUT at the last instant BEFORE contact — fingertips a hand's width from the links; the catch is not in this shot.




SHOT 3 · 2.5–4.5s · THE CATCH AND THE FACE — WIDE from ABOVE in 3/4 on @Nate_arena, camera high over the giant's shoulder line looking down past the blank nape and mantle onto the back: the hands SLAM onto the X-crossing on the cut-in, read as impact — body swinging through and slapping flat against the bare back, knees sinking into the fat, dust jumping off the skin, the giant jolting, the chain grinding link on link; his legs swing free over the drop. His upturned FACE stays readable — the acting beat plays through his eyes in this order: a darting glance AROUND, one flick each way across the arena far below; then the gaze CLIMBS the chain link by link along the taut crossed runs, up toward the mantle and the blank stitched nape — and arrives at what the chain is wrapped AROUND: the giant himself. The arrival is a freeze — pupils stop, breath catches, BEWILDERMENT flooding the upturned face between his straining arms. (Safety: gaze always engaged, alive and focused; natural blink cadence.)




SHOT 4 · 4.5–6.5s · THREE-QUARTER — HE TRIES TO SEE IT — MEDIUM, @giant in 3/4 front view: the stitched eye-holes and sutured mouth seam readable at the angle, the heavy chest, the axe fist hanging still. Both arms stay DOWN — no swinging, no reaching. He tries to SEE what has climbed onto him: the hooded head cranks over one shoulder as far as the short thick neck allows, torso twisting a little after it — ONCE to the RIGHT — then whips back and cranks over the other shoulder — ONCE to the LEFT — each turn falling short, the eye-holes never getting the back into view, the mantle creasing, the chain shifting on his skin. The boy stays INVISIBLE behind the bulk — only a jolted flash of an elbow or sandal past the silhouette's edge. The muffled bellow builds, more baffled than enraged.




SHOT 5 · 6.5–9.5s · ONE REVOLUTION AND THE SNAP — SUPER-WIDE from GROUND LEVEL, camera in the sand: the whole @giant against the blown-out sky and colonnade. ONE full heavy revolution around his own axis — sandals shuffling and stamping a tight circle, a ring of dust punching outward, belly and chest fat lagging a beat behind the skeleton, the axe swinging wide on the outside of the turn, the boy clamped to the rotating back, hidden and revealed as the bulk comes around — then he PLANTS one sandal hard and STOPS the several-ton torso dead against it. The sudden stop does everything: the boy's inertia rips his fingers from the links and he is SLUNG off on the tangent toward frame-right, a streak of motion blur, and exits the frame right. The chain snaps flat back with a heavy rattle; the giant is left planted and heaving in his own dust wall. His hands never touch the boy.




SHOT 6 · 9.5–13.5s · THE LANDING AT THE SWORD — static ground-level camera, 35mm, locked off, one focus rack; the landing and the pickup are ONE shot. At the start the frame holds ONLY sand and the sword: the short gladius lies completely FLAT on its side in the foreground third, hilt toward camera, every centimetre of blade and hilt resting on the surface, lightly dusted, nothing pointing at the sky. FOCUS sits on the open patch of sand deeper in frame — the exact spot where he is about to land — the sword soft in the foreground. @Nate_arena is SLUNG IN from the LEFT edge low and fast, crashes shoulder-first into that focused patch and the momentum carries him through a tumbling GROUND ROLL — shoulder, hip, shoulder — that dumps him right BESIDE the blade, at ground level, sand blasting up. Dazed, propped on his hands: LOST and FRIGHTENED, breath panicked, quick jumpy glances around, understanding nothing — until his eyes CATCH the sword a hand away. The FOCUS RACKS from the landing spot onto the BLADE — and inside that sword-focused frame the change lands: one beat of stillness, the




jaw sets, the eyes harden, resolve replacing fear. He SNATCHES the sword off the sand in one sharp move, rises and sprints OUT of frame toward frame-RIGHT — toward the unseen giant. The giant never appears in this shot; the camera never moves, only the focus shifts once.




SHOT 7 · 13.5–15.5s · THE CHARGE — low angle from the sand, directly BEHIND @Nate_arena, handheld pursuit energy: the boy runs away from camera across the open sand toward @giant, who stands mid-arena against the backlit colonnade and blown-out sky, dust rolling around his feet, the huge axe in his fist, sword now in the boy's right hand, sand kicking off his heels — his small dark running figure center-framed against the giant's silhouette, growing with every stride.

— PHYSICS —

The leap: a real ballistic arc off the crater rim, path fixed once airborne, already falling as the hands meet the iron. The catch: a snatch at the limit of reach — fingertips first, hard shock load, knuckles blanching, the chain rolling a couple of centimetres across sweat-slick skin before it bites. The giant: everything initiates from the ground and hips and cannot be recalled mid-motion; sandals sink and plow furrows; flesh lags a beat behind the skeleton; he is never destabilised by 70 kg. The rotation: angular momentum, one revolution, slow to build, heavy through the middle; the boy is thrown by the SUDDEN STOP, released along the tangent. The axe blows in the fight: full commitment, real craters, the boy thrown clear only by his own timed evasions. The falls: the landing tumble dumps momentum ugly and involuntary; the giant's fall is two-stage biomechanics — knee first, then the mass tipping belly-up. The sand craters and sprays ballistically; dust persists shot to shot; correct contact shadows always, including the flight shadow in shot 2 and the giant's long shadow carrying a small human shape in the wides.




— CONSTRAINTS —

Exactly two figures on the sand in all thirteen shots; the crowd stays behind the perimeter wall. Exactly ONE chain exists — crossed flat in an X on the giant's back, no loose ends, never duplicated, never a weapon. In shots 1–5 the boy is on the giant's BACK, never his front; the catch happens ON THE CUT into shot 3; the giant's hands never touch the boy anywhere in the sequence. The boy's hands are empty until the snatch in SHOT 6; from then on the gladius stays in his right hand. In the fight his strikes land ONLY on the BACKS of the giant's calves, from behind, exactly twice, each leaving a thin blood-seamed scar — dark clotted blood held inside the cut, beading and staining its edges, never flowing or dripping — each followed by a scream AFTER the visible contact. The giant's fall is knee-first, then belly-up, and he stays alive, breathing, fighting the pain. The giant keeps hood, mantle and axe-hand consistent across all shots. The boy travels left to right through the entire sequence. All shots handheld except SHOTS 3 and 6.
