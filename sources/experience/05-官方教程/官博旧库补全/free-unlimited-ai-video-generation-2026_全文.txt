The Concurrency Limit

Unlimited generations run at 1 concurrent image, video, or audio generation at a time. This concurrency limit is shared across all models, meaning you cannot run a Seedance 2.0 generation and a Kling 3.0 generation unlimited at the same moment, both draw from the same single concurrency slot. Your plan's normal maximum concurrency for regular credit-based generations still applies as usual, it just does not extend to the unlimited generations during this trial. In practice, this means the trial is built for sequential generation and iteration rather than running a large batch simultaneously, so plan your 24 hours around generating one output at a time, reviewing it, and moving to the next rather than queuing many jobs in parallel.
