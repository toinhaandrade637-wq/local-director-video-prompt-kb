How to Turn Claude Into a Full Creative Studio With Higgsfield
Higgsfield
Sep 2, 2026
10 min

One MCP connection gives Claude access to Higgsfield's full creative pipeline. Video, images, audio, and Cinema Studio are all part of it. From the same Claude conversation, you can create images, videos, and audio, reuse past assets, run creative workflows, and use supported Higgsfield editing tools without switching between apps. This guide covers turning it on, what's available, and a full workflow.

What Is Higgsfield MCP for Claude, and How Do I Connect It?

Higgsfield MCP connects Higgsfield’s creative tools to Claude, letting Claude generate and work with Higgsfield assets directly from the conversation. Generations use credits from your existing Higgsfield account, so there is no separate MCP balance or API key to manage.

Setting it up is a one-time process that takes a few minutes.

Сopy the connector URL : https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp.
In Claude Desktop or on claude.ai, go to Customize → Connectors, add a new connector, name it Higgsfield, and paste the URL into the field provided.
Click Connect, and sign in with an existing Higgsfield account when prompted, no separate account or sign-up needed.

Once authorized, Higgsfield remains connected to your Claude account. Depending on the Claude interface, you may still need to make sure the connector is enabled in the current conversation.

What Tools Are Available Through Claude MCP?

Higgsfield gives your agent access to 30+ models, including Soul, Cinema Studio, Seedance 2.5, Kling, and more. If you don’t name a model, the agent can select one based on the task. You can also specify the exact model when you want more control.

Generation covers a wide range across image, video, and audio:

Images: up to 4K resolution, generated from a text prompt, a reference image, or a combination of both.
Videos: generate clips through Higgsfield's video models, use reference assets where supported, and refine outputs with operations such as upscaling, reframing, background removal, and motion controls.
Audio: generate voiceovers and use voice cloning, voice change, and video dubbing through the same connection.
Full production skills: beyond single generations, Claude can run entire skill-based workflows through MCP, a complete ad from a product photo and a creator reference, a faceless explainer video with voiceover and subtitles, or a multi-variation ad campaign built off one existing ad.

Claude can also browse your Higgsfield generation history, so previous images and videos can be found and reused as references in later steps instead of being generated again from scratch.

Step 5: Review and refine. Check the output against the original brief. If something needs to change, describe the specific issue and let Claude run the appropriate Higgsfield editing operation or skill.

Step 6: Download or continue from Assets. Finished MCP generations appear in your Higgsfield Assets as well as in the connected workflow. From there, you can download, share, or continue working with the result.

5 Tips
Name the model when you want predictable control. Otherwise, Claude can choose one automatically based on the request.
Use references when consistency matters. Lock the character, product, or visual direction before building later assets around it.
Be specific about the output, not just the subject. Include format, action, framing, style, pacing, and intended use.
Review one generation before scaling the workflow. Confirm the direction before asking Claude to produce multiple variations.
Check the credit cost before generating when budget matters. You can tell Claude to show the expected credit cost and wait for your approval before it runs a generation.
