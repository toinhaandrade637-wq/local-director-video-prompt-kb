Meet Soul Cinema Preview 

Higgsfield's latest photo model is built for the creatives and everyone who do not want basic AI generated images. It is designed to give cinematic touch in every generation.

What Is Soul Cinema Preview?

Soul Cinema Preview is another of our sophisticated AI photo models that joins the Soul family. While Soul 2.0 - our flagship AI Photo Model - was designed to produce high-quality aesthetic visuals, Soul Cinema Preview mainly targets generation of cinematic-grade images. 

“NB: Be the first to try Soul Cinema Preview! This model's version is a teaser, in a way, and the full version will be released next week. Stay tuned!”

Soul Cinema Preview can also operate with the following core systems of the Soul family:

SOUL ID

A personalization layer designed for character consistency. SOUL ID allows creators to build recurring visual identities, digital personas, or campaign characters with improved coherence across generations. For best results, reference images from the same time period are recommended to create the best Soul ID character and maintain stylistic consistency.

SOUL HEX

An important feature that gives you precise color control in your AI image generations. Extract palettes from 

reference photos and generate brand-consistent and color-matched visuals in minutes.

Soul HEX. Color Control and Consistency

Soul HEX enables the best color control across generations for creators to build continuous visuals in the scope of a campaign, a creative project, or their next AI movie. 

SOUL ID. Character and Identity Consistency 

Soul ID enables the strongest character consistency across generations as creators can build recurring visual identities. This is especially useful for high-end fashion campaigns, AI films, and narrative visual systems.

Consistency improves significantly when reference images are drawn from the same time period and lighting context, helping the model maintain continuity in tone and structure.

How To Use Soul Cinema Preview?

Soul Cinema Preview is very simple in use, similar to Soul 2.0. 

Go to the "Image" tab in the navigation bar and select "Soul Cinema Preview". 
The workframe, just like in Soul 2.0, will show you options to choose HEX or your Soul ID character. 
Apart from the nature and aesthetics of the output between Soul 2.0 and Soul Cinema Preview, the latter also does not have preset selection panel.
