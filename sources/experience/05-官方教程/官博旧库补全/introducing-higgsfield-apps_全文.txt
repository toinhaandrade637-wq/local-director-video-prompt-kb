Introducing Higgsfield Apps: Your Way to AI-Powered Apps with No Code
Higgsfield
Jul 10, 2026
9 min

Building apps from scratch has always meant writing code, managing a stack, and wiring in AI models through API keys and integration layers. Higgsfield AI just shipped something that skips all of that. Apps, a new product inside Supercomputer, lets you describe what you want in chat and get a working generative application back: design, code, database, and Higgsfield models connected automatically. Users generate on their own credits, so the creator pays nothing when others use it.

What Are Higgsfield Apps?

Higgsfield Apps is a new product inside Supercomputer, the builder platform that also includes Websites and Games. 

The core idea is straightforward: you describe an app in plain language, an agent plans and builds it, design, code, database, and generative model integration included, and you publish it to a live URL. Users who open the app sign in with their own Higgsfield account and generate on their own credits, so the creator's cost is zero no matter how many people use it.

What makes Apps different from other no-code builders is the generative-by-default architecture. The agent connects to Higgsfield's 15+ generation models, image, video, 3D, automatically. An AI photobooth, an anime filter, a music video generator, an ad factory: any app where the core experience is AI-generated output gets built without writing a single line of integration code. The agent wires the models in on its own.

There are four app types: Custom, Simple App, Studio, and Preset App. Four style options: Custom, Higgsfield, Y2K, and Modern. Two generation modes: Efficient for faster builds, Smart for more complex logic.

How to Build Your First App: Step by Step

Step 1. Open Supercomputer and go to Products.

Navigate to higgsfield.ai/supercomputer/apps. You will see the Apps section under Products in the left sidebar, alongside Websites and Games.

Step 2. Start a new chat and describe your app.

Type what you want to build in plain language in the chat field. One sentence is enough to get started. Example: "Build an AI ad generator where users upload a product photo and get 20 different ad styles." The agent reads the description and starts planning.

Step 3. Choose your app type, style, and mode.

Select from Custom, Simple App, Studio, or Preset App depending on your structure. Pick a style: Custom, Higgsfield, Y2K, or Modern. Choose Efficient mode for a faster lighter build or Smart mode for more complex logic and deeper reasoning.

Step 4. Answer the agent's follow-up questions.

The agent asks about specific details: who the app is for, what inputs users need to provide, which generation models should power it, what the output format should be. Answer in chat. The agent fills in the rest.

Step 5. Watch the build come together.

The agent handles design, code, database, sign-in, and generative model integration simultaneously. You do not touch the stack. Every change the agent makes is readable in the chat.

Step 6. Ask for changes in the same chat.

"Make it dark." "Add a gallery view." "Change the button copy to Generate." The agent updates the app and returns a fresh preview link in minutes.

Step 7. Publish.

Hit Publish. Before the app goes live, an LLM runs an automated check for malicious intent, NSFW content, and IP issues. If it passes, SEO optimization and a security check run automatically. The app's cover image, icon, and description are generated automatically, all editable before the final publish. You can also publish directly through the agent in chat. The app goes live at name.higgsfield.app.

What Happens After You Publish

Publishing is not the end of the workflow. A few things happen automatically.

The app runs itself. App users sign in with their own Higgsfield accounts and generate on their own credits. A thousand users cost the creator zero. The creator builds and publishes. Users bring their own credits.

It lives on the marketplace. Published apps appear in the community Explore feed under All Apps, Community, and Made by Higgsfield tabs. Other users find the app, use it, and remix it. You submit to the marketplace directly from the Apps page with the Submit an App button.

My Apps section. A dedicated section shows all your apps with hover-to-edit controls, clear visibility into what is published and what is not, like/dislike, report options, and Credit Spend tracking per app.

Build limits. Builds spend regular credits covering text and any image or video generations the app includes. Up to 20 apps per day.
