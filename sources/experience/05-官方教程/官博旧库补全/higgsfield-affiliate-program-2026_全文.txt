The Higgsfield Affiliate Program lets creators with an audience earn up to 25% commission on qualifying subscription purchases, renewals, and upgrades for up to 12 months. Anyone with an audience can apply and get a personal affiliate link to share. This guide covers eligibility and how commission works. How to join, tracking, and refunds are covered further down.

What Is the Higgsfield Affiliate Program?

The Higgsfield Affiliate Program gives any user a personal link to share with their audience. When someone subscribes to a paid Higgsfield plan through that link, the affiliate earns a commission on the purchase.

The core of the program is its recurring structure. Commission doesn't stop after the first payment. It continues for as long as the referred user keeps their subscription active, for up to 12 months from the day they became a referral. A referral who upgrades their plan or renews month after month keeps generating commission the whole time.

Referrals also get a personal discount of up to 50% off, active for three hours from the moment they log in through the affiliate link.

Who Is Eligible to Join the Program?

Anyone with an audience can apply, and the application process is short. The link goes live immediately after signing up, with no waiting period before it's usable.

The program fits a specific kind of user particularly well: creators, influencers, educators, newsletter authors, and community owners, people who already have a channel where mentioning a tool like Higgsfield fits into what they're already doing. A YouTuber who edits with AI tools, a newsletter that covers AI video, or a course that teaches generative content, all of these already have the kind of audience that converts well on a recommendation.

Established creators can also reach out directly at affiliate@higgsfield.ai.

How to Join: Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Apply for the affiliate program at Higgsfield's affiliate page. The form asks where you plan to share Higgsfield, along with details about your channel, audience, and platform.

Step 2: Get your personal affiliate link. It's available immediately after applying, with no approval delay before it's active.

Step 3: Share the link with your audience. A video description, a newsletter, a course, a thread, anywhere it would naturally make sense to mention a tool like Higgsfield.

Step 4: Earn commission on qualifying purchases, renewals, and upgrades made through your link.

What You Need to Know:
A refund on a referral's purchase cancels the commission tied to that specific transaction; no payout happens on a purchase that gets refunded.
Clear tracking is built into the program, so subscriptions and commissions attributed to a specific link stay visible.
Paid advertising on the link isn't allowed, including bidding on the Higgsfield brand name or posting to coupon and cashback sites. Organic sharing through existing channels is fine.
