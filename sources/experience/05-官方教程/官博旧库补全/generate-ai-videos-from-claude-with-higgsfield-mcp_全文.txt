How to Generate AI Videos in Claude With the Higgsfield MCP Connector (2026)

Higgsfield

Oct 27, 2026

7 min

To generate AI videos in Claude, add Higgsfield as a custom connector in Claude, sign in to your Higgsfield account, and describe the video in a chat. Claude picks a Higgsfield model, sets the parameters, runs the generation and returns the clip in the conversation. Each generation uses Higgsfield credits. This guide covers setup, your first video, what you can make, what it costs, and how to fix common problems.

TL;DR

Add Higgsfield as a custom connector in Claude with the URL 
https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp
, then sign in to Higgsfield. No API key is needed.

Describe the video in plain language. Claude chooses the model, or you can name one in the prompt.

Every generation through the connector uses credits. Unlimited models and free generations don’t apply.

What Is the Higgsfield MCP Connector?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants use external tools. The Higgsfield MCP connector gives Claude access to Higgsfield’s video, image and audio tools, so you can generate from a chat instead of switching to the Higgsfield site.

What Is the Higgsfield MCP Connector?

You need

Details

A Claude account

claude.ai or Claude Desktop. On the Free plan, Claude allows one custom connector.

A Higgsfield subscription

Generations use the credits on your plan.

Permission to add connectors

On Team plans, an Owner or Primary Owner adds the connector in Organization settings → Connectors. On Enterprise plans, people with a custom role that includes Manage access to Libraries can add it too. Members then connect it in Customize → Connectors.

Higgsfield also works with ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code and other compatible AI agents. The setup differs by client: Claude uses a custom MCP connector, ChatGPT uses the Higgsfield plugin, Cursor adds it from its Marketplace, and coding agents such as Claude Code can use the Higgsfield CLI.

How Does the Connector Work?

Three parts share the work. Claude turns your request into generation settings, MCP connects Claude to Higgsfield’s tools, and Higgsfield runs the generation and saves the result to your account. You sign in on Higgsfield’s own page, so Claude never sees your Higgsfield password.

Claude
 reads your request and the conversation, picks a model and settings, and writes the prompt. In our test, it split a one-paragraph brief into four shots before generating.

MCP
 is the open standard that lets Claude call Higgsfield’s tools. You connect once with the connector URL, and no API key is needed.

Higgsfield
 runs the model, generates audio when the model supports it, and returns the clip to the chat. The same clip is saved in History and Assets on 
higgsfield.ai
.

How Do You Connect Higgsfield to Claude?

You set it up once.

Step 1: Open Connectors.
 In 
claude.ai
 or Claude Desktop, go to 
Customize → Connectors
.

Step 2: Add a custom connector.
 Click 
+ Add
, then 
Add custom connector
, name it Higgsfield and paste the URL:

https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp

Step 3: Sign in.
 Click 
Connect
. A Higgsfield window opens: sign in and authorize access. If you’re already signed in on 
higgsfield.ai
, this happens automatically.

Step 4: Check the connector.
 Higgsfield now appears in your list of connectors. In a chat, open the 
+
 menu → 
Connectors
 and make sure Higgsfield is turned on.

Step 5: Test the connection.
 Open a new chat and ask, “What is my Higgsfield credit balance?” or “List my recent Higgsfield generations.” If Claude answers using Higgsfield, the connection is working. If it answers without calling Higgsfield, check that the connector is turned on and reconnect it.

Claude Code
 connects through the Higgsfield CLI instead of the connector URL:

npm i -g @higgsfield/cli
higgsfield auth login
npx skills add higgsfield-ai/skills

How Do You Generate Your First Video in Claude?

Open a new chat and describe the video: the subject, the action, the camera and the length. You don’t need to name a model, but you can, for example “Use Seedance 2.5”.

Our prompt:

Make a 10-second 16:9 video with Seedance 2.5 in 1080p. A young guy in a blue t-shirt and white pants rides a black bike down a palm-lined city boulevard at sunset, weaving between cars and taxis. He rides along the roof of a black stretch limo past an old theater marquee, then drops back onto the street. A quick POV shot shows his front wheel racing over the asphalt, then he leans into the lens with a smile and rides off into the sun. Before you generate it, tell me how many credits it will cost.

Copy

Recreate

Before generating, Claude split the prompt into four shots, showed the cost (160 credits) and asked for confirmation. After we approved, it ran the generation and returned the clip in the chat.

Your browser does not support the video.

Every generation is also saved to your Higgsfield account, in the History tab of the Video page and in Assets, where you can reuse past results as references.

To change the result, reply in the same chat: ask for another camera move, a different model, or a few variants of the same shot.

How Much Does Generating Video in Claude Cost?

Every generation through the connector deducts credits, regardless of your plan. Unlimited models and free generations work only on 
higgsfield.ai
, not through MCP.

The cost depends on the model, resolution and length:

The connector has no built-in spending limit. To keep control, ask Claude in the chat to show the plan and wait for your approval before each generation. Upscaling is the exception: it doesn’t show the cost in advance.

Don’t confuse the MCP connector with the Higgsfield API: the API is a separate product with its own billing.

What If Something Goes Wrong?

What If Something Goes Wrong?

Problem

What to do

Authorization failed

Disconnect the Higgsfield connector, then connect and authorize again

Claude doesn’t use Higgsfield

Start a new chat, mention Higgsfield in the prompt, and check that the connector is turned on for this chat

Generation completed, but no video appears

Wait up to 60 seconds, then check Manage Account → Usage to confirm the generation ran. If credits were deducted but no result appears, contact support with the generation ID and time.

Is the Higgsfield MCP Connector Worth Setting Up?

The Higgsfield MCP connector is worth setting up if you already work in Claude and want to generate video without switching to the Higgsfield site. Setup takes five steps and no API key. Every generation uses credits, so ask Claude to show the cost before it runs.

Use it for
 quick text-to-video clips, running one brief on several models, and image-to-video with files added through the Higgsfield upload window.

Check the cost first
 for longer or higher-resolution clips: a 10-second Seedance 2.5 clip costs 70 credits at 720p and 160 at 1080p.

Got any questions left?

Do I need an API key?

No. Add the MCP URL in Claude and sign in to your Higgsfield account.

Do I need a paid plan to use Higgsfield in Claude?

Yes. Generations through the connector use the credits on your Higgsfield plan.

Does Unlimited work through MCP?

No. Every generation through the connector uses credits. Unlimited models and free generations work only on higgsfield.ai.

Can I use Higgsfield in Claude Code?

Yes, through the Higgsfield CLI: install it with 
npm i -g @higgsfield/cli
, sign in with 
higgsfield auth login
, then add the skills with 
npx skills add higgsfield-ai/skills
.

Can I use Higgsfield in ChatGPT?

Yes, through the Higgsfield plugin at 
chatgpt.com/plugins/higgsfield
. Open it, sign in to your Higgsfield account, and Higgsfield appears in the ChatGPT sidebar.

Where are my videos saved?

In the chat and in your Higgsfield account: the History tab of the Video page and Assets. You can reuse past results as references in new generations.
