How to Use Veo 3.1: A Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: 
Select Your Generation Engine 

Start by navigating to the "Video" tab and selecting the Google Veo 3.1 model as your generation engine.

Step 2: 
Choose Your Generation Mode 

Before you generate, choose the model that best fits your creative idea. Decide between Veo 3.1 Standard & Veo 3.1 Fast according to your creative intentions. 

Step 3:
 Provide Your Creative Input 

You can generate from a text prompt, a single image, multiple images, or define the start and end of your video by uploading a Start Frame and an End Frame.

Step 4:
 Generate Your Video 

Click "Generate." Veo 3.1 will then create your video, incorporating your chosen controls to deliver a high-quality, 8-second, 1080p clip that matches your vision.

What is Google Veo 3.1?

Veo 3.1 is latest AI video generation model  by Gooogle, that creates high-quality videos in 1080p from simple text prompts or reference images. It offers a new level of creative control through three powerful generation modes.

What is the difference between the &#x27;Standard&#x27; and &#x27;Fast&#x27; models?

Standard model uses Reference-to-Video for keeping subjects consistent and ideal for complex scenes, while Fast uses Start & End Frame for controlling motion and has faster generation time.

What is the maximum video length I can generate using Veo 3.1?

You can generate videos with a selectable duration of 4 seconds, 6 seconds, or 8 seconds.

What video formats and resolutions are supported?

Veo 3.1 supports both 720p and 1080p resolutions at 24 FPS. You can generate videos with durations of 4, 6, or 8 seconds in either 16:9 (landscape) or 9:16 (portrait) aspect ratios.

What is Subject Consistency?

Exclusive to the Standard model, this feature allows you to upload 1-3 reference images of a character or object. The AI then maintains the subject&#x27;s identity and appearance across all frames of the video, ensuring perfect continuity.

Does Veo 3.1 support dialogues?

Yes, the Standard model supports speaking characters with realistic facial expressions and lip-syncing, making it perfect for storytelling, marketing content, or any video featuring dialogue.

What aspect ratios are supported?

You can create videos in the two most popular aspect ratios. 16:9 (Landscape), perfect for cinematic shots, YouTube, and standard television formats and 9:16 (Portrait), ideal for mobile-first platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
