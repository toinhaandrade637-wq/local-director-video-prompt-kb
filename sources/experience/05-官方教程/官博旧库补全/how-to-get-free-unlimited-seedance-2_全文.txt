How the Referral Program Works

When you open the referral flow, you generate a unique referral link. The moment that link is generated, a 24-hour countdown starts. Everything that needs to happen for you to earn the reward has to happen inside that window.

Your friend needs to complete three steps in sequence: follow your referral link, purchase a Plus or Pro monthly plan or higher, and make at least one credit-paid generation on Higgsfield. All three have to land within 24 hours of you generating the link.

If your friend completes all three steps within the window, you earn Unlimited access to Seedance 2.0. The number of days depends on how many friends complete the full flow:

Who Can Use the Referral Program

The referral program is available to users on Pro and Plus plans and above. If you are on a Starter or Basic plan, the invite option will not appear in your account. B2B plan users are also excluded from the program.

Open Higgsfield and the referral modal will appear if your account is eligible. From there you can generate your unique link and start inviting.

What Your Friends Get

The referral program works both ways. When a friend follows your referral link, they see a discount code for up to 60% off their plan purchase at the point of checkout. That makes it easier to share the link with a genuine recommendation rather than a cold ask: your friend gets a meaningful discount on a paid plan, and you earn Unlimited Seedance 2.0 access if they follow through.

What Happens After 24 Hours

Once the 24-hour window closes, you can no longer generate a new referral link. The block showing your invited friends stays visible in your account so you can track who completed the steps.

If all invited friends fulfill the criteria within the window, you receive a modal notification with the result: how many friends you successfully referred and how many days of Unlimited access you earned.

What Unlimited Seedance 2.0 Actually Means

Seedance 2.0 is one of the most capable commercial generation models on Higgsfield. It accepts up to 9 reference inputs simultaneously in a single generation call: a character photo, a location image, a product reference, a style image, and an audio track all at once. Native audio generates alongside the video in the same pass. For productions built around spokesperson content, product shoots, or scenes that require multiple precise references to hold together, Seedance 2.0 is usually the model doing the heavy lifting.

During the Unlimited period, every Seedance 2.0 generation at 720p and 8 seconds per clip runs without touching your credit balance. For anyone mid-production or planning a content push, that is a meaningful window to generate at volume without watching the credit counter.

If the referral window closes before you hit the 7-day maximum, Higgsfield also runs 30-day Unlimited promotions for Seedance 2.0 separately from the referral program.

What Counts as a Credit-Paid Generation

A credit-paid generation is any generation that deducts credits from your balance. Free-tier generations and Unlimited model generations that do not consume credits do not count. Your friend needs to make at least one generation that actually deducts credits.

If You Did Not Receive Your Reward

If your friend completed all three steps within 24 hours and you did not receive the reward, contact Higgsfield support. Before reaching out, confirm that all three steps were completed within the 24-hour window from when you generated the link.
