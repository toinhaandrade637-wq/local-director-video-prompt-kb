Higgsfield just launched a new Unlimited Seedance offer, running up to 33 days, available to anyone who activates it before August 9. It covers both Seedance 2.5 and Seedance 2.0, with access starting at Plus and scaling up from there by plan and billing period. Duration and max clip length both scale with plan tier and billing period. This guide breaks down what each tier actually receives. It also covers how to turn the offer on before the window closes.

What's Inside the New Offer
The offer covers up to 33 days of unlimited generation on Seedance 2.5, at up to 720p, with the clock starting the moment access activates rather than from a fixed calendar date
On top of that, Plus and Ultra plans also unlock unlimited Seedance 2.0 generation at up to 1080p, running on its own separate duration rather than sharing days with the 2.5 access
Neither duration nor max clip length is a flat number across the board, both scale with plan tier and billing cycle, so two people on different plans can be looking at very different windows even though they're technically on the same offer

“The Unlimited toggle inside the model menu has to be switched on before generating. Without it, generations pull from your regular credit balance even while unlimited access is active on your plan.”

Seedance 2.5: The Breakdown by Plan

Coverage starts at 22 days on the smallest annual tier and reaches the full 33 days on most plans above that, which means the gap between the lowest and highest tier is smaller than it looks at first glance, everyone who qualifies is getting close to a full month either way. On monthly billing, access to Seedance 2.5 starts at Plus, there's no tier below it that qualifies. Resolution stays fixed at 720p across every tier, so clip length is where the tiers actually separate from each other, climbing from 10 seconds on the entry plans up to 30 seconds on the highest one, so the real upgrade isn't more days so much as more room per generation.

Seedance 2.0: Duration by Tier

This runs as a separate benefit on top of Seedance 2.5, not a replacement for it, generating at up to 1080p. Clip length stays flat across every plan that gets it at all, fifteen seconds regardless of tier. What actually moves is who qualifies in the first place and how long the window lasts once they do, and both of those depend heavily on billing cycle rather than just plan tier.

Getting Access

The offer follows the same access pattern as before: available directly on qualifying plans, and through the Unlimited Models Marketplace for anyone already at the top tier with no higher plan left to upgrade into. Getting it running comes down to three things, done roughly in this order.

Start with the tables above, matching your actual plan and billing cycle. Duration and clip length both depend on this, and the same plan name means something different depending on whether it's billed monthly or annually, so it's worth checking both columns rather than assuming the number that applied last time still applies now.

Then turn on the Unlimited toggle in the model menu before generating anything. This is the step that's easiest to skip, and skipping it is the difference between generating for free under the offer and quietly drawing down your regular credit balance instead, even while the plan itself technically qualifies.

From there, generate on Seedance 2.5 for as many days as your plan covers, and switch over to Seedance 2.0 if your plan includes it. That second window runs on its own separate clock rather than sharing days with the 2.5 access, so using one doesn't shorten the other.
