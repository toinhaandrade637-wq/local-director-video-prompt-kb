Higgsfield Meets DaVinci Resolve: Generate B-Roll Right in Your Edit
Higgsfield
Jul 28, 2026
10 min

Higgsfield's plugin for DaVinci Resolve puts seven AI tools, an AI LUT creator, and Cinema Studio and Marketing Studio into one panel inside Resolve: generating B-roll from a text prompt, matching color to a reference frame, and reframing, removing backgrounds, or upscaling clips, all without leaving the app. This article covers what's in the panel, how the B-roll and color-matching workflows work, installation and requirements, cost, and what the plugin doesn't do.

What's Included in the DaVinci Resolve Panel?

One installer adds a single panel to Resolve with seven core tools, plus an AI LUT creator for color, and two utilities for moving generations into a project.

Last Generations gives quick access to recent Higgsfield output, and Import to Resolve places any result into the Media Pool or directly onto the Timeline.

How Do You Generate B-Roll Without Leaving Resolve?

Take the active clip or preview in Resolve, add a reference if you have one, and describe the shot. Once the job finishes, the result goes into the Media Pool or onto the Timeline through Import to Resolve. Nano Banana 2 is the default model for images and Seedance 2.0 the default for video, with a wider set of image and video models available under the hood.

If the clip doesn't fit the sequence, regenerate it from the same panel. If it fits but the color is off against surrounding footage, that's a separate step, covered by the AI LUT creator.

How Does the AI LUT Creator Match Color to a Reference?

The tool takes a source frame from your footage and a reference frame, a still, a graded shot, a photo, and builds the look from that pairing. The output comes back two ways: as a LUT in .cube format, or as color nodes applied directly to the current clip.

This addresses a specific and common problem with generated B-roll: a clip that looks fine on its own but doesn't sit right in color next to camera originals. Matching it to one reference frame from the sequence is meant to close that gap inside the same panel, without a separate grading pass.

Does the Plugin Include Cinema Studio and Marketing Studio?

Cinema Studio, Higgsfield's camera-controlled video tool, and Marketing Studio are both accessible directly from the Resolve panel, alongside the seven core tools. That reflects where Higgsfield sits as a product: not a single generator but an AI-native creative suite, where character consistency (Soul ID), cinematic generation (Cinema Studio), ad creative (Marketing Studio), and editing tools all run on one account and one credit balance. A character built with Soul ID on the platform, for instance, can be pulled into a Resolve project through Last Generations rather than regenerated from scratch. Higgsfield also ships separate plugins for Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, and Figma, all sharing that same account.

How Do You Install the Plugin, and What Do You Need?

Step 1. Download the installer from higgsfield.ai/plugins/davinci: a native installer for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, no helper app) or an .exe for Windows.

Step 2. Run the installer. It detects DaVinci Resolve automatically.

Step 3. Open Resolve and find Higgsfield under Workspace → Workflow Integrations, or under Workspace → Scripts on some setups. The panel can be docked anywhere in the workspace.

Requirements: DaVinci Resolve 19 or newer, on macOS or Windows. The App Store build of Resolve is not supported; use the version from blackmagicdesign.com instead. An internet connection is required for every generation, since inference runs on Higgsfield's servers and results are sent back into Resolve automatically.

What Does It Cost to Generate Through the Plugin?

Every generation through the plugin spends credits from your Higgsfield plan balance, the same balance shared across the web app, MCP, CLI, Supercomputer, and every other Higgsfield plugin. There's no separate account to set up for Resolve specifically. Any generation made through Resolve, or through any other third-party integration, draws from your credit balance regardless of a model's Unlimited status on the web platform.

What Are the Plugin's Limitations?
No offline mode: Every generation requires an internet connection.
Resolve version requirements: The plugin requires DaVinci Resolve 19 or newer, and the App Store build isn't supported.
Credits apply to every generation: This includes models that are Unlimited on the Higgsfield web platform.
Short-form video only: Higgsfield's video models generate clips in the 4 to 15 second range, which works well for B-roll, cutaways, and inserts, but not for long master shots.
