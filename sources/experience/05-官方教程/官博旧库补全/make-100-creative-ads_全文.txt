Why Volume Matters in Ad Creative

The math on paid social creative is straightforward. Most ads do not work. The ones that do tend to work for a limited window before performance drops. The brands that consistently win are the ones constantly testing new angles, new formats, and new hooks at a pace that keeps ahead of creative fatigue.

A team producing two or three ads a week tops out around 12 per month. A solo operator using the right tools can produce 100+ per month at a fraction of the cost, test every angle simultaneously, and scale the winners. The bottleneck is not the idea. It is production.

What Marketing Studio Actually Does

Marketing Studio is Higgsfield's ad production tool. It starts the same place most ad workflows start: a product URL. The Full Workflow: From Product to 100 Ads

There is no single workflow that fits every ad strategy. How you use Marketing Studio depends on what kind of creatives you are producing. Here are three distinct approaches.

Workflow A: Spokesperson-Led Video Ads With a Consistent Face

The right workflow for brands built around a real person, a founder, or a recurring character who appears in every ad. Soul ID is the core of this approach.

Step 1: Train Soul ID once. Go to higgsfield.ai, navigate to Soul, and select Train new character. Upload 20 or more reference photos of the person who will appear in your ads. The platform builds a trained identity model in a few minutes. This step happens once. Every ad after this uses the same face automatically without re-uploading.

Step 2: Open Marketing Studio and paste your product URL. The platform reads your product page and extracts images, the product name, key features, and pricing information automatically. Edit the fields manually if needed.

Step 3: Review and edit the script. The generated script is a starting point. For volume production, create variation here: the same product with five different scripts covering different angles (problem-solution, social proof, feature highlight, lifestyle, price-value) gives you five creative directions to test.

Step 4: Choose the ad format. For most DTC products with a spokesperson, UGC and Product Review perform strongest on TikTok and Meta. If you are testing angles, try the same script in multiple formats.

Step 5: Attach the Soul ID. The trained identity applies automatically to every generation.

Step 6: Set aspect ratio and generate. 9:16 for TikTok and Reels, 16:9 for YouTube, 1:1 for feed ads. Marketing Studio produces the video with native audio and lip sync in one pass. No separate voiceover step. At 8 seconds and 720p, expect approximately $2 per clip. 100 clips costs approximately $200 in generation credits.

Step 7: Review and scale winners. The ones that perform go into rotation. Use Ad Reference to upload a high-performing ad and recreate its concept, scene composition, pacing, and hook with your own product. Use Virality Predictor to forecast performance before the ad goes live.

Workflow B: Product-Only Ads From a URL

The right workflow for brands that do not need a spokesperson: product shots, reveal formats, and visual-first creatives where the product is the hero.

Step 1: Open Marketing Studio and paste your product URL. The platform extracts product images, the product name, key features, and pricing automatically. No Soul ID setup needed.

Step 2: Review and edit the script. For product-only formats, focus the script on the product benefit rather than a personal story. Keep it short and direct.

Step 3: Choose the format. Unboxing, Product Review, Hyper Motion, and TV Spot all work well for product-only creatives. Tutorial works for anything with a learning curve.

Step 4: Skip the spokesperson step. Set aspect ratio based on placement and generate. The output is a product-focused video with native audio and no human character.

Step 5: Test and iterate. At $2 per clip, testing 10 different format and script combinations costs $20. Run the test before committing to volume.

Workflow C: High-Volume Static and UGC Aesthetics

The right workflow for brands producing aesthetic image assets, banner creatives, or UGC-style content at scale without video production overhead.

Step 1: Prepare your visual inputs. Product photos, brand color references, lifestyle imagery. Marketing Studio accepts multiple reference inputs simultaneously.

Step 2: Choose UGC or Wild Card format. UGC produces authentic-looking creator-style content for TikTok and Meta. Wild Card is the format for experimental, unconventional creative that does not follow a standard formula.

Step 3: Set the hook and setting. For UGC, Tutorial, Unboxing, Product Review, and UGC Virtual Try On formats, you can set a Hook (the attention-grabbing mechanic) and a Setting (the location and vibe) separately. These are two alternative approaches to Ad Reference: use one or the other for a given ad, not both.

Step 4: Generate at volume. Use DTC Ads for static image formats alongside video. Generate multiple variations and use Virality Predictor to prioritize before spending on distribution.

Building a Production System

Running 100+ ads a month is not about generating 100 ads at once. It is about building a repeatable weekly rhythm.

Week structure for 25 ads per week:

Monday: generate 5 variations of a new angle (same product, different script focus). Tuesday: generate 5 variations in a different format (same script, UGC vs. TV Spot). Wednesday: review performance from the previous week, identify what is working. Thursday: generate 5 variations based on the week's winner (same angle, different hooks). Friday: generate 10 variations of the top-performing format with Ad Reference.

This structure produces 25 ads per week, 100 per month, with a built-in feedback loop that compounds over time. The ads get better each week because each week's generation is informed by the previous week's performance data.

What Marketing Studio Cannot Do

Marketing Studio generates 15-second maximum clips. For longer-form video ads, you need a different workflow or post-production assembly of multiple clips.

The platform is web interface only, with no public API for fully automated pipelines. If you want programmatic ad generation at scale, Higgsfield MCP connects Marketing Studio to Claude and other agents for workflow automation without opening the web interface.

There is no static ad output in Marketing Studio. For static image ads, use Higgsfield's image generation tools separately.
