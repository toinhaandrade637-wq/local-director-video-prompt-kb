What Is Higgsfield Original Series?

Higgsfield Original Series is the first complete AI streaming platform where full episodes are produced from scratch using generative AI and released to a global audience.

With the AI video generators to produce cinematic-quality content and Original Series to release it at scale, AI filmmakers now have a complete ecosystem in one place.

AI Video Generators

20+ tools, including Cinema Studio, SOUL 2.0, and Soul ID let anyone produce cinematic-quality work.

The platform

Original Series is where that work goes, a streaming destination where AI series get seen and distributed.

How Creators Get In

Getting on Original Series starts with Higgsfield contests. That is how independent creators and studios get discovered, sponsored, and added to the platform alongside Higgsfield's own productions.

The most recent Higgsfield Action Contest received 8,752 submissions from 139 countries and distributed $500,000 to independent filmmakers.

Muhannad Nassar and Simon Meyer, a Detroit and Berlin-based duo who had never met in person, built their winning short GRANDMA vs WASP entirely through a remote workflow on Cinema Studio. 

Third-place winners, brothers Ash and Aram Gevorkyan, made SCRATCH in five days and audiences thought it was a real studio trailer.

"A lot of people messaged me asking for a link to the full movie. They actually thought it was a real trailer for an existing film."  – Ash Gevorkyan, 3rd Place Action Contest Winner

Through future contests, the most talented creators will have the chance to publish their work on Original Series and reach a global audience.

The best entries get sponsored production, distribution support, and a spot on the platform alongside Higgsfield's own content.

What Comes Next

Higgsfield Original Series launches with ten pilots and grows with the creators who build on it. Independent filmmakers, studios, and teams who use contests as the entry point and the platform as the stage.

The question of whether AI can produce real cinematic work has already been answered. Arena Zero answers it in ten minutes. What comes next is about scale, how many stories get told, from how many places, by how many people who previously had no path to an audience.
