What is Higgsfield Earn?

Higgsfield Earn
 is a monetization platform for content creators - post content & get paid. 

This is a direct monetization for creators who understand that consistent content equals consistent income.

How It Works & How to Start Your AI Influencer Monetization Here? 

Instagram Creators

Step 1: Go to Higgsfield Earn

Open the 
Higgsfield Earn
 platform.

earn

Step 2: Log In

Log in using any available method.
This does 
not
 need to be an existing Higgsfield account – an account will automatically be created when you log in.

earn

Step 3: Link Your Instagram Account

Go to 
My Accounts
 and link your Instagram by adding your Instagram handle.

earn

You will receive a 
unique verification code
.
Copy this code and paste it into your Instagram bio. 

Once the code is added, click 
“Verify My Account.”

This proves the account actually belongs to you. It prevents someone from trying to monetize your content without permission.

Example bio with verification:

Content creator | Daily posts
Verification: HF-47392

The verification number can go anywhere in your bio - make sure it&#x27;s visible when someone views your profile.

earn

Step 4: Browse Active Campaigns

Scroll down in your Higgsfield Earn dashboard to see 
Active Campaigns
. 

These are current earning opportunities. Each campaign shows its own brief:

Campaign type

 (what content we&#x27;re looking for)

Requirements

 (format, style, topic)

Earnings structure

 (how you get paid)

Duration

 (how long it runs)

Guidelines

 (what to include)

Step 5: Join Campaigns

Click on campaigns that align with your content style and audience interests.

Step 6: Create and Post Your Content

Create content according to campaign guidelines. The system tracks your post performance automatically from the moment it goes live.

Step 7: Track Your Earnings in Real-Time

Your Higgsfield Earn dashboard shows your 
earnings to date.

earn

Withdrawal Process

You can withdraw your earnings at any time.

Payments are processed through your 
Garna account
 (using the same email address).

Once the payment is sent:

You will receive an email from Garna

Follow the step-by-step instructions

Complete a quick verification process

The verification process is fast and straightforward.

YouTube Creators

The YouTube monetization process follows similar principles with platform-specific requirements and verification steps.

Step 1: Go to Higgsfield Earn

Open the Higgsfield Earn platform.

Step 2: Log In

Log in using any available method.
This does 
not
 need to be an existing Higgsfield account – an account will automatically be created when you log in.

earn

Step 3: Link Your YouTube Account

Go to 
My Accounts
 and link your YouTube by adding your YouTube handle.

You will receive a 
unique verification code
.
Copy this code and paste it into your YouTube channel description.

Once the code is added, click 
“Verify My Account.”

earn

What Happens After Verification?

If your account meets the internal requirements, it will be successfully verified.

If you see a message that your account does not qualify – don’t worry.
Your account is still saved in our system, and once a campaign matches your profile, you will receive a notification.

Verification Is Successful

Your YouTube account will be permanently linked.

Only 
one YouTube account
 can be connected

The linked account cannot be changed later

After linking your account, you can access your 
dashboard
, where you’ll see all active campaigns available to you.

How Campaign Participation Works

Step 1 – Choose a Campaign

Click on a YouTube campaign you want to join.

Step 2 – Review the Campaign Details

Inside the campaign page, you will see:

The exact payout amount you will receive after completing all requirements

A clear list of campaign requirements

The campaign deadline (displayed at the top)

A 
“Submit Your Video”
 button

A detailed brief (checklist including required links, hashtags, and media kit assets)

Some campaigns include a 
“Generate Your Promo Code”
 button.
This applies to campaigns that require subscriptions, personal generations, or content observations.

Step 3 – Submit Your Video

Once you complete all requirements, submit your video through the campaign page.

After your submission passes verification:

The payment will appear in your account balance

Withdrawal Process

You can withdraw your earnings at any time.

Payments are processed through your 
Garna account
 (using the same email address).

Once the payment is sent:

You will receive an email from Garna

Follow the step-by-step instructions

Complete a quick verification process

The verification process is fast and straightforward.

Why This Completely Changes Creator Monetization

Old model:
 Build huge following → Hope brands notice you → Negotiate deals → Maybe get paid well → Repeat inconsistently

Higgsfield Earn model:
 Create quality content → Earn based on performance → Scale earnings as you improve → Consistent income from day one

The live earnings counter updates constantly. Every creator posting content moves that number higher.

Your earning potential scales with your skills. 

No followers threshold blocking you. No brand deals requiring connections. 

Just you, your content quality, and transparent performance-based earnings starting today.
