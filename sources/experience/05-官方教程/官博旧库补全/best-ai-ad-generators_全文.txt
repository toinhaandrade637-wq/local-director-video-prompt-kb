11 Best AI Video Ad Generators
Higgsfield
Oct 21, 2026
14 min

The best AI video ad generators in 2026 are Higgsfield, Creatify, Topview, Arcads, HeyGen, Runway, Synthesia, Predis.ai, AdCreative.ai, Pika, and OpusClip. They cover four video ad jobs: turning a product page into a video, making UGC-style ads with an AI presenter, rebuilding an ad that already works, and producing many versions for testing. This guide shows what each tool starts from, what kind of ad it makes, its main limit, and what one clip costs.

How to Choose an AI Video Ad Generator

Each tool fits a different starting point, so start with what you already have. Check four things before you compare prices.

Ad format. Pick a tool for the ads you make most often: product videos, UGC-style ads, avatar explainers, short clips, or cut-downs of longer footage.

Starting point. Some tools start from a product link or product photos. Others need a script, a prompt, an avatar, or existing video.

Pricing unit. Tools charge per credit, per second, per minute, per clip, or per download. Compare cost per finished clip, not plan price.

Consistency. If one presenter or product appears across a campaign, check how the tool keeps them the same between videos.

Higgsfield: Product Ads and Reference Ad Reuse

Higgsfield is an AI-native creative suite with three ad workflows. Marketing Studio makes ads from a product link or template, Ad Multiplier makes new versions of a reference ad, and Genjutsu changes existing footage.

In Marketing Studio, you paste a product link in Product Link, and it pulls the product images, logo, colors and copy into a template. The library has 1,500+ templates in categories such as Product shot, Motion, UGC, TikTok, Ads, Posters and Marketplace. Videos run up to 15 seconds. For UGC Videos, Ad Reference lets you upload an existing ad instead of a template, and Marketing Studio repeats its structure and format with your product.

Ad Multiplier in Ads Studio creates new variations from a reference ad. You upload a 4–30-second reference video and up to 20 images, then replace the product, character, clothing or location, or test a different hook, script or CTA while keeping the reference’s motion, framing and timing. One setup can produce several versions.

Genjutsu works with a clip you already have. Motion transfer keeps the movement and timing of a source video and rebuilds the scene with your characters or products. Objects swap changes only the element you pick, such as the product in a presenter’s hand, and keeps the rest of the footage. You can add up to 30 reference images, and output goes up to 1080p.

For a recurring spokesperson, Soul ID keeps one face recognizable across generations. You train it once on 20–80 photos of the same person for 25 credits, generate new images of that character, and use them as the character in Genjutsu or Ad Multiplier.

A 15-second Marketing Studio video at 720p costs 90 credits (≈$4.41). Genjutsu pricing depends on the length of your source video: a 10-second clip at 720p costs 70 credits (≈$3.43) with Motion transfer and 77 credits (≈$3.77) with Objects swap. An Ad Multiplier variation with a new hook and CTA at 720p uses up to 140 credits (≈$6.86). Plans start at $15/month (Starter), with Plus at $49/month and Ultra at $129/month.

What to keep in mind

Marketing Studio videos are limited to 15 seconds, so longer ads are put together from several clips.
Ad Reference works only in the UGC Videos category; Ad Multiplier and Genjutsu need a reference video of 4–30 seconds.
Genjutsu cost grows with source video length and resolution; Objects swap costs more than Motion transfer.
Creatify: Product Pages to Avatar Ads

Creatify turns a product URL into a video ad. It imports product images, descriptions and brand colors, then adds an AI avatar and a generated voiceover. Ad Clone remakes an existing ad from its URL.

You can set up the prompt, avatar and product before rendering, and credits are charged only when the video is generated. You can test several scripts, presenters and angles for one product this way.

The Pro plan costs $55/month for 280 credits, and a 15-second avatar video uses 5 credits, about $0.98. The Standard plan costs $15/month for 55 credits, and Max costs $95/month for 730 credits.

What to keep in mind

Ad Clone starts on the Pro plan.
Product demos may need extra images or footage beyond what the URL provides.
Higher resolution, longer clips and premium models use more credits.
Topview: Product Videos and Reference-Based Clips

Topview makes product marketing videos. URL to Video starts from a product page, and Product Photography and Virtual Try-On create product shots from your images.

Omni Reference works from references: you upload product images and a reference video, tag them in the prompt (for example, @Image1 and @Video1), and the new clip follows the reference’s motion or style with your product. It makes clips up to 30 seconds at 720p, up to four at a time. Separate tools handle Motion Control, Video Character Swap, Video Extend and Clip Edit, so you can also change an existing clip.

Credits are charged per model and length. Seedance 2.5 at 720p uses 6 credits per 4 seconds. The Plus plan costs $36/month for 100 credits, about 66 seconds of Seedance 2.5 at 720p, so a 15-second clip costs about $8.10. Business ($75/month) and Ultra ($150/month) add a discounted extra credit pack and unlimited generations on selected models. API and MCP access are included from Plus.

What to keep in mind

Omni Reference builds a new clip from your references; it doesn’t edit the original footage.
Credit cost varies by model, so the same clip length can cost different amounts.
Unlimited generations work only in the Topview web interface, not through the API.
Arcads: UGC-Style Talking-Actor Ads

Arcads makes UGC-style ads with AI actors: reviews, testimonials, demos and unboxings for TikTok, Instagram and Meta. In the direct workflow, you choose an actor, add a product image and type the script or upload a voice recording. Other formats include Unboxing POV and cinematic product shots that use your product image as the start frame.

Mark, Arcads’ AI agent, can create ads using product website information: it reads your site, researches the brand and builds ads from what it finds. Presets and remix tools reuse an actor and format across new ads, and MCP connects Arcads to tools like Claude and Cursor.

The Starter plan costs $110/month for 8,000 credits. An Arcads 1.0 talking-actor video uses 800 credits per generation, up to 2 minutes, so one video costs about $11 on Starter, whether it runs 15 seconds or a minute. The plan’s monthly limits (up to 600 seconds of talking actor and 50 AI video clips) can’t be split into separate 15-second videos at a lower price. Starter includes 300 actors; Creator ($220/month) includes 1,000 and Pro ($550/month) includes 1,500, plus custom actors cloned from a real person. Plans and prices are shown inside the app, and a free trial is available.

What to keep in mind

A short talking-actor video costs the same as a longer one, up to 2 minutes.
Prices are visible only after signup.
The full actor library and custom actors need higher plans.
HeyGen: Avatar-Led and Multilingual Video

HeyGen makes presenter videos with AI avatars. You create a Digital Twin from a short recording and then generate videos with it using supported avatar models such as Avatar V. Videos can be made in 175+ languages and dialects.

HeyGen supports script-led avatar videos, while its Video Agent can develop a video from an idea or prompt, including the script and scenes. It doesn’t pull structured product details from a URL the way URL-to-video tools do.

The Creator plan costs $29/month for 600 credits, with videos up to 30 minutes, 1080p export and limited Avatar V usage. The Pro plan costs $49/month for 1,000 credits and adds 4K export and more models. Credit use depends on the avatar model and video length.

What to keep in mind

No product import from a URL.
Avatar V usage is limited on the Creator plan.
Credit use depends on the avatar model and video length.
Runway: Directed Shots and Video Editing

Runway is a general AI video platform. For ads, the usual workflow is to create or upload a product image and animate it with Gen-4.5. Aleph 2.0 edits existing video, such as changing an object or the setting, while keeping the rest of the shot.

Runway has no URL-to-ad workflow. Prompts, images and edits are prepared by hand, so a finished ad usually takes several generations and editing steps.

The Standard plan costs $15/month for 625 credits. Gen-4.5 uses 12 credits per second, so a 10-second clip costs 120 credits, about $2.88. Aleph 2.0 editing at 720p uses 28 credits per second.

What to keep in mind

No product URL workflow; you prepare assets and prompts yourself.
A complete ad usually needs several generations plus editing.
Monthly credits don’t roll over on Standard and Pro.
Synthesia: Scripted Presenter Video

Synthesia turns a written script into a presenter video with an AI avatar. Starter includes 125+ stock AI avatars and three personal avatars, plus the AI Video Assistant and dubbing. Pro increases the personal avatar allowance to five.

It fits training, onboarding, internal updates and product explainers. It has no product URL workflow or ad formats, so scripts and ad concepts are prepared separately.

The Starter plan costs $29/month for up to about 12 minutes of video, on top of a free allowance, about $2.42 per minute. The Pro plan costs $89/month for about 60 minutes and 180+ stock avatars.

What to keep in mind

Built for structured videos, not fast ad testing.
One-click translation into 140+ languages is on Enterprise.
Many ad variations use up the monthly minutes quickly.
Predis.ai: Social Videos and Scheduling

Predis.ai creates social content from a product description or keywords: captions, images, carousels and short videos. Posts can be scheduled from the same workspace, with a calendar view, best-time-to-post suggestions and posts tailored to each channel, including Instagram Reels, Stories and Google Business Profiles. Auto Posting is included from the Rise plan; Core doesn’t include it.

It fits small teams that need one tool for making and publishing posts. Videos and images use the same credit balance, and videos use more credits.

The Core plan costs $32/month for 1,300 credits, about 33 videos or 325 images a month, so one video costs about $0.97. The Rise plan costs $79/month for 3,200 credits.

What to keep in mind

Video formats are simpler than in dedicated video tools.
Videos use credits faster than images.
Auto Posting starts on Rise; Core includes scheduling without it.
AdCreative.ai: Ad Creatives for Ad Platforms

AdCreative.ai creates ad creatives from your brand and product. Video features (Ad Videos, Product Videos, Storytelling Ads, Fashion Videoshoots and Blog Videos) start on the Professional plan.

Generations are unlimited on every plan, and one credit is used each time you download a finished, watermark-free creative. Ad platform integrations, competitor insights and iStock photos are included on all plans, and Professional adds Creative Scoring AI, Batch Creatives, Compliance Checker AI and Custom Templates.

The Professional plan costs $249/month for 300 credits, about $0.83 per downloaded creative, with 10 brands and 10 users. The Starter plan costs $39/month for 20 credits and has no video features.

What to keep in mind

Video starts on the Professional plan.
Credits are spent on downloads, so the plan sets how many finished creatives you can export each month.
Fine-tuning is only on the Ultimate plan ($999/month).
Pika: Short Product Clips and Object Swaps

Pika is a creative platform with video apps and several video models. Its video apps include Product Ad, Swap Anything (replace an object in existing footage), Motion Transfer and Extend Video, and its models include Seedance 2.5, Pika 2.5 and Pikaframes.

For ads, Pika fits short clips: a product shot, a swap inside an existing video, or a clip that works as the opening hook. Clips usually need extra footage, voiceover or editing to become a full ad.

The Creator plan costs $35/month for 3,150 credits. Seedance 2.5 at 720p uses 122 credits per 5 seconds, so a 10-second clip costs about $2.71. The Starter plan costs $10/month for 900 credits.

What to keep in mind

Focused on short clips, not presenter-led ads.
Clips need editing to become a complete ad.
The commercial license starts on Creator, so Starter doesn’t cover published ads.
OpusClip: Repurposing Existing Video

OpusClip starts from long footage you already have: a podcast, webinar, interview or founder video. ClipAnything picks the moments for short clips, AI Reframe resizes them for each platform, and the editor adds animated captions. A social scheduler and AI video dubbing are also built in.

OpusClip primarily repurposes existing footage into short clips. It can also generate AI B-roll to enhance those clips, but it isn’t designed to create complete ads directly from a product URL.

One credit equals one minute of imported source video, no matter how many clips come out of it. The Pro plan costs $29/month for 300 credits, about $0.10 per minute of source footage. On monthly plans, credits last 60 days.

What to keep in mind

You need existing footage to start.
It repurposes video and adds B-roll; it doesn’t build ads from a product URL.
Cost depends on source video length, not on the number of clips.
Which AI Video Ad Generator Is Right for You?

There is no single best AI video ad generator. There is a best one per job, and the first question is what you start from: a product link, a reference ad, a script, or existing footage.

Product videos from a product page. Example: a 15-second ad for a skincare serum from its store page. Consider Higgsfield (Marketing Studio with Product Link), Creatify or Topview.

UGC-style ads. Example: a talking-head review of a protein bar for TikTok. Consider Arcads for AI actors, Higgsfieldfor UGC Videos templates and Ad Reference, or Creatify for avatar ads at volume.

Variations for testing. Example: the same winning ad with five new products or hooks. Consider Higgsfield (Ad Multiplier and Genjutsu), Creatify (Ad Clone) or Topview (Omni Reference).

Other jobs:

Recurring presenter in many languages: HeyGen
Scripted explainers and training videos: Synthesia
Directed product shots and editing existing video: Runway
Short product clips and object swaps: Pika
Social posting and scheduling: Predis.ai
Ad creatives linked to ad platforms: AdCreative.ai
Short ads from long recordings: OpusClip

You can also combine tools, for example Higgsfield or Creatify for the main video, Pika for a short hook and Predis.ai for scheduling.
