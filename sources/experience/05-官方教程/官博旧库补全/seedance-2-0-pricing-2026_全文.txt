Seedance 2.0 Pricing: Cheapest Platforms and Cost per Clip (October 2026)
Higgsfield
Jun 25, 2026
5 min
Last updated: 3d ago

An 8-second Seedance 2.0 clip costs from about $1.30 to $6.91 at 720p and from about $3.12 to $11.20 at 1080p, depending on the platform. Among the six platforms compared, Dreamina has the lowest effective cost for an 8-second Seedance 2.0 clip at both 720p and 1080p, based on the cheapest eligible monthly plan. On Higgsfield, the same clip costs about $2.35 at 720p and $4.70 at 1080p, the second-lowest price at 1080p, and less on plans with more credits. This guide compares six platforms: Dreamina, Higgsfield, OpenArt, Magnific, fal.ai and Runway.

TL;DR

Among the six platforms compared, Dreamina has the lowest effective cost for Seedance 2.0 at 720p and 1080p. Runway is the most expensive at 720p, Magnific at 1080p.
Higgsfield costs $4.70 for an 8-second 1080p clip, second only to Dreamina, and less on plans with more credits.
Seedance 2.0 Fast and Mini cost less than the standard model and suit drafts and short clips.
How Is Seedance 2.0 Priced?

Most platforms sell credits, and each generation uses a set number of them. The cost depends on the model version, the resolution and the clip length. fal.ai charges per second of video instead of credits.

The Seedance 2.0 family has three versions:

Costs are calculated by dividing the monthly subscription price by the included credits. They show the effective cost of one generation, not the amount you pay upfront. fal.ai charges per second of video instead.

What Do You Get on Each Platform?

Dreamina. The lowest effective cost for all three Seedance 2.0 versions among the platforms compared here. Seedance 2.0 Fast is available up to 4K: an 8-second 4K clip costs 224 credits, about $2.13.

Higgsfield. Seedance 2.0 up to 4K, plus Fast and Mini, on one credit balance with the other video and image models. A High bitrate setting gives higher video quality at the same credit cost. Seedance 2.5 is also available; see [Seedance 2.5 Pricing].

OpenArt. Seedance 2.0 from 480p to 4K and Seedance 2.0 Mini, with up to 15 visual references per generation. Seedance 2.0 Fast isn’t listed in its model selector.

Magnific. Credits work across all models in the suite. Seedance 2.0 is also available through the Magnific API and MCP with the same credits.

fal.ai. No subscription: you pay per second of video through the API. It suits developers who generate from code.

Runway. Seedance 2.0 up to 4K. Among the six platforms compared here, it has the highest cost at 720p and for Seedance 2.0 Fast and Mini, based on entry-level monthly plans.
