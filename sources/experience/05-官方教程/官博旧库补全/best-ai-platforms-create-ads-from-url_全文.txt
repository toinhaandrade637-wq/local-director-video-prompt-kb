Best AI Product Video Generators for Ecommerce in 2026
Higgsfield

 Most ecommerce product pages already contain what a product video needs: images, a name, a description, and brand colors. URL-to-ad tools read that page and build a first draft from it. This guide compares eight AI video platforms for ecommerce, covering product ads, content repurposing, and presenter videos: Higgsfield AI, Creatify, Pippit, AdCreative.ai, InVideo, Pictory, HeyGen, and Synthesia, on how they work and what they cost.

What Should You Look for in an AI Product Video Generator for Ecommerce?

Most AI product video generators start from a product page or photos, read the product details, and build a first draft with a script, visuals, and voiceover. Ecommerce teams use those drafts for different jobs: listing videos, paid ads, social posts, and marketplace assets. These six criteria show which platform fits which job. Use these criteria to evaluate the platforms below:

Input: Does it start from a product URL, product photos, a script, or a prompt?
Product accuracy: Does the output show your product’s real shape, color, and label? Compare generated shots with your product photos.
Formats and length: Does it export the aspect ratios and clip lengths your channels need, such as vertical video for social feeds?
Variations at volume: Can you produce many versions or many products from one setup, for catalogs and ad testing?
Presenter and audio: If you use an avatar or spokesperson, does it stay consistent across videos, and does the platform include voiceover or lip sync?
Cost and credits: Credits, clip length, and rollover rules differ, so compare the cost of the same output on each platform.

Higgsfield AI: Photo and Video Ads From 1,500+ Templates

Higgsfield is an AI-native creative suite with 35+ models and tools, and Marketing Studio is its advertising tool. It builds photo and video ads from a product link or photos, using 1,500+ templates across six categories: Product Shots, Ads, Marketplace, Posters, UGC Videos, and Motion. For anything a template doesn't cover, you can describe the ad in a prompt, and UGC Videos and Motion generate video up to 30 seconds.

Click to Ad is the URL workflow inside Marketing Studio. Once you paste a link, it pulls the product name, description, up to 8 photos, brand colors, and logo automatically. You review the extracted assets, swap anything that's off, pick a template, and generate. The same subscription includes Cinema Studio 4.0 for camera, lens, and lighting controls, Ads Studio for image ads built from your website and brand kit, and Ad Multiplier for variations of a reference ad.

Where Higgsfield falls short:

Credits reset monthly with no rollover on individual plans
Creatify: Quick Ad Drafts From a Product URL

Creatify is built around one idea: paste a product URL and get a video ad back. The platform reads your product page, pulls images, the product name, description, and brand colors, writes a script, adds a voiceover, and packages it into a formatted video.

For teams that need to test a lot of ad angles, this is useful. You can run 10 or 15 variations of the same product at different angles, see which one gets traction, and put serious money behind it only after you have a winner.

Where Creatify falls short:

Custom avatars are available on Pro and higher plans, so teams that need their own recurring spokesperson will need more than the entry-level plan.
Pippit by CapCut: Ad Generation Inside the CapCut Ecosystem

Pippit is CapCut’s dedicated ad generation tool. It accepts a product URL, extracts assets, and generates a video with an AI presenter. It also covers image generation, AI avatars, and publishing in one workspace, with product links from stores such as Shopify and TikTok Shop as inputs.

Pippit combines product-link video generation with a built-in editor. After generating a draft, you can edit it, download the finished video, or publish directly to supported social platforms. On the avatar side, it offers stock avatars with synced voice, custom video avatars built from your own footage, and AI Talking Photo, which animates a still image with voice or music.

Where Pippit falls short:

The main value sits in the CapCut ecosystem, so it fits less well for teams that edit elsewhere
AdCreative.ai: Ad Creative Connected to Performance Data

AdCreative.ai is built for teams running paid ads at volume across multiple channels. The platform generates static ad creatives, product and fashion photoshoots, social post graphics, and ad copy from a brand kit and product brief, and its Instant Ads feature builds creatives from a webpage. Video formats include product videos, UGC-style videos, and storytelling ads.

Connect your Google or Meta ad account and AdCreative.ai analyzes which creatives perform best, giving you performance insights to guide future creative decisions. Creative Scoring AI scores each ad variant before you spend money testing it, and the score is a prediction, so use it as one input when testing. Competitor Insights shows a competitor’s best-performing ads across Meta and Google from their URL.

Where AdCreative.ai falls short:

Creative Insights on your own historical ad performance requires connected ad accounts.
InVideo: AI Video Generation and Editing From a Brief

InVideo can turn a product link, script, or brief into a video using generated visuals, stock media, AI avatars, voiceover, and an editable timeline. The AI avatar feature adds a presenter layer on top of the stock footage workflow.

For content marketing teams producing explainer videos and social clips at volume from written content, InVideo handles the conversion step efficiently. The credit pool covers AI minutes, stock downloads, voice clones, and background removal, so it is worth tracking usage across features.

Where Invideo AI falls short:

AI agent tasks and media generation both consume credits, so usage depends on the work performed, models, and settings. Manual timeline edits do not use AI credits.
Pictory: Blogs and Scripts Turned Into Video

Pictory converts blogs, scripts, and URLs into videos using stock footage from Getty Images and Storyblocks. The URL-to-video workflow reads the page content, selects relevant stock clips, and exports a formatted video with AI voiceover. Pictory also offers AI image, video, and avatar generation, with allowances that vary by plan.

It is optimized for converting long-form text into video. For content marketing teams that produce a lot of written content and want video versions without manual editing, Pictory handles that conversion.

Where Pictory falls short:

Pictory is strongest for turning existing content into video. For product-led ads where exact packaging, shape, or branding matters, add your own product media and check the generated visuals carefully.
HeyGen: Presenter Video in 175+ Languages

HeyGen is built for teams that need a consistent on-camera presenter without a camera. Avatar V, its latest avatar model, creates a video-based Digital Twin from a short recording of you, and the twin then delivers new scripts with your learned motion and expressions. Creator also includes voice cloning and unlimited Photo Avatars, and translation covers 175+ languages and dialects.

HeyGen also offers URL-to-Video, so it can start from a product page. For brands running international campaigns where the same spokesperson needs to appear across multiple markets, that covers the localization step.

Where HeyGen falls short:

Video generation is limited by the plan’s credit allowance, and higher-resolution output may require an upgrade.
Synthesia: Scripted Presenter Video for Corporate Teams

Synthesia is built for structured, scripted video: corporate training, product explainers, and presenter-style content at scale. You type a script, choose an avatar, and the platform renders a presenter video without filming.

Starter includes 125+ ready avatars and three personal avatars. Pro increases that to 180+ ready avatars and five personal avatars, and adds features such as interactive video and branded pages. A personal avatar can be built from a photo or a few minutes of your own recorded footage, so the same presenter can appear across a whole video library. Pro also adds interactive videos and branded pages, and scripts can be delivered in 160+ languages.

Where Synthesia falls short:

Synthesia is primarily designed for business communication and presenter-led content, so assess whether that format suits your product ads.
