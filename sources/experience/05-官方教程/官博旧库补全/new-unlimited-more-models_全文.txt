Introducing New Unlimited: Higher Resolutions, More Models
Higgsfield
Jul 28, 2026
8 min

Higgsfield just expanded Unlimited to cover more models at higher resolutions than before, running until July 29 at 1:30 PM UTC. Three separate paths get you there: a one-day free trial for new users, a 7-day window that comes with any Plus, Max, or Ultra plan, and a paid bundle in the Unlimited Models Marketplace if you need extra days. This post breaks down exactly what's included, which parameters apply to each path, and who qualifies for each one.

The Three Ways to Get Unlimited Access

There are three separate paths into Unlimited, and they don't all give you the same thing.

The one-day Free Trial. New users get 24 hours of unlimited generation across the eligible model list before any subscription starts. This is the lowest-commitment path, at the entry resolution and duration tier.

The 7-day window with Plus, Max, or Ultra. Buy a Plus, Max, or Ultra plan, monthly or annual, and a 7-day unlimited window comes included with that purchase. What you get during those 7 days depends on the specific plan and billing cycle, not just which of the three tiers you're on.

The Unlimited Models Marketplace bundle. A separate paid option that adds 1 or 3 extra days of unlimited access on top, for anyone who needs more runway than the free trial or the plan-tied window gives them, without changing their underlying subscription.

The image models hold at a fixed ceiling, 2K across every single column, regardless of path, plan, or billing cycle. The video models scale mostly by duration: 8 seconds on Trial and Plus monthly, jumping to 15 seconds on Max monthly, Ultra monthly, Max annual, and Ultra annual, with Plus annual landing at 10 seconds in between. How to Access: Step by Step

Step 1: Confirm you're eligible. New to Higgsfield, you qualify for the Free Trial automatically. Already have an account and considering Plus, Max, or Ultra, the 7-day window comes with any of the three plans at either billing cycle.

Step 2: Activate the path that fits. For the Free Trial, this happens automatically on signup. For the 7-day window, it activates the moment your Plus, Max, or Ultra subscription goes live. For the Marketplace bundle, purchase it directly from the Unlimited Models Marketplace inside your account.

Step 3: Generate on higgsfield.ai. This is the part worth being precise about: Unlimited models and free generations are available only on higgsfield.ai. They are not included on MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer, Marketing Studio, or Shorts Studio. If you generate on any of those surfaces, the generation spends credits from your balance instead. That's expected behavior, not a bug, so if you're trying to use your Unlimited window and see credits being deducted, check which surface you're generating from first.

Step 4: Check exactly what applies to your account. Go to Profile → Manage Account → Subscription → Active unlimited models. This shows exactly which models Unlimited is currently active on for your specific account, which matters since the model list and the resolution ceiling both depend on which path and plan you're using.

How Do I Cancel Before the Trial Renews?

Three clicks, no support needed.

How to cancel:

Open Manage Account → Subscription
Click Cancel auto-renewal
You're done

What happens if you cancel:

Nothing is charged
You keep Unlimited access until the end of your trial period, so cancelling early doesn't cut your trial short
After the trial ends, your account moves to the free plan, and you can subscribe to any plan later whenever you want

What happens if you don't cancel:

Plus activates automatically when your 24 hours are up, and $49 is charged to your card
Your exact renewal time is shown on the Subscription page
You can still cancel Plus at any time afterward, and it stays active until the end of the period you already paid for
You'll also get an email warning 6 hours before you're charged, letting you know the subscription is about to renew
How Unlimited Speed Works?

Unlimited runs on a shared queue by design, and that's the main reason. Unlimited allows 1 image, 1 video, and 1 audio generation at a time, one of each type running simultaneously rather than one generation total. Credit generations run on the priority queue instead, which is faster and allows more generations in parallel. Generation speed and concurrency can also vary during periods of high system load or peak hours on Unlimited .

Which Path Is Right for You?

Unlimited on Higgsfield isn't one offer, it's three separate paths into the same model list, each with its own eligibility and its own resolution ceiling. The Free Trial gets any new user 24 hours to test the waters at the entry tier. Buying Plus, Max, or Ultra comes with a 7-day window where the exact ceiling depends on which specific plan you chose, Max and Ultra annual in particular are the only tiers that unlock 4K on Seedance 2.0. The Marketplace bundle exists for anyone who needs more than 7 days without upgrading their underlying plan.

None of it works outside higgsfield.ai itself, so if you're building a workflow that touches MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer, Marketing Studio, or Shorts Studio, budget credits for that work regardless of your Unlimited status. And within the window itself, remember that Unlimited runs on a shared queue by design, 1 image, 1 video, and 1 audio generation at a time, rather than the priority queue credit generations use. Switch the Unlimited toggle off any time you need the faster queue instead. Check Profile → Manage Account → Subscription → Active unlimited models whenever you're unsure exactly what's active on your account, since that page reflects your specific plan and path rather than the general promotional ceiling.
