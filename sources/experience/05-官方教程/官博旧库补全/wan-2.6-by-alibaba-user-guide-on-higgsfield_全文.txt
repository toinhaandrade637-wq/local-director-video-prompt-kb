Introduction to WAN 2.6

Before diving into the step-by-step process, let&#x27;s quickly go over what WAN 2.6 is and why it&#x27;s significant.

WAN 2.6 is an upgraded version of the previous WAN models on Higgsfield. The latest iteration offers improved accuracy, faster processing times, and greater flexibility when it comes to video generation. WAN 2.6 can generate more complex and sophisticated outputs, making it suitable for various applications, including educational content, marketing videos, and even creative storytelling.

The key improvements in WAN 2.6 over previous versions are:

Enhanced Quality
: Videos generated with WAN 2.6 have crisper visuals, improved transitions, and smoother animations.

Faster Processing
: The updated model can process inputs and generate outputs faster, saving you valuable time.

Better Text-to-Speech Integration
: The voiceovers in WAN 2.6 are more natural-sounding and can match the tone and pace of the script.

Increased Customizability
: WAN 2.6 allows for deeper customization in terms of scene transitions, themes, and character animations.

Now that we know a bit about WAN 2.6, let’s move on to how you can use it.

Step 4: Setting Up Your Project

Now that you have selected 
WAN 2.6
, it’s time to configure the specifics of your project. This involves choosing your video’s purpose, input method, and any additional settings.

Choose Video Duration
:

WAN 2.6 allows you to set the duration of your video. You can choose a specific time range or let the model automatically adjust the length based on the content you input.

Select Input Method
:

Depending on what type of content you want to create, you can choose from different input methods:

Text
: You can type out a script or outline, and WAN 2.6 will use this to generate the video.

Voiceover
: You can upload a pre-recorded voiceover, and the model will create animations and scenes that align with it.

Image-Based
: Upload images, and WAN 2.6 will generate a video around them.

Choose the input method that best suits your project.

Adjust Customization Options
:

Here, you can choose a theme for your video (e.g., futuristic, natural, corporate). WAN 2.6 offers multiple themes, and each one affects the video’s look and feel.

Step 5: 
Generating
 the Video

With all your settings in place, it’s time to generate the video using WAN 2.6. This step may take a few minutes depending on the complexity of the content.

Preview the Video
:

Before fully generating the video, Higgsfield will offer a preview of the initial scenes or a brief demo. This allows you to make sure everything looks good and flows properly.

You can adjust scenes or settings during the preview if necessary.

Generate the Full Video
:

Once satisfied with the preview, click on the 
Generate Full Video
 button. The model will now process your input and render the video.

The generation process may take anywhere from a few minutes to half an hour, depending on the length and complexity of your project.

Step 6: Sharing Your Video

After downloading the video, you’re ready to share it with the world! You can upload it to platforms like YouTube, Vimeo, or share it directly with clients or colleagues. If you’re using it for marketing purposes, ensure you include proper titles, descriptions, and tags to maximize its reach.
