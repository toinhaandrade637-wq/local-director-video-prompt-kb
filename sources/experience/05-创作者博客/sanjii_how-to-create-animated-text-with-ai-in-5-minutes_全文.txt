Step 1 — Upload the Skill
higgsfield-animated-text.skill
SKIL

First, connect Higgsfield to Claude and upload the Animated Text Skill. You only need to do this once.




After the Skill is loaded, Claude already knows how to create the animation for you.

Step 2 — Type the Word You Want to Animate

Now simply type the word or phrase you want.

For example:

Animate the word “CREATE”

That’s it.




Claude takes your text and automatically turns it into an animated typography clip.

You don’t need to create keyframes, open After Effects, or manually build the animation.




A few moments later, you’ll get the finished animated text on a green background.

Step 3 — Remove the Green Background

Now download the animation and place it above your footage in your editing software.




All that’s left is removing the green background using chroma key.

In Premiere Pro, you can use Ultra Key. In After Effects, use Keylight. In DaVinci Resolve or CapCut, use their built-in chroma key tools.





Once the green is removed, only the animated text remains. And that’s the entire workflow.




Upload the Skill, type your word, remove the green background.




What would normally take hours of manual animation can now be done in around five minutes.
