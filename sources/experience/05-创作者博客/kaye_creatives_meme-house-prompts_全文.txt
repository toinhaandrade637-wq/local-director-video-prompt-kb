Very early dawn in Los Angeles, 4:30 AM: a pale lightening sky, streetlights still on, an empty quiet street; inside the house, warm practical lamps and pockets of shadow — the party that never ended. Realistic shaky handheld: hand tremor, walking bounce, whip pans with motion blur, autofocus hunting, digital zoom with quality drop, mild high-ISO noise in dim light. Deep focus, no cinematic bokeh, no color grading. Everything photorealistic. No music anywhere — only live sound: her voice, crowd murmur, shuffling feet, first birds outside.
