Okay, this started as a video of me walking down the steps outside a house and getting into a white SUV. I used Higgsfield to turn it into a private jet scene, with a new outfit and a red McLaren. Here’s how we made it. You can download the exact prompt below.

Here’s the finished video. Click it to watch. The comparison farther down shows the AI version on top and my real footage on the bottom, so you can see what changed.




Start with a real video

I filmed a 26-second clip of myself walking down the steps, talking to the camera, and getting into a car. That gave the AI real movement to follow.

If you try this, film the actions you want in the final scene. Keep your feet in view on the steps, and make sure we can see you open the car door. Watch the comparison below: AI on top, real footage on the bottom.

AI version on top. Original footage on the bottom. Same walk, new scene.




Make four clear reference images

We made four reference images before editing the video. These are pictures that show the AI what we want. Each one had a clear job.

First, the outfit. This image shows the dark coat and suit from the front and back. We asked Higgsfield to put those clothes on me.




Next came a close view of the jet stairs, a wider view of the airport, and the red McLaren. The close view helped with the first few steps. The wide view showed the wet ground and cloudy sky. The car image showed the shape of the car and how its door opens.




Line up the new stairs with your steps

We took a frame from my real video and used it to plan the jet image. The goal was to put the plane’s stairs where the house steps were, seen from a similar camera angle.

Think about where your feet land. If the new steps are in the wrong place, your feet can look like they’re floating. Build the new scene around the movement you filmed. We also left people out of the jet reference so it clearly showed the stairs and plane.




Tell the AI what to keep and what to change

We used Higgsfield Genjutsu to edit the video. I set it up through the Higgsfield connector in chat, using my original clip and the four reference images.

A prompt is simply the written instructions you give the AI. Ours started with what to keep: my face, voice, lip movements, gestures, and the way the camera moved. Then we explained what each reference image was for.

We asked to change my clothes, turn the house and driveway into an airport, and replace the SUV with the McLaren. We also asked for softer light to match the cloudy sky. Otherwise, I could still look like I was standing in sunshine.

Explain the ending one step at a time

The ending needed a little extra care. Getting into a low sports car is different from getting into an SUV. We wrote out the order: open the door upward, lower into the seat, bring both feet inside, close the door, then turn on the lights.




I wanted the car to stay parked the whole time. We told the AI to keep the tires in the same spots, even while the camera moved around the car.

I also asked to keep my original audio, with no added engine noise, rain sounds, or music. The headlights would show the car turning on.

Test it, then make the final version

We made a 480p test first. Once I liked the result, we used the same clip, images, and prompt to make a 1080p version. That second version was a fresh AI result, so we still needed to check it.

The final clip is just over 26 seconds long. Before posting yours, watch the whole thing at full size. A sharper video can make small mistakes easier to spot.

Check the small details

Look closely at the feet on the stairs, the hand opening the door, and the body getting into the seat. Check that the face still looks right, the coat moves naturally, and the car stays parked. Listen to the sound too.




If something looks wrong, describe the exact problem. “My left foot floats above the step” is more useful than “make it better.” That gives you a clear fix to ask for.
