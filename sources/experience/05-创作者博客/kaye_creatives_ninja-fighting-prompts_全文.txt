distant blue mountain ridges and Mount Fuji under a broken overcast sky.

Standoff, mirrored stances, symmetrical composition, deep perspective

across the field.

weathered wooden torii gate in the middle distance, layered blue mountain

ridges and Mount Fuji on the horizon, broken overcast sky with hard sun

no people.

Photorealistic full-body shot of a young West African woman warrior,

athletic build, standing in a wide grand stone hall. She wears a fitted
