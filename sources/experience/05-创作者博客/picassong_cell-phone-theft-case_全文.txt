Find My Phone

Turn your own photo into a figurine, dress it for a cozy morning, and bring one very stubborn sneeze to life.




Hi, I’m Picassong! Have any of you ever had this happen? Anyone who’s ever held their phone in their hand, thought it was gone, and spent ages looking for it—even just once!!!  In this episode, I’ll show you how to turn those little everyday moments into something fun.




I designed this workflow so you can start with your own photo and make your own version. The example below follows a model reference through a figurine sheet, a homewear change, and the finished video. The image prompts are reusable recreations for this tutorial; the final video prompt is the approved template from this episode.

Make your version in three steps
Upload your own photo and create a figurine reference sheet.
Change the figure’s “outfit,” then prepare reference images of your phone and bedroom.
Copy the final video prompt, connect the references, replace the pet’s name, and generate and review your scenes.

Create ONE horizontal 16:9 three-panel 3D doll character sheet using the attached photograph as the reference for facial identity, natural skin tone, iris color, and hair color.

REFERENCE PRIORITY

Extract the subject’s recognizable face contour, eye shape and spacing, iris color, eyebrow shape, nose, lips, and distinctive permanent facial features.

Preserve the source person’s natural skin tone and hair color, interpreted under neutral lighting.

Do not copy the source clothing, accessories, hairstyle, body proportions, pose, background, lighting, or makeup.

If the photograph shows multiple views of the same person, treat them as one identity.

The source determines the facial identity, skin tone, iris color, eyebrow color, and hair color.

All body proportions, clothing, accessory colors, pose, and presentation are fixed below.

FIXED DOLL PROPORTIONS — HIGHEST PRIORITY

Use approximately FIVE HEADS in anatomical height.

One head unit is measured from the anatomical crown to the chin.

Total height from anatomical crown to bare soles is approximately five head units, excluding towel volume.

Use a visibly enlarged head, narrow shoulders, a short delicate neck, compact torso, small hands, and proportionately short, slender limbs.

Maintain an elegant collectible fashion-doll silhouette.

Enlarge the entire skull and face, not merely the hair or towel.

Do not inherit the photographed person’s body proportions.

Do not generate realistic 7–8-head fashion-model anatomy.

Do not lengthen the legs or shrink the head to fill the panel.

Avoid extreme chibi proportions and toddler anatomy.

Use a smooth seamless satin-finish resin body.

No visible ball joints, hinges, or mechanical seams.

FACIAL IDENTITY AND SKIN TONE

Translate the source face into softly sculpted doll anatomy while preserving recognizable facial relationships.

Use enlarged glass-like doll eyes that retain the source eye shape, spacing, and iris color.

Preserve the characteristic nose profile, lip shape, face contour, and eyebrow shape.

Maintain identical facial geometry across all three panels.

Preserve the source person’s natural skin tone and undertone.

Do not lighten, whiten, darken, or recolor the skin to a preset beige.

Remove makeup coloration and environmental color casts while retaining the underlying complexion.

Apply the same skin tone consistently to the face, ears, neck, arms, hands, and legs.

Keep the original identity’s apparent age within the doll stylization.

COMPLETELY MAKEUP-FREE FACE

Show the character after all makeup has been thoroughly removed.

This is a bare face, not a natural-makeup look.

Remove eyeshadow, pearl shimmer, glitter, eyeliner, mascara, false lashes, blush, contour, highlighter, foundation, lipstick, lip tint, and lip gloss.

EYELIDS:

Natural skin color with no cosmetic tint, shimmer, black lash band, eyeliner wing, or highlighted inner corners.

Preserve eyelid shape and eye size.

EYELASHES:

Only short, fine, sparse natural lashes.

No thick curled lashes, extensions, mascara, or dramatic lash styling.

Do not change eye anatomy when removing false lashes.

EYEBROWS:

Preserve the source shape and natural color.

Use individual soft brow hairs without cosmetic fill or sharply penciled edges.

CHEEKS AND NOSE:

Remove applied blush and contour.

Preserve natural complexion variation without adding artificial pink patches or making the skin gray.

LIPS:

Preserve the source lip shape and volume.

Use a subdued natural lip color appropriate to the person’s complexion.

Do not impose a fixed pink or beige lip color on every subject.

Use a soft satin-matte finish without gloss, lipstick edges, or bright tint.

Keep the resin skin smooth without adding realistic human pores.

No glasses, under-eye patches, masks, skincare stickers, smears, or makeup residue.

HAIR COLOR AND TOWEL

Preserve the source hair color, including clearly visible natural color variation.

Do not automatically change the hair to brown or black.

Do not copy the source hairstyle.

Tuck the hair inside a large pale-lavender bath towel turban.

Leave a few loose, slightly damp strands around the forehead, temples, and ears in the source hair color.

The towel has thick overlapping wrapped folds and a twisted end tucked securely at the top.

Use realistic miniature terry-cloth fibers.

Keep the towel construction identical across views.

FIXED CLOTHING AND PROP PALETTE

These colors apply only to clothing and props, not skin or hair:

Pale lavender: #C9B6E4

White: #FFFFFF

Cream white: #F3EEE5

Dark navy: #29334F

Deep-red piping: #9B3444

Dark-teal sock accents: #24545A

Use the same pale lavender for the towel, pajama stripes, blanket checks, sock bodies, and pillow.

Preserve these base colors under neutral lighting.

Do not inherit clothing colors or color casts from the source photograph.

FIXED PAJAMA SET

A matching short-sleeved button-up shirt and relaxed shorts.

Fine vertical pale-lavender and white stripes in soft, lightweight miniature cotton.

The shirt has a notched collar, one chest patch pocket, small white buttons, and thin deep-red piping along the collar, front opening, sleeve hems, and pocket edge.

Fasten every front button below the natural collar opening.

Keep the front placket aligned and fully closed, with no gaps.

Keep the shirt untucked.

The shorts have matching stripes and thin deep-red piping near both hems.

Keep garment length, stripe scale, and fit consistent across subjects.

Fit the outfit to the fixed doll proportions.

FIXED BLANKET

Drape a thick soft fleece blanket over both shoulders.

Use large checks in pale lavender, cream white, and dark navy.

Let the blanket hang loosely along both sides to approximately ankle height.

Leave the center front open so the pajamas remain visible.

Allow both arms to emerge naturally.

FIXED SOCKS

Slouchy knitted mid-calf socks.

Pale-lavender bodies with dark-teal cuffs, toes, and heels.

Soft gathered folds around the ankles.

No shoes.

FIXED PILLOW AND POSE

One plain pale-lavender square cotton pillow.

Its side length is approximately 1.3 anatomical head lengths.

Include stitched edges and natural compression at the grip.

The full-body doll faces forward with an upright head and a relaxed neutral expression.

Legs straight, feet parallel and slightly apart.

The doll’s left hand, on the viewer’s right, holds the pillow by its upper corner beside the leg.

The opposite arm rests naturally at the side.

No handbag, jewelry, or extra props.

FIXED THREE-PANEL LAYOUT

One horizontal 16:9 image with three equal vertical panels and narrow white gutters.

LEFT:

Complete straight-on full-body view.

Show the entire towel and both sock soles with small margins.

Clearly display the enlarged head and compact five-head proportions.

CENTER:

Large straight-on face close-up with upper shoulders.

Both eyes visible, face centered.

Clearly show the bare eyelids, sparse lashes, and natural matte lips.

RIGHT:

True left-facing side-profile close-up with upper shoulders.

Show the forehead, nose, lips, chin, ear, and wrapped towel.

Match the center panel’s facial geometry exactly.

Use the same identity, skin tone, hair color, outfit, and materials in every panel.

Do not copy the source pose or composition.

LIGHTING AND MATERIALS

Pure-white background, #FFFFFF.

Soft neutral studio lighting with gentle shadows and controlled highlights.

No warm color grading, colored rim lights, dramatic contrast, or scenery.

Smooth satin-finish resin character.

Realistic miniature textile detail on the towel, pajamas, blanket, socks, and pillow.

Clean individual hair strands.

No added human pores.

CLEAN QUALITY

Smooth, uniform colors and continuous tonal transitions.

No blotchy color bleed, cloudy shading, patchy gradients, color banding, digital noise, coarse grain, compression artifacts, or artificial texture buildup.

Preserve fine textile detail without adding image grain.

No distorted anatomy, extra fingers, duplicate objects, visible joints, text, logos, panel labels, or watermarks.

FINAL CONSISTENCY RULE

Between different source photographs, vary only the facial identity, natural skin tone, iris color, eyebrow color, and hair color.

Keep doll proportions, makeup-free styling, towel construction, clothing, prop colors, pose, layout, and lighting fixed.

A short bright two-note message chime interrupts. Sit beside the furniture and stay there until the end. For a flip phone, LEFT opens the lid to about 150 degrees while RIGHT supports the lower half. Clearly expose the upper INTERNAL screen and lower PHYSICAL keypad before typing. Screen: "もう出た？" The friend reads the incoming message; protagonist stays silent.



First reply: stable over-shoulder close-up of the ENTIRE OPEN phone, showing screen, hinge and lower keypad together. RIGHT holds steady.
