I filmed this whole outfit video on a tripod, then turned it into a multi-angle fashion edit with Seedance 2.5. Here is every prompt and every reference I used, so you can recreate it with your own look.




Step 1. Film your base video

Put your phone on a tripod with your full body in frame and some space around you. Film one take and show off the pieces you want to feature: touch the bag, bring your hand to your face, move your feet.




Step 2. Your photo sheet




Right after filming, take a few photos of yourself: front, side profile, full body, and a close-up of your jewelry. Merge them into one image. This is your reference for every shot.

Stand in front of a plain wall and don’t skip the side profile. It keeps your face consistent when the camera moves around you.







Step 3. Change the angle




Upload your clip to Seedance 2.5 together with your photo sheet, paste the prompt and tap generate. Only describe the camera you want. Your movement comes straight from your video. I cut my video into short pieces, one per sentence, and gave each piece its own angle. All my angles are below.

CAMERA

Close detail shot at chin height, 20° FOV telephoto, framed from the nose down to the upper chest. Starts from the front, then arcs slowly to the side, about 25° across the clip, with a gentle push in, so the back of the hand and the watch dial turn toward the lens. Focus pulls from the rings down to the watch dial and back to the rings. Shallow depth of field, the room melts into soft bokeh, light glints on the metal as the angle changes.
