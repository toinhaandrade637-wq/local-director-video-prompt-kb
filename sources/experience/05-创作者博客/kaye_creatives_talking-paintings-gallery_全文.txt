16:9 HORIZONTAL. 30 SECONDS. ONE SHOT, ONE CAMERA, NO CUTS. One single continuous unbroken take. THE CAMERA MOVES BY FAST HORIZONTAL WHIP PANS AND ONE FAST SLIDE, NEVER BY CUTS.




=== FIRST HARD RULE: EVERY PAINTING IS ALWAYS SHOWN WHOLE ===

IN EVERY SHOT OF EVERY PAINTING, THE ENTIRE PICTURE AND THE ENTIRE GILDED FRAME ARE IN SHOT — all four carved gold edges visible, with a margin of dark teal wall around them. THE WHOLE COMPOSITION MUST READ.

NEVER A CROP. NEVER A DETAIL. NEVER ONLY THE MAIN FIGURE. Every secondary figure and every part of the background stays in frame.




=== SECOND HARD RULE: EVERY LINE IS SPOKEN ALOUD, ON CAMERA, WITH THE MOUTH ===

WHENEVER A FIGURE SPEAKS, HER MOUTH OPENS AND MOVES AND FORMS THE WORDS FOR THE WHOLE LINE, in time with the sound. Jaw dropping and closing, lips shaping the sounds, throat and cheeks moving, head and shoulders moving with the emphasis.

THIS APPLIES TO EVERY LINE, INCLUDING KARIMA'S. NOTHING IS VOICEOVER. A FIGURE WITH A CLOSED OR MOTIONLESS MOUTH IS NEVER THE SOURCE OF A VOICE.

Because the paintings are shown whole and their faces are small, THE MOUTH MOVEMENT IS BROAD AND CLEARLY READABLE AT THAT SIZE.




=== THE FIVE FRAMES ARE FIXED ===

ALL FIVE HANG IN A ROW ON ONE CONTINUOUS FLAT WALL, LEFT TO RIGHT, EACH BELONGING TO ITS OWN FRAME AND NO OTHER:




FRAME A — [tag Mona Lisa here] FAR LEFT, THE SMALLEST FRAME, hung alone with a wide margin of empty wall. IN IT: — A SEATED WOMAN IN DARK CLOTHING, a dark veil over long hair, her hands folded one over the other in her lap, a hazy blue-green mountain landscape with a winding path and water behind her, a faint closed-mouth smile. SHOWN WHOLE, from her folded hands up.

HER GOWN IS HIGH AND MODEST AT THE NECK — the neckline sits close up under her collarbones, covering her chest completely. NO LOW NECKLINE, NO OPEN CHEST, NO CLEAVAGE, NO BARE SKIN BELOW THE THROAT.

SHE HAS NO CHILD. SHE HOLDS NOTHING. No turban, no pearl earring, no animal, no crowd, no black background.




FRAME B — LEFT OF CENTRE, A WIDE HORIZONTAL FRAME. IN IT: — A CROWDED OPEN-AIR DANCE in dappled light: DOZENS OF SMALL FIGURES dancing in the left half and sitting around tables in the right half, straw hats, dark suits, pale striped dresses, GLOBE LAMPS STRUNG OVERHEAD, trees above, a bench in the foreground. SHOWN WHOLE, edge to edge.

No single large portrait. No child. No animal. No black background.




FRAME C — [tag Sistine Madonna here] DEAD CENTRE, BY FAR THE LARGEST FRAME, nearly floor to cornice. IN IT:  — SHOWN COMPLETE, THE ENTIRE COMPOSITION:

— TWO HEAVY GREEN CURTAINS drawn back at the top left and top right, hanging from a rod with rings across the very top

— IN THE CENTRE, A STANDING WOMAN IN A RED GOWN AND A GREAT BLUE MANTLE, a cream veil over her hair, BAREFOOT, STANDING ON CLOUD, HOLDING A NUDE CHILD ON HER HIP

— AT THE LOWER LEFT, AN OLD BEARDED MAN KNEELING IN A HEAVY GOLD EMBROIDERED COPE, looking up at her, one hand on his chest — and BESIDE HIM ON A LEDGE, A PALE PAPAL TIARA

— AT THE LOWER RIGHT, A YOUNG WOMAN KNEELING in green, yellow and blue, her head bowed

— ALONG THE VERY BOTTOM EDGE, TWO SMALL WINGED CHILDREN LEANING ON A LEDGE, chins on their hands, looking up and bored

— BEHIND ALL OF THEM, PALE CLOUD FULL OF FAINT SUGGESTED FACES

ALL SIX FIGURES, BOTH CURTAINS, THE TIARA AND THE LEDGE ARE IN SHOT AT ALL TIMES. THIS IS NOT A CLOSE-UP OF A MOTHER AND CHILD.

EVERY SINGLE FIGURE IN THIS PAINTING IS ALIVE AND MOVING — see the timing below. Not one of them is frozen.

THE CHILD EXISTS ONLY IN THIS FRAME AND IN NO OTHER FRAME IN THIS FILM.




FRAME D — [tag Girl with Pearl Earring here] RIGHT OF CENTRE, A MEDIUM UPRIGHT FRAME. IN IT: — A YOUNG WOMAN AGAINST A BLACK BACKGROUND, turned away and looking back over her shoulder, A BLUE AND YELLOW CLOTH TURBAN, A LARGE PALE PEARL DROP EARRING, a mustard-yellow jacket with a white collar. SHOWN WHOLE, from mid-chest up.

No child, no animal, no landscape, no veil, no crowd. THE TURBAN AND THE PEARL EARRING EXIST ONLY IN THIS FRAME.




FRAME E — [tag Lady with an Ermine here] IMMEDIATELY TO THE RIGHT OF FRAME D, CLOSE BESIDE IT ON THE SAME FLAT WALL. IN IT: — A YOUNG WOMAN IN A RED AND BLUE GOWN with a fine dark band across her hair and a dark bead necklace, turned in three-quarter profile, HOLDING A WHITE ERMINE IN HER ARMS with both hands, against a black background. SHOWN WHOLE, from the waist up.

THE WHITE ANIMAL EXISTS ONLY IN THIS FRAME AND IN NO OTHER FRAME. No child, no turban, no pearl earring.




FRAMES D AND E HANG SIDE BY SIDE ON THE SAME FLAT WALL. THERE IS NO CORNER ANYWHERE IN THIS ROOM'S CAMERA MOVE.




KARIMA. Take her face, her long dark curly hair, and her costume: AN OVERSIZED GREY WOOL SUIT — a boxy jacket with wide shoulders and wide straight trousers — over A PALE PINK SHIRT, with A DARK GREY SATIN TIE, and DEEP BURGUNDY LEATHER LOAFERS.

TAKE FROM [tag your character sheet here] ONLY her face, her hair and this costume — nothing of the white studio background, NONE OF ITS FLAT STUDIO LIGHTING, none of the panel layout.

THERE IS EXACTLY ONE KARIMA. ONE PERSON, ONCE, IN THE LAST EIGHT SECONDS. No second Karima, no duplicate, no reflection, no figure in the background, and she is never inside a frame.

SHE IS NOT CHEWING ANYTHING. NO GUM, NO BUBBLE.

SHE IS AN ORDINARY VISITOR WHO HAS WALKED INTO THIS ROOM AND FOUND THIS HAPPENING, AND SHE IS GENUINELY FRIGHTENED BY IT.




THE GALLERY. THIS IS THE LOCATION. Reproduce it exactly: the deep teal-green walls, the dark herringbone parquet, the moulded cornice, the ornate gilded frames, the dark tufted bench off to the left, the low brass stanchion rail, the small warm ceiling spotlights pooling on each frame, the deep shadow between them.




=== THE VOICES ARE LOCKED TO THE FRAMES ===

FRAME C — warm, low, deep, exhausted. The hushed furious voice of a mother trying not to wake a baby, then losing it.

FRAME B — young, bright, hoarse from shouting over music, breathless.

FRAME A — dry, flat, low, completely unhurried. The least energy of anyone.

FRAME D — young, light, slightly nasal, casual, everything rising like a question.

FRAME E — cool, precise, older, quiet. Never raised.

KARIMA — young, American, and at the end SHAKEN AND OUT OF BREATH.

EACH VOICE COMES ONLY FROM ITS OWN FRAME, AND ONLY WHILE THAT FIGURE'S MOUTH IS VISIBLY MOVING.




=== HOW THE PAINTINGS BEHAVE ===

THEY ARE ALIVE INSIDE THEIR FRAMES — they move, breathe, gesture and react like real people — BUT THEY REMAIN PAINTINGS: visible brushwork, aged cracked varnish, their own internal light, flat inside the frame. THEY NEVER LEAVE THE FRAME, never reach out of it, never step onto the floor.




=== THE THIRTY SECONDS ===




0.0 – 3.8s — THE FILM OPENS ON FRAME C [tag Sistine Madonna here], SHOWN COMPLETE, AND EVERY FIGURE IN IT IS MOVING.

THE STANDING WOMAN shifts the child higher on her hip, turns her head sharply to the left toward the noise, AND HER MOUTH OPENS AND SHE HISSES: **"Hey— hey. Can you keep it down?"**

NOBODY HEARS HER. The party noise off-screen does not change. She waits, jaw tight, THEN HER MOUTH OPENS WIDE AND SHE SHOUTS while still trying not to: **"I have a BABY."**

AND ALL AROUND HER THE OTHERS ARE ALIVE TOO: THE CHILD ON HER HIP SQUIRMS and grabs a fistful of her blue mantle. THE OLD KNEELING MAN IN GOLD shifts his weight, adjusts the heavy cope on his shoulder, glances up at her and away. THE KNEELING WOMAN ON THE RIGHT lifts her head slightly and looks off to the side, then lowers it again. AND ALONG THE BOTTOM, THE TWO SMALL WINGED CHILDREN FIDGET — one shifts his chin on his hand and sighs, AND WHEN SHE SHOUTS, THE OTHER ONE PUTS BOTH HANDS OVER HIS EARS. The green curtains stir at the top.




3.8 – 4.1s — FAST HORIZONTAL WHIP PAN LEFT — wall and gold smearing — landing on FRAME B.




4.1 – 7.3s — FRAME B, SHOWN COMPLETE, the whole crowded dance in motion and very loud @Image 10.

ONE YOUNG WOMAN IN THE FRONT OF THE CROWD TURNS OUT OF IT TOWARD THE CAMERA, dishevelled, AND HER MOUTH OPENS WIDE AND SHE SHOUTS: **"I've been here since eighteen seventy-six! Somebody— get me OUT—"**

The camera nudges in a little but NEVER CLOSE ENOUGH TO LOSE THE FRAME EDGES. People dance straight past her throughout.




7.3 – 7.6s — FAST WHIP PAN LEFT, landing on FRAME A.




7.6 – 11.4s — FRAME A, [tag Mona Lisa here] SHOWN COMPLETE — the seated woman with her high modest neckline, her folded hands, the landscape behind her, the whole small gold frame with a wide margin of teal wall around it.

SHE BARELY MOVES — but HER MOUTH OPENS AND CLEARLY FORMS EVERY WORD. Her eyes slide to the camera and away. Flat, cutting across the shouting: **"I sat still for sixteen years for this. And he never finished painting me."**

One slow blink. Her folded hands do not move.




11.4 – 11.7s — FAST WHIP PAN RIGHT, back to FRAME B.




11.7 – 13.0s — The same young woman, mouth still open mid-shout — she stops, deflates: **"...okay that's worse."**




13.0 – 13.4s — FAST WHIP PAN RIGHT, past the huge central frame, landing on FRAME D.




13.4 – 15.7s — FRAME D, [tag Girl with Pearl Earring here] SHOWN COMPLETE. She is turned over her shoulder. SHE LOOKS OFF TO HER RIGHT AND GIVES A LONG SLOW APPRAISING LOOK FROM BELOW UPWARD — chin down, eyes travelling up, unimpressed. Her pearl swings.

HER MOUTH OPENS: **"Okay wait. Why do you have an animal in here?"**




15.7 – 15.9s — A SHORT FAST WHIP PAN RIGHT — the frames are adjacent — landing on FRAME E.




15.9 – 18.7s — FRAME E, [tag Lady with an Ermine here] SHOWN COMPLETE. She does not answer. She breathes out slowly. SHE STROKES THE WHITE ANIMAL TWICE — AND IT IS ALIVE: it blinks, resettles, turns its head toward the camera.

THEN SHE RUNS HER EYES SLOWLY DOWN AND BACK UP AGAIN — a full silent top-to-bottom appraisal LASTING A FULL SECOND AND A HALF WITH HER MOUTH CLOSED.

THEN HER MOUTH OPENS: **"...Your pearl's fake."**




18.7 – 19.0s — THE CAMERA PULLS BACK AND WIDENS TO HOLD BOTH FRAME D AND FRAME E IN ONE SHOT. NO WHIP.




19.0 – 20.6s — BOTH IN FRAME. The girl in FRAME D, caught off guard, HER MOUTH OPENING: **"It's not—"**

And in FRAME E, without looking up, HER MOUTH BARELY MOVING: **"Mm."**




20.6 – 21.3s — AND THE WHOLE WALL ERUPTS. All five start talking at once, all five mouths moving, loud and completely unintelligible.




21.3 – 22.8s — A FAST SMOOTH SLIDE FROM RIGHT TO LEFT ALONG THE WHOLE WALL @ — quick, urgent, the five lit frames passing through the shot one after another, EACH ONE STILL COMPLETE IN FRAME.

THE PICTURES THEMSELVES ARE STILL. The figures hold their positions. THEY DO NOT GESTURE, DO NOT WAVE, DO NOT SHAKE. ONLY THEIR MOUTHS MOVE.

AND OVER ALL OF IT, FROM SOMEWHERE OFF-SCREEN AHEAD OF US, A SCREAM. A real startled human scream — a short sharp shocked yell, then another — GETTING LOUDER AND CLOSER AS THE CAMERA RUSHES TOWARD IT. WE HEAR HER BEFORE WE SEE HER.




22.8 – 24.0s — THE SLIDE ARRIVES ON [tag your character sheet here] AND STOPS.

SHE IS ALREADY MID-PANIC — backing away from the wall, hands half up in front of her, shoulders hunched, eyes wide, letting out one more short involuntary yelp. She is genuinely frightened. She looks from one end of the wall to the other, at all five of them talking, and takes another step back.




24.0 – 25.5s — She gets a grip on herself, still breathing hard, and says shakily toward the paintings: **"...Y'all good?"**




25.5 – 26.1s — AND EVERYTHING STOPS DEAD. All five fall silent at once, every mouth closing. EVERY PAINTED FACE TURNS AND LOOKS AT HER. TOTAL SILENCE — AND ONE CRICKET, chirping twice.




26.1 – 27.8s — She stares back at them. Then, still shaken, half to them and half to the lens: **"No. You're going crazy, guys."**




27.8 – 30.0s — AND SHE RUNS. She turns and bolts, out of the frame and out of the room, her footsteps hammering away on the parquet and fading.

THE CAMERA DOES NOT FOLLOW HER. It holds on the empty gallery — the bare parquet where she was standing, and beyond it ALL FIVE PAINTINGS, silent and motionless, ALL STILL LOOKING AT THE EMPTY SPACE WHERE SHE WAS.

Nobody speaks. Nobody moves. Hold to the last frame.




=== HOW THE CAMERA MOVES ===

ONE UNBROKEN TAKE. IT NEVER CUTS.

EVERY MOVE BETWEEN FRAMES IS A FAST PHYSICAL HORIZONTAL WHIP PAN of about three tenths of a second, WITH THE WALL AND THE GOLD FRAMES SMEARING HORIZONTALLY IN HEAVY MOTION BLUR. THE SMEAR MUST BE VISIBLE. If a new painting simply appears without the wall streaking past, it is a cut and it is wrong.

ALL MOVES ARE PURELY HORIZONTAL ALONG ONE FLAT WALL. No corner, no tilt, no vertical move.

THE CAMERA NEVER PUSHES IN CLOSE ENOUGH TO CROP A PAINTING.

THE SLIDE AT 21.3s IS FAST AND URGENT, one continuous lateral rush, but still readable — each painting passes through the shot whole.

FROM 22.8s THE CAMERA IS LOCKED ON KARIMA AND THEN ON THE EMPTY ROOM. IT DOES NOT FOLLOW HER OUT.




=== THE LOOK ===

Photoreal and cinematic. The gallery is real: deep matte teal walls, real worn herringbone parquet, real carved and chipped gilding, real warm spotlight pools with soft falloff, real deep shadow between the frames, real dust in the beams.

THE PAINTINGS STAY PAINTINGS EVEN WHILE THEY SPEAK — visible brushwork, aged cracked varnish, their own internal light, flat inside the frame. NOT photoreal people, NOT video screens, NOT windows, NOT holograms.

KARIMA IS PHOTOREAL and lit only by the gallery — dim warm spill off the walls, her face falling into soft shadow. The flat white studio light of her reference appears nowhere.

Natural film colour, gentle contrast, deep shadows holding detail, soft filmic highlight roll-off, fine grain, real motion blur. No HDR, no digital sharpening, no oversaturation, no teal-and-orange grade, no lens flare.




=== SOUND, NO MUSIC ===

THE NOISE BUILDS AS THE PAINTINGS WAKE UP. Each painting that has spoken KEEPS MURMURING UNDERNEATH.

0.0–3.8s — the loud roar of a crowd off-screen. The deep tired voice of FRAME C over it.

4.1–7.3s — FRAME B at full volume.

7.6–11.4s — the crowd drops back but never stops; the flat dry voice of FRAME A on top.

11.7–20.6s — three, then four, then five voices layered. A hard stone-and-parquet acoustic with a long slap-back.

20.6–24.0s — ALL FIVE AT FULL VOLUME AND COMPLETELY OVERLAPPING — AND OVER THEM, FROM OFF-SCREEN, HER SCREAM: a short sharp startled human yell, then a second one, getting closer and louder as the camera rushes toward her. Not a horror scream, not sustained — the involuntary shout of somebody badly startled.

24.0–25.5s — the five voices still going. Her voice on top, breathless and unsteady.

25.5–26.1s — EVERY SOUND STOPS AT ONCE. Absolute silence. AND ONE CRICKET, chirping twice.

26.1–27.8s — her voice alone, close, shaken.

27.8–30.0s — running footsteps hammering on parquet and fading away, a door somewhere. Then the enormous quiet of an empty gallery.

No music, no score, no song, no narration.
