What is this

Official blog and thoughts dump for GOTCHA!: A post-mystery murder, my submission for the Higgsfield Global Film Festival. Check back for frequent updates about the movie's progress.

Background

This is my first crack at using Higgsfield Cinema Studio and building an AI film in public.

Initial Thoughts

Of course, typical, my idea turns out to be way more complicated, lengthy and expensive than I intended. 




Due to costs I initially planned to drop out of the contest, but became too attached to the story and want to see it completed.

BTW, what is the story?

GOTCHA is a post-murder mystery, or rather, a post-mystery murder. It's a nod to classic mansion/sleuthy murder mysteries, except it takes place after the murder has been committed and begins with the detective exposing the killer.

From there the story takes a surprising turn. It is no longer a traditional murder mystery, but something completely different.




I will use this space as a place to post updates

September 15, 2026 - Literally Cutting It Close

As usual, I underestimated the time (and credits) it would take to complete the film. There was a frantic rush to get it out before the original Sep. 14 dead.ine.




THANKFULLY, the deadline was extended by 24 hours. Even then, I submitted just in the nick of time, only to learn it was extended again! Used the extra time to make a few adjustments.




The final film cut is still in progress, but will be out before the new deadline.




BIGGEST LESSON: The biggest hurdle is lag. My first submission was in 720 HD because I didn't have time to upscale it before deadline. Slow computer, slow rendering, slow up-resing and slow models really suck up A LOT of time!




Thanks to the extension, the final cut should be in a higher resolution.

September 5, 2026  - Conserving credits

Another lesson learned. The best way to conserve credits:

Only use Unlimited for image generations
Use Hailou MiniMax H3 and Kling 3.0 as much as possible. Especially for scenes that will later be color-corrected in black and white. H3 is a really solid model, and a great alternative to Seedance and Kling models!
September 4, 2026  - OOPS! Lesson learned translating prompts!




I learned a trick from Higgsfield to handle restrictive character limits. You use a chatbot to translate the prompt into Chinese characters.




However, remember to keep the dialogue in English (or whatever the language should be).
