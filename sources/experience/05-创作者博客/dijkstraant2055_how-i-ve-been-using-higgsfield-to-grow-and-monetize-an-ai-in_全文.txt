A little while ago, I came across a post on X about an AI character named Jean Phil who was blowing up on Instagram. I checked out his page and saw the insane amount of views and engagement he was getting. That got me thinking about trying something similar myself.

That’s where Percy Pickett came from.

I started by creating a character that was funny and recognizable. Once I had a reference image I was happy with, I started messing around with different ways to bring him to life using Higgsfield.

Most of my videos are made using Higgsfield’s Genshutsu model. I’ll find a trending video, dance, or sound that I think would be funny with Percy in it, upload the original video along with my character reference, and have the AI replace the person with Percy. I try to keep the original lighting, movements, and overall quality so it looks like Percy was actually there.

It’s definitely taken some trial and error. Some generations come out almost perfect on the first try, while others look absolutely terrible. I’ve found that keeping prompts short and straightforward usually gives me better results than trying to explain every little detail.

I’ve been posting Percy on both TikTok and Instagram, and the results have been pretty encouraging. Some videos do better than others, but I’ve been able to build an audience and get some solid engagement. One thing I’ve noticed is that the simplest videos sometimes perform the best. You really don’t need some crazy concept to get people’s attention.

I’ve also been experimenting with different ways to monetize the account. One of the main ones I’ve been using is Launchpoint, a platform that pays creators for views on eligible Instagram reels. The campaigns I’ve been participating in pay $2 per 1,000 views, with a cap of $2,000 per million views on each video. It’s a pretty interesting way to monetize content without needing a massive following or waiting for traditional brand deals.

On top of Launchpoint, I’ve also started getting approached for paid promotions, especially from artists and promoters wanting their music featured in Percy’s videos. I’ve already secured paid collaborations, which has been pretty cool considering Percy is literally just an AI character I created.

The nice thing about using Higgsfield is that once you get a workflow figured out, making content becomes pretty straightforward. I can test different ideas without having to film anything myself. Obviously, credits aren’t free, and bad generations can be annoying, but I’ve been getting better at making the most out of each one.

I’m still experimenting with different content styles and figuring out what performs best. I’ve also started working on other AI characters to see if I can build multiple accounts using a similar approach.

For anyone wanting to try something like this, I’d recommend spending some time getting your character right before jumping straight into making videos. Having a consistent reference image makes a huge difference. After that, it’s mostly about finding trends that fit your character and staying consistent with posting.

There’s still a lot I want to try, but so far it’s been a fun way to experiment with AI while building something that can actually generate income.
