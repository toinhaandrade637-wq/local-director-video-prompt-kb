2.Make it your own ♡

Edit the uploaded 3D mannequin dance video into a realistic live-action-style video featuring exactly two adult women, aged 22–25.




MOTION REFERENCE

Use the source video for choreography, timing, hand gestures, footwork, body movements and relative positions. The mannequin that first appears on the right is Woman A; the other is Woman B. Keep these assignments when they turn or cross. Replace the mannequin appearance and original background.




IMAGE REFERENCES

Image 1: Woman A's facial identity, hairstyle, complete outfit, body proportions and accessories.

Image 2: Woman B's facial identity, hairstyle, complete outfit, body proportions and accessories.

Image 3: The environment.




Each character reference contains different views of the SAME woman, not additional performers. Preserve each woman's assigned face, hairstyle and clothing throughout. Do not blend or swap their identities.




SCENE

Recreate the classroom in Image 3 on a bright summer afternoon. Natural window light, green foliage outside, soft highlights and gentle sunlight rays create a peaceful, nostalgic atmosphere.




VISUAL STYLE

A live-action look inspired by early-2000s Japanese youth films and consumer CCD cameras. Natural skin texture, gentle optical softness, subtle digital grain, soft highlight bloom and bright natural colors. Avoid CGI surfaces, plastic skin and excessive sharpening.




PERFORMANCE

Follow the reference dance rather than inventing new choreography. Preserve its sequence and timing, with natural weight shifts, fluid gestures, relaxed expressions and believable hair and clothing movement.




FRAMING

Vertical 9:16. Keep both dancers and their important gestures visible. Use restrained handheld movement without distracting cuts or zooms. Preserve the source video's duration.




Exactly two adult women. No additional people. The result should feel like a candid summer dance recording.
