Step 3: The Locations

Feel free to use mine or you can go ahead and recreate yours.

I built each set piece as its own separate prompt - the living room, the kitchen and the exterior house.

THE LIVING ROOM

Create an image of an Empty 90s multi-camera sitcom apartment living room set, bright, warm and cozy, lived-in and full of personality — a Nigerian-American household. A large deep burgundy velvet sofa, Ankara-print throw pillows in bold orange, green and indigo patterns, a woven aso-oke textile draped over the back, a low wooden coffee table with carved legs in front of it on a patterned rug in warm rust, gold and brown tones. A cane-backed accent chair on one side, a wooden stool with carved details and a leather pouf in the foreground. Step 5: The video

Once the character sheets, locations, and voices were all sorted, the last step was creating the full video prompt.

Quick reminder: tag yourself (and everything else — Cookie, the location, the outfit, etc) in the full video prompt as well. 

Use a photo that actually looks like you on a normal day — no filters, no heavy edits. The AI reflects whatever you feed it, filters and all.

Final thoughts

If there's one thing I'd tell someone starting their own AI series from scratch, it's this: your first character sheet probably won't be your best one, and that's fine. Mine took a full redo before it actually looked like me. Treat it like any creative process — regenerate, don't be afraid to simplify when something isn't working.
