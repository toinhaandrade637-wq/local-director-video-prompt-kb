Chapter 2. Building the environment and scene stills
Environment

Cinematic 16:9 film still, photoreal. A grand old theater with an empty stage, seen from the middle of the stalls looking straight down the centre aisle; symmetrical composition, camera at standing height, slightly above stage level so the stage floor is visible. No people.




THE THEATER: tall rounded proscenium arch framed by richly carved gilded plasterwork — scrolls, garlands, a crest at top centre; the gold aged with soft patina and a few worn patches, but grand and loved, not ruined. Heavy crimson velvet main curtain drawn open and gathered at both sides, under a velvet valance with gold scalloped fringe. A wide, completely empty stage of old wooden floorboards, warm honey-brown, worn and softly polished, with a curved front edge and a few central steps down to the aisle. Deep behind the stage, black velvet drapes fade into darkness. On both sides, tiers of opera boxes with gilded fronts and red drapes; small two-arm candle sconces with warm bulbs on the pillars beside the stage; ornate gilded ceiling above. In the foreground, rows of red velvet seats on both sides of the centre aisle.




THE LIGHT — warm, cinematic, inviting, not gloomy:




a wide soft warm spotlight from above and in front pools golden light on the centre of the empty stage; its beam shows in light haze and fades gently at the edges, not a hard circle;

a cool pale-blue backlight from deep backstage glows through the haze behind the pool, giving depth and separating the stage from the dark drapes;

house lights at half: sconces and box lamps glow warm amber, soft light washes over the gilded arch, boxes and ceiling, the whole auditorium clearly readable;

tops of the red seats catch a soft warm sheen; tiny dust motes float in the beams.




LOOK: rich warm palette — gold, crimson, honey wood — with soft teal-blue in the shadows; gentle contrast, open shadows with detail everywhere, no crushed blacks; subtle anamorphic flare from the spotlight; soft 35mm film-still texture, fine grain.




Avoid: any furniture, props or objects on the stage; people; dark, gloomy or horror mood; crushed black shadows; heavy smoke or fog; ruined walls, trash or heavy decay; closed curtain; harsh hard-edged spotlight circle; text, signs, logos or watermarks.
