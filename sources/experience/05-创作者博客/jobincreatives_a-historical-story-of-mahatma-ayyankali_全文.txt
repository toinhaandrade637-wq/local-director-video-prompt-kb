Final Editing and Post-Production

I edited the entire video in Adobe Premiere Pro, where I assembled the best shots from the generated clips into the final film. The text elements and typography were created in Adobe After Effects, and I used the music from the Tamil movie Karuppu for the film’s soundtrack.

A Key Learning from the Process

Not every AI generated shot came out as expected. I generated multiple versions of certain scenes, selected the best results, and used those shots in the final edit.

One important lesson I learned was to think about the final edit even while generating the shots. Before generating a scene, it helps to visualise how the shot will fit into the overall sequence, what transition will follow, and how it will connect with the next shot.

By planning the edit during the generation process, selecting the strongest results, and refining the sequence step by step, I was able to complete the video more efficiently while avoiding unnecessary credit consumption.
