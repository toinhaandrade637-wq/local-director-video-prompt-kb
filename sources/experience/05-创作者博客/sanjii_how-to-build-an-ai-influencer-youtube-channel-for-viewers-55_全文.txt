Stage 1 — The Niche

You can't pick a random topic. If older viewers don't care about it, nothing else works. These five niches are pulling big numbers with a 55+ audience right now:

Natural Home Remedies. Simple kitchen recipes for bad knees, bad sleep or low energy. You sell your own e-book from day one. The catch: no medical claims.
Golden Era Nostalgia. The 50s, 60s and 70s: old cars, small towns, how life used to be. Huge comment sections, but you live on YouTube ads because there's no obvious product to sell.
Retirement and Social Security. Pension rules, simple tax hacks, avoiding scams. High ad rates, around $10 per 1,000 views, but your facts have to be bulletproof.
Daily Faith and Blessings. Short prayers to start the day that grandparents share on Facebook and WhatsApp. Devotional e-books plus ads, but lower ad rates.
Life Wisdom and Aging. An older mentor talking about peace of mind and lessons learned late in life. Loyal fans, but the stories run dry.

Home Remedies is the fastest way to make money: a link to your own recipe guide works from the very first video, with no waiting on YouTube monetization. Keep it to everyday foods, teas and simple habits so YouTube doesn't flag it.
