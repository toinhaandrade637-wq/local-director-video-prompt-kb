LES LIGNES CAMERA À COPIER (une seule par plan)

La caméra recule :
Starts as a medium shot from the front at chest height, then one smooth continuous dolly-out straight backwards, ending on a wide full-body shot that reveals the whole location around him.

La caméra se rapproche :
Starts as a medium-wide shot from the front at eye level, then one smooth continuous dolly-in toward him, ending on a tight close-up of his face and shoulders looking into the lens.

Nouvelle caméra là où tu pointes :
The new camera stands exactly at the spot he points to in @video1, on a tripod, eye level, medium shot from the waist up. At the start he is seen in three-quarter view; when he turns his head and points, he looks straight into this new lens with direct eye contact and points at the viewer. Completely static, locked-off.

Vue du ciel (drone) :
Aerial drone shot from the sky, high above, looking down at a steep high angle onto him. One slow, smooth descending drone movement toward him.

Contre-plongée :
Low-angle medium shot from his front-left, camera at waist height tilted up, three-quarter view with the building and sky behind him. Static camera, no movement.

Plongée (grue) :
High-angle shot from a camera crane above and in front of him, looking down at about 45 degrees, medium-wide framing, his face clearly visible. Static.

Gros plan de côté :
Tight close-up on his face in near side profile, telephoto lens, shallow depth of field with the background blurred. Static.

LES 6 RÈGLES POUR UN RENDU PARFAIT

Garde ton format : une vidéo en 16:9 reste en 16:9 du début à la fin.
Un angle par phrase : des morceaux de 4 à 6 secondes, coupés entre deux phrases.
Un seul mouvement de caméra par plan : recule, avance ou reste fixe.
Garde ton vrai son sur tout le montage, la synchro reste naturelle.
Pour un regard caméra, place la nouvelle caméra exactement là où tu regardes.
Finis sur du vrai : pour l'appel à l'action, un zoom dans ta prise 4K.
