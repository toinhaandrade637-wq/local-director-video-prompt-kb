CHARACTER:

Use my uploaded image @Image 1  &  @Image 2 as the identity and wardrobe reference for ONE adult MALE protagonist. Preserve his exact face, natural eye colour, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportions, clothing, footwear and accessories throughout every shot. His outfit must come exclusively from my image. Keep the same outfit during the explosion, underwater transition and final falling sequence. Adapt wind and floating hair movement to his actual hair length.




SETTING AND VISUAL STYLE:

An isolated asphalt road at night. Alternating yellow-and-white painted curb blocks, dry roadside grass, sparse small trees, utility poles and overhead wires. A few distant lights and a small roadside sign. Mostly black sky, no crowds or traffic near the protagonist.




Begin with cool, restrained frontal lighting against deep darkness. The explosion later transforms the scene into intense amber-orange light. The water transition changes the palette to deep blue and cyan. Finish inside a completely black, featureless void. Realistic skin, fabric, shadows, atmospheric dust and cinematic VFX.




SHOT-BY-SHOT TIMELINE:




0.0–1.9 seconds — Waiting on the curb.

Static frontal wide shot from across the road. The man sits alone near the centre of the frame on the low yellow-and-white curb, feet on the asphalt. He briefly looks sideways, then lowers his gaze to a smartphone held in both hands. His posture is relaxed and slightly hunched.




1.9–3.0 seconds — Phone close-up and reaction.

Hard cut to a frontal medium close-up. He looks down at the phone, then turns his head toward screen-right as something attracts his attention. His expression shifts from distracted to curious.




3.0–3.8 seconds — Standing up.

Cut to a low, tighter shot showing his lower torso, hands, legs and shoes. He leans forward, rises naturally from the curb and steps onto the road, keeping the phone in one hand.




3.8–5.4 seconds — Walking.

Cut to a wide side-profile shot. He walks slowly from screen-left toward screen-right across the empty road, then stops and looks ahead. Show his full body and the roadside vegetation.




5.4–7.5 seconds — Distant impact.

Cut behind him to a medium-wide rear view looking down the road. His back is in the foreground, with the road, poles and wires leading toward the distant horizon. A narrow, brilliant orange streak descends vertically from the sky and strikes the road far away. A white-hot fireball rapidly expands into a towering orange mushroom-shaped explosion. The blast remains distant. Its light illuminates his silhouette, clothes and the asphalt.




7.5–8.6 seconds — Frontal reaction.

Hard cut to a close-up of his face, now lit by flickering orange light. He looks startled and raises one hand beside his ear and cheek. Wind begins disturbing his hair and clothing.




8.6–9.8 seconds — Profile reaction.

Cut to a tight side-profile view. He stares toward the explosion with parted lips and tense shoulders. Amber light grows stronger; wind pushes his hair and fabric away from the blast.




9.8–11.3 seconds — Shockwave approaching.

Cut to a rear full-body wide shot. He stands small in the foreground while the enormous glowing mushroom cloud dominates the distant road. A low wall of dust spreads outward from the explosion and races toward him. His long shadow stretches toward the camera.




11.3–12.1 seconds — Shockwave impact.

Cut to a wide side view, with the man facing screen-right. A dense orange dust wave rushes in from screen-right toward screen-left. He starts upright; as it hits, his shoulders recoil, his torso arches backward and his feet lift off the asphalt.




12.1–16.4 seconds — Extreme slow-motion suspension.

Continue the same side view. The blast throws him backward toward screen-left in dramatic slow motion. His head and shoulders lead, feet trail toward screen-right, knees bend slightly and his arms float forward and upward. His body gradually becomes almost horizontal above the road.




Keep the camera largely locked and his full body visible. Clothing ripples, dust and small particles drift through orange light. Movement is continuous and extremely slow, not a frozen still. He does not land.




16.4–19.7 seconds — Expanding water portal.

While he remains suspended in exactly the same falling pose, a small deep-blue circular opening appears behind his torso. It expands into a large upright ring of turbulent water surrounding his body.




The portal has a thick, translucent, rippling water rim with irregular splashes and suspended droplets. Its interior reveals dark blue underwater space. The expanding circle progressively replaces the orange road background. Preserve his position, scale and momentum through the transition.




The camera moves toward the opening as its watery edge grows beyond the frame. He continues drifting backward inside the blue water, surrounded by fine bubbles and suspended particles.




19.7–22.5 seconds — Explosion reflected in his eye.

Hard cut to an extreme close-up of his face underwater, framed across the nose bridge and one clearly visible eye. Preserve HIS actual eye shape and iris colour from the uploaded image.




A miniature glowing orange mushroom-cloud explosion is reflected on the glossy surface of his eye. Tiny bubbles pass in front of his face. Slowly push closer, accelerating near the end until the iris, pupil and fiery reflection fill the frame. Enter the darkness of the pupil to transition into the next shot.




22.5–24.3 seconds — Falling into darkness.

Reveal the same man falling in a pitch-black void. Medium-wide side view: head toward screen-left, feet toward screen-right, torso reclined, knees bent and arms reaching upward. Soft cool rim lighting reveals his face and original outfit. His clothing floats gently as he sinks.




24.3–26.6 seconds — View from above and behind.

Cut to a high angle looking down past the back of his head and shoulders. Both arms reach toward the camera while his body falls away into darkness. His hair moves naturally according to its actual length. Keep the void completely black.




26.6–29.3 seconds — Extreme wide fall.

Cut to a very distant wide shot. His entire body is now a small, faintly blue-lit figure near the centre, continuing to fall and recede into the enormous black space. Gradually reduce his apparent size and brightness.




29.3–30.0 seconds — Ending.

Let his silhouette disappear into darkness and finish on full black. No title or end card.




CONTINUITY:

Use clean cinematic cuts between the specified shots. Make the expanding water portal and eye-to-void transition fluid and spatially coherent. Maintain one consistent male identity and one unchanged outfit. Preserve realistic anatomy, hands and facial features. No wardrobe changes, extra characters, random locations, cartoon effects, captions, logos or watermarks. The protagonist remains intact, with no burns, gore or visible injuries.
