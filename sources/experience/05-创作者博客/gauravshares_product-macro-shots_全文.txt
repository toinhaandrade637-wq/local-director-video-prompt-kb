Step 1: Macro Shots

Use the uploaded product image as the EXACT visual reference and source of truth for the product.




Create an ultra-photorealistic EXTREME MACRO product photograph of the uploaded product.




The product identity must remain perfectly consistent with the reference image. Preserve the exact shape, proportions, materials, colors, textures, finishes, logos, typography, engravings, buttons, seams, edges, hardware, surface details and all recognizable design elements. Do not redesign, simplify, add, remove or alter any product features.




CAMERA:

Extreme close-up macro photography, professional high-end macro lens, approximately 90–120mm macro, camera positioned extremely close to the product. The frame should be dominated by a small but visually important section of the product. Extremely shallow depth of field with a razor-sharp focal plane and smooth natural optical falloff.




FOCUS:

Choose the most visually premium and detailed area of the uploaded product and make it the primary focus. Reveal tiny material details that are normally invisible at normal viewing distance.




LIGHTING:

Sophisticated professional commercial studio lighting. Large soft light source creating elegant gradients and controlled specular highlights. Subtle directional light revealing surface texture and material quality. Realistic reflections appropriate to the product's actual material.




MATERIAL:

Physically accurate materials. Realistic metal, glass, leather, fabric, plastic, ceramic, rubber or other materials according to the uploaded reference. Preserve the actual material characteristics of the product.




COMPOSITION:

Minimalist luxury product photography. No unnecessary props. No people. No hands. No additional products. Clean premium background with subtle tonal gradients and natural shadows.




OPTICAL QUALITY:

True macro photography aesthetic, realistic lens characteristics, realistic depth of field, subtle bokeh, natural reflections, high micro-contrast, extremely detailed surface rendering, cinematic photographic realism.




The result should look like a photograph captured for an elite premium advertising campaign, not a generic CGI render.




DO NOT change the product.

DO NOT invent product features.

DO NOT alter logos or text.

DO NOT distort geometry.

DO NOT duplicate the product.

DO NOT create floating or impossible components.




Only change the camera perspective, framing, lighting and depth of field while preserving the uploaded product exactly.
