SCENE CONTEXT

Photorealistic character reference sheet: ONE young woman shown in four panels on a seamless white studio background — three full-body views (front, side, back) plus one close portrait. Documentary photographic realism, for identity and wardrobe lock.




ACTIVE REFERENCES

[tag your selfie here]: HER EXACT FACE — the primary identity source, 100% match, zero drift. Ignore its car interior, lighting and clothing.

[tag your selfie here] HER EXACT SIDE PROFILE — controls profile geometry only: forehead-to-brow slope, nose bridge line and slightly upturned tip, philtrum and lips in profile, softly rounded chin and jaw contour, cheek line, and the ear's shape and placement with its piercings. Locks the side panel. Ignore its brick-wall setting, clothing and warm light.

Face conflicts always resolve to  [tag your selfie here]  .




THE SUBJECT

A 20-year-old woman of Kazakh / Central Asian appearance, 163 cm tall, slim and lightly built with narrow shoulders, a slender neck and a delicate frame. Natural body proportions, realistic and consistent across all panels.




FACE — DETAILED LOCK

A soft rounded oval face: smooth moderately high forehead, high but softly padded cheekbones giving fullness across the mid-face, a gently rounded jaw tapering to a small softly rounded chin with no cleft — widest at the cheeks, narrowing smoothly downward, no sharp angles anywhere.

EYES — dark warm-brown almond eyes with a slight upward tilt at the outer corners; a low, shallow, partially hidden upper-lid crease so the lid space above the lash line reads smooth and full; inner corners softly rounded with a faint epicanthic fold; a soft natural fullness beneath each eye with faint shadows under them; dark, moderately long, naturally straight lashes with the lower lashes clearly visible; a calm, direct gaze into the lens.

BROWS — thick, dark brown, straight and low-set, running almost horizontally with only a slight rise toward the outer third; hairs visibly brushed upward in a soft feathered texture with individual hairs readable at the top edge; natural, unshaped, never thin or drawn.

NOSE — small and neat: a narrow, low, straight bridge beginning softly between the brows with no pronounced dip, widening slightly toward the base; a small soft rounded tip; small softly flared nostrils with rounded outer edges; in profile the line is straight with a subtle upward lift at the tip and no bump.

LIPS — full with a clearly defined cupid's bow, two soft peaks and a shallow philtrum groove above; the lower lip noticeably fuller and rounder than the upper; natural rose tone, slightly dry and faintly chapped with fine vertical texture lines; mouth closed, corners neutral.

SKIN — warm light-beige with a golden-olive undertone: real photographic texture with visible pores across the nose, inner cheeks and chin, a light natural sheen on the forehead and nose bridge, fine peach fuzz catching light along the jawline and hairline, a few small freckles and tiny beauty marks scattered on the cheeks and the bridge of the nose, one or two small blemishes, slight natural redness around the nose and unevenness in tone. NO retouching, NO beauty smoothing, NO poreless or waxy finish, NO filter.

EARS — small and set close to the head; multiple small piercings matching [tag your selfie here] exactly: two slim silver hoops on the lower lobe, one with a tiny hanging charm, plus a small stud higher in the upper cartilage.

EXPRESSION — neutral and relaxed in every panel: brows unfurrowed, mouth closed, eyes level and calm. No smile, no posing, no tension.




HAIR — per [tag your selfie here]

Dark brown, straight to very slightly wavy, falling to just below the shoulders, with a soft natural texture and no product. A long CURTAIN FRINGE parted off-centre sweeps across the forehead and down past the cheekbones on both sides, framing the face; the rest falls loose and unstyled behind the shoulders, with fine flyaway strands around the crown, temples and nape catching the light. Individual strands clearly resolved, natural soft sheen — never glossy-plastic, never a solid block of colour, never salon-perfect. Identical cut, length and parting in every panel; no hair tie, no clips.




WARDROBE

A plain GREY heavyweight cotton jersey HOODIE, oversized and relaxed: a soft mid-to-dark heather grey, drawstring-free hood sitting loosely around the neck and shoulders with visible bulk at the collar, long sleeves reaching the wrists, ribbed cuffs and hem, a dropped shoulder line, no print, no logo, no graphics of any kind. Beneath it, dark indigo-black straight-cut jeans, no rips, no belt. BAREFOOT. No jewellery beyond the ear piercings, no watch, no rings, no nail polish, no glasses.

The clothes are dry and hang with real fabric weight — the hoodie fully opaque with genuine jersey drape, soft creases at the elbows and hem, and it never clings to the body.




THE EARPHONES — keep exactly

White wired in-ear earphones in BOTH ears: glossy white plastic buds seated in the ear canals, a thin flat white cable running down from each ear and joining across her chest with a small inline control block on one side, the silver metal plug hanging free below. The cable has real weight — slight natural curves and one soft kink, never floating, never perfectly straight, never tangled. Visible in all four panels.




SHEET LAYOUT — FOUR PANELS

Four panels in one row on an identical seamless white studio background, with identical lighting, identical scale relationship and identical wardrobe detail in every panel.

Panel 1 — FULL BODY, FRONT: standing straight and still, facing the camera square on, feet parallel at shoulder width, arms relaxed down at her sides with palms turned slightly inward and fingers relaxed and fully visible, shoulders level, eyes into the lens. Whole figure from the top of her head to below her bare feet inside frame with clear headroom and floor space.

Panel 2 — FULL BODY, SIDE: a true 90-degree profile facing frame right, same stance and proportions as Panel 1, arms relaxed at the sides. The face profile — nose, lips, chin, jaw and ear with its piercings — matches [tag your selfie here]   exactly. The hoodie's side drape, the fringe's fall and the earphone cable all readable from the side.

Panel 3 — FULL BODY, BACK: back fully to the camera, same stance; the hood lying against her upper back, the hoodie's back drape and hem, her hair falling over the shoulders, and the cable visible passing behind the ears.

Panel 4 — CLOSE PORTRAIT: framed from the top of the head to the collarbones, direct frontal view at eye level, face filling most of the frame and fully visible — no hair across the eyes, no shadow obscuring the features. Every facial detail must read clearly at this scale: skin pores and texture, the freckles and beauty marks in their exact positions, the faint under-eye shadows, individual brow hairs, the eyelash detail and catchlights in both eyes, the moist inner lip line and the fine dry texture on the lips, the curtain fringe's strands, the ear piercings on the visible ear, and the white earbud seated in the ear with the cable running down out of frame. Neutral closed-mouth expression, eyes into the lens. The hoodie's grey collar and hood bulk visible at the bottom of frame.




CAMERA & OPTICS

Panels 1–3 (full body): 47° diagonal field of view, standard normal lens character, camera 3.5 metres from the subject, lens at her hip height, level, no tilt. Natural body proportions, zero distortion, straight vertical lines, deep even focus from her hair to her feet.

Panel 4 (portrait): 29° diagonal field of view, short telephoto portrait character, camera at her eye level, level, no tilt — natural facial perspective with no wide-angle enlargement of the nose or cheeks; focus critically sharp on the eyes with the ears and collar still fully sharp.




LIGHTING

Identical across all four panels: a large soft light source above and 30 degrees camera-left, white bounce fill from camera-right, so shaping is soft and the far side of the body keeps a gentle falloff. The background is lit separately to a clean bright white, exposed about half a stop under the grey hoodie so the figure separates cleanly; a soft edge light traces the outline of her shoulders and hood. In Panel 4 the same key and fill give clean catchlights in both eyes and gentle modelling across the cheek without hard shadow.

Exposure set for her skin. No hard shadow on the background, no coloured light, no rim flare, no vignette.




BACKGROUND

Seamless white studio background with no visible floor line, no horizon, no corner, no seam, no props. A soft contact shadow sits directly under her bare feet in the full-body panels so she is grounded on the floor and not floating.




REALISM

Documentary photographic realism: real skin texture, individual hair strands, honest cotton jersey weave and drape, real denim texture, real glossy plastic on the earbuds and cable. Fine natural film grain, natural colours, sharp clarity. No illustration, no anime, no CG gloss, no plastic skin, no beauty-filter smoothing.




POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS

Face, skin and features 100% match [tag your selfie here]; the profile in Panel 2 100% matches [tag your selfie here]; hair per [tag your selfie here] with the curtain fringe. The same person, same face, same hair, same wardrobe and same earphones in all four panels, with zero drift.

Four panels only, in this order: full-body front, full-body side, full-body back, close portrait.

One person only. No reflections, no duplicate limbs, no extra hands or fingers, no text, no labels, no watermark, no logo anywhere in frame.
