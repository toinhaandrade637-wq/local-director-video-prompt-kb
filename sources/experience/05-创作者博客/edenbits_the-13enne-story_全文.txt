THE 13ENNE

Age: 13. Allegedly. Since 1998.

Nobody knows where The 13enne came from. Some say he was born in a small Italian village. Others believe he escaped from a government experiment designed to create the world's most awkward teenager.

According to his birth certificate, he's 13 years old. According to his face, he's 47. According to his knees, he's already retired.

His ears can detect Wi-Fi signals from three kilometers away. His nose grows one millimeter every time someone lies to him, and his haircut has been legally classified as an architectural structure.

He has worn the same number 13 jersey for 26 years. Nobody has ever seen him wash it.

Despite his appearance, The 13enne lives a completely normal teenage life. He goes to school, fails mathematics, falls in love with girls who don't know he exists, and spends six hours a day arguing with strangers on TikTok.

His greatest enemy is puberty, which started in 2003 and still hasn't finished loading.

Scientists have tried to study him, but every time they approach, he whispers:

"I'm not weird. I'm just early access."

His ultimate dream is to become the most famous teenager on Earth before his 14th birthday.

Unfortunately, that birthday has been postponed indefinitely.

THE 13ENNE — Forever 13. Built like 47. Mentally unavailable.
