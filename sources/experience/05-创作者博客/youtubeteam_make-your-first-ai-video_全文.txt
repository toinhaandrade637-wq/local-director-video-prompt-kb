In just three prompts, you’ll make your first AI video. It starts with one character: an old fisherman in a red scarf. The first prompt creates him, the second locks in his whole look from every angle, and the third gives him his own 15-second film. In a bonus step, you’ll put him into a completely different video. Every prompt is below, so you can follow along and finish before the end of the day. 

Before You Start

All you need is a Higgsfield Plus account and three tools, all on one plan and one credit balance:

GPT Image 2.5 Flare for your character, and GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst for the character sheet.
Seedance 2.5 for the film, with movement and sound.
Higgsfield Genjutsu to put your character into a trending video. Plus includes free Genjutsu generations.
Step 1: Create your character

Go to Image → Create image, choose GPT Image 2.5 Flare, set the aspect ratio to 3:4 and paste the prompt below. Change the character to your own idea, like a girl in a yellow raincoat or a knight in silver armour, so your video doesn't look like everyone else's. Already have a photo of your character? Upload it with + and skip to Step 2.

Step one: the old fisherman, made with GPT Image 2.5 Flare

Cinematic portrait of a weathered fisherman in his 70s, deeply lined sun-beaten face, white stubble, squinting pale eyes. Thick red wool scarf over navy sweater and oilskin jacket, hand on a coiled rope. 85mm f/2, chest-up, off-center left. Misty harbor at dawn, boats as soft grey silhouettes. Cold blue side light, red scarf as the only warm accent. Photorealistic, detailed skin texture, film grain, documentary style.

Step 2: Lock his look with a character sheet

This is the step that separates a polished, consistent video from one where the face changes every shot. Click +, attach your character, switch to GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, set 16:9 and paste the prompt below. You get the same man from the front, the back and a three-quarter view on one image, and every later step uses it as his reference.

Step two: his character sheet, from the front, back and three-quarter view, made with GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst

Character sheet, three panels of the same person from the attached reference: full-body front view, full-body back view, chest-up three-quarter portrait. Identical face, skin tone, hair, body proportions and outfit in all views; back view shows the same hairstyle and back of the garments. Neutral standing pose, relaxed empty hands, full figure head to shoes, seamless white studio background, soft diffused lighting. Unretouched natural skin with pores, fine lines and subtle asymmetry, no makeup, matte finish, muted catchlights. Photorealistic, high-end commercial photography.

Step 3: Give your character a film

Go to Video → Create video and choose Seedance 2.5. Click Add references, upload your character sheet and paste the prompt below. Where it says @Image1, type @ and select your sheet. Set the duration to 15s, the aspect ratio to 16:9 and the quality to 720p. Seedance 2.5 creates the movement and the sound together, in clips up to 30 seconds, and 1080p looks sharper but costs more credits.

Step three: his own 15-second film, made with Seedance 2.5

Cinematic scene, 15 seconds, multi-shot, photorealistic, realistic water physics. @Image 1 is the main character: elderly fisherman, short grey hair, white stubble, deeply weathered face, worn black leather jacket, navy knit sweater, long dark-red knitted scarf, grey work trousers, worn leather boots. Keep his face, hair and clothes exactly as in @Image 1.




Shot 1 (0–3s): Slow push-in at water level through light fog drifting over a calm turquoise-emerald northern river, dense dark spruce forest and mossy granite boulders along the banks, soft diffused overcast dawn light. The camera approaches a small faded-blue wooden rowing boat in the middle of the river. The old man sits in it with a fishing rod, relaxed, eyes half closed, quietly humming a simple old folk melody to himself, the float bobbing gently.




Shot 2 (3–6s): Top-down overhead shot slowly rotating above the boat. The clear water around it turns murky as dark silt clouds up from the depths. The water slowly begins to rotate around the boat, thin lines of foam and floating needles spiraling inward. The float is pulled under. His humming stops, he leans over the side, confused.




Shot 3 (6–9s): A realistic river whirlpool forms right beside the boat: the water surface sinks into a narrow funnel-shaped depression, swirling faster with a deep gurgling sucking sound, foam spinning into its center. The current drags the boat sideways, it tilts and starts circling toward the funnel. Handheld close-up: he panics, eyes wide with terror, mouth open, shouting, grabbing the sides of the boat with both hands as the rod is torn from his grip.




Shot 4 (9–11s): Desperately, he stands up in the rocking boat and leaps toward the calmer water, arms reaching forward. Slow motion side angle: the current catches him mid-air and he plunges into the swirling water, the boat tipping behind him.




Shot 5 (11–13s): Underwater, top-down close-up of his face: dark murky water, eyes squeezed shut, cheeks puffed, bubbles escaping from his mouth, grey hair and the red scarf swirling around his face as the current pulls him down.




MATCH CUT: identical framing and head position.




Shot 6 (13–15s): Complete silence. Top-down close-up of the same face in the same position, now lying on a white pillow in a dim bedroom, cold grey-blue morning light, wearing a simple grey undershirt. His eyes snap wide open in terror, he gasps sharply for air, sweat on his forehead, staring at the ceiling, chest heaving.




Style: anamorphic lens, soft diffused overcast light, cool palette of deep greens, turquoise water and blue-grey fog, colors growing darker and more desaturated as the whirlpool forms, cold muted grey-blue tones in the bedroom, the red scarf as the only warm accent, ultra detailed.

Sound: calm river flow, distant birds, his soft humming, then the humming stops; a low gurgling sucking sound growing louder, creaking of the boat, his panicked shouts, a heavy splash, muffled underwater rumble. At the match cut: total silence, then a sharp desperate gasp, heavy breathing and a quiet clock ticking.

Avoid: visible breath vapor, giant fantasy vortex, walls of water, orange or warm tones, golden foliage.
