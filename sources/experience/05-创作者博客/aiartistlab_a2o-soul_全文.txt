Create a 30-second music video featuring ONLY the single person from the reference image, using the source video as the choreography, timing, camera, and editing reference.




SOLO PERFORMER

Use the reference image as the identity reference for the sole performer. Preserve their exact face, hairstyle, clothing, accessories, and body proportions consistently throughout all 30 seconds.




The source video contains multiple people. Select ONE main dancer’s performance and follow that same dancer throughout the entire video. Do not switch between different dancers or combine their movements. Transfer the selected dancer’s full-body choreography, gestures, footwork, head movements, expressions, rhythm, and timing to the reference person.




The reference person performs the choreography alone as the featured solo artist in the music video. Maintain natural body mechanics, physical weight, grounded foot contact, and smooth continuous motion. Reconstruct movements naturally whenever the selected dancer is obscured by other performers.




REMOVE ALL OTHER PEOPLE

Remove every other dancer, background performer, crowd member, and human figure from every shot. Reconstruct the background seamlessly where they previously appeared. Exactly ONE person is present throughout the entire video, including wide shots. No duplicates, clones, backup dancers, additional faces, or human figures on screens or posters.




CAMERA AND MUSIC VIDEO CONTINUITY

Preserve the source video’s original 30-second timeline, shot order, cuts, camera movements, framing, perspective, lighting, color grading, and environment. Integrate the reference person naturally into each shot with matching shadows, reflections, depth of field, and motion blur. During close-ups, show only the reference person. During wide shots, show the same person performing alone within the original setting.




Keep the performance synchronized with the source music and beat. Preserve the original choreography’s energy and musical accents. No frozen poses, repeated motion loops, invented choreography, identity changes, costume changes, or additional cuts.




FINAL RESULT

A complete 30-second solo music video in which the person from the reference image is the only performer, faithfully executing one selected dancer’s choreography from the source video.
