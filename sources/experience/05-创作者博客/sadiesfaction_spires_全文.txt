SCENE CONTEXT

A lone person walks across a vast grassy headland toward two impossible blade-shaped rock spires leaning out of the hills. The shot is about scale and nerve: a tiny human advancing on monumental stone.




ACTIVE REFERENCES

@Image 1: the location — grassy green headland by the sea, two enormous dark curved rock spires like giant thorns leaning in the same direction, pale blue sky, distant ocean horizon on the left. Geometry, materials and atmosphere match the reference 100%.

@image_2: the person. Match their identity, facial features, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, complete outfit and shoes to this reference. Use only the person from this image, not its background. Keep their appearance and clothing consistent throughout the shot.




LOCATION MAP

A wide rolling meadow of short green grass fills the foreground. Behind it, grassy slopes rise to the bases of two colossal gray stone spires: the larger one right of center and closer, the smaller one further left and further away, both curving to needle points high in the sky and leaning screen-left. The sea sits far behind on the left horizon under a clear pale sky.




FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING

First frame: the full landscape is already in view, both spires rising from their hills into the sky. The person is a small full-body figure low in the frame on the meadow, at x ~48%, y ~88%, seen from behind, mid-step, walking away from the camera straight toward the base of the larger spire. They are tiny against the spires, about one-fifteenth of the frame height.




FORMAT MODE

A single continuous shot, no cuts.




OPTICS

84° diagonal field of view, classic wide rectilinear character. The spires fit from grass to needle tips with sky above. The person reads as a small but clear figure. Lens locked for the whole shot.




CAMERA

Locked-off camera at standing eye height on the meadow, behind the person, level with no roll. It never moves.




ACTION TIMING

Throughout the entire shot, they walk away from the camera in one continuous, determined stride toward the larger spire — a brave, purposeful walk: chin up, shoulders squared, arms swinging with intent, steady unbroken rhythm, slightly faster than a stroll. They shrink with distance while the spires remain fixed in the frame, and by the end they are a small resolute figure deep in the meadow, still advancing. They never slow, never hesitate, never look back.




PHYSICS

Each step lands with weight and drive: grass compresses under their shoes. Clothing moves naturally with the stride according to the garments and fabrics in @image_2. Their hair responds naturally to the coastal breeze according to its length and style in the reference. Wind moves through the meadow grass in slow waves. The spires are absolutely static and solid. High thin clouds drift almost imperceptibly.




LIGHTING

Clear midday coastal daylight from high screen-right, soft shadows on the grass, the spires' eastern faces lit and their western faces in cool shade. Natural sky gradient.




AUDIO

STYLE

Photorealistic fine-art landscape photography in motion, muted natural palette, fine film grain, calm surreal minimalism with quiet heroism.




QUALITY

Sharp clarity, stable picture, natural colours, controlled highlights in the sky. Stable natural motion cadence with subtle motion blur only on the person's stride. No ghosting, no flicker.
