Her long dark hair is soaked and whipping with every movement, heavy wet strands lagging behind her head turns and snapping forward on impacts, water flicking off the ends in arcs, loose strands sticking to her cheeks and forehead.

@opponent is bare-chested in loose dark athletic shorts, full-body traditional Japanese irezumi tattoos across chest, back and both arms, dense black linework with deep indigo and muted crimson fills, rain and sweat running off his skin catching the firelight. SHOT 04 — 6.97–9.05

First real impact. @heroine slips inside and lands a hard counter, a burst of water exploding off the point of contact, @opponent's head snapping back and his soaked hair whipping across his face, amusement wiped away and replaced by surprise. Spray hanging in the firelight.




SHOT 05 — 9.05–11.65

@opponent drives forward angry now, jaw set, hair flying wide. @heroine absorbs it and answers heavier, driving him back into a wet pine trunk, bark cracking and a sheet of water sluicing off the impact, his eyes wide, teeth bared. Handheld medium shot, mud churning underfoot.




SHOT 06 — 11.65–14.05

@opponent throws a desperate high kick with a raw open-mouthed shout, water flung in an arc off his leg. @heroine catches it on the forearm without flinching, expression flat and methodical, spray bursting off both arms. He staggers back off balance, splashing into a puddle. Low angle.




SHOT 07 — 14.05–16.42

Extreme close-up on @opponent's eyes, soaked hair plastered across his face with water running off the strands, rain and flickering firelight moving over him, embers drifting past. The mockery is gone, only cold recognition that she will not stop. One hard swallow, jaw setting.




SHOT 08 — 16.42–19.42

Rapid exchange, @opponent swinging wild now, form falling apart, hair whipping in every direction, water flying off both fighters on every strike. @heroine stays composed, slipping and countering, mud and wet leaves kicking up around their feet, spray hanging in the air between them.




SHOT 09 — 19.42–22.88

@heroine lands a heavy blow with a burst of spray, @opponent dropping to one knee in the mud, hands braced, muddy water splashing up around him, hair hanging in wet ropes over his face. She circles slowly, spent but unshaken. He forces himself back upright, water pouring off him.




SHOT 10 — 22.88–25.08

@opponent comes forward one last time, exhausted, more will than strength, splashing through the standing water. @heroine sets her feet, no expression at all, hair and gi soaked and heavy. The beat before it lands, flames surging behind them.




SHOT 11 — 25.08–29.38

@heroine lands a spinning kick, water and mud exploding off the contact, @opponent's body going slack in the air and hitting the ground hard, throwing up a huge splash, sliding to a stop, face turned up into the rain, hair fanned out in the mud, eyes half open. @heroine stands over him, chest heaving, hair dripping, adrenaline draining out of her face leaving only exhaustion and hollow grief, the village burning and collapsing behind her. Slow push-in.
