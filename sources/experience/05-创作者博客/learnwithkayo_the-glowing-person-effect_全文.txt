I recreated the viral glowing person effect on a rooftop in Dubai. Three images and one Seedance 2.5 prompt. Every prompt and reference I used is below.




Step 1: Create your character sheet


Take three photos of yourself: front, side profile and full body. A plain wall and even light work best.




Don't skip the side profile. It's what keeps your face consistent once you start moving in the video. Here is the prompt I used ⬇️

Create an ultra-photorealistic horizontal 16:9 character sheet of the person in @Image 1, @Image 2 and @Image 3. Keep their exact face, hairstyle, build and outfit from the photos, including any hat or accessories. Four views on a seamless light grey studio background: full-body front view, full-body side profile, full-body back view, and a large close portrait on the right. Soft, even studio lighting.

Step 2: Create your character sheet

Now create the glowing person. No references needed, just this prompt.

My first version looked like a neon outline from a video game. What fixed it: describing her as a solid, blown-out shape with no outline and no details, and making her a real light source.

Create an ultra-photorealistic horizontal 16:9 character sheet of a girl made entirely of light, on a seamless dark charcoal studio background.




She is a simple, flat silhouette of a slim girl with a short bob and a plain knee-length dress, filled with pure overexposed white light, as if the film burned out where she stands. Nothing inside the shape: no face, no features, no fabric, no body lines, not see-through. A soft cool blue-white halo bleeds out from her edges and lights the floor around her.




Views: large close side profile portrait on the left, then full-body front view, full-body side profile, full-body back view, and a seated back view on a low concrete block.




Shot on 35mm film, heavy grain, soft halation. Not CGI, not a hologram, no neon outline, no sparkles, no particles.

Step 2: The location

Then your location: an empty rooftop above your city. No people, they come in the next step.

Swap in your city and a landmark. I used Dubai and the Burj Khalifa. Keep the film look lines: my first city came out as a crisp HDR postcard and looked fake.

Create a horizontal 16:9 photo of an empty rooftop high above [your city] at dusk. No people.




The camera sits low behind a plain weathered concrete ledge that crosses the lower part of the frame, big and out of focus. Further out, the corner of a rough concrete parapet in the middle of the frame. Beyond it the city spreads out far below, mostly dark, with scattered small warm lights and soft streaks of traffic. [Your landmark] stands far away in the haze. A small hazy moon in the deep blue sky, a band of pink and violet on the horizon.




Look: a snapshot on a cheap 35mm film camera. Everything soaked in deep indigo blue, faded colors, lifted blacks, heavy grain, soft focus, vignette. Not HDR, not crisp, not a postcard.

Step 2: The Video







Upload all three images to Seedance 2.5 and paste this prompt. Nine shots in ten seconds, cut like a memory.

If she looks stiff in the air, keep the line about her moving like a real person, weightless, with her hair and dress moving in the wind. That fixed it for me.

References: @Image 1 your character sheet, @Image 2 the glowing person sheet, @Image 3 the location




@Image 1 is the person: their face, build and outfit exactly as in @Image 1. @Image 2 is the girl: a flat silhouette of a girl with a short bob in a plain dress, filled with solid overexposed white light, with a soft blue-white halo bleeding off her edges. @Image 3 is the location and the look: the concrete rooftop and the city skyline at blue hour. Use @Image 1 and @Image 2 only for the characters, not their backgrounds or layout.




Camera Simulation: 35mm film look, handheld, cinematic framing. Deep indigo blue night, soft grain, halation around every light, shallow depth of field in the close-ups.




The girl is a real light source: her light falls on the person, the concrete and the ground around her. She never has a face, features, an outline or inner lines, and is never see-through. She moves like a real person, relaxed and weightless, never stiff: her short hair and the hem of her dress move softly in the wind.




Single 10-second sequence. 9 shots, hard cuts.




00:00-00:01.1 · Laughing

Low angle. The person sits on the concrete ledge laughing and looking up at her. She stands right next to them on the right, life-size and glowing, her light falling on their clothes.




00:01.1-00:02.4 · Walking the Edge

Wide from the side. The person walks along the narrow top of the parapet with arms out, smiling with their head tipped back. She floats beside them over the city, legs bent playfully, arms out like she is flying.




00:02.4-00:03.3 · Hands

Close-up. Their hand holds her glowing hand above the blurry concrete ledge. In this shot her light is warm gold and lights their fingers. City lights blur behind.




00:03.3-00:04.3 · Together

Wide from behind, the camera low behind the out-of-focus ledge in the foreground. They sit on the corner of the parapet facing the skyline, the person's arm around her, her head on their shoulder. The moon above, a pink line on the horizon.




00:04.3-00:05.3 · From Far Away

Extreme wide. The person sits alone at the far end of the parapet. High above the city she floats upright in the sky, small and glowing, the moon above her.




00:05.3-00:06.4 · Reaching

Close-up against the deep blue sky. The person's hand reaches up and her glowing hand reaches down from above until their fingertips almost touch.




00:06.4-00:07.5 · Looking Up

Over the person's shoulder from behind, the back of their head soft in the foreground. She floats above them toward the moon, her hair and dress drifting, one hand reaching down.




00:07.5-00:08.4 · Her Reflection

Extreme close-up of the person's eye. Her small glowing figure is reflected in their pupil.




00:08.4-00:10.0 · Last Glow

Wide from behind and slightly to the side. The person sits on the ledge looking up. She drifts slowly upward into the night sky, and her light fades as she rises.




SOUND

Soft wind and a distant city hum throughout, soft laughter at the start, a faint airy shimmer as her light fades at the end.




POSITIVE LOCKS

The person's face, build and outfit match @Image 1 in every shot.

The girl stays a solid figure of white light like @Image 2 in every shot, warm gold only in the Hands shot, and moves naturally, never stiff.

The rooftop and skyline match @Image 3.

No music, just sound effects. No dialogue, no subtitles, no on-screen text.
