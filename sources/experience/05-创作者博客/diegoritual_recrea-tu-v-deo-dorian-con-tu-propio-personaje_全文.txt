A. Haz la foto (si es tu propia foto): de frente, con una pared lisa de fondo, luz natural suave que entre por una ventana, el teléfono a la altura de los ojos y sin filtros. Debe ser tu propio rostro o uno que tengas permiso para utilizar.

Usa este PROMPT junto con tu foto adjunta para generar tu FACE SHEET

Two-panel face reference card of the exact same person as @image1, shot on a phone against a plain off-white seamless studio background. Copy their face, facial features, skin tone, hair colour, hair length and hairstyle exactly from @image1 with no changes. One person only, identical in both panels.

BACKGROUND AND LAYOUT: one continuous soft off-white seamless field behind both panels, a shade below pure white so the hair edge, jaw and shoulder stay crisp against it. The two panels sit directly against each other with no gap, no divider line, and no border around the outside.

CROP — identical in both panels: tight. The top of the head runs off the top edge of the frame, the chin sits near the bottom edge, and the face fills almost the whole panel. Same head size in both.

LEFT PANEL: frontal close-up, head and shoulders squared straight to camera, chin level, no tilt. Eyes looking straight down the lens.
RIGHT PANEL: strict side profile facing frame right, chin level, same head size. The nose, lips and chin read as a clean silhouette. The ear is fully visible with the hair behind it.

EXPRESSION — identical in both panels: relaxed and neutral, no smile. Jaw loose, lips softly parted with the edge of the upper teeth just visible.

LIGHT — identical in both panels: one very large soft source close, high and directly in front, with soft bounce filling both sides. Bright and open with no hard shadow, but gentle real modelling under the brow, nose, lower lip and jaw. Matching catchlights in both eyes.

A real wet gloss on the lips with a sharp highlight on the lower lip. A soft natural sheen on the forehead, nose and cheekbones.

SKIN — real and unretouched: fine vellus hair catching the light along the jaw and hairline, faint natural unevenness across the nose and cheeks, any small marks kept exactly as they are in @image1, never darkened or added to. Natural facial asymmetry preserved. All of it low and quiet — never rough, pitted, blotchy or aged.

HAIR: exactly as in @image1, a little undone, with a few fine strands loose across the forehead and temples.

EYES: the sharpest thing in both panels, with visible iris detail, a soft limbal ring, a moisture line on the lower lid, separated lashes, both eyes converging on one point in the frontal panel.

Plain black crew-neck t-shirt, only the neckline visible. No earrings, no necklace. Light natural makeup only.

Genuine high resolution, crisp edge detail in every hair and lash. Neutral accurate colour, low saturation, soft open contrast. No grain, no noise, no haze, no background blur. Two real phone photographs of the same person from the same few seconds.

NEGATIVE: head tilt, off-lens eyeline, loose wide crop with space above the head; rough or blotchy skin; grain; any divider line, seam or border; two different people.
