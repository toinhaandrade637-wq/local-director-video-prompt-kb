An AI-made character called Jean Phil picked up roughly a quarter of a million followers in under a month. Lil Miquela, the best-known virtual creator, is estimated to earn up to $12 million a year from campaigns with brands like BMW, Prada and Calvin Klein, including a Calvin Klein ad next to Bella Hadid.




Most people assume building something like that takes a powerful PC, code, endless prompts and ten apps stitched together. It doesn't. In Higgsfield it's a few taps and one upload, and it works from your phone. The demo build takes about 30 seconds. Here's the full process: build the character, copy a viral Reel's motion onto her, then grow the page toward the first brand deal.

Before You Start

Everything happens on one Higgsfield page, AI Influencer, which combines two tools:




The AI Influencer builder designs your character from simple trait picks instead of a prompt.
Genjutsu copies the movement, camera and timing of any clip onto that character, frame for frame.

Build the character once and keep her. Every Reel you post after this reuses the same face, so the page reads as one real person, not a new AI face every day.

You don't need a single prompt for the core build. The prompt cards below are optional extras that run in Supercomputer: finding trends, doing the whole build in one message, planning content and pitching brands.

Create your AI influencer

Stage 1 — Build Your Influencer
Pick the Look

What it does: Turns a few taps into a finished character: portrait and full-body shots on a white background, no prompt needed.

Open AI Influencer and hit Generate Your Influencer.
Tap through the traits: face shape, eyes, hairstyle and hair color, build, style (Streetstyle, Y2K, Sporty, Goth, Retro and more), small details like freckles or a beauty mark, accessories.
Want a version of yourself? Add your own photo as the starting point.
Generate, and keep the one that looks like someone you'd actually follow.

Match the style to the niche you'll post in. A Sporty character fits fitness trends, a Streetstyle one fits fashion.

Stage 2 — Copy a Viral Reel

You don't need a content idea. Take a Reel that already went viral and let your influencer perform it.

Find the Trend

What it does: Researches what's spreading on Reels right now and keeps only the clips that work well as motion references.

Find 10 Instagram Reels and TikTok trends from the last 7 days that would work as motion references for an AI influencer in the [fashion / fitness / lifestyle] niche. Only keep clips with one person, the full body in frame, arms and hands visible, clear movement and a length of 3–30 seconds. For each trend: what happens in the clip, why it's spreading, and a short on-screen hook for my version.

Pick the Motion

What it does: Tells Genjutsu which movement, camera move and timing to copy onto your character.




Pick a motion from the built-in trend library, or upload a 3–30-second clip. The best references have:




one person in frame, full body, arms and hands visible
clear, readable movement: a dance, a walk, a pose sequence
steady framing that's close to your character's shot

Upload clips you shot yourself or have the right to use. The built-in trend library is the safe shortcut.

Generate the Reel

What it does: Genjutsu puts your influencer into the clip: same moves, same camera, same timing, her face.




Hit Generate and download a vertical video ready to post. Want variations of a take that already works? Swap the outfit, product or location and Genjutsu keeps the motion and camera intact.

What it does: Has Supercomputer design the character and animate her to your reference clip in one conversation, if you'd rather describe her than tap.

Stage 3 — Grow the Page
Plan the First 30 Days

What it does: Writes a month of daily posts for your influencer: what to film, the hook on screen, the caption and hashtags.

My AI influencer is [NAME], a [AGE]-year-old [NICHE] creator. Her vibe in three words: [VIBE]. Plan her first 30 days on Instagram Reels, one post a day, mostly trend remakes plus a few original formats. For each day give the trend or format, an on-screen hook of up to 7 words, the caption and 5 hashtags. Add one weekly recurring series she can become known for.

A few rules that keep the page growing:

Same face, every post. Consistency is what makes people follow a character instead of scrolling past one clip.
Post daily. Trend remakes take minutes, so volume is cheap. Use it.
Label it. Turn on Instagram's AI label when you post. The biggest virtual creators are openly virtual, and it keeps the account safe.
Land the First Brand Deal

What it does: Finds brands that fit your niche and writes a short, honest pitch for each.

[NAME] is an AI influencer with [FOLLOWERS] followers on Instagram, posting daily [NICHE] Reels with an average of [VIEWS] views. Find 15 small and mid-size brands in [NICHE] that already work with creators or virtual influencers. For each, write a short DM pitch: who she is, why her audience fits the brand, one concrete Reel idea featuring their product, and a starter offer. Be upfront that she is AI-generated.
