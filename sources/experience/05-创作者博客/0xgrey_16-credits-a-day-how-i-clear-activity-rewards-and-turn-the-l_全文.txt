Activity Rewards Season 1 runs until December 12. I clear the daily tasks every day and wanted them to produce something I'd use anyway, at the lowest cost I could get. Here is the exact setup, with the prompts, and what the generations turned into.




What counts toward the tasks

The daily tasks are worth 45 XP: 10 images (+10), 3 videos (+15), one download (+5) and one generation through Claude or ChatGPT (+15, optional, it doesn't affect your streak).

What I learned the hard way:

Only generations paid with credits count. Unlimited and free generations don't.
Only the models listed under the "i" icon count. For images: Nano Banana 2, 2.1 and Pro, Seedream 5.0 Pro, GPT Image 2.0 and 2.5. For video: Seedance 2.5 and 2.0, Genjutsu, Minimax H3 and H3 Max, Kling 3.0, Kling Motion Control. Kling 3.0 Turbo is not on the list. I lost a video task to it on day 2.
The download only counts through the Download button in Assets. Saving the file from a direct link doesn't count.




The cheapest set that counts: 16 credits

10 images, 2.5 credits. GPT Image 2.5, Flare, quality Low, 1K costs 0.25 credits per image. Low quality looks rough on photos, so I don't make photos with it. I make line-art sprites on a pure black background. They look clean even at Low, and black cuts out perfectly later.

What the sprites became

I run a channel that explains how payments and security work under the hood. The day's sprites became a 42-second explainer on how an authenticator app shows a code with no internet: the phone in airplane mode opens the video, the key is the shared secret from the QR code, the stopwatch is the 30-second window, and the server checks the same six digits.




Seven sprites cost 1.75 credits. Black backgrounds came off with a simple brightness key, no background removal needed. The voiceover is ElevenLabs inside Higgsfield, about 0.15 credits a line. The reel itself is in Russian for my audience, so in this post you only see what it was built from.

Let Claude do it

The Claude task is optional, but it's 15 XP for something I'd do anyway. Higgsfield has an MCP connector for Claude, so I give Claude one message and it runs all 13 generations itself:

Close today's Higgsfield Activity Rewards tasks: 10 images in GPT Image 2.5 Flare, Low, 1K and 3 videos in Kling 3.0 std, 3 seconds, sound off, no Turbo. Images: line-art sprites on pure black for my next reel about <topic>. Then show me how many credits you spent.

Then I open the rewards page, download one generation and press Claim. Two minutes a day.

Why 16 credits a day is worth it

The rewards track gives 100, 300, 1,000 and 10,000 credits at levels 2, 6, 11 and 14: 11,400 credits. Levels 4, 8 and 10 give 85 community rating points instead. Most XP comes from streak bonuses: +100 on day 3, +150 on day 7, +250 on day 10, +500 on day 14, +700 on day 21, +1,000 on day 28, +2,000 on day 35. There is also +4,000 on day 50, but if you start now, day 50 falls after the season ends.




Level 14 needs 5,000 XP. At 45 XP a day you have around 3,960 XP on day 28, and only the day-35 bonus pushes you over. The season ends December 12, so day one of your streak has to be November 7 or 8 at the latest, with no missed days. The day closes at 00:00 UTC.

Then there's the community rating. At 100 rating points Higgsfield gives another 5,000 credits. The season gives you 85; a profile (+3), Discord (+5), the Academy beginner course (+15), your first remix (+2) and your first open project (+4) add 29 more. That's 114. Skip the Academy course and you stop at 99.




Over the season that's about 800 credits spent and up to 16,400 back. Bonus credits do expire (my level-6 credits last about a month), so plan to use them.

Numbers as of October 26, 2026. The program is in beta, so check your own rewards page before you start.
