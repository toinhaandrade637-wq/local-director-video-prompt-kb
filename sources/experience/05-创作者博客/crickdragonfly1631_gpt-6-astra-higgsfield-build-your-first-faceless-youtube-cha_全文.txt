Step 2: Pick One Niche

Start with a subject you can explain clearly and return to for more than one video.

The tutorial considers finance, technology, and education, then chooses edutainment. Its worked example is a documentary about the Great Pyramid.

Finance: explain one everyday money question.
Technology: make a complicated invention understandable.
Education: turn a historical or scientific question into a story.

Study a few successful channels in your niche. Look at the questions they answer, how their openings work, and what keeps the story moving. Use that research to find an original angle of your own.

Understand the Money Before You Scale

The $39,500/month figure comes from the example discussed in the video. It is not a verified result for this workflow or a forecast for a new channel.

RPM means revenue per 1,000 views. To understand the arithmetic, at an assumed $10 RPM, 100,000 views would equal $1,000 in revenue. At an assumed $31 RPM, the same views would equal $3,100. Those are illustrations, before production costs.

You still need to qualify for monetization. As of September 14, 2026, the long-form ads route requires 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 qualified watch hours in the past 12 months, along with other eligibility requirements and a channel review. Check YouTube’s current requirements before applying.

Can AI videos be monetized?

YouTube’s policy focuses on original, authentic content and rules out mass-produced or repetitive material. Using AI tools does not itself establish eligibility. Your research, editorial decisions, and the value of the finished videos still matter. Read the channel monetization policy.
