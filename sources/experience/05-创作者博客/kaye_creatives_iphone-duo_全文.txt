GLITCH LANGUAGE:

All glitches read as failures of a simulated world, not as a screen overlay. No magenta, no purple, no rainbow.

Building glitches come in two families that ALTERNATE: every DIGITAL hit on one building is followed within a second by a MOVEMENT hit on another building, and at any moment from Stage 3 on at least one building of each family is active.

DIGITAL family, fast and hard-edged: (D1) WIREFRAME — a building or large section flickers for 0.2 seconds and becomes glowing bright green wireframe mesh lines over a solid black body, thin lines following its polygon edges, no surfaces between the lines except black, windows and ledges as line geometry; holds 0.5 to 2 seconds, flickers back to solid; (D2) PIXEL SORT — a region pulls upward into thin vertical streaks of its own colors, 5 to 30 meters tall, ragged top; (D3) FRAME TEAR — horizontal strips of a facade slide sideways at different offsets for 0.3 seconds and snap back. A digital glitch may cross object boundaries: a wireframe region can continue from a facade across the sidewalk and up a tree.

MOVEMENT family, heavy and physical, the building stays solid and fully textured while it moves: (M1) SLIDE — an entire building slides 2 to 4 meters sideways or toward the street from its footing as one rigid mass, holds, slides back; (M2) LIFT — a building rises 2 to 3 meters straight up off its foundation, hangs, drops back with a settle; (M3) TILT — a building leans 8 to 12 degrees toward the street as one rigid block and rights itself; (M4) SWAP — two neighboring buildings exchange positions in a single blink, hold for 1 to 2 seconds, blink back; (M5) STEP — a building's floors slide out of alignment like a stack of boxes shoved sideways, each floor offset from the one below, then restack. Movement glitches are slow relative to digital ones — 1 to 3 seconds — with visible mass, a low rumble, dust from the footing, and shadows that move with the building.

Rotation lock: digital and movement hits rotate through their lists (D1, M1, D2, M2, D3, M3, D1, M4, D2, M5 …) so no two consecutive building glitches are the same form and no two consecutive are the same family.
