VIDEO — KARIMA PULLS THE PLANT FROM THE POT, IT SCREAMS (HANDHELD, FILMED BY SOMEONE ELSE)




SCENE CONTEXT: Someone else is filming Karima on their phone, handheld, inside a school greenhouse full of plants and fellow students going about their own business. Karima holds a terracotta pot in one hand — at first we only see the leafy crown of a small root-plant creature poking out of it. The camera operator whip-zooms in on the creature — and it suddenly lets out a loud scream, eyes staying shut the whole time. The scream is so loud and sudden that it startles the person filming immediately, their camera jolting and shaking the instant the sound hits, and within moments the phone falls from their grip.




FORMAT

One continuous shot, 7 seconds, 9:16 vertical, styled exactly like real handheld UGC phone footage — natural phone-camera color science, computational HDR look, faint natural phone-camera softness and digital sharpening rather than a cinematic lens look. No cinematic lens flares, no anamorphic distortion, no artificial film grain — this must read as real, unfiltered phone footage shot by a bystander.




THE THREE MOST IMPORTANT RULES ON THIS SHOT




RULE ONE — THE CREATURE'S EYES ARE ALWAYS CLOSED. From the very first frame to the last, including the moment it screams, the root-creature's eyes remain fully shut. It never opens them, even at the loudest and most violent point of its scream — the scream is expressed entirely through its wide-open mouth, tensed brow-ridge, and thrashing root-arms, never through open eyes.

IF THE CREATURE'S EYES ARE OPEN AT ANY POINT IN THE 7 SECONDS, INCLUDING DURING THE SCREAM, THE GENERATION IS WRONG. THIS IS A HARD FAILURE CONDITION.




RULE TWO — THE BACKGROUND STUDENTS ARE ACTIVELY BUSY AND ONLY REACT THE INSTANT THE SCREAM STARTS. Every background student is doing their own distinct task right up until the scream begins — closely examining a leaf, scooping soil into a pot with a trowel, watering a plant, repotting a seedling — none of them looking at Karima or paying her any attention beforehand. The exact instant the creature's scream begins, they all react together, sharply turning their heads toward the sound and clapping their hands over their ears — the reaction is immediate and simultaneous with the scream's onset, not delayed, and not exaggerated beyond a sharp wince and covered ears.

IF ANY BACKGROUND STUDENT IS IDLE, STATIC, OR ALREADY WATCHING KARIMA BEFORE THE SCREAM, OR IF THEIR EAR-COVERING REACTION LAGS NOTICEABLY BEHIND THE SCREAM'S ONSET, THE GENERATION IS WRONG.




RULE THREE — THE CAMERA SHAKE IS AN IMMEDIATE, DIRECT REACTION TO THE SCREAM'S VOLUME. The instant the creature's scream begins, the handheld camera jolts and shakes hard, as if the operator was physically startled by how loud it is — this is not a delayed or gradual reaction but a sudden, sharp jump in camera movement occurring in the same instant as the scream itself, leading quickly into the phone being dropped.

IF THE CAMERA SHAKE BEGINS NOTICEABLY AFTER THE SCREAM RATHER THAN SIMULTANEOUSLY WITH IT, THE GENERATION IS WRONG.
