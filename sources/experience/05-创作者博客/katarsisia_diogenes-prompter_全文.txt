A technological thriller about the humans hidden behind artificial intelligence.

She lives alone in a cramped suburban apartment surrounded by discarded televisions, radios, cables, obsolete computers and forgotten machines. To anyone watching from the outside, she appears to be trapped in poverty.

But she is not poor.

Every morning, she leaves her analogue refuge and travels to the immaculate headquarters of a powerful artificial intelligence corporation. There, she works as one of its best prompt analysts: a human filter performing tasks that millions of customers believe are handled entirely by AI.

The anomaly

During another monotonous shift, she detects a disturbing pattern hidden across several prompts. Their owner claims to be writing a novel, but the fragments appear to describe something real: a selected woman, a planned hunt and a murder waiting to happen.

Her supervisor orders her to ignore it. The company cannot admit that employees inspect private prompts, and nobody can prove that a fictional project represents a genuine threat.

She investigates anyway.

A digital scavenger

Like Diogenes searching through the physical remains discarded by the city, she searches through digital waste: abandoned instructions, metadata, conversations and behavioural traces. What appears meaningless to the system becomes evidence in her hands.

Her strange domestic life and her professional abilities are not opposites. They are expressions of the same instinct: she keeps what others throw away because she understands that discarded things still contain information.

The meaning of the title

DIOGENES PROMPTER combines two worlds. “Diogenes” evokes accumulation, rejection of conventional status and life among society’s discarded objects. “Prompter” identifies her hidden profession inside the artificial intelligence industry.

She collects what others discard: objects, prompts and secrets.

Visual approach

The film contrasts a warm, overcrowded analogue home with cold corporate architecture, fluorescent offices and anonymous digital systems. Its deliberately slow opening establishes her repetitive private life before the story accelerates into surveillance, infiltration and technological investigation.

VHS distortions connect both worlds and transform digital imperfections into part of the film’s language. The recovered machines surrounding the protagonist are not decoration: they represent memory, resistance and everything modern systems attempt to erase.

Created with artificial intelligence

DIOGENES PROMPTER was conceived as a complete narrative rather than a collection of disconnected AI-generated images. Character continuity, physical geography, recurring objects and cause-and-effect storytelling remain central throughout the film.

The result is a nine-minute thriller about privacy, corporate secrecy, invisible human labour and the uncomfortable possibility that the most dangerous information submitted to an AI may be seen by someone capable of understanding it.
