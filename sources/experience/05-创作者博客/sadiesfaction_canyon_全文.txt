The LOCATION is the main character. Hold an extreme-wide view of both towering green canyon cliffs, the deep narrow water channel, distant vanishing point and broad foreground grass. ACTION — CONTINUOUS FORWARD TRAVEL AND VISIBLE WIND

They make eight to ten distinct, unhurried, purposeful steps and travel several body lengths deeper into the landscape. Their position steadily changes relative to the fixed rocks. No standing in place, walking in place, sliding, idle pose, freeze or sudden teleportation.
