Living room

Empty 90s multi-camera sitcom apartment living room set, bright, warm and cosy,

lived-in and full of personality. A large deep red velvet sofa with soft armrests

faces the camera, mustard and cream throw pillows, a knitted blanket draped over the

back, a low mid-century coffee table with thin tapered legs in front of it on a

geometric striped rug in beige, terracotta and red tones. A red barrel armchair on

one side, a folding wooden chair with woven seat and a leather ottoman in the

foreground. Cream painted walls, one white-painted brick wall, pale honey wood trim

and hardwood floor, open shelves crammed with books, boxes, cameras and a globe,

lots of houseplants in woven baskets, trailing ivy on the shelf, a warm fairy-light

string along the shelving, candles on the table, framed posters, a paper lantern

floor lamp.

It is EVENING: the back wall has two windows with wooden venetian blinds, and beyond

them a deep blue dusk sky with the warm yellow lit windows of the building across

the street. Inside the apartment is bright and warm — multiple warm lamps lit at

once, ceiling light, floor lamp and table lamp, soft even fill light, no dark corners.

On the RIGHT side of the frame: a heavy wooden front door with brass locks and a coat

rack beside it. On the LEFT side of the frame: a wide open archway leading into the

kitchen, cold blue monitor glow spilling out of it onto the floor.

No people, multi-camera sitcom staging, 35mm film grain, 9:16 vertical.

Data center kitchen

90s sitcom kitchen set completely taken over by computers, wide vertical shot.

A kitchen island counter facing camera with a bar stool, behind it a pyramid wall of

12 mismatched monitors — beige CRTs and early flat panels stacked three tiers high on

phone books and cardboard boxes, all glowing with scrolling code. Three beige tower

PCs covered in stickers, an open motherboard on a cutting board, a chunky old laptop

open on a stand, three mechanical keyboards at different heights, trackball, joystick,

headphones on a hook. Tangled cables and daisy-chained power strips taped to the

furniture, stack of floppy disks, desk fan aimed at a tower.

Contrasting cosy kitchen details remain: gingham curtains, ceramic flour and sugar

jars, garlic braid on the wall, cast iron pan on a hook, kitten calendar, a cooking

pot sitting on top of a computer tower, a basil pot on the windowsill, a warm

fairy-light string above the window. Cream walls, white painted brick, pale honey

wood trim and hardwood floor matching the adjoining living room. On the RIGHT side of

the frame, an archway back into the warm-lit living room with the edge of a red sofa

visible. It is EVENING: deep blue dusk visible through the kitchen window.

The kitchen itself is bright and warm — a pendant lamp over the counter, under-cabinet

lighting and a ceiling light all on, soft even fill, no dark corners — mixed with the

cold blue-green glow of the monitors.

No people, no cat. Multi-camera sitcom staging, 35mm film grain, 9:16 vertical.

Shot on 35mm film, bright evenly lit cosy apartment, high-key warm tungsten

characters face camera, subtle film grain, sitcom set brightness.

All dialogue in English.




CHARACTERS —

WOMAN:  [tag your character sheet here] early 20s, long straight dark-brown hair with centre parting, thick

natural eyebrows, 90s brown lipstick. Oversized chunky ribbed knit sweater with

horizontal stripes in purple, mustard, orange, dark green and burgundy, dropped

shoulders, hem below the hips, midriff fully covered, sleeves pulled over her

hands. High-waisted baggy light-wash mom jeans, black high-top canvas sneakers,

stacked beaded bracelets and a scrunchie on her left wrist, thin gold choker,

red nail polish. Speaks American English.

CAT: @图片 2 @图片 3 photorealistic real chubby grey-blue Scottish Fold, small folded ears

pressed flat, large round copper-orange eyes, round grumpy face, plush dense

fur. Burgundy red baseball cap worn backwards so the folded ears stick out,

oversized red and black plaid flannel shirt worn open over its fur with rolled

sleeves, rectangular thin-framed blue-light computer glasses. Real cat anatomy

and proportions, four legs, sits on its haunches with front paws on the

keyboard, NOT anthropomorphic, no human arms, no human shoulders. Speaks with

an elegant posh British accent.




SET —  @图片 4 Cosy lived-in apartment. Living room with a deep red velvet sofa facing

camera, mustard and cream pillows, knitted blanket, low mid-century coffee

table, geometric striped rug, cream walls, white painted brick, pale honey wood

trim, open shelves crammed with books and clutter, houseplants in woven baskets,

warm fairy lights, candles. Heavy wooden front door with brass locks on the

RIGHT. Wide archway on the LEFT into a bright kitchen @图片 5 taken over by a pyramid wall of 12 beige CRT and flat-panel monitors glowing with scrolling code,

tangled cables, three mechanical keyboards, beige tower PCs, with cosy kitchen

details still visible: gingham curtains, ceramic jars, a cooking pot sitting on

top of a computer tower, basil on the windowsill.




0:00–0:03 — WIDE SHOT, static. The woman enters through the front door on the

RIGHT carrying a large brown paper grocery bag, baguette and greens sticking

out. She pushes the door shut with her foot, takes two steps in, turns her head

to the LEFT toward the kitchen archway. Her eyes widen, her jaw drops. The bag

slips from her hands and hits the floor, oranges rolling across the rug.




0:03–0:06 — SLOW PUSH-IN LEFT through the archway into the kitchen. The cat in

the backwards cap and glasses types furiously across three keyboards, tail

flicking a mouse, tugging a cable with its teeth, scrolling code reflected in

its lenses, the monitor wall glowing behind it.

[LAUGH TRACK: big burst + applause]




0:06–0:09 — MEDIUM TWO-SHOT. She storms up to the counter, arms crossed,

bracelets visible where the sleeve rides up.

WOMAN (annoyed): "What are you doing? I need to cook dinner!"




0:09–0:12 — The cat slowly turns its head to camera, smug, looking over the top

of its glasses.

CAT (elegant British accent): "I'm vibecoding. Leave me alone."

It turns dismissively back to the screen.

[LAUGH TRACK]




0:12–0:15 — She jabs a finger at the monitor wall.

WOMAN (firmer): "Okay, clear all this junk out of here! I want to cook dinner!"




0:15–0:18 — CLOSE-UP on the cat. It turns its whole body toward her.

CAT (amused, posh): "You? Can you even cook?"

It throws its head back in exaggerated laughter, one front paw clutching its

belly, the other slapping the counter, cap knocked sideways over one ear,

glasses sliding down its muzzle.

[LAUGH TRACK: over the cat's laugh]




0:18–0:21 — WIDE on the monitor wall. A thin wisp of grey smoke, one small

spark pops, the central screen goes black, then all the other monitors shut off

one by one in a cascade. The cold blue glow disappears, the kitchen left in even

warm lamp light — still bright, just warmer. The cat freezes mid-laugh, mouth

open, the blue reflections in its lenses going dark, a tiny curl of smoke rising

from the brim of its cap. No explosion, no fire, just a quiet burnout.

[LAUGH TRACK: long "ooooh" then a burst]




0:21–0:27 — MEDIUM SHOT. She smirks, one eyebrow raised, nudges a rolled-away

orange toward herself with the toe of her sneaker and picks it up.

WOMAN (calm and satisfied): "Right. I'm going back to the store. In fifteen

minutes I want all this junk gone."

She turns and walks RIGHT across the living room to the front door, pauses,

glances back over her shoulder to the left, exits and slams the door. The top

monitor on the stack wobbles and tips over from the impact.




0:27–0:30 — CLOSE-UP on the cat staring at the closed door. It mockingly mouths

her words with its tongue slightly out, then mutters aloud.

CAT (sulking under its breath): "Blah, blah, blah... 'all this junk'..."

It straightens its crooked cap with one paw with wounded dignity, nudges its

glasses back up, turns to the dead black monitor and presses a single key.

Nothing happens. FREEZE FRAME.

[LAUGH TRACK + applause + upbeat 90s sitcom outro sting]
