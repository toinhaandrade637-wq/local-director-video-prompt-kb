A giant bucket of dry spaghetti, a helicopter and a pool of molten lava. Here is how I made this surreal 15-second reel in Higgsfield using a character sheet and Seedance 2.5.

1. Make your character reference

Upload a clear photo of yourself to GPT Image 2.5. Generate a simple two-column sheet: a large face close-up and a full-body view in a black flight suit, gloves and aviation headset. Check your likeness before moving on.

Keep this reference neutral. I discarded helicopter scene images because they placed the lava too far away and looked artificial. The character sheet supplies identity and clothing; the video prompt supplies the setting.

2. Set up the video

Choose Seedance 2.5 with image references (Omni Reference). Attach your approved character sheet as @image1. Set 15 seconds, 9:16, 1080p, audio on. Use the sheet as an identity reference, not as the opening frame.

3. Make the lava unmistakable

My first attempt looked like pasta landing on black volcanic rock. The revised prompt specifies a continuous, bubbling red-orange liquid pool, filling most of the downward view, with black rock confined to the outer rim. The pasta must visibly hit liquid and create ripples.

The pour and eruption share one fixed point. Dry spaghetti falls as rigid sticks; only the erupting cooked spaghetti becomes flexible. The giant bucket should visibly empty.

4. Keep the payoff on time

0–3s: show the bucket, nod and turn. 3–7.5s: pour. 8–10s: pause. 10–13.5s: cooked spaghetti grows and erupts. 13.5–15s: noodles flood the helicopter.

Generate rotor noise, wind, pasta rattles, lava bubbling and wet noodle slaps natively. No music or dialogue. Timing and physics are prompt targets, so inspect the result and regenerate if a key beat is missing.

One continuous photorealistic handheld phone shot from INSIDE an open-door helicopter hovering only several metres above a broad POOL OF LIQUID MOLTEN LAVA. The red-orange incandescent pool lies immediately below the doorway. Its surface has moving viscous currents, glowing folds, swelling bubbles and liquid ripples. Black rock is confined to the narrow distant crater rim. When looking down, at least 80% of the exterior view is glowing liquid. The pasta target is open liquid, with no rock or crust beneath it. Show molten fluid movement before pouring. Bright daylight, believable phone exposure, natural skin, subtle warm bounce from lava. No cinematic grade, excessive bokeh or studio lighting.




Preserve the exact face, hair, skin tone, age and build from @Image 1. One man in black flight suit, black gloves and aviation headset. No character-sheet layout or duplicate people. Camera belongs to another passenger. Keep door frame and helicopter floor visible as spatial anchors.




One MASSIVE red industrial bucket, about 65cm tall and 55cm wide, full of normal thin straight 25cm-long DRY spaghetti. Realistic weight and two-handed support. Dry rods slide over the rim in uneven bundles, catch briefly, separate and tumble downward at varied angles. Visible air gaps, no liquid-like stream or endless strands. Bucket empties.




0–2s: Medium shot seated at doorway holding bucket. Look at camera and give one confident closed-mouth nod. The bubbling red-orange liquid pool is already visible close below.

2–3s: Turn and tip bucket toward open liquid center.

3–7.5s: Dump all pasta. Camera tilts down following it, threshold remains visible. Dry sticks land IN LIQUID at ONE fixed point slightly left of center. Show small viscous orange splashes, surface depression and ripples around impact. Early sticks land during the pour; final ones by 7.5s.

7.5–8s: Empty bucket. A golden pasta patch sits partly submerged at that same point, molten red liquid surrounding it on all sides.

8–10s: Two-second suspense pause on the same bubbling liquid patch. No cut or relocation.

10s: First pale cooked spaghetti curls rise FROM THAT EXACT IMPACT POINT.

10–12s: Huge mound of glossy pale-gold cooked spaghetti swells from the liquid, flexible individual food strands and light steam.

12–13.5s: Massive cooked-spaghetti geyser shoots toward camera from the SAME POINT. Heavy tangles and loose strands have believable inertia. Red molten pool remains visible around eruption base.

13.5–15s: Wet spaghetti slaps into doorway, covers headset and shoulders, drapes across frame, piles into cabin and splats lens. Same man recoils without speaking. Handheld shake, noodles fill frame. No injury or aircraft crash.




Same camera, same pool, same impact point throughout. No eruption from another location. The transformation is fantastical but materials and motion should look real. Native sound only: rotor, wind, dry pasta rattles, lava bubbling and hissing, eruption rush and wet noodle slaps. NO MUSIC, dialogue, narration, captions, logos or watermark. Highest priority: unmistakable bubbling RED-ORANGE LIQUID MOLTEN POOL, not black rocky ground.
