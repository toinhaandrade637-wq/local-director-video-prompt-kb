Hey! Here is full workflow of the reel: the exact prompts, the assets and the order we ran them in.

We filmed Adil at his desk with one static camera. From that single clip we changed everything else with Genjutsu and Seedance 2.5 on Higgsfield: the camera angle, the light, the location, his outfit, the props and the color. 




Below is the full step-by-step guide, with every prompt we used in the video.




How It Works
Shoot one take. One wide, locked-off shot with clean audio and the whole person in frame. That is the only footage you actually need.
Work in chunks of up to 30 seconds. Seedance 2.5 takes a video reference of up to 30 seconds, so we cut the take into short pieces and ran each tool on its own piece.
Change one thing per generation. New camera, new light, new outfit, new prop: one change per pass, then chain the passes. Every prompt locks the performance (second N of the output equals second N of the original), so lips, hands and timing stay 1:1 and you can cut back to the real take on any frame.
Let Claude write the long prompt. Describe the shot in a few lines, for example "after 2 s the camera orbits behind him and lands on his right profile at 7.5 s", and ask Claude to turn it into a full Seedance 2.5 prompt that locks the person, the room and the timing. Every prompt below came out of that loop, and each one ends with a list of failures to avoid, written after the takes that didn't work.
