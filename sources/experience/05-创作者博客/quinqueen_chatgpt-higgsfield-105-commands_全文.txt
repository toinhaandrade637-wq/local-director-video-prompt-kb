中文版

ChatGPT × Higgsfield（HVL） 可以讓你在對話中用自然語言或 /command 製作圖片、影片、廣告、動態設計，亦可連接 After Effects、Blender、Premiere Pro 等桌面軟件。

本教學收錄 105 個入口：36 個功能指令、45 個圖片預設、18 個影片預設、4 個模板庫入口，以及 2 個已確認 Viral 模板。

如何在 ChatGPT 設定 Higgsfield
打開 ChatGPT 或 Codex 的 Apps／Plugins 管理頁。
搜尋並安裝或啟用 Higgsfield。
按畫面提示連接你的 Higgsfield 帳戶。
開啟新對話，在輸入框選擇或 @ 提及 Higgsfield。
輸入「列出可用的 Higgsfield commands」測試連接。
如需控制桌面軟件，先輸入相應設定指令，例如 /use-after-effects。
建議 Prompt 公式

指令＋素材＋用途＋畫面比例＋時長＋動作＋風格＋必須保留內容＋輸出要求

例：/floatingfall。使用我上載的人物照及三張產品圖，製作 9:16 社交媒體短片。人物向後跌落，產品在空中漂浮；Logo 必須清晰，保持人物樣貌一致，輸出 720p 預覽。

使用前要知道
指令只係入口，仍應寫清楚主體、用途、比例、風格與輸出。
圖片及影片預設不同；例如 /unboxing 在本清單屬於圖片預設。
Viral 與 Marketing Studio 會持續更新，名稱可能新增、下架或改動。
105 是本文收錄數量，並非 Higgsfield 全平台永久總數。

English Version

ChatGPT × Higgsfield (HVL) lets you create images, videos, ads, and motion design through natural-language prompts or a specific /command. It can also connect to desktop tools such as After Effects, Blender, and Premiere Pro.

This guide documents 105 entries: 36 functional commands, 45 image presets, 18 video presets, four gallery entry points, and two confirmed Viral templates.

Set Up Higgsfield in ChatGPT
Open the Apps or Plugins manager in ChatGPT or Codex.
Search for and install or enable Higgsfield.
Follow the on-screen steps to connect your Higgsfield account.
Start a new conversation and select or @mention Higgsfield.
Test the connection by asking: “List the available Higgsfield commands.”
For a desktop app, begin with its setup command, such as /use-after-effects.
Recommended Prompt Formula

Command + assets + purpose + aspect ratio + duration + motion + style + protected details + delivery format

Example: /floatingfall. Use my uploaded portrait and three product images to create a 9:16 social video. The subject falls backward while the products float in midair. Keep each logo legible and preserve the subject’s identity. Deliver a 720p preview.

Before You Start
A command is an entry point; still specify the subject, purpose, aspect ratio, style, and output.
Image and video presets are different. For example, /unboxing is an image preset in this list.
Viral and Marketing Studio libraries change over time.
105 is the number documented here, not a permanent platform-wide total.
