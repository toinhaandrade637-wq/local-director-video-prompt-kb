SCENE CONTEXT

24 seconds, 16:9, a controlled twelve-shot cinematic sequence. A tiny kitten knight challenges Kaye and Bob on a foggy stone bridge. As they charge toward each other, Bob's weary expression melts into affection and five brief memories of the kitten interrupt the action. Return to the present-day charge for the final wide shot. Follow the exact timed order below.




ACTIVE REFERENCES

@character_sheet — KAYE: the 20-year-old, 165 cm female knight. Exact identity, dark jaw-length bob, silver plate armour, sword and ivory satin cape from this sheet; 100% matches the reference.

@Image 2 — BOB: the grey Scottish Fold knight with folded ears, copper-orange eyes, blue rectangular glasses, engraved silver armour, dark-red tabard with gold trim and ivory cape. 100% matches the reference.

@Image 3 — THE KITTEN: the tiny fluffy grey-beige kitten with huge dark eyes, pink nose, pale-pink ear bow, dark riveted armour, linen cape and oversized long sword. 100% matches the reference.

@Image 4 — THE BRIDGE: wet cobbles, stone parapets, weathered angel statues, dim iron lanterns, gothic castle towers and a deep fog-filled gorge.

@Image 5 — THE CORRIDOR: vaulted gothic stone passage, worn flagstones, leaded windows, heavy wooden doors, candles and iron lanterns.

@Image 7 — THE MARSH: grassy banks and a winding still creek leading toward the distant castle on its rocky mount.

@Image 6 — THE SWORD HILL: damp moss, immense swords planted in the earth, a tall distant monument and drifting grey mist.




Character sheets control appearance only; discard their white backgrounds and studio lighting. Location references control geography, materials and atmosphere; use the camera positions specified below.




BLOCKING AND CONTINUITY

In the present, the kitten charges from screen-left toward screen-right; Kaye and Bob charge from screen-right toward screen-left. Frontal shots preserve their respective travel directions in world space. Bob stays beside Kaye, his head only knee-high. Bob is 45 cm tall upright; the kitten is 18 cm tall upright and never grows. All three keep their reference costumes. Bob keeps his blue glasses in every shot; the kitten keeps his bow and full armour.




Both cats run upright on two hind legs throughout the present-day confrontation. Only the remembered bridge race uses a real four-legged feline gallop. Front paws remain feline paws inside gauntlets. Swords are drawn in the present and in the monster memory; otherwise Bob's sword stays sheathed at his belt and the kitten's sword stays secured on his back. Kaye appears only in the present.




ACTION TIMING

0.0–2.0s — The first frame is an extreme close-up of the kitten's face on the bridge: dark glossy eyes, damp whiskers, pink bow. He immediately squeaks, "FIGHT MEEE!" His mouth matches the words. Rapidly pull back to a low wide view, revealing his tiny armoured body and oversized sword, and continue retreating as he starts charging upright toward Kaye and Bob. His cape snaps behind him.




2.0s HARD CUT. 2.0–4.6s — Low medium-wide two-shot of Kaye and Bob at the opposing side of the bridge, Kaye on frame-left and Bob beside her on frame-right. Kaye holds her drawn sword in her right hand, looks clearly down at Bob and says briskly, bright and determined, "Bob! You ready? Let's take him down. Let's go!" Bob looks up at her. Keep her adult height and his knee-high scale immediately readable.




4.6s HARD CUT. 4.6–6.1s — Close on Bob at his eye level, with Kaye's armoured legs and lowered blade at the left edge. His shoulders sink; a brief heavy sigh, then a low, tired, deadpan "Ugh... here we go again." He leans forward and starts running with Kaye toward the kitten. His glasses stay fixed on his nose.




6.1s HARD CUT. 6.1–7.3s — Brief slow-motion hero shot of the charging kitten. Camera just above the cobbles, looking up and travelling backward ahead of him. He runs upright with the sword raised, ears back, determined face, cape and fluffy fur trailing. The close wide perspective makes him heroic while the large cobbles establish his tiny real scale. One weighted footfall, a small wet splash and a deep exaggerated thud; no separate reaction inserts.




7.3s HARD CUT. 7.3–8.4s — Frontal low wide tracking shot of Kaye and Bob running directly toward the retreating camera. Kaye is frame-left, Bob frame-right at her knee height. Her long strides contrast with his quick upright steps. She briefly glances down at him without slowing. Capes, armour and hair react to their momentum.




8.4s HARD CUT. 8.4–10.5s — Tight tracking close-up of Bob running, with Kaye's ivory cape soft at the left edge. Slow motion. His eyes shift toward the oncoming kitten, soften behind the blue glasses, then close in a long affectionate blink; his muzzle relaxes into a faint dreamy smile. Footsteps and armour sounds become distant and muffled. During the final fraction of this beat, begin a short soft dissolve into the first memory.




10.5–11.7s — MEMORY: milk in the corridor, @Image 5. Floor-level side two-shot, Bob left and kitten right, already leaning over opposite sides of one shallow ceramic bowl of milk, about 30 cm across. Keep their bodies separate across the bowl. They lap once, lift their eyes and meet each other's gaze; a tiny milk drop clings to the kitten's whiskers. Gentle forward camera drift. Candle crackle and quiet lapping. Their swords remain sheathed.




11.7s HARD CUT. 11.7–13.4s — MEMORY: the threat on the sword hill, @Image 6. Low rear wide view, Bob left and kitten right, already upright with drawn swords, facing into dense fog. Enormous planted swords surround them. A colossal hunched, ram-horned silhouette, about seven metres tall, is already dimly visible ahead and slowly lifts its head; long arms and faint amber eyes remain obscured by mist. Camera creeps forward behind the two small knights. Wind, distant breathing and a low rumble. Hold the confrontation without an attack.




13.4s HARD CUT. 13.4–15.2s — MEMORY: play on the sword hill, @Image 6. Low medium two-shot. Bob sits upright on his bottom at frame-left, hind legs stretched out, front paws resting on his belly. The fully armoured kitten at frame-right makes one playful upright hop over Bob's outstretched feet and lands in front of him. Bob watches with a relaxed slow blink. A slight lateral camera drift, a tiny chirp and a soft armour clink. Swords are sheathed; no monster in this memory.




15.2s HARD CUT. 15.2–16.8s — MEMORY: the race on the bridge, @Image 4. Enter mid-race, low side tracking, both cats galloping left-to-right on all fours. Bob is nearest the camera; the much smaller kitten runs parallel beside him and draws slightly ahead. Their paws strike wet cobbles and splash through a shallow puddle; capes stream backward and sheathed swords bounce against their straps. Natural feline weight and bounding strides. Show only this running fragment, without a start or finish.




16.8s HARD CUT. 16.8–20.1s — MEMORY: quiet at the marsh, @Image 7. Medium rear three-quarter view at cat-eye height. Bob left and kitten right sit on their haunches beside the creek with a small visible gap between them, capes pooled on the grass, tails resting naturally. Both gaze across the winding water at the distant castle. Hold this longer, peaceful beat with a gentle handheld drift, a small ear twitch and a light breeze through fur and fabric. Soft water and distant birds; swords sheathed.




20.1s HARD CUT BACK TO THE PRESENT. 20.1–24.0s — Wide lateral view across the bridge deck, slightly elevated, wet cobbles below and fog behind the parapet, angel statues framing the sides. All three are visible immediately: kitten in the left third charging right, Kaye and Bob in the right third charging left. They are enemies in this moment, no longer racing together. All swords are drawn; both cats run upright. A restrained lateral camera drift keeps the open space between them readable as the gap narrows. Restore crisp footsteps, armour clatter and bridge wind. End at exactly 24 seconds while several metres still separate them, before any contact or sword strike.

Preserve readable eyes without studio key light, artificial rim light or glowing fur. Memories have gently faded colour and soft contrast, without fantasy bloom.
