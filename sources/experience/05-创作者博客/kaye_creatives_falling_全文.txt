Rooftop parapet of a Manhattan skyscraper @Image 2 during late sunset, just after rain. 

Approx. 300 m above street level. Wide concrete ledge, weathered stone coping, wet 

and glossy, shallow puddles on the rooftop surface mirroring the sky. HVAC units and 

antenna masts as dark silhouettes behind, water dripping from their edges.




Sky: sun just below the horizon line, breaking through torn storm clouds. Burning 

gradient — deep orange and coral at the horizon, rising through pink and lilac into 

dark cobalt at the top of frame. Heavy grey clouds still hanging on one side, their 

undersides lit molten gold. The clouds drift slowly and continuously across the 

frame. Thin atmospheric haze, low mist moving between the towers.




RAIN: the downpour is ending. Fine drizzle still falling, individual droplets 

catching the low sun and reading as bright sparks against the dark buildings. Water 

running down the concrete, dripping off the ledge, rippling the puddles, beading on 

her cardigan and hair. Everything is wet and reflective.




LIVING CITY BELOW: the streets far below are alive with continuous movement — 

streams of cars crawling along the avenues, headlights and tail lights smeared into 

moving glowing lines on the wet asphalt, traffic signals cycling red to green, 

crosswalk traffic flowing. Thousands of lit windows across the towers, some 

flickering, occasional windows switching on and off. Red aircraft warning lights 

pulsing rhythmically on distant rooftops. Reflections of the whole moving city 

doubled in the wet streets. Steam rising steadily from street vents and rooftop 

exhausts. Far in the distance a single bird crosses the frame, gliding slowly 

between the buildings. A plane's lights move slowly across the sky near the horizon.




ACTION:

Wide shot. A young woman [tag your character sheet here]  walks slowly toward the edge of the roof. She reaches the 

parapet and stops, leans forward slightly and looks down at the street far below. 

Her body reacts instinctively — a small involuntary flinch, and she steps back half 

a pace, shoulders tightening, weight shifting away from the edge. She stands there, 

tense. She lifts her head and looks up at the sky, holding for a moment. Then she 

looks down again. Her hands clench into fists and open, clench and open — visible 

stress, hesitation, breathing unsteady, small shifts of weight.




Then stillness. A decision settles into her body.




She steps UP ONTO the parapet and TURNS AROUND, so that her back is now to the drop 

and she faces the rooftop. She stands on the narrow ledge for a beat. Then she 

spreads her arms wide to both sides and lets herself fall BACKWARDS — back first, 

face toward the sky, body straight, arms open, no twisting, no rotation. She drops 

out of frame downward.




At the moment she leaves the ledge, several fireworks LAUNCH from the rooftops 

100-200 m to the side — only the launch, no explosion. Thin bright trails shoot 

upward through the drizzle, leaving faint smoke tails. They do NOT burst within this 

shot.




Strong wind: her hair whipping wet across her face, the cream cardigan billowing 

open and heavy with water, the wide-leg jeans flapping, the earbud cable snapping in 

the air. She is in soaked white socks on the wet concrete, no shoes.




Lighting: strong warm backlight from the horizon, hard rim on her wet hair and 

shoulders, subject largely in silhouette. Cool blue fill from the sky above. Golden 

reflections bouncing off the wet glass facades of neighbouring towers and off the 

puddles. Rain droplets backlit as bright specks. High dynamic range, deep shadows.




CAMERA:

Locked-off wide shot, tripod-mounted, fixed position for the entire take. Absolutely 

no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no dolly, no push in. The framing never changes. The only 

movement is a subtle handheld-style shake — small organic vibration and breathing 

micro-movement, as if the operator is barely holding still. The full figure stays in 

frame at all times with the city and sky filling the background. 35mm lens, T2.8, 

deep focus, both the woman and the city readable. Water droplets occasionally on the 

lens. Anamorphic flare, warm haze bloom.




The background must never be a static image — continuous motion of traffic, lights, 

rain, mist and clouds throughout the entire shot.




Look: cinematic film still, 2.39:1, fine 35mm grain, warm amber and cyan palette, 

wet reflective surfaces, gentle highlight roll-off, natural vignetting. Photoreal, 

observational documentary feel, no CGI look.

Shot 2. Falling
Input character sheets and location reference
Make sure you tagged your character sheet with @ in the prompt where it is marked [tag your character sheet here]  
and just tap ✨recreate✨

Real time, normal speed, no slow motion. Close-up, side view, camera tracking alongside 

a young woman as she falls at high speed. She falls FLAT ON HER BACK, face turned up 

toward the sky, body horizontal. Her head, shoulders and upper body fill most of the 

frame, seen in profile — the line of her throat and jaw exposed, chin raised toward the 

sky. [tag your character sheet here] 




The background is entirely motion-blurred @Image 2 — the facades of buildings rushing upward past 

her as smeared vertical streaks of concrete, glass and window reflections, all detail 

dissolved by speed. Warm sunset light bleeding through the blur at the top of frame, the 

lower part of the background darker and colder. Nothing in the background is readable; 

it is pure vertical motion.




She falls fast. Her long hair is torn upward and away from her face by the airspeed, 

streaming and thrashing above her head. The collar of her white t-shirt hammers against 

her neck, the cream cardigan snapping and flapping violently at the edge of frame, 

sleeves whipping. Her arms are spread out to the sides. Her skin and lips are pushed by 

air pressure, cheeks trembling under the force. Eyes squinting and watering against the 

wind, mouth slightly open, breath torn away. Her face is not calm — the body is reacting 

to real aerodynamic force.




Her back stays turned downward toward the ground the entire time. She does not rotate, 

tumble or turn face down at any point. Small involuntary shifts as the air catches her, 

the body never perfectly still, never posed.




CLEAN FRAME — NO RIGGING: nothing connects her to anything. No cable, no wire, no rope, 

no harness, no rigging.




Camera: 85mm, T2.0, shallow depth of field — her face sharp, the background completely 

dissolved. Handheld tracking alongside her, unsteady, small overshoots and corrections, 

frame vibrating from the wind. No zoom.




Look: photoreal, hyperrealistic, documentary, 2.39:1, fine 35mm grain, heavy vertical 

motion blur, warm sunset light against cool shadow, high contrast. No CGI look, no 

stylisation.

Normal speed, real time. Extreme close-up of a young woman's [tag your character sheet here]   face as she falls back 

first through the air, face turned up toward @Image 2 the sky, camera hovering directly above 

her and locked to her face, falling with her. Behind and beyond her head the city 

rushes away in vertical perspective — wet streets, rooftops and moving cars with 

smeared headlights seen from great height, traffic flowing along the avenues, lit 

windows sliding past, wisps of cloud passing, everything in cold blue shadow while 

the horizon behind still glows dull orange. The city below is in constant motion and 

rushing upward toward camera.




Violent wind and rain slam into her: droplets streaking past the lens and breaking 

against her skin, her wet hair streaming and whipping across her cheeks, the collar 

of her soaked white t-shirt fluttering hard, water running off her face, eyes 

squinting against the spray. Her arms are spread wide, one hand entering the edge of 

frame. Her body stays flat on its back, no rotation.




She holds her eyes open, staring upward, then slowly closes them. Final beat: eyes 

fully shut, face calm, wind still tearing at her hair.




Lighting: warm light bouncing up from the wet glass facades below, cool skylight from 

above, distant firework glow adding brief magenta accents on one side of her face.




Camera: 50mm lens, T1.8, very shallow depth of field, face sharp, city falling out of 

focus behind her. Handheld operating style, aggressive shake from the wind, frame 

vibrating. Water droplets hitting the lens.




Look: cinematic film still, 2.39:1, 35mm grain, high contrast, motion blur in the 

background, photoreal, no CGI look. In the last frames the image is nearly abstract 

with speed and blur.

Small city bedroom @Image 3 in the evening, seen from a high corner angle near the ceiling, 

looking down into the whole room. Single bed against the wall, rumpled white sheets, 

duvet half thrown off. Nightstand with a phone face-down, a glass of water, a lamp 

switched off. Clothes on a chair, photos taped to the wall, books stacked on the floor. 

Lived-in, slightly messy, personal.




A young woman [tag your character sheet here]  lies on her back on the bed, awake, staring up at the ceiling. Her chest 

rises and falls with slightly heavy, uneven breathing, slowly settling. She is fully 

dressed — wide-leg jeans, white t-shirt, cream cardigan half off one shoulder, white 

socks on the sheets — she fell asleep in her clothes. The white wired earbuds are still 

in her ears.




She lies still for a long moment. Then she slowly pushes herself up and sits on the 

edge of the bed, unhurried, shoulders heavy. She reaches up and pulls the earbuds out 

of her ears one at a time, letting the cable drop into her lap. She turns her head and 

looks toward the window, where the fireworks are bursting over the city.




Large window beside the bed, curtains open, no lights on inside the room. Outside: the 

last band of sunset glowing amber low on the horizon over the water, the sky above 

deepening to blue-violet, city windows lit and flickering, cars moving on the street 

below with headlights, traffic flowing continuously. Fireworks exploding over the bay, 

pulsing warm gold and magenta across the walls, ceiling and bedsheets in irregular 

rhythm. Between bursts the room falls back into cool blue darkness. The curtain shifts 

slightly in a draft.




Lighting: the window is the sole light source. Soft residual dusk as ambient base, hard 

intermittent firework flashes as accent. Long shadows across the floor, strong contrast 

between the warm window side and the cold interior side. The firework flashes wash 

across her face while she lies looking at the ceiling.




Camera: static locked-off high angle from the upper corner of the room, wide 24mm lens, 

slight barrel distortion, entire room visible, absolutely no movement.




Look: cinematic film still, 2.39:1, 35mm grain, cool blue base with warm accents, 

naturalistic, photoreal.
