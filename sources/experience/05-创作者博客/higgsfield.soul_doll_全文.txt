SCENE CONTEXT

She spots her dream dress in the wardrobe and magically changes into it. The tiny flying cat styles her hair. She proudly reveals the outfit, but the giant window turns to night in a cartoon way, and she is crushed in a cute, caricatured way. Multi-shot edit with varied angles and close-ups.
