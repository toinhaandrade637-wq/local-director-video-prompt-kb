2. MAKE YOUR LOOKS IN SEEDREAM ★

upload the outfit photo as IMAGE 1 and a clear selfie as IMAGE 2. choose Seedream and 3:4, then paste this prompt. generate one image at a time.

TASK: identity swap. Use IMAGE 1 as the outfit and body reference. Replace the face, hairstyle and hair color with the person from IMAGE 2. Output a single full-body 3:4 photograph.




FROM IMAGE 1 (keep exactly): full outfit — every garment, layer, fabric, color, print, accessory and footwear — and body proportions. Keep the original body skin tone. Change the pose to standing relaxed, both arms hanging loosely at the sides with empty hands. Remove held objects: phone, bag and props.




FROM IMAGE 2 (transfer exactly): facial identity — face shape, eyes, brows, nose, lips, skin texture and complexion — and full hairstyle: cut, length, color, texture and fringe. Neutral expression, mouth closed. Do not blend the two faces; the result must read as the person from IMAGE 2 wearing the clothes from IMAGE 1.




FRAMING: entire figure visible from head to feet, camera far enough back to include the floor beneath the shoes and clear margin above the head and below the feet, eye-level, frontal.




ENVIRONMENT: seamless pure white studio backdrop (#FFFFFF), evenly lit, no props, subject fully isolated on white.




STYLE: unretouched RAW-style photography, natural soft studio lighting, true-to-life skin texture, visible pores, fine lines and subtle imperfections, natural facial asymmetry, visible makeup texture, individual hair strands and flyaways, realistic fabric weave and seams, soft contact shadow under the shoes, neutral color grading, sharp focus.




NEGATIVE: no face blending, no hybrid face, no face or hairstyle from IMAGE 1, no outfit from IMAGE 2, no changed garments, no altered colors or prints, no held objects, no phone, no selfie pose, no beauty retouching, no filters, no doll or plastic skin, no cropped feet or head, no split-screen, no CGI, no illustration, no added text or watermark, no extra limbs or fingers, no distorted anatomy, no blur. Preserve existing text and graphics printed on the clothes.
