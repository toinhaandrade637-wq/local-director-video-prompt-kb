2. Catching the lightning (6s)

References: @Image1 your long-coat sheet, @Image2 the temple roof at night (a darker grade of the roof photo). This take gave me the catch.




SCENE CONTEXT

A black, stormy night on the top of an old temple roof high above the city, a violent thunderstorm right overhead and the roof in near darkness. Someone walking along the wet ridge toward the hero films him: a wide shot, the hero small on the ridge with the city spread out below him. He is not performing for the camera; he is doing this for himself, already in his stance with his left arm straight up, two fingers pointing at the clouds. In the very first moment a bolt of lightning hits his fingertips, all of it at once, and its blinding light hits him from above, blazing on his upturned face and raised arm and throwing hard black shadows across the roof. Calmly, he sweeps that hand down into his body and across in front of his torso into his other hand, then spreads his arms apart and fires it: a thick, violent, branching bolt rips out of his right hand far out over the city, the camera swinging with it to show the city below, and its light blazes on the side of him that faces it while his other side falls to black, his shadow thrown hard across the roof. 6 seconds, one continuous handheld shot, real time, no cuts.




SCENE GOAL

The hero can catch lightning from a storm and throw it, and he is completely calm about it; the person filming wants the clip because nobody will believe it.
