The pipeline in one picture
Film one clean take. Locked-off, good light, the subject talks straight to camera. That take becomes the MASTER.
Inspect the clip: ffprobe for duration, fps and frame size, then a transcription with word-level timestamps (I use faster-whisper). You need the second AND the word of each cut point.
Decide the cuts like a director. Which line gets the side shot, which one gets the close-up, which one deserves the weird angle.
Let the skill write the prompt. I asked Claude with my skill benetti-seedance-prompt-writer: "write a Seedance 2.5 video-to-video prompt for this clip, change only the camera at these timecodes". The rules that make it hold

The model will happily invent a new person and a new room if you let it. These are the clauses that stop it, learned the hard way across three versions.

Say who is MASTER. The first line of the prompt states that @video1 is master for identity, face, voice, dialogue, lip-sync, timing, gestures, wardrobe, set, lighting, color and duration, and that the ONLY change is the camera. Without that sentence the model treats the video as a loose reference.
Cut on a word, not just a number. "8.6s HARD CUT (on 'você consegue mudar o ângulo')" holds far better than "8.6s: cut". The word anchors the timecode to the audio, so the mouth stays in sync after the cut.
The room never changes. Describe the set geometry once (what is screen-left, what is behind the shoulder, where the tripod stands) and then say new angles reveal more of THIS room. New vantage points otherwise get a new wall.
Match the duration. If the source is 18.3s, set the generation to 18s. Longer stretches the performance and breaks the sync.
Describe camera, never lighting. The moment you describe a new light, the model re-renders the subject with it and the face drifts. Lighting is part of the locked set.
Negatives do not work. "No teeth", "no music" get read as descriptions. Write what you want, not what you do not want.
Detail equals lock. Version 1 said "black polo shirt". Version 2 said "black short-sleeve polo with a darker tonal band across the chest, short zip placket, small black lavalier at the center, black smartwatch on the left wrist, thin gold ring on the right ring finger". Version 2 kept the identity in every angle. That is what the VFX skill pass buys you.
