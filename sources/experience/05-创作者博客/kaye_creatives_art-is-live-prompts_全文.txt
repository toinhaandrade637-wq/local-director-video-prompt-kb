9:16 VERTICAL. 30 SECONDS. ONE SHOT, ONE CAMERA, NO CUTS. One single continuous unbroken take from the first frame to the last. THERE IS NOT ONE CUT ANYWHERE IN THIS FILM.




=== SIX HARD REQUIREMENTS. READ ALL SIX BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE ===




1. THIS IS FIRST PERSON — THE CAMERA IS HER EYES — FOR EVERY SECOND EXCEPT ONE STRETCH.

FROM 0.0 TO 5.0s AND FROM 7.0 TO 30.0s: we see only what she sees. Her face and body are not in frame. The only part of her that appears is HER OWN HAND AND FOREARM entering from below when she reaches for something, and a glimpse of her skirt or shoes when she looks down. She speaks from behind the camera.

FROM 5.0 TO 7.0s ONLY: the view is turned around onto HER OWN FACE while she runs, with the world behind her.

THE TWO CHANGES BETWEEN THESE — at 5.0s and at 7.0s — ARE PHYSICAL ROTATIONS OF THE POINT OF VIEW, fast and blurred, NEVER CUTS.




2. NO PHONE. NO CAMERA. NO DEVICE. NOTHING OF THE KIND IS EVER VISIBLE IN ANY FRAME — not in her hand, not at the edge, not reflected, not as a shadow, no screen, no interface, no lens, no strap, not even during the frontal stretch. There is no object doing the filming. There is only her point of view.




3. THE CHAIN OF PAINTINGS. EACH FRAMED CANVAS SHOWS, AS AN OIL PAINTING, EXACTLY THE PLACE SHE IS ABOUT TO ARRIVE IN. NEVER ANYTHING ELSE:

— IN THE DARK HALL, the canvas shows AN EMPTY SUN-BAKED DESERT PLAIN WITH SOFT DROOPING WATCHES ON IT.

— ON THAT PLAIN, the canvas shows A WHITE HORSE REARING IN A SNOWSTORM ON A MOUNTAIN PASS WITH A RIDER IN A BLUE COAT AND AN ORANGE CLOAK.

— ON THAT PASS, the canvas shows A COLOSSAL BLUE OCEAN WAVE CURLING OVER SMALL BOATS.

Whatever is painted on a canvas is what she finds on the other side of it. This mapping is absolute.




4. EVERY ENTRY INTO A CANVAS IS A CIRCULAR LIGHT SPIN, AND IT IS UNMISSABLE. It happens the same way every time, over about six tenths of a second:

— the gilded frame edge sweeps past both sides of the lens as she goes through

— AND THE WHOLE PICTURE BEGINS TO ROTATE AROUND THE CENTRE OF THE FRAME, accelerating hard through more than a full turn, the horizon wheeling right over

— AS IT SPINS, A RING OF WHITE LIGHT EXPANDS OUTWARD FROM THE CENTRE OF THE FRAME and swallows everything, blowing the picture out to pure white

— EVERYTHING SMEARS INTO CIRCULAR ARCS AROUND THE CENTRE — heavy rotational motion blur, the light drawn out into concentric streaks

— the rotation decelerates and settles back level, and the white falls away — AND IT IS ALREADY INSIDE THE NEW WORLD, still turning slightly as it comes to rest

NO DISSOLVE, NO CROSS FADE, NO MORPH, NO PORTAL RING GRAPHIC, NO VORTEX TUNNEL, NO PARTICLES, NO SLOW OR GENTLE TRANSITION. IT IS A SPIN AND A BLAZE OF LIGHT. THE CAMERA NEVER CUTS THROUGH IT.




5. WHAT IS INSIDE A PAINTING IS A REAL THREE-DIMENSIONAL PLACE, NOT A PICTURE. The instant the spin settles she is STANDING INSIDE A FULL DEEP PHYSICAL SPACE with real distance, real parallax and real air. THINGS ARE BEHIND OTHER THINGS. She can walk forward into it, walk around objects, and see them from new angles; when she turns her head the whole space moves past her correctly, near objects sliding fast and far ones barely at all. There is space above her, below her, to both sides and BEHIND her.

IT IS NOT A FLAT IMAGE, NOT A PRINTED PICTURE, NOT A BACKDROP, NOT A MURAL, NOT A PARALLAX CARD SET.




6. SHE TAKES HER LIGHT FROM WHEREVER SHE IS STANDING, ALWAYS. Her face, her hands and her forearms are lit ONLY by the world she is in — the same direction, the same hardness, the same colour temperature, the same level as everything around them.

COMPLETELY IGNORE THE LIGHTING OF image_1. Its flat white studio light must appear NOWHERE. She must never look brighter, cleaner or differently lit than her surroundings.

=== THE FRAMED CANVASES ===

A LARGE CANVAS IN A HEAVY ORNATE GILDED WOODEN FRAME, taller than she is, STANDING ON THE GROUND or LYING FLAT ON IT, never hanging on a wall.

ITS SURFACE IS AN OLD OIL PAINTING AND NOTHING ELSE: VISIBLE BRUSHSTROKES with ridges of thick paint catching the light, FINE CRACKED CRAQUELURE, UNEVEN YELLOWED VARNISH with a patchy sheen and a hard glare sliding across it as she moves, THE CANVAS WEAVE at the edges. COMPLETELY FLAT, NO DEPTH, NO PARALLAX, NOTHING INSIDE IT MOVES.

It is paint on cloth until she goes through it.




=== THE THIRTY SECONDS ===




0.0 – 4.4s — THE HALL, INSIDE @Image 2. A WIDE VIEW, FROM A DISTANCE, AND IT IS ALIVE FROM THE FIRST FRAME.

0.0–2.0s — SHE IS STANDING WELL BACK IN THE DARK HALL, TWENTY METRES FROM THEM, LOOKING AT THE WHOLE COMPANY AT ONCE. THEY ARE SMALL AND FAR OFF AND THEIR ENTIRE ARRANGEMENT IS VISIBLE — the two men at the centre, the packed rows behind, the pikes crossing above, the girl in gold to the left, the drummer at the right, the stone arch over all of it. THE COMPOSITION READS WHOLE AND INSTANTLY.

AND THEY ARE ALREADY MOVING FROM THE VERY FIRST FRAME. NOT ONE OF THEM IS STILL: THE TWO CENTRAL MEN ARE WALKING FORWARD, the black-clad one gesturing with his open hand as he talks; THE DRUMMER IS BEATING HIS DRUM, sticks moving; THE MAN IN RED IS LOADING HIS MUSKET, working the ramrod; PIKES AND MUSKETS SHIFT AND SWAY at their angles as men adjust their grip; heads turn and lean toward each other in conversation; THE GIRL IN GOLD MOVES ACROSS BEHIND THEM; the dog trots along the right. Hats bob, ruffs move, breath shows in the cold. THIS IS A LIVING ROOM FULL OF BUSY PEOPLE, NOT A TABLEAU THAT COMES ALIVE.

She shifts her weight and turns her head slightly and THE SPACE MOVES WITH FULL DEPTH — the near floor slides, the far arch barely moves. THEY PAY HER NO ATTENTION AT ALL.

2.0–3.4s — STILL LOOKING AT THEM ACROSS THE DISTANCE, SHE SAYS, half laughing, bewildered: "Whoa. Who are these guys?"

3.4–4.4s — AND THEY NOTICE HER. Far off, the two central men turn their heads and look straight down the lens. Heads turn all through the company. THE DRUM STOPS DEAD. Pikes come down level. AND THE WHOLE COMPANY BREAKS FORWARD AND STARTS RUNNING AT HER ACROSS THE FLOOR, boots hammering on stone.




4.4 – 7.0s — SHE TURNS THE VIEW ONTO HERSELF AND RUNS. NO CUT ANYWHERE HERE.

4.4–5.0s — THE VIEW SWINGS ROUND ONTO HER OWN FACE in one fast blurred rotation — the dark hall smearing past — and settles.

5.0–7.0s — HER FACE FILLS THE LOWER PART OF THE VERTICAL FRAME, at arm's length, LIT ONLY BY THE DIM WARM LANTERN LIGHT OF THE HALL. She is running hard: the frame pounds and bounces with every stride, her hair whipping across her face, her mouth open, breathing fast, her expression caught between terrified and delighted. She glances off to the side and back.

AND BEHIND HER, FILLING THE UPPER PART OF THE FRAME, THE ENTIRE COMPANY IS SPRINTING AFTER HER — the same men, hats, ruffs, buff coats, pikes lowered and bouncing, boots hammering, faces set, GETTING STEADILY CLOSER AND LARGER as they gain on her. The dark hall streaks past on both sides.

7.0 – 7.6s — SHE SWINGS THE VIEW BACK ROUND TO FORWARD in one fast blurred rotation, and she is running at A FRAMED PAINTED CANVAS STANDING AGAINST A STONE WALL AHEAD OF HER. ON IT IS PAINTED, IN CRACKED VARNISHED OIL: AN EMPTY SUN-BAKED DESERT PLAIN, A BARE DEAD TREE, A DISTANT PALE CLIFF, AND SOFT DROOPING POCKET WATCHES DRAPED OVER A BRANCH AND OVER A STONE BLOCK. Flat and still, varnish glinting.

7.6 – 8.0s — SHE HITS IT AND GOES THROUGH. CIRCULAR LIGHT SPIN — the frame wheels over, the ring of white light expands from the centre, everything smears into arcs, the white takes it, and it settles level in the new place.




8.0 – 14.2s — THE PLAIN, INSIDE @Image 3. SHE ARRIVES AT A DISTANCE.

8.0–10.3s — She comes out still running and STAGGERS TO A STOP, AND THE WHOLE PLACE IS LAID OUT IN FRONT OF HER AT A DISTANCE — the dead tree with its drooping watch off to the left, the stone block, the pale organic form ahead, the long cliff and flat sea to the right, the enormous sky. THE COMPOSITION READS WHOLE AND INSTANTLY. THE FRAME HEAVES WITH HER BREATHING.

SHE TURNS SLOWLY THROUGH A FULL LOOK AROUND and the space moves with real depth — the near cracked ground rushes past, the far cliff hardly moves. Then she walks forward and the tree and the block grow and shift against the horizon.

10.3–11.8s — she walks all the way up to the low block with the soft watch folded over its edge, the frame bouncing with her steps.

11.8–13.2s — HER HAND COMES INTO FRAME FROM BELOW AND PICKS THE WATCH UP BY ONE EDGE — AND IT HANGS AND FLOPS AND DROOPS OVER HER FINGERS LIKE A PIECE OF WET CLOTH, heavy and soft, its face bending under its own weight. She turns her hand over and it slides and re-drapes. She jiggles it and it wobbles. HER HAND IS LIT BY THE HARD HIGH SUN OF THIS PLACE AND NOTHING ELSE.

13.2–14.2s — holding it up in front of the lens, she says: "Oh my god. Are you seeing this?"




14.2 – 16.3s — She lowers her hand and turns. Out in the middle of the empty plain, a hundred metres off and completely alone, STANDS ANOTHER FRAMED PAINTED CANVAS ON THE GROUND. ON IT IS PAINTED, IN CRACKED VARNISHED OIL: A WHITE HORSE REARING UP ON ITS HIND LEGS IN A SNOWSTORM ON A GREY MOUNTAIN PASS, WITH A RIDER IN A DARK BICORN HAT AND A BLUE COAT, HIS ARM RAISED, AN ENORMOUS ORANGE-RED CLOAK BLOWN OUT BEHIND HIM. Flat and still, sun glaring off the varnish.

SHE SAYS: "Okay. I'm ready." — AND RUNS AT IT, the ground pounding past, the canvas growing fast.

16.3 – 16.7s — CIRCULAR LIGHT SPIN. THROUGH.




16.7 – 24.0s — THE PASS, INSIDE @Image 4, AND EVERYTHING IS FROZEN. THIS IS THE LONGEST SCENE AND IT MUST NOT BE HURRIED.

16.7–18.1s — She staggers to a halt AT A DISTANCE, and the whole thing stands in front of her — the reared horse, the rider, the cloak, the rock, the soldiers, the mountains. THE COMPOSITION READS WHOLE AND INSTANTLY. AND NOTHING IS MOVING. EVERY SINGLE SNOWFLAKE HANGS MOTIONLESS IN THE AIR. The cloud is stopped. THE HORSE IS REARED UP AND HELD THERE, mane and tail stopped mid-stream. THE RIDER IS STOPPED, arm raised, cloak frozen mid-snap. The soldiers are stopped mid-step.

18.1–21.4s — SHE WALKS INTO IT AND EXPLORES. THE ONLY THINGS MOVING IN THE WHOLE WORLD ARE HER AND HER EYES. She walks slowly forward between the hanging flakes and THE LENS PASSES WITHIN CENTIMETRES OF INDIVIDUAL SUSPENDED SNOWFLAKES THAT DO NOT MOVE, sliding past on both sides with real parallax. HER HAND COMES INTO FRAME AND PUSHES SLOWLY THROUGH THEM AND THEY STAY EXACTLY WHERE THEY WERE. She looks up — the frame tilts up the horse's chest to the frozen hooves and the raised arm against the stopped cloud. SHE WALKS ALL THE WAY AROUND THE HORSE, tracking along its frozen flank, past the stopped tail, round to the front.

21.4–22.6s — she comes in close to the horse's shoulder. She stops. HER HAND REACHES OUT SLOWLY INTO THE FRAME.

22.6–22.9s — SHE TOUCHES IT.

22.9–24.0s — AND EVERYTHING SNAPS INTO MOTION AT ONCE. The wind roars in, the snow tears sideways past the lens, the cloud rips across the sky, the cloak cracks open like a sail, THE HORSE COMES DOWN OFF ITS HIND LEGS AND GALLOPS PAST HER AND AWAY into the storm. She staggers back and the frame lurches hard and swings after them.




24.0 – 25.6s — She turns, and LYING FLAT IN THE SNOW AT HER FEET IS THE LAST FRAMED PAINTED CANVAS, FACE UP. ON IT IS PAINTED, IN CRACKED VARNISHED OIL: A COLOSSAL BLUE OCEAN WAVE REARING UP AND CURLING OVER, ITS CREST BREAKING INTO CLAWED FINGERS OF WHITE FOAM, WITH LONG OPEN BOATS IN THE TROUGH BELOW IT. Flat and still, set into the ground like an open hatch. SHE JUMPS DOWN INTO IT, FEET FIRST.

25.6 – 26.0s — CIRCULAR LIGHT SPIN. THROUGH.




26.0 – 30.0s — AND SHE COMES OUT INTO HAND-DRAWN ANIME.

THE STYLE CHANGES COMPLETELY AND INSTANTLY AS THE SPIN SETTLES AND NEVER CHANGES BACK. EVERYTHING IS CLASSIC HAND-DRAWN CEL ANIMATION: flat cel shading in two or three tones, clean dark ink outlines, hand-painted backgrounds, no photoreal texture anywhere, no 3D, no CGI.

IT IS FIRST PERSON AGAIN — we see only what she sees, and her drawn hand when it enters frame.

26.0–27.2s — SHE IS FALLING OUT OF A HAND-PAINTED SKY. The drawn horizon spins and tumbles, painted clouds wheel past, the drawn sea rushes up toward her, HER OWN DRAWN HAND flailing in and out of frame, speed lines streaking at the edges.

27.2–27.7s — SHE HITS THE WATER. Everything goes under — drawn bubbles as clusters of white circles, hand-inked churn.

27.7–28.4s — SHE BREAKS THE SURFACE. Drawn water sheets off the lens, drawn droplets scatter. Her gasping breath.

28.4–29.3s — AND THERE IT IS, EXACTLY AS IN @Image 5 BUT DRAWN: THE COLOSSAL WAVE AT THE LEFT, REARED UP AND CURLING OVER TOWARD HER, ITS CREST BREAKING INTO LONG CLAWED FINGERS OF FOAM AS HAND-INKED SHAPES, deep flat teal against white; the second wave in the trough; the long boats with their crews; the small snow-capped cone tiny on the horizon; a soft painted sky. IT FILLS THE ENTIRE TOP OF THE VERTICAL FRAME AND BLOTS OUT THE SKY. She looks up at it.

29.3–30.0s — AND IT FALLS AND TAKES HER. Drawn foam swallows the frame, the ink lines tumble, everything goes under and darkens. END UNDERWATER, still animated.




=== HOW IT MOVES ===

ONE UNBROKEN TAKE. IT NEVER CUTS — not when they charge, not when the view turns onto her face, not when it turns back, not inside the spins, not at the change into animation.

THE POINT OF VIEW IS ALIVE THE WHOLE TIME — it pounds when she runs, heaves when she breathes, whips when she turns, jolts when she lands, drifts loosely when she stands still. Real motion blur, real unsteadiness, occasional brief loss of focus.

NO SLOW MOTION, NO SPEED RAMP, NO FRAME BLENDING, except inside the spins.

SHE IS NEVER RUSHED.




=== THE LOOK ===

FROM 0.0 TO 26.0s: MAXIMUM PHOTOREALISM AND ONE UNCHANGING GRADE. Neutral white balance, moderate contrast with slightly lifted blacks, natural slightly-muted saturation, soft highlight roll-off, fine consistent grain, the same slightly wide lens throughout.

THE WORLDS DIFFER ONLY IN THE AMOUNT AND DIRECTION OF LIGHT: the hall dim with one strong upper-left source; the plain very bright with hard high daylight; the pass bright but flat and sourceless under heavy overcast. Objects keep their own real colours.

NO WORLD IS GRADED WARM AND NO WORLD IS GRADED COLD. No teal-and-orange, no stylised grade, no colour shift between scenes.

FROM 26.0s: FULL HAND-DRAWN CEL ANIMATION, and none of the above applies.




=== SOUND, NO MUSIC ===

0.0–4.4s — a large stone hall with a long reverb, BUSY FROM THE FIRST FRAME: a drum beating steadily, many boots on stone, low voices talking in an old language, metal on metal, a ramrod in a barrel, a dog. All of it far off across the room. Her voice, close and slightly off-mic. Then the drum stops dead and a rush of many boots breaking into a run toward us.

4.4–8.0s — the whip of the view turning, her running, hard breathing right on the mic, the boots and shouting closing behind her, the whip back, then A DEEP RISING SWELL AND A SHARP ELECTRICAL CRACK as she goes through.

8.0–14.2s — ENORMOUS SILENCE. No wind, no birds, no room tone. Only her breathing, loud and ragged, and her loafers on dry cracked ground. Her voice, close.

14.2–16.7s — running on hard ground, her voice, then the swell and crack.

16.7–22.6s — DEAD SILENCE. Frozen air, no wind whatsoever, only her breath and her feet on rock. THE SILENCE MUST BE TOTAL AND IT MUST LAST NEARLY SIX SECONDS.

22.6–24.0s — AND THEN ON THE TOUCH, THE ENTIRE STORM ARRIVES AT ONCE — a wall of wind and hooves and cracking fabric, deafening — and gallops away.

24.0–26.0s — wind, her jumping, the swell and crack.

26.0–30.0s — STYLISED DRAWN-ANIMATION SOUND: a clean whoosh of falling air, a sharp bright splash, her animated gasp, the huge designed roar of the wave. Then everything muffles and goes deep.

No music, no score, no song, no narration.
