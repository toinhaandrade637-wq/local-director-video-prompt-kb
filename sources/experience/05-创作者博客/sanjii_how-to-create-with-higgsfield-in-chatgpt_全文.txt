HOW TO CREATE WITH HIGGSFIELD IN CHATGPT
@sanjii
75

1. Connect Higgsfield

Open the Higgsfield setup page, click Add Higgsfield plugin, and sign in. Start a new ChatGPT conversation with Higgsfield enabled. Generations use your Higgsfield credits.

2. Add Your References

Tell ChatGPT what you want to create, who it is for, and the format. For product content, provide reference photos and verified features. Ask ChatGPT to make those references available to Higgsfield before generating.

3. Copy a Prompt

Create a video

Use Higgsfield to create an 8-second vertical video of [IDEA]. Use cinematic lighting and smooth camera movement. Choose a suitable available model, develop the prompt, and generate the clip.

Build a product campaign

Using my product references and verified features, create three campaign images and two short vertical video ads with Higgsfield. Keep the product and visual style consistent. Include captions and CTAs.

Create a month of content

Build a 30-day content calendar for [BRAND/TOPIC], with eight videos, twelve image posts, and ten Stories. Include hooks, captions, and CTAs. Once I select the direction, generate the first week's visuals with Higgsfield.
4. Refine the Result

Give specific feedback: “Keep the product unchanged, use warmer lighting, and slow the camera movement.” Review the revised assets, then continue with the next batch.
