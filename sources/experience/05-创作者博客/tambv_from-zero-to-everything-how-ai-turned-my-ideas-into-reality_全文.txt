💭 The problem

I've always been super creative. Never had the budget.

Every big idea stayed in my head: music videos with robots, anime films, cinematic spots. They needed crews, studios and money, so they stayed ideas.

⚡ The turning point

I started with AI from zero and used Higgsfield from day one.

Suddenly one person could do the work of a whole production: script, shots, characters, worlds, edit and motion.

For the first time, what I imagined was what I shipped.
