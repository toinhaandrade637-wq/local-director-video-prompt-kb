Step 4: Generate the fight

Settings for all three: Seedance 2.5, 4:3, and I made every one as a 480p draft first. Each prompt lists its references at the top as @Image1, @Image2 and so on; add your images in that same order. Run each one a few times. Every take gets some shots right and some wrong, and you'll cut the best ones together.

1. The first half (12s): her taunt on the stairs, her blast, your jump and landing with the two wings of fire over the lens, the jets racing across the courtyard, and the clash seen from the roof. References: your sheet, your selfie, her sheet, the stairs, the south end, the side-on view.

SCENE CONTEXT

Dusk in a vast royal palace courtyard, the last light gone dull orange behind the roofs. A firebending duel, filmed like real handheld footage by one camera operator in the courtyard. A wild, unhinged young woman sways and laughs on the bottom step of the palace stairs, raises her hands, spins, and blasts a fan of blue fire. The man leaps in, lands hard right by the camera and swings both arms down, sending two long streams of orange fire curving low along the ground like wings that roll over the lens, all in one shot. Across the courtyard their two jets shoot at each other along the ground; seen from high above, they smash together and lock in a ragged cross of blue and orange flame on the stone, each one pushing. It must look like real footage shot on a real camera, not visual effects. 12 seconds, real time.




SCENE GOAL

A duel to the end for the throne. She is unhinged and sure she will burn him down; he has come to stop her and will not give ground. Every attack is answered.




ACTIVE REFERENCES

@Image 1 (THE MAN, FULL BODY + FACE: your character sheet; use for identity, body and wardrobe ONLY and ignore its even studio lighting): the man exactly as in the sheet, wearing a loose oxblood-red cotton robe-tunic to mid-thigh with a short stand-up collar and a diagonal wrap front fastened by three small brass toggles, wide sleeves, a wide black cloth sash knotted at the left hip, very loose black trousers wrapped at the shins with black cloth bands, flat black boots. 100% matches.

@Image 2 (HIS REAL FACE: your selfie, face and hair identity ONLY; ignore the room, the clothes and its lighting): his true face. 100% matches in every close shot.

@Image 3 (THE WOMAN, FULL BODY + FACE; use for identity, body and wardrobe ONLY and ignore its even studio lighting): slim, athletic East Asian woman; long straight black hair come loose from a high topknot, a lopsided remnant of the knot at her crown, a choppy self-cut fringe, strands across her face; a fitted black high-collared tunic with dull-gold piping, a crimson leather shoulder mantle ending in sharp points over each upper arm, crimson sleeves, black sash, slim black trousers, black boots to mid-calf. 100% matches.

@Image 4 (THE STAIRS AT DUSK, GEOGRAPHY FOR SHOTS 1 AND 2): the stairs and the hall above them. Place and light only.

@Image 5 (THE SOUTH END AT DUSK, GEOGRAPHY FOR SHOT 3): the courtyard looking south to the gate wall and roofs, what is behind the man. Place and light only.

@Image 6 (THE COURTYARD SIDE-ON AT DUSK, GEOGRAPHY FOR SHOTS 4 AND 5): the hall and stairs at the left, the south wall at the right, the long floor across the middle. Place and light only.




LOCATION (fixed for the whole video)

A vast royal palace courtyard paved with huge worn sandstone slabs, cracked and dusty, about 150 metres across.

NORTH: the main hall on its stone terrace, its two-tier roof with gilded horn finials, and the wide stone stairs. She is at the north end, by the stairs.

SOUTH: the high red outer wall and its gate tower. The man is at the south end.

EAST: a long colonnade of thick red pillars under a tiled roof. WEST: a long low gallery.

In every side-on shot she is on the LEFT of the frame and he is on the RIGHT.

Time: dusk. The sun has just gone down behind the west gallery: only a dull, dark orange band low in the sky and heavy cloud above turning blue-black. The courtyard is in deep shadow. A light wind carries smoke across the paving. No other people anywhere.




FORMAT MODE

One continuous sequence of 5 shots joined by hard cuts, 12 seconds, 4:3, REAL TIME at normal speed. No slow motion, no speed ramps, no freeze frames, no fades. Rough, shaky handheld camera in every shot, clearly held in someone's hands and always moving: the operator steps, ducks and backs away from the heat, the frame shakes and jostles with his steps and breathing, he whips and tilts hard to follow every jump and every blast of fire, sometimes loses it for a moment and finds it again, and focus hunts in the dark. The shake never stops, from the first frame of every shot to its last. Never still, never locked-off, never gimbal-smooth, never a perfectly composed shot. States persist: the paving the fire touches stays scorched black and smoking, the smoke keeps drifting.




SHOT 1, 0.0s–3.1s. HER TAUNT. 50 degree lens, handheld at her chest height about three metres in front of her and a little below her, facing north; the dark stairs rise behind her and fill the frame. From the first frame she stands on the bottom step of the palace stairs, facing south toward the camera and toward the man far behind it, framed from the waist up, and she is ALREADY moving: swaying and lurching from side to side, her weight tipping left, then right, as if she can barely keep her balance, her head lolling, a breathless open-mouthed laugh, loose hair swinging across her face. At 1.2s she drops her chin, lets the laugh fall into a fixed grin and lifts both hands in front of her chest: her left hand by her cheek with the index and middle fingers pointing up together, her right hand open beside it with the fingers curled like claws. She glares over her fingers straight into the lens. At 2.4s she flings her right hand out to the side and swings her whole body round in one turn, a little off balance; for an instant her back is to the camera. The camera sways with her.




SHOT 2, 3.1s–3.5s. HER BLAST. 28 degree lens, handheld low just in front of the bottom step, facing north and tilted up past her toward the dark hall and its stairs behind her. She comes out of the turn with her right palm thrown forward, straight down the courtyard at the man, and a thick jet of blue fire blasts out from the air just in front of her palm straight at the camera and rolls over the lens. It is so bright that the exposure blows out to white with deep blue at its edges, and the heat knocks the camera back and shakes it hard.




SHOT 3, 3.5s–6.8s. HIS JUMP, HIS FIRE, OVER THE LENS: ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT, NO CUT. 28 degree lens, handheld very low on the paving about four metres in front of the man, tilted up at him against the dark roofline of the south gate and the last dull orange light. At 3.5s he is standing and winding up: both arms swing up, then out wide, then he drops into a crouch with his hands by his head. At 4.1s he jumps high, knees tucked, robe and sash flying; the camera tilts up hard to follow him into the sky and back down. At 4.6s he comes down right in front of the lens: his legs fill the frame and his right boot slams flat onto the stone a metre from the camera; the thud goes through the ground and the frame shudders hard and stumbles, then steadies. He rises out of the crouch and throws both arms straight up over his head, then at 5.4s swings them down hard to his sides: as his hands sweep down, fire streams out from the air just in front of each hand, DOWNWARD and outward, two long streams of real orange flame that hit the stone and curve forward low along the ground on both sides of him like wings, black smoke peeling off them. The two streams sweep in toward the camera and at 6.1s roll straight over the lens: the frame fills with streaming orange flame and black smoke with dark gaps, the brightest tongues clipping, while the camera staggers back from the heat.




SHOT 4, 6.8s–7.4s. THE TWO JETS, FAST. 28 degree lens, handheld from far away at the east side of the courtyard, at chest height, an extreme wide of the whole duelling ground: the dark hall and its stairs at the left, the south wall at the right, the sky a dark band of dull orange under blue-black cloud; the frame shakes in the wind. Two low, wide jets of real fire shoot toward each other across the paving at knee height, fast, each one thick, ragged and smoking, never a thin line: her blue fire from the left, starting at her small dark figure in front of the stairs, and his orange fire from the right, starting at his small dark figure near the south wall. Every fire is powerful and far too bright for the camera: where it leaves the bender and wherever it is thickest, it blows the exposure out, the core clips to pure white, the frame around it blooms, and its colour shows only at the edges. Every attack is aimed at the other fighter and drives toward them; nothing is thrown into the sky, and nothing fades out on the way. Every fire is a jet or a swung sweep of flame streaming from a hand or foot, like a flamethrower: never an explosion, a fireball, a puff, a ring, a swirl, a cloud, a fog or a flood. The fire suddenly lights everything for real: the paving, the pillars, the stairs, the smoke, their faces and clothes. The camera's exposure blows out and blooms, then struggles to adjust. The fire hits the camera: whenever it comes close, its heat, blast and roar reach the person holding the camera, and the frame shudders, flinches and backs away.




REALISM

It must look like real dusk footage from a real camera, never like visual effects or a film with a lighting crew. The fire behaves like real fire: it has weight, it is pushed by the wind, it throws off smoke, it is uneven and ragged, and it lights the real surroundings. No glowing energy, no beams, no clean lines, no sparkles or particles, no glowing balls or domes, no magic aura. Their skin never glows, and their hands are never on fire: the flame always starts a little in front of the hand. Clothes hang heavy and swing with their weight; boots scuff and slide on the stone.




LIGHTING

There is NO light on them except what a real dusk gives: no key light, no fill light, no rim light, no studio light, nothing evenly lit. Everything is underexposed and moody: their faces are mostly in shadow; in the wide shots they are dark shapes against the last light. The only strong light in the whole video comes from the fire, for exactly as long as it burns. Deep blacks, with detail barely holding in the shadows.




AUDIO

Fire and environment sounds and non-verbal sounds only. No music. Throughout: wind across the courtyard. Shot 1: wind buffeting the microphone high up, a deep boom where the fires meet far below, then the tearing roar of both fires splitting apart. Shot 2: the deafening roar of the giant blue river and his jet, clipping the microphone. Shot 3: the roar of his fire close to the microphone. Nobody says any words.




CHARACTER ACTING

The woman: far away at the north end, too small to see; only her blue fire reaches him.

The man: calm, focused and stubborn under pressure. Jaw set, mouth closed (no teeth showing), breathing through his nose, eyes locked on her; never grimacing or snarling. His stance is planted and powerful; his back foot slides a little and digs in again.




STYLE

Real handheld dusk footage shot on a 1990s 35mm film camera pushed hard for the dark: an old camera look, not an antique film. Vintage spherical lenses with soft edges and heavy vignetting. Kodak colour negative underexposed: muddy blue-black shadows, heavy visible grain, restrained colour, natural skin. Real film halation only around the fire. Imperfect observational framing, the subject sometimes partly cut off. Not a video game, not CGI, not a VFX shot, not an anime, not a cartoon, not a commercial.




CAMERA ON HIS FACE

Whenever his face is close, the camera is at or slightly above the level of his eyes at a three-quarter angle, never below his chin looking up his nose.




QUALITY

Stable identity from first frame to last: The man matches @Image 1 and @Image 2, the woman matches @Image 3. Correct anatomy, hands with five fingers, fists that stay fists. The same courtyard throughout.




POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS

SPEED LOCK: normal real-time speed; any slow motion = failed take. REAL FIRE LOCK: every fire is real turbulent flame with black smoke that lights its surroundings; a beam, glowing energy, a glowing ball or dome, a neon line, particles or a burning or glowing hand = failed take. LIGHT LOCK: dusk, underexposed; no studio, key or rim light; an evenly lit face = failed take. CAMERA LOCK: shaky handheld in every shot to its last frame; a steady, locked-off or smooth shot = failed take. AIM LOCK: every attack is aimed at the other fighter; fire thrown into the sky = failed take. VOICE LOCK: no spoken words. ONLY THESE TWO PEOPLE. NO TEXT on screen.

What made the fire look real
The world is dark and the fire is the only real light. When it fires, it lights up the stone, the smoke and your face, and then it's dark again.
It's blown out. Big fire is too bright for a camera, so the middle clips to white and the picture blooms, then the exposure struggles back.
It hits the camera. The camera shakes and backs away from the heat in every shot. The shake is what makes it feel like someone was really there filming.
The fire is a jet, like a real flamethrower, with black smoke peeling off it. Never fireballs, beams or glowing energy, and never words like "electric" or "energy" in the prompt.
The fire starts in the air just in front of the fist. A burning or glowing hand always looks fake.
Every attack is aimed at the other person. Fire thrown into the sky looks pointless.
It's filmed like an old 35mm film camera: grain, soft edges, a dark vignette, and a glow only around the fire.
Don't use the cartoon as a video reference. It copies the moves, but it also copies the cartoon fire.
