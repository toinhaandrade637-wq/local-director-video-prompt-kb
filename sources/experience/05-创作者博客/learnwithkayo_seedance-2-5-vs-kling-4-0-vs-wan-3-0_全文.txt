Too many AI video models? I tested the three most popular ones on Higgsfield. Here's which one to use for what, and what each one costs.

The quick answer
Seedance 2.5: films and anything with high production value. The best overall quality, and the highest price.
Kling 4.0: transitions, motion control and projects with a lot of generations. Great quality at about half the price of Seedance.
Wan 3.0: animations and hypermotion. About 4.5 times cheaper than Seedance.
Same prompt, three models

I ran the exact same prompt on all three models, with the same settings. That's the fairest way to see the difference.

If you compare models yourself, keep everything else the same: resolution, length and references. Otherwise you're comparing settings, not models.

Seedance 2.5: the quality winner

If you don't care about the cost and just look at the overall quality, this is the clear winner. Use it for films, ads and anything with high production value. It comes with a steeper price than the other two.

What it can do: up to 50 references (images, videos and audio), clips up to 30 seconds, and it can edit or extend a video you already have.

With a video reference, describe only what changes. When I described the whole scene, Seedance redrew everything.

Kling 4.0: transitions and motion control

Great overall quality, amazing for transitions and motion control, and about half the price of Seedance. If your project needs a lot of generations, this one is perfect.

What it can do: up to 10 keyframes to control the key moments, up to 15 references, clips up to 30 seconds, and it can edit a video you already have.

You get fewer references than in Seedance: 15 instead of 50. If your scene needs more, use Seedance.

Wan 3.0: animations and hypermotion

Amazing for animations and hypermotion, and about 4.5 times cheaper than Seedance. If you're creating a lot of animations and want to post a lot without spending much, this one is for you.

What it can do: start from text, a first and last frame, or references (images, video and audio), clips up to 30 seconds, with sound.

What they cost

At the same resolution and length, Seedance 2.5 costs the most. Kling 4.0 costs about half of that. Wan 3.0 is about 4.5 times cheaper than Seedance.

The exact credit cost shows on the Generate button before you start, so you always know what a clip costs.

Try them yourself

Go to higgsfield.ai, click Create Video, choose your model, write your prompt and hit generate. All three models are in the same place, so you can switch between them for every shot.
