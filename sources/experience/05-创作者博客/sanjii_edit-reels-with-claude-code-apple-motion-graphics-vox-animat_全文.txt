Before You Start

This is the exact workflow behind the reel: raw talking-head clips edited end to end by Claude Code. The cuts, the Apple-style motion graphics, the Vox animation, the new color grade and the massive word-by-word captions all come from one sentence typed into Claude. Editing one minute of video used to take 12 hours in a timeline; this way it takes about 10 minutes.




You need three things: Claude Code, your raw clips and one reference video in the style you want. The skill handles everything else. It carries the rules a good editor keeps in their head, reads your reference, and writes the whole edit for you: the cut list, the grade, the captions, the motion graphics and the final render.




Under the hood it all runs on your machine: ffmpeg for the cuts, the grade and the sound, Whisper for word-level timing, and Remotion for the motion graphics and captions. Claude installs whatever is missing on the first run.

Stage 1 — The Inputs
Your Raw Clips

Put everything in one folder and open Claude Code there:

my-reel/
  raw/            your clips, any number, any order
  reference.mp4   one reel in the style you want
  brand/          optional: font files, logo, colors
  music/          optional: one track
  sfx/            optional: whoosh, pop and click sounds
Film vertical if you can. Horizontal clips work too: Claude crops around your face.
Don't stop between takes. Flubbed a line? Pause and say it again. The skill keeps the last clean take and cuts every pause.
Sound beats picture. A clip-on mic in a quiet room matters more than the camera.
Leave room above your head. That's where the motion graphics go, and the captions sit below your face.
The Reference Video

One reel, 15–60 seconds long, that looks the way you want yours to look. Claude can't watch video, so it reads the reference frame by frame and measures it: how often it cuts, how big the captions are and where they sit, how warm and contrasty the color is, what kind of graphics show up and how often. That becomes a style card for your edit, and anything you say in the prompt overrides it.




No reference? The skill falls back to a clean Apple look with bold captions, but you get far closer to what you want with one.

Optional Extras
Brand font files, colors (hex) and logo.
A music track. Claude ducks it under your voice and masters the reel to streaming loudness.
Sound effects. Claude only uses what's in the folder: no sfx folder, no sound effects.
Missing a B-roll shot or an image for the Vox segment? With the Higgsfield MCP connected to Claude, the skill generates it in 9:16 and drops it into the edit.
Stage 2 — The Prompting Framework

Download the skill and unzip it into ~/.claude/skills/ (you'll get a reel-editor folder there), then start Claude Code in your project folder. Claude picks the skill up on its own whenever you ask it to edit footage. It needs Claude Code rather than the chat app, because it runs ffmpeg and renders the video on your computer.

What's inside is everything an editor and a motion designer carry around in their heads:

The cut. Every pause longer than about a third of a second goes. When you repeat a line, the last clean take stays. Cuts land between words, never inside one.
The hook. The first two seconds get the biggest caption, a punch-in and a motion graphic. No logo, no intro.
Pacing. Something changes on screen every 2–4 seconds: a punch-in, a graphic or a caption emphasis. Punch-ins also hide the jump cuts.
Apple-style motion graphics. Frosted glass cards over your footage, clean type, one accent color, and motion that glides in and settles without a bounce.
Vox-style animation. Paper textures, you as a photo print or a cut-out, a yellow highlighter, hand-drawn arrows and film grain, all moving on twos like stop-motion.
Color. Six tested looks, from clean to cinematic, matched to your reference with skin tones protected. The new grade can sweep in behind a line of light.
Captions. One to three words at a time, huge and bold, with the active word highlighted, landing on the exact frame you say it. They can even sit behind you.
Tech specs. 1080 × 1920, safe zones that keep text clear of the Reels and TikTok buttons, and audio mastered to −14 LUFS.

Two strict rules before you hit enter:

Always give it a reference. Without one, Claude guesses the style. With one, it copies the pacing, captions and color you actually want.
Stills before the render. The skill checks key frames first (the hook, every graphic, every transition), so mistakes get fixed before the full render instead of after it.
