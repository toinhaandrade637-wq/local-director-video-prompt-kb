One take in an empty white room can became four different worlds.

This is how I used Higgsfield Genjutsu to turn one flat studio take into a cinematic scene. The idea came first. Then I shot the footage already knowing what every object in the frame was going to become.




THE ORIGINAL FOOTAGE




An empty white studio, one camera on a tripod, one take. On set: a white round table, my laptop, a dress-form mannequin, a wicker basket on a pedestal, and one small fern on the floor. Nothing else. I talk to camera through the whole take, so the voice and the performance come from the same moment.

The camera never change angle or cut.
