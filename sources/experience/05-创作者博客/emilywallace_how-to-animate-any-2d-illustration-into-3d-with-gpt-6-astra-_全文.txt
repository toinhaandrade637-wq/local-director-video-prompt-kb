Ready to Make Your Own?

Start with one clear character design and a small action. Prepare useful references, build the scene, test the rig and review the motion before adding a generative finishing pass.

For each result, ask three questions: Do the proportions look right? Does the movement feel natural? Does it keep the intended look? A model can get the process moving, but an animator’s eye still decides what needs fixing.

Keep the editable Blender scene, compare each new pass against the original illustration, and make the next request about a specific visible problem.
