No followers, no face, and no editing experience required!

Did you know you can make money by turning existing videos into short-form clips without filming anything yourself?

It's called clipping.

Platforms like Whop offer Content Rewards campaigns where creators can get paid for turning podcasts, interviews, and other videos into short-form content for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

And with Higgsfield Katana + Claude, you can use AI to handle much of the editing for you.

Here's exactly how to get started.

What You'll Need

Before starting, create an account on these three platforms:

1. Higgsfield AI — Your AI Clipping Tool

Sign Up for Higgsfield Here →

Create your Higgsfield account first. You'll use Katana to edit your clips with AI.

2. Whop — Find Paid Clipping Campaigns

Create a Whop Account →

Find campaigns that pay you based on the eligible views your clips generate.

3. Claude — Run Higgsfield Katana

Create a Claude Account →

You'll use Claude to give Katana your footage and editing instructions.

Step 1: Find a Clipping Campaign on Whop

Head over to Whop.com and:

Click Discover → Content Rewards.
Browse the available campaigns.
Look for campaigns with high CPMs and enough remaining budget.

CPM means how much you earn per 1,000 eligible views.

For example, a campaign paying $1.75 CPM, with a $1,500 maximum payout per video, could earn you:

Step 2: Join the Campaign and Download the Footage

Once you've found a campaign, read its requirements and join.

Download the provided source footage, which might include podcasts, interviews, or other long-form videos.

This is the footage you'll turn into short-form clips.

Make sure the campaign allows you to edit and publish the material.

Step 3: Find a Viral Reference Video

Go to Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts and find a viral video covering the same topic as your source footage, or at least something in the same niche.

Look for videos that meet these two criteria:

Posted within the last 30 days
Over 1 million views

This helps you identify what's currently performing well instead of guessing.

Once you've found one, save it as your reference video, where permitted. Katana will use its editing style as inspiration for your clip.

Step 4: Install Higgsfield Katana in Claude

If you haven't already, sign up for Higgsfield here →.

Then:

Navigate to Katana.
Click Install Higgsfield in Claude.
Follow the installation instructions.
Open Claude.
Step 5: Upload Your Videos to Katana

Inside Claude, start a new conversation and type:

/katana

Then provide:

1. Source footage: The original video you downloaded from Whop.

2. Viral reference: The video you found with over 1 million views in the last 30 days.

Your source footage provides the content, while your viral reference provides the editing style.

Step 6: Edit Your Clip With AI

Once you've uploaded both videos, give Katana instructions to recreate the editing style of your viral reference.

Copy and Paste This Prompt Into Claude
Use Higgsfield Katana to turn my source footage into a high-quality, vertical short-form clip.


>


Video 1 is my source footage, and Video 2 is a viral reference.


>


Analyze the reference video's editing style, including its cuts, pacing, captions, zooms, transitions, music, and sound design.


>


Apply a similar editing style to my source footage while preserving the speaker's original message and context.


>


Remove unnecessary pauses, add synchronized captions, and format the video in 9:16 for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.



>


Give me a polished, ready-to-review final clip.

Katana will use your footage and reference to guide the edit.

Step 7: Review and Download Your Clip

Watch your finished video and check that:

Captions are accurate and synchronized.
Cuts, transitions, and audio sound natural.
The video is properly formatted in 9:16.
The speaker's message remains accurate.
The clip follows the campaign's requirements.

If something needs fixing, ask Katana to revise it.

Once you're happy with the result, download your clip.

Step 8: Post Your Clip and Submit It to Whop

IMPORTANT: Check the campaign's posting requirements before publishing.

Every campaign has different rules, including required hashtags, mentions, video lengths, approved platforms, and account eligibility.

If you don't follow these requirements, your submission could be rejected, and you might not get paid—even if your video goes viral.

Once you've checked everything:

Post your clip on an approved social media platform.
Copy the link to your published video.
Return to the Whop campaign.
Submit your video link.
Monitor your submission status and eligible views.

Your clip may need approval before it qualifies for payment.

Step 9: Repeat the Process

Once you've completed your first clip, repeat the workflow:

Find a Campaign → Download Footage → Find a Viral Reference → Edit With Katana → Post → Submit to Whop

Try different campaigns, topics, and reference videos to see what performs best.

Always follow each campaign's rules and avoid posting prohibited duplicate content.

Ready to Start Clipping?

You don't need to film yourself or become a professional video editor to get started.

With Whop, you can find paid clipping opportunities. With Higgsfield Katana and Claude, you can streamline the editing process.
