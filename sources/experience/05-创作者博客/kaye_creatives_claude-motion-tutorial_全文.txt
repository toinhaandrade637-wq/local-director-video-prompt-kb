Step 1. Setup
Part 1. Setting up Higgsfield MCP




First, go to Higgsfield and find ✨MCP✨ button on the top bar ⤵️







In the opened page click on ✨Claude✨ → tap ✨Connect Higgsfield✨ button







It will automatically open your Claude → tap ✨Continue✨







Then scroll to the very end of the window and tap ✨Add✨




Part 2. Adding skill

Now we need to upload the skill that will make the edit




Download this skill ⤵️

Claude_edit_skill.zip
ZIP







Then go back to Claude → find ✨Customize✨ on the left panel







On the top bar of opened page tap ✨+ Add✨ → then tap ✨Upload Skill✨







Drag the skill here and tap ✨Upload✨




And that's it - we finally added the skill that is ready to work




We are finally set up, so let's start ✨creating✨
