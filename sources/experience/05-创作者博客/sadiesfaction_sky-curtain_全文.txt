SCENE CONTEXT

A green summer landscape where the sky is secretly a huge soft blue fabric sheet printed with clouds, laid over the world like a bedcover. Near the horizon the sky-fabric stirs by itself. A person walks to it, lifts the hem like a curtain, slips under it — and the moment they let go, the heavy fabric drops shut, hiding them and the sunlit world completely.




ACTIVE REFERENCES

@image_1: the person. Match their face, apparent age, gender presentation, body proportions, hairstyle, clothing and footwear to this reference. Ignore the reference photo's background and props.




LOCATION MAP

A meadow of tall green grass fills the foreground, rising gently toward a low crest in the mid-ground. The sky above is not a real sky: it is a vast heavy fabric sheet that carries a photorealistic sky image across its surface — a deep saturated cobalt-blue gradient, paler near the horizon, with volumetric cumulus clouds: bright sunlit white tops, soft gray-blue shadowed undersides, crisp billowing edges. The illusion breaks only where the material behaves as fabric: heavy folds, wrinkles and draped creases run through the sky image, bending the realistic clouds along the cloth. Its lower hem meets the land along the crest, ending in a visible cloth edge that sags in soft scallops where it rests on the grass. Hidden behind the hem lie bright sunlit hills and a low golden sun, in warmer and clearer light than the dimmed meadow.




FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING

First frame: the meadow and the fabric sky are in view, the hem line running along the crest in the upper-mid frame. The person is already in the mid-ground on the right side of the frame at x ~72%, y ~70%, wading through the tall grass toward the hem, seen at three-quarters from behind, mid-step. At the hem near x ~65%, directly ahead of them, the sky-fabric gives a slow visible ripple, like a bedsheet moving over something breathing, and their head is turned toward that spot. The left half of the frame holds the open meadow and the long sagging hem line.




FORMAT MODE

A single continuous shot, no cuts.




OPTICS

84° diagonal field of view, classic wide rectilinear character. The grass, the person and the full hem line stay in frame; the fabric folds of the sky stay readable. Lens locked for the whole shot.




CAMERA

Locked-off camera at chest height (about 1.3 m) in the meadow, level with no roll, aimed at the crest. The camera stays on the meadow side of the fabric and never passes under it. It never moves.




ACTION TIMING

0–2.5s: they wade through the tall grass toward the rippling spot at the hem, grass parting around their legs, their eyes on the cloth.

2.5–4.5s: reaching the hem, they bend, take the cloth edge in both hands and heave it up over their head like a heavy curtain. A bright gap opens under it: warm golden sunlight floods out over them and the grass around them.

4.5–5.5s: holding the hem up with one hand, they duck through under it to the far side — their body passes behind the raised cloth — and they release it.

5.5–7s: the instant they let go, the heavy fabric drops: the hem swings straight down under its own weight in under a second and lands on the grass with a soft billow, sealing the gap and cutting off the golden light. They are gone from view behind the cloth — their run is never shown.

7–12s: the fabric sways once from the impact and settles still. The sky-sheet hangs whole again over the quiet dimmed meadow, a few last folds relaxing through the printed clouds as the shot ends.




PHYSICS

The sky behaves as enormous heavy cloth. Lifting takes visible effort — their shoulders and back engage, their clothing pulls and folds naturally with the effort, the hem sags between their hands. The drop is pure gravity and fast: released, the hem falls straight down like a heavy stage curtain, hits the grass, sends one soft wave up the cloth, and stops. The printed clouds are fixed to the fabric and deform only with its folds, never drifting on their own. The gap's golden light exists only while the hem is up and vanishes the moment it lands.




LIGHTING

Soft diffuse daylight under the fabric sky, slightly dimmed and cool like light through a curtain, the cloth's folds casting soft shadow bands across the printed clouds. The tall grass glows backlit yellow-green at the tips against the deep blue. While the hem is raised, warm golden sunlight pours from the gap over them and the grass; the drop cuts it off at once, returning the whole frame to even cool dimness.




AUDIO

Meadow wind, grass brushing their clothing, a deep textile rustle as the fabric lifts, then a fast heavy whump as the cloth drops and lands, the warm ambience cut dead with it. No music.




STYLE

Photorealistic magic-realist art photography in motion on soft vintage film stock: deep saturated cobalt blue and luminous white, the person's reference outfit retaining its own colours and textures, backlit yellow-green grass, gentle contrast, slightly lifted blacks, fine film grain, subtle halation around the bright clouds and the glowing gap.




QUALITY

Sharp clarity, stable picture, rich natural colours, readable fabric folds, photorealistic cloud detail on the cloth, controlled highlights in the gap. Stable natural motion cadence with subtle motion blur on the lifting cloth and the fast drop. No ghosting, no flicker.




POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS

Exactly one person appears, identical to @image_1. The sky is one continuous fabric sheet carrying a photorealistic cloud image; the clouds deform only with the cloth's folds. The sequence is strict: they lift the hem, duck under to the far side, release it, and the fabric drops immediately and seals shut. After the drop they are completely hidden behind the cloth and never visible again — no running figure, no silhouette, no re-opening. The gap never re-opens once closed. The camera stays on the near side of the fabric and never moves or cuts.
