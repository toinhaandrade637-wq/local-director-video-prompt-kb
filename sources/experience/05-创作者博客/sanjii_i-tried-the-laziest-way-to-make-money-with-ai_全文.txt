Before You Start

This is the full prompt library from our 7-day AI influencer experiment. Build the assets first: a profile, a character sheet and a location for each influencer. They show up in every clip, so building them once keeps the face and the world consistent from post to post.




Claude Opus 5.5 writes the profiles, scripts and the digital product. The prompts below use Nano Banana Pro for character sheets and locations, Seedance 2.5 for the clips, and Higgsfield's audio tool for the voices. The Claude Skills write the scripts and the video prompts for you, so all you bring is the idea.

The Voices

What it does: Paste each description into Higgsfield's audio section together with the character's image and a short test line, such as "Hi, my name is Martha."

Leo

A very old American man from the rural Midwest, over a hundred years old. Low, gravelly, slightly breathy voice with gentle warmth. Speaks slowly, with unhurried pauses, as if every sentence has been tested for decades. Calm, kind and a little amused. Close mic, quiet room, no music.

Martha

A glamorous 72-year-old American woman. Smooth, low, polished voice with a hint of rasp. Crisp, deliberate delivery with a small pause before the punchline, like she has already decided the answer. Confident, dry and amused, never rushed. Close mic, dry room, no music.

Kira

A 24-year-old American woman. Bright, clear voice with light vocal fry at the ends of sentences. Fast, casual, conversational pace, like she is telling her best friend what happened last night. Playful, a little dramatic, very expressive. Close mic, quiet room, no music.
Stage 2 — The Prompting Framework
Connect Higgsfield to Claude. In Higgsfield, click MCP and copy the link. In Claude, open Customize, go to Connectors, choose Add, then Add Custom Connector. Paste the link and click Add.
Install the skills. In Claude, open Customize, choose Add, then Upload a skill, and drop the files in.
Call a skill. Type a forward slash in the chat and pick the skill you need: content-engine for post ideas and scripts, video-prompt to turn a simple idea into a detailed video prompt, and digital-product for the workbook.
Create the Elements. Save each character sheet and location from Stage 1 as an Element, named exactly as below. Every video prompt calls them by name.
@LEO — Leo's character sheet
@LEO_KITCHEN — Leo's farmhouse kitchen
@MARTHA — Martha's character sheet
@MARTHA_TERRACE — Martha's afternoon-tea terrace
@KIRA — Kira's character sheet
@KIRA_APARTMENT — Kira's living room

Keep everything in the same Claude chat where you created the characters, so Claude always knows who it is writing for.

Scene 1 — Kira: The 1 A.M. Text

Style header: The first paragraph locks the look for every scene: a vertical phone-style video with natural light, a real room and real skin. Reuse it word for word in every clip. There's no music in any prompt; add it in the edit.

What it does: Animates a silent reaction shot of Kira on her sofa, with clean space at the top for a caption you add in the editor, such as "when he texts 'wyd' at 1am".

Vertical 9:16 video. It looks like a real creator filmed it on a phone: natural light, a real lived-in space, slight handheld movement, true-to-life color, real skin texture with pores. No music, no on-screen text, no subtitles.




@KIRA in @KIRA_APARTMENT at night, curled up on the sofa in her oversized cream sweater, lit by the table lamp and her phone screen.

0–3 s: she scrolls, bored. The phone buzzes and she glances at the screen.

3–6 s: she reads it, pauses, slowly lowers the phone and stares straight into the camera, flat and unimpressed, one eyebrow raised.

6–10 s: she exhales through her nose, turns the phone face down on the cushion and pulls the blanket up to her chin, trying not to smile.




Static camera at sofa height, medium shot. Room tone only: the phone buzz and the rustle of fabric. Keep the top third of the frame clean for a text caption.
