VIDEO — KARIMA STRANDED ON TRACK, THE CRANE RISE AND THE ARC TO HER FACE (V2)




SCENE CONTEXT: A premium commercial spot. Karima's car has broken down and stopped dead in the middle of the circuit while the race goes on around her. One single unbroken shot: it opens low and wide on the side of the stopped car with her standing beside it, rival cars tearing past, she kicks the tyre three times in fury and plants both hands on her hips; the camera cranes steadily up the side of the car revealing the smoking engine, then arcs smoothly around from the side to face her head-on as she pinches the bridge of her nose with her eyes shut — and finally she lowers her hand, opens her eyes, and her furious expression turns sly as her gaze slides sideways. She has a plan.




FORMAT

ONE single continuous shot, 13 seconds, with NO cuts, NO dissolves and NO jumps of any kind. 2.39:1. Full photorealism, premium commercial cinematography. No on-screen text, no captions, no subtitles, no watermark, no music baked into the audio.




THE NINE MOST IMPORTANT RULES ON THIS SHOT




RULE A — ONE UNBROKEN TAKE. NO CUTS ANYWHERE. The entire 13 seconds is a single continuous camera move: a low wide side view, a steady vertical crane rise, and then a smooth horizontal arc around to her face, all flowing into one another without a single edit, dissolve, jump or reframe.

IF THERE IS ANY CUT, DISSOLVE, JUMP OR VISIBLE EDIT AT ANY POINT, THE GENERATION IS WRONG. HARD FAILURE CONDITION.




RULE B — THE OPENING IS A WIDE, LOGICAL LOW SIDE SHOT, NOT A CRAMPED MACRO. THIS IS A CORRECTION. The shot begins at a comfortable, believable camera distance from the car — low to the ground but far enough back that the framing makes physical sense, taking in both wheels on the cockpit side, a good length of the car's flank and floor between them, and Karima standing beside it from the boots up to roughly her waist. It is a wide, grounded, establishing low angle — never a tight macro pressed between the tyres where no camera could physically sit.

IF THE OPENING FRAME IS A CRAMPED EXTREME CLOSE-UP WEDGED BETWEEN THE WHEELS, OR IF THE CAMERA POSITION IS PHYSICALLY IMPOSSIBLE, THE GENERATION IS WRONG.




RULE C — SHE KICKS THE TYRE THREE TIMES IN FURY, THEN PLANTS BOTH HANDS ON HER HIPS. THIS IS A CORRECTION AND IT IS MANDATORY. She does NOT hit the car with her hand. Instead her boot slams into the tyre three separate times — three distinct, hard, angry kicks, each one shoving her body back off the wheel with the recoil, her weight rocking as she plants and swings again. After the third kick she steps back, squares up and drops both gloved hands onto her hips, standing there seething.

IF SHE SLAPS OR PUNCHES THE CAR WITH HER HANDS, IF SHE KICKS FEWER OR MORE THAN THREE TIMES, IF THE KICKS ARE SOFT OR HALF-HEARTED, OR IF SHE DOES NOT END WITH BOTH HANDS ON HER HIPS, THE GENERATION IS WRONG. HARD FAILURE CONDITION.




RULE D — DURING THE CRANE RISE ONE HAND STAYS ON HER HIP AND THE OTHER GOES TO THE BRIDGE OF HER NOSE. As the camera climbs, her left hand remains planted on her hip for the rest of the shot, while her right hand lifts and pinches the bridge of her nose, her eyes closing as it lands.

IF BOTH HANDS LEAVE HER HIPS, IF BOTH HANDS GO TO HER FACE, OR IF THE HIP HAND DROPS AWAY, THE GENERATION IS WRONG.




RULE E — AFTER THE RISE THE CAMERA ARCS SMOOTHLY FROM THE SIDE AROUND TO A HEAD-ON VIEW OF HER FACE. THIS IS A CORRECTION. Having reached her head height, the camera does not stop at the side angle: it continues in one seamless motion into a slow, smooth horizontal arc around her, travelling from the strict side profile around to directly in front of her, ending square-on to her face in a chest-up framing. The move is gentle, weighted and continuous, with a small natural settle at the end — never a snap, never a whip, never a cut.

IF THE CAMERA STOPS AT THE SIDE, IF IT CUTS TO A FRONTAL ANGLE INSTEAD OF ARCING, OR IF THE ARC IS FAST, JERKY OR INCOMPLETE, THE GENERATION IS WRONG. HARD FAILURE CONDITION.




RULE F — THE FINAL BEAT IS A CLEAR EXPRESSION CHANGE: TENSE AND FURIOUS, THEN SLY AND SCHEMING, WITH HER EYES SLIDING SIDEWAYS. With the camera settled in front of her, she slowly lowers her hand from her nose and opens her eyes. Her brows release from a hard tense furrow, the corner of her mouth lifts a fraction, and the whole face resolves into a knowing, cunning, slightly mischievous look — she has thought of something. Her eyes then deliberately slide off to one side, not looking at the camera, as if measuring up whatever she has just decided to do. Hold that to the final frame.

IF THE HAND DROPS FAST, IF THE EYES SNAP OPEN INSTEAD OF OPENING SLOWLY, IF THE BROWS STAY TENSE, IF THE SLY EXPRESSION IS MISSING, OR IF HER EYES DO NOT SLIDE SIDEWAYS AT THE END, THE GENERATION IS WRONG. HARD FAILURE CONDITION.




RULE G — THE STADIUM AND THE WEATHER ARE NEVER STATIC. THIS IS MANDATORY. At every moment the world is alive: the packed grandstands in constant motion with thousands of spectators shouting, arms raised, waving, jumping, phones held up, flags and banners flapping; a steady medium wind moving continuously through frame, whipping her loose hair, dragging the smoke sideways, rippling banners and fencing tape and lifting dust off the tarmac. Nothing in the background is ever frozen or standing still.

IF THE CROWD IS MOTIONLESS, IF THE FLAGS AND BANNERS DO NOT MOVE, IF HER HAIR IS STILL, OR IF THE BACKGROUND READS AS A STATIC PLATE, THE GENERATION IS WRONG. HARD FAILURE CONDITION.




RULE H — THE CROWD AND BACKGROUND STAY OUT OF FOCUS THROUGHOUT, AND RIVAL CARS SCREAM PAST EARLY WHILE HERS SITS DEAD STILL. The grandstands, spectators, light towers, barriers and passing cars are always soft and blurred, never sharp, never readable as individual faces. Early in the shot rival cars in different liveries tear through the frame at enormous speed — one across the near foreground, others flashing behind her stationary car — heavily motion-blurred, throwing dust and a visible wind blast. Her own car never moves a millimetre.

IF THE BACKGROUND COMES INTO SHARP FOCUS, IF NO CARS PASS, IF THEY ARE SLOW OR SHARP, OR IF HER OWN CAR MOVES, THE GENERATION IS WRONG.




RULE I — THE SCALE BETWEEN KARIMA AND THE CAR MUST BE PHYSICALLY CORRECT. BANS

Cut inversions: any cut, dissolve, jump, reframe or visible edit anywhere in the 13 seconds — hard rule.

Opening inversions: a cramped macro wedged between the tyres, a physically impossible camera position, or an opening that is not a wide grounded low side angle.

Action inversions: hitting the car with a hand, fewer or more than three kicks, soft kicks, or failing to end the kicks with both hands on her hips.

Hand inversions: both hands leaving her hips during the rise, both hands going to her face, or the hip hand dropping away.

Camera inversions: stopping at the side instead of arcing to the front, cutting to a frontal angle, a fast or jerky arc, panning, handheld shake, or crossing to the other side of the car.

Expression inversions: a fast hand drop, eyes snapping open instead of opening slowly, brows that stay tense, a missing sly look, or eyes that do not slide sideways at the end.

Background inversions: a motionless or frozen crowd, still flags and banners, unmoving hair, a static background plate, or a sharply focused crowd.

Car inversions: her own car moving; passing cars that are slow or sharply rendered; a car of the wrong scale against her height.

Smoke inversions: no smoke, a thin wisp, a single puff, smoke from the front of the car, or smoke rising straight up unaffected by wind.

Colour inversions: any orange or amber cast on the asphalt, a warm grade, a daylight or black-night sky.

Livery inversions: any logo, sponsor mark, team name or readable text on her car, her suit or the barriers.

Lens inversions: lens flares, light streaks, floating round bokeh circles, a flat undistorted frame with no anamorphic character.

Style inversions: no illustration, no painterly rendering, no CGI-clean surfaces, no video-game look, no HDR over-processing.

General: no on-screen text, no logos, no watermark, no music baked into the audio.

Logo section: pure flat even white, no gradient, no vignette, no shadow, no texture — a clean brand field.




AUDIO

Diegetic only through the live section — the hammering drum of hooves, his deep chesty bellow as he crosses the line, the screaming engines of the pursuing cars falling away behind, the eruption of the crowd, wind. As the slow motion deepens the sound stretches and drops in pitch, the crowd smearing into a long low roar. At the freeze, everything cuts to near-silence except one last low resonant note, and the logo section plays clean and silent. No music track baked in, no subtitles, no on-screen text other than the wordmark in the final lockup.




COUNT

Exactly one bull with one rider in the live section, one finish line crossed first, one beaten field behind, one speed ramp, one match frame, one wash to white, two graphic bulls and one disc in the lockup, one pull-back, zero cuts, zero visible crew, zero marshals on the racing surface.




BANS

Angle inversions: any three-quarter, front, rear or orbiting view during the live section; the bull drifting in frame while tracking.

Pose inversions: a high head, horns not pointing forward and down, a hanging or lowered tail, legs not in the folded-front and extended-rear charging position, or any hoof touching the ground at the match frame — hard rule.

Ramp inversions: constant speed throughout, an abrupt jump into slow motion, or slow motion that never reaches near-stillness.

Transition inversions: a hard cut, a fade to black, or an environment that vanishes without the progressive wash and flattening.

Lockup inversions: only one bull, a non-mirrored second bull, bulls that do not meet head-to-head, a missing or misplaced gold disc, a push-in instead of a pull-back, or an off-centre final frame.

Logo inversions: any redrawing, reshaping, recolouring, rotation, cropping, stretching or modification of the mark; any generated or garbled lettering — hard rule.

Result inversions: a finish line that is not visibly crossed, a rival crossing first or alongside, or no pursuing field behind.

Crowd inversions: a motionless or frozen crowd before the deliberate ramp, still flags, or a sharply focused crowd.

Scale inversions: a bull the size of a car, a bull shorter than the cars, or Karima not riding above the pursuing cockpit line.

Colour inversions: any orange or amber cast on the asphalt, a warm grade, a daylight or black-night sky, a hide reading brown or grey, or an off-white or textured logo field.

Anatomy inversions: a missing neck crest, a missing dewlap, short or modest horns, uncloven hooves, a cow or steer instead of a mature bull; any drool, foam or distressed expression.

Lens inversions: lens flares, light streaks, floating round bokeh circles, or a live-action frame with no anamorphic character.

Style inversions: no illustration or painterly rendering in the live section, no CGI-clean fur, no cartoon, no anthropomorphic expression.

General: no watermark, no music baked into the audio, no on-screen text other than the official wordmark in the final lockup.
