Seedance 2.5 video prompt:




SCENE CONTEXT




Three adult friends get ready together for a costume party in a cozy bedroom. They apply makeup, help each other with their costumes, then gather for a playful camera selfie. The mood is warm, spontaneous and mischievous, like a candid home video between close friends.




Create a 22-second video that follows the action and character continuity below. Use natural cuts between moments. Keep the same three women throughout; costume changes happen between shots, never as magical transformations.







ACTIVE REFERENCES — USE THIS EXACT ORDER




@Image 1 — Bedroom reference. Use it for the dusty-pink bedroom, canopy bed, sheer curtains, vanity area and warm fairy lights.




@Image 2 — Brunette friend’s appearance before the costume change. Use it for her face, long straight dark hair, body proportions and casual white outfit.




@Image 3 — The same brunette friend’s vampire costume. Use it for the black dress and dramatic high collar with red lining.




@Image 4 — Red-haired friend’s appearance before the costume change. Use it for her copper-red hair and turquoise tracksuit with white stripes.




@Image 5 — The same red-haired friend’s vintage clown costume. Use it for the cream ruffled blouse, burgundy skirt and curled copper-red hairstyle.




@Image 6 — Curly-haired friend’s appearance before the costume change. Use it for her face, warm brown skin, voluminous dark curls and casual party-preparation look.




@Image 7 — The same curly-haired friend’s werewolf costume. Use it for the zebra-striped top, purple skirt, furry details, ears and black boots.




Keep each woman’s identity consistent with her references. Do not swap faces, hairstyles, costumes or roles between the friends.







LOCATION AND FIRST FRAME




The entire video takes place in the warm, dusty-pink bedroom from @Image 1. A canopy bed with white sheer curtains and small fairy lights fills part of the background. A vanity mirror, makeup and everyday beauty items are visible nearby.




Begin close to the vanity with all three friends gathered in frame:

- The red-haired friend wears her turquoise tracksuit from @Image 4.

- The curly-haired friend wears her casual preparation outfit from @Image 6.

- The brunette wears her white casual outfit from @Image 2.




The room feels lived-in and intimate, with warm practical lighting and a slightly messy party-preparation atmosphere.







FORMAT MODE




Exactly 22 seconds. Playful, candid costume-party preparation montage with natural cuts between close-ups, mirror shots and one wider bedroom shot.




Real-time movement, no slow motion and no speed ramps. Preserve the original horizontal framing. Keep the women recognizable throughout and make the sequence easy to follow.







OPTICS AND CAMERA




Use a natural handheld home-video feel: gentle camera movement, close framing around the vanity, and occasional wider shots that show the bedroom and the three friends together.




Capture expressive faces, makeup being applied and costume details clearly. Use the mirror naturally in the background. Keep lighting and room layout consistent between shots.




Use simple, clean cuts that follow the action. Avoid dramatic transitions, fast flashy editing, artificial zooms or excessive camera shake.







ACTION TIMING




0.0s to 2.5s — GETTING READY TOGETHER




Open on a close, selfie-like view of the three friends leaning toward the camera near the vanity. The red-haired friend holds lipstick, the curly-haired friend smiles toward the camera, and the brunette holds a pink makeup sponge.




They look relaxed and amused, sharing the mirror and makeup as they prepare.




2.5s to 5.5s — MAKEUP HELP




The red-haired friend gently applies lipstick to the curly-haired friend while the brunette dabs her face with the pink sponge.




Keep their movements small and natural. Show friendly glances and smiles as they help one another.




5.5s to 8.5s — EYESHADOW




Cut to a closer view. The red-haired friend carefully applies purple eyeshadow to the brunette’s closed eyelid.




The brunette holds still and reacts with a playful smile. Keep the makeup brush, eyelid and red-haired friend’s focused expression visible.




8.5s to 11.5s — COSTUME PREPARATION




Cut to the red-haired friend now dressed as the vintage clown from @Image 5. Her copper hair is curled, and she wears the cream ruffled blouse and burgundy skirt.




Her friends help adjust her hair and costume near the vanity. Show a few quick, candid gestures and mirror reflections. Make the costume change clear through the cut; do not morph her clothing on screen.




11.5s to 14.5s — WEREWOLF REVEAL




Cut to the curly-haired friend in the werewolf costume from @Image 7, with the zebra-striped top, purple skirt, furry details and ears.




Show her playful close-up by the mirror. She poses with a mischievous expression and makes a light paw-like gesture toward the camera. The red-haired friend in her clown costume can be seen nearby or reflected in the mirror.




14.5s to 17.5s — VAMPIRE ARRIVES




Cut to a wider view from near the foot of the canopy bed. The red-haired clown and curly-haired werewolf turn toward the bedroom doorway as the brunette enters in the vampire costume from @Image 3.




Her black outfit and tall red-lined collar are clearly visible. The other two react with delighted surprise and turn toward her.




17.5s to 19.5s — GROUP SELFIE




The three friends huddle closely together for a compact-camera selfie. They squeeze into frame, smile and pose shoulder-to-shoulder.




A bright camera flash pops once, briefly lighting their faces and the room.




19.5s to 22.0s — FINAL PARTY POSE




After the flash, hold on the final playful pose. The vampire kisses the red-haired clown on the cheek. The clown smiles at the camera. The werewolf sticks out her tongue, places one hand on her hip and lifts one leg behind her.




Keep all three faces and costumes clearly visible. End on their joyful pose without adding text or a title card.







PHYSICS AND PERFORMANCE




Keep gestures believable and spontaneous: careful makeup application, small fabric and hair adjustments, a natural entrance through the doorway, and a close group huddle for the selfie.




Preserve each woman’s face, hairstyle and costume consistently from shot to shot. Use cuts to show costume changes. Avoid face changes, extra people, duplicated limbs, distorted hands or flickering costume details.







LIGHTING AND COLOR




Use soft, warm bedroom lighting with a gentle glow from the fairy lights and vanity area. Keep the dusty-pink room, sheer white curtains and cozy bedroom atmosphere consistent.




Maintain natural skin tones and readable facial expressions. Let the turquoise tracksuit, burgundy clown skirt, zebra-striped werewolf costume and vampire’s red-lined collar provide the main color accents. Keep the look realistic, warm and slightly playful, like a candid party-preparation video.







AUDIO — NO MUSIC, SFX ONLY




NO MUSIC. NO BACKGROUND MUSIC. NO SCORE. SFX ONLY.




Use quiet bedroom room tone and subtle synchronized sound effects: makeup brush strokes, a sponge lightly tapping skin, a lipstick cap clicking, fabric rustling, soft footsteps as the vampire enters, friends’ brief natural giggles, a camera shutter click and one bright flash pop.




No voice-over, no intelligible dialogue and no musical sounds.
