HER PERFORMANCE — GLAMBOT SLOW MOTION, ALMOST MOTIONLESS:

Shot and played like an awards-show glambot: everything she does is in HEAVY SLOW MOTION with real high-speed-camera weight. Out of each spin she DECELERATES SMOOTHLY INTO HER POSE and comes almost completely to rest, and then SHE BARELY MOVES AT ALL for the rest of the look — no second pose, no gesture, no repositioning. The only things still alive in the hold are the slow trailing settle of her hair and fabric, a slow blink, and the faintest continuing drift of her weight. She is deliberately, luxuriously still. The stillness is the point.

ONE POSE PER LOOK, LANDED SLOWLY AND HELD. The spin is the only fast movement in the film, and even the spin carries slow-motion weight and inertia rather than snapping.




THE POSES ARE FIXED AND MUST NOT BE SWAPPED — one per look, no others:

LOOK 1, THE SLEEVES: both arms opened out wide to the sides at chest height so the enormous sheer organza sleeves spread and catch the light, chin lifted, weight on one leg, gaze straight into the lens.

LOOK 2, THE COLLAR: chin lifted high and clear of the tall standing leather collar, one hand raised to the collar's edge at her jaw, the other arm behind her back, feet close together, shoulders squared, a cool level stare into the lens.

LOOK 3, THE SILHOUETTE: a three-quarter turn away from camera, both hands closed on the corseted waist, one hip pushed back so the mermaid hem flares wide across the floor, shoulders squared, looking back over her shoulder into the lens.

LOOK 4, TEXTURE: one hand gripping the fur lapel and pulling it across her chest, the other set on the studded belt at her hip, one metallic boot pushed forward, head tipped down with her eyes coming up to the lens.

LOOK 5, PLAYFUL: knees together with toes turned slightly inward, both hands clasped in front over the metallic belt, one shoulder shrugged and her head tilted onto it, a small closed-mouth smile, eyes into the lens.

No pose is ever repeated; no two looks share the same arms, hips and head angle. She never stands with her arms hanging limp.




THE TRANSITIONS — THE FRAME MUST GO FULLY BLACK AND WHITE, THIS IS A PRIORITY FIX:

At each transition SHE SPINS one full revolution on the spot. AT THE EXACT FRAME THE SPIN BEGINS, THE ENTIRE IMAGE BECOMES PURE MONOCHROME — one hundred percent desaturated, true black and white, ZERO colour anywhere: her skin, her hair, her clothes, the giant phone, the swatch fan, the floor, everything. It is an instant hard flip to greyscale, not a fade, not a ramp, not a partial wash, not a tint, not a cool grade — the colour is simply gone.

THE WHOLE SPIN PLAYS OUT IN BLACK AND WHITE. During it the phone slides up and out through the top of frame while the next rises in from below, and the swatch cards reshuffle.

AT THE EXACT FRAME THE SPIN ENDS, FULL COLOUR SNAPS BACK instantly, and she is already in the next outfit, decelerating into the new pose. Again an instant hard flip, never a fade.

Every single transition works this way, all four of them, identically.

The clothes change entirely inside the monochrome spin — never a dissolve, never a morph, never a fade, never a half-changed outfit.

THE CAMERA PLATEAU AND ALL THREE LENSES STAY FULLY VISIBLE AND UNOBSTRUCTED above and beside her at all times.




IT FACES THE CAMERA SQUARE AND STANDS STRAIGHT: parallel to the camera sensor, ZERO tilt into depth, ZERO foreshortening, ZERO rotation — perfectly vertical and level, its edges parallel to the edges of frame. No lean, no wedge shape, no trapezoid, no narrowing toward one end, no compression, no stretch, no bending. It stands clearly BEHIND her, never in front, never touching her.




THE VERTICAL SLIDE: each new phone RISES INTO FRAME FROM BELOW THE BOTTOM EDGE, sliding smoothly straight up until fully in view and centred behind her, decelerating into place; it then HOLDS COMPLETELY STILL for the whole of her hold; on the spin it SLIDES ON UPWARD and out through the top edge while the next is already rising in from below, so the frame is never without a phone. Always straight vertical motion, always upward, never downward, never sideways, never diagonal, never tumbling or rotating.
