Prompt 1 — Character sheet from your selfie
Input your selfie
Make sure you tagged your selfie with @ in the prompt where it is marked [tag your selfie here]  
and just tap ✨recreate✨

Character reference sheet of the person in [tag your selfie here] 




IDENTITY — CRITICAL: reproduce the exact face, skin tone and texture, 

eye shape and colour, brows, nose, mouth, jawline, hairline and 

facial hair of [tag your selfie here]  Do not restyle, idealise, beautify or 

average the face. Do not change their apparent age, gender or build. 

Same identity in every panel.




HAIR — CRITICAL: reproduce the exact hair of [tag your selfie here] — same length, 

same texture, same curl or wave pattern, same volume, same density, 

same colour and same hairline. If the hair is curly or coily, keep 

the full curl pattern and volume; never straighten, smooth, relax or 

flatten it. If the hair is straight, keep it straight. Worn loose and 

down under the cap, visible on both sides of the face, pushing out 

around the brim and at the nape. Never tucked inside the cap, never 

tied, never hidden.




BUILD: natural realistic proportions matching [tag your selfie here]  Consistent 

scale and proportions across all four panels.




EXPRESSION: calm, neutral, lips closed, no smile, in every panel.




OUTFIT — identical in every panel, full cream-white monochrome look:

Plain six-panel baseball cap in cream white #F4F1EA cotton, curved 

brim worn straight forward, no logo, no text, no embroidery.

Oversized quilted puffer jacket in cream white #F4F1EA, horizontal 

quilted panels, stand collar, worn open, hem at the hip, elasticated 

cuffs, no logo, no print.

Plain ribbed knit crew-neck top in cream white underneath, tucked in.

High-waisted ribbed knit flared trousers in soft cream #EFEAE0, long, 

breaking over the shoes.

Chunky sneakers in cream white with a thick textured sole.

No bag, no jewellery, no watch, no phone, no scarf, no sunglasses, 

no belt, no logos, no prints, no patterns anywhere.




Clean four-panel collage on a plain light grey seamless background, 

thin white gutters between panels, minimalist composition, no text, 

no logos, no watermarks. All four panels at the same scale, same 

ground line, same lighting.




PANEL 1 (left, tall): full body from the front, head included, 

standing straight, arms relaxed at the sides, feet together, looking 

into the lens from under the cap brim, full outfit visible from cap 

to shoes.




PANEL 2 (centre-left, tall): full body from DIRECTLY BEHIND, head 

included, standing straight, arms relaxed at the sides, showing the 

back of the cap, the back of the jacket and the hair. Face not 

visible.




PANEL 3 (centre-right): frontal head and shoulders close-up, looking 

directly into the lens from under the cap brim, hair falling from 

under the cap, top of the outfit visible at the bottom of the frame.




PANEL 4 (right): head and shoulders in strict side profile facing 

screen-left, same neutral expression, cap brim pointing screen-left, 

full hair silhouette visible against the background.




Photoreal, natural skin texture, visible pores, no retouching, no 

beauty filter, no stylisation, even soft neutral studio light, 

identical on all four panels. Colour neutral — the cream white must 

read identically in every panel.

[tag your character sheet here]   is a four-view character sheet defining CHARACTER A — front, 

back, frontal close-up and side profile. Take CHARACTER A's face, 

hair, cap, outfit, footwear and build entirely from [tag your character sheet here]  , exactly

REPLACE the lead in @Video 1 with CHARACTER A. CHARACTER A inherits 

the original lead's exact position, body orientation, gaze direction, 

every movement, gesture and facial expression, frame by frame, at 

their original timestamps. CHARACTER A's face is [tag your character sheet here]  's face at 

all times — close, far, and when turning back toward camera. When 

turning away, the back matches the back panel of [tag your character sheet here]  . Skin is 

clean, no tattoos. The full outfit from [tag your character sheet here]  stays on CHARACTER A 

for the whole clip; the cap never comes off. The original lead's body 

does not show through.




REPLACE every other person's FACE and HAIR only. Each crowd member 

keeps the exact body, wardrobe, position, spacing, facing direction, 

timing and movement of the person they replace. Every crowd member 

has GOLDEN BLOND hair #D9B36C and a new face, with real variation in 

face shape, features, age and build, so the same face never sits next 

to itself. No two crowd members share a face. Their garments, colours 

and cut do not change. Nothing is added to or removed from the crowd. 

No gaps where a person stood.




CHARACTER A is the ONLY person in cream white, the ONLY person in a 

cap, and the ONLY person whose hair is not blond. Only ONE of 

CHARACTER A at any time.




EDIT SCOPE: only the lead's identity and the crowd's faces and hair 

change. Camera, cuts, framing, lighting direction, background, 

wardrobe and choreography are not repainted.




CONTENT TO PRESERVE: camera position, height, movement and every cut 

exactly as in @Video 1. Shot sizes and depth of field. Daylight 

direction, exposure and colour balance. The full background and every 

set element. The crowd's exact count, rows, spacing and every 

person's ground position. Every beat at its original timestamp.




TIMELINE INHERITANCE: every action keeps its original timestamp from 

@Video 1. No action is added, shortened or extended. Identity, 

wardrobe and hair state stay constant across all cuts and the whole 

clip.




PHYSICS: CHARACTER A's hair moves with a delay and settles after each 

turn, keeping its own texture through the motion — curly hair never 

straightens out. The cap stays seated through every movement. The 

puffer jacket swings with light bulk and settles on stopping. The 

knit trousers flare and swing with real weight. The sneakers press 

flat onto the ground with real contact. Crowd hair lags behind each 

head movement and settles after it. No floating, no sliding.




POSITIVE LOCKS: CHARACTER A's face is always [tag your character sheet here]  's face, sharp 

and readable at any distance, eyes visible under the cap brim, hair 

texture visible from every angle and never altered. CHARACTER A is 

the only person in cream white, the only one in a cap, the only one 

not blond. Every other person has golden blond hair and a face that 

is not CHARACTER A's, on their original body and in their original 

wardrobe, from first frame to last, near and far, still and moving. 

Same framing, same lens, same camera move, same cuts, same background 

as the master. Faces and hair keep the master's daylight pattern — no 

flat front fill.
