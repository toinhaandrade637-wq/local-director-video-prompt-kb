OBJECT SWAP — REPLACE THE HEROINE'S FACE ONLY. Image 1 = the new face.




SOURCE: 28.74 s, 24 fps, 1920×1080, six scenes cut together. One heroine appears in every scene in a different outfit, hairstyle and visual style; in scene C she appears twice at once; in scene E she is a 2D cartoon. Nothing about the motion, camera, cuts, timing, lighting, VFX or grade may change. The only edit is her facial identity.




WHAT CHANGES

In every frame where the heroine's face is visible — scene A (0.00–5.46), scene B (5.46–10.33), scene C (10.33–14.83, BOTH figures), scene D (14.83–20.05), scene E (20.90–25.83, redrawn in cartoon style — see CARTOON RULE), scene F (25.83–28.74) — replace her face with the woman from Image 1: face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, skin tone. The new face follows the original head frame by frame: every turn, tilt, blink, yawn, laugh, smirk, every eye direction and lip position. Take NOTHING else from Image 1: not hair, not clothing, not lighting, not background, not expression.




WHAT NEVER CHANGES

– hair as filmed in each scene: long dark hair down (A, F); spiky top-knot bun with loose strands (B); long hair under the cap (C); hair under the shako, then released when the hat comes off (D); long messy drawn hair (E)

– wardrobe and accessories in each scene: navy blazer, white shirt, dark tie, white wired earphones (A, F); mustard sweater and earphones (B); red baseball cap worn askew, thin-framed glasses, white long-sleeve under the yellow KARIMA 21 basketball jersey, chain necklace, earphones — on both figures (C); shako with plume and emblem, ornate military-band jacket with frogging and epaulettes, white gloves (D); cream baseball cap, white jersey with red 21, earphones (E)

– lipstick and makeup as filmed

– the KARIMA 21 lettering on the jersey

– every background: the classroom with students, the night road and fireball, the white studio and cream sofa with the dancing figure, the sepia airfield with the aircraft, the cartoon island with the lighthouse and sunset sky

– all camera moves (static in A, C, D, E, F; a steady backward track at walking pace in B), all cuts and their exact timing, the black beat 20.28–20.65, the sepia grade of D, the 2D drawing style of E

– the audio




LOCKED TIMELINE — preserve exactly.




SCENE A (0.00–5.46) — bright classroom, static camera, medium shot, she sits at a desk facing the lens, rows of uniformed students writing behind her.

0.00–0.45 looking down at the desk.

0.50–1.35 right hand rises to her right ear (screen-left), adjusts the earbud.

4.50–5.00 mouth closes, cheek settles onto her fist.

5.00–5.46 head slumps, dozing off. Cut.

TWO-FIGURES RULE (scene C)

The small dancing figure on the sofa is the same heroine and receives the same new face, at its own scale, on every frame. Two figures, one identity.




CARTOON RULE (scene E)

Keep the exact 2D art style — line weight, flat colour, oversized stylised eyes, the drawn hair and cap. Redraw only the character's facial features as a cartoon caricature of the woman in Image 1 (her face shape, eyebrow shape, nose, mouth) in that same style. Do not make any part of scene E photoreal. The giant eye in 22.79–24.00 stays a drawn eye.




HAND-OVER-LENS RULE (19.60–20.90)

The live gloved palm covering the lens, the black beat and the drawn palm pulling away are the transition. Keep their frame timing exactly; the black stays black.




OCCLUSION RULE

Glasses (C), the cap brim (C, E), the shako (D), her hands at her ears (A, E, F) and her palm over the lens (D) stay in front of the new face exactly where they were. The new skin is reconstructed only where the original skin was visible.




BANS

Do not change hair, wardrobe, glasses, hats, jewellery, makeup or the KARIMA 21 lettering. Do not change any cut, the length of the black beat, or the jump-zoom timing. Do not change the fireball, the sepia grade or the cartoon style. Do not take expression, lighting, hair or background from Image 1. Do not add camera movement, text or any new element. Do not leave either figure in scene C with the original face.
