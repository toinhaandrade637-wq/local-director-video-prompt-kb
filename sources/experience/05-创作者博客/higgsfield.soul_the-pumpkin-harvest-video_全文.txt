SCENE CONTEXT

A 29.5-second fairytale horror mood film in six shots with hard cuts. In a foggy field, a calm doll-like young woman harvests pale green carved pumpkins by piercing them with a giant sewing pin. Every pumpkin starts with a neutral carved face; the moment it is pierced its face turns frightened. She hauls the two frightened pumpkins home while the pumpkins left in the patch turn to watch her with their neutral faces. At her desk she carves new smiles into the frightened faces and smiles with satisfaction. Finally she places the last one in a cupboard full of smiling pumpkins, all staring at the camera. She never reacts to the pumpkins. No pumpkin ever glows.

ACTIVE REFERENCES

@Image 1 controls the woman's face in every shot: fair skin, bright blue eyes, defined dark brows, full rosy lips, long straight dark-brown hair with a centre part, pastel flower earrings (lilac on one ear, light blue on the other). Ignore 's headband, collar and background.

@Image 3 controls the wide composition of shot 2, her outfit and headwear (white polka-dot headscarf with a large pink button and a pale green ribbon, dusty-pink pinafore dress over a lilac-striped blouse with cream cuffs, white tights, pink shoes), the giant sewing pin (long teal shaft about 1.5 m with a glossy pink ball head and a sharp tip), the pink wheelbarrow with a grey tub, black tyres and pink hubs, the foggy field, the striped cottage with a pink arched door and checkered turret, the trees and the fog.

@Image 2 controls the cottage interior: blue-green painted wooden walls and beams, round foggy windows, the pink plaster wall with vintage scissors and tools, the wooden desk with pink drawers and carving tools, the spindle chair, the painted cupboard with open doors and two shelves, the marble statue, the small wall sconces with warm bulbs.

@Image 4 controls the NEUTRAL carved face style: small round hollow eye holes, a small nose hole, a short calm mouth cut.

@Image 3 controls the FRIGHTENED carved face style: round hollow eye holes, a triangular nose hole, a wide gaping oval mouth with rough jagged edges.

@Image 5 controls the SMILING carved face style: slanted eye holes, a triangular nose hole, a wide jagged grin with upturned corners.

PUMPKIN FACE RULES

Every pumpkin is pale light sage-green with greyish-white ribs, as in @Image 3 and @Image 4. Every face is a realistic cut through the rind: hollow, dark openings with visible pale rind thickness at the edges. No eyeballs, no pupils, no painted features, nothing inside the holes; the face exists only as carved openings. Each pumpkin has its own individual cut shapes, but all pumpkins in the same state share the same emotion.

Sequence: every pumpkin is NEUTRAL until it is pierced. It turns FRIGHTENED at the instant of piercing and stays frightened until she carves it. Carved pumpkins are SMILING. Pumpkins that are never pierced stay neutral. Expression changes happen by the carved openings smoothly reshaping in the rind; the pumpkin keeps its shape, size and colour.

GLOBAL RULES

One woman only, face from @Image 1, outfit from @Image 3. Her expression is calm and neutral in every shot except the satisfied smile in shot 4B. No pumpkin glows; carved openings are always dark and hollow. Colour grade constant across all shots: foggy grey-green, pale sage, dusty pink and teal outdoors; faded blue-green, dusty pink and warm wood indoors. Photorealistic fairytale magical-realism film, fine natural film grain. Natural motion cadence, no ghosting, no flicker, no morphing of pumpkins, pin or wheelbarrow.No voice.

She walks away from camera up the path toward the striped cottage with the pink door in the background, pulling the wheelbarrow behind her by its handles; the pin lies across the tub, and the two FRIGHTENED faces of pumpkins A and B look back toward camera. By 16.0s every patch pumpkin faces her as she nears the pink door.

Sound: rolling wheelbarrow creak, soft footsteps, slow dry grinding of pumpkins turning.

SHOT 4A — 16.0-21.0s — MEDIUM, ACROSS THE DESK, VERY SLOW PUSH-IN

Inside the cottage from @Image 2. She sits at the wooden desk on the spindle chair, facing camera. Camera frontal to the open painted cupboard from @Image 2 at shelf height, about 2 m away, 50° field of view, both open doors at the frame edges. Lighting: cool foggy daylight from the round window plus the soft warm spill of a wall sconce, as in @Image 2; carved openings dark and hollow.

Sound: footsteps on floorboards, a soft knock of the pumpkin on the shelf, then silence.

POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS

Pumpkin faces are only realistic carved openings, never eyeballs. Neutral until pierced, frightened from piercing until carved, smiling only after carving. In shot 3 all patch pumpkins stay neutral while turning toward her. In shot 4A no pumpkin face is visible. Smiling faces appear only in shot 5, all looking at the camera.
