Seedance 2.5 — One Continuous 20-Second Shot

⚠️ CHARACTER REFERENCE — MANDATORY: Face — Reference [tag your selfie here] (real photo). Outfit — short dark bob, black fitted crop jacket, distressed dark cargo denim shorts, gray hoodie tied at waist, teal satin ballet pointe shoes, pink tights, black headphones (start on shoulders, pulled ON EARS before the fall and stay on for the rest of the shot), white gloves.

⚠️ CRITICAL — CHARACTER ANATOMY. NON-NEGOTIABLE: She is a young woman, 165cm tall, 47kg. Slender, lean, athletic, controlled. Proportions consistent every frame. Joints bend naturally, spine curves correctly. NEVER ragdoll. NEVER limp. Core always engaged. Moves like a trained gymnast/dancer — precise, effortless. Body follows physics logically. No floating limbs. No broken joints. No inconsistent scale.

⚠️ CRITICAL — FACE VISIBILITY. NON-NEGOTIABLE: Face is fully visible ONLY in the opening window moment (the glance back, the smirk, the line). From the moment she falls out of the window, camera follows her from behind for the entire rest of the shot. We see her back, her silhouette, her body in motion. Face visible only briefly and accidentally after the fall — a quick profile, a glimpse as she turns into a swing. Never a direct frontal face shot after the window. Never a close-up of her face after the window. From the fall onward, emotion is read through body language and sounds only.

⚠️ CRITICAL — PHYSICAL CONTACT WITH ENVIRONMENT. NON-NEGOTIABLE: She interacts with the city physically at every opportunity — feet push off walls, body grazes fire escapes, feet bounce off car roofs. Every contact: knees absorb, muscles engage, body reacts. Parkour + web-swinging. Not floating.

⚠️ CRITICAL — BODY POSITIONS PER MOVEMENT TYPE: Back-first fall from window: completely calm, arms slightly out, eyes closed, face peaceful, body totally relaxed — hoodie flowing up, hair floating Web swing: arms extended forward gripping web, legs together trailing, core tight, back straight, chin up Wall push-off: both feet plant flat, knees bend deep, legs explode into full extension, arms punch forward Running on wall: body leans 45 degrees toward wall, feet striking in running gait, arms pumping Free flight peak: arms naturally open to sides, body slightly arched back, physically released Street level run: full sprint, arms driving, head forward, teal pointe shoes striking pavement

⚠️ CRITICAL — EMOTION THROUGH BODY. NON-NEGOTIABLE: After the window, no face needed — emotion lives in the body: The fall — total calm, surrender, peace First swing — body tense with focus, grip tight Feet skim car — quick instinctive laugh heard, body jolts with surprise Running on wall — explosive, driven, determined body language Low between taxis — body compressed and streamlined, pure aggression Taxi honk — audible laugh, head tips back briefly "WOOHOO!" — chest opens, arms spread, head back, body screams joy Final peak — completely open, still, surrendered

⚠️ CRITICAL — SOUND. NON-NEGOTIABLE: NO MUSIC. SFX ONLY. City ambience, distant traffic, the soft thud of headphones shifting, the creak of the windowsill, her line spoken casually. Then: wind rushing. Web snap and twang. Feet on car roof. Feet on wet pavement. Taxi horn. Her laugh. "WOOHOO!" echoing. Wind cutting as she rises. Silence at peak. No score. No soundtrack.

⚠️ CRITICAL — ENVIRONMENT. NON-NEGOTIABLE: Real lived-in residential NYC. High-floor apartment with a messy teenage bedroom visible through the window — cluttered desk, unmade bed, posters on walls, stacked books, energy drink cans, clothes on floor. Below and beyond: brownstones, fire escapes, bodegas, pedestrians — people walking, delivery guy, someone with a dog. Real city life. Not empty streets. Not tourist Manhattan.

⚠️ CRITICAL — LIGHTING. NON-NEGOTIABLE: Early evening, overcast-leaning — NOT peak golden hour. Soft warm light on building facades. Natural grey-blue sky with gentle pink at horizon. Overcast brick buildings reflected in the window glass at the start. Subtle. Real. Like a regular NYC Tuesday at 6pm.

⚠️ CRITICAL — REALISM. NON-NEGOTIABLE: Real Hollywood film. Shot on RED Monstro 8K, anamorphic lens. Real depth of field, real lens flare, real motion blur, real fabric physics, real atmospheric NYC haze. Indistinguishable from real footage. No AI artifacts. No floating. No broken anatomy. REAL.

⚠️ CRITICAL — CAMERA. NON-NEGOTIABLE: ONE continuous shot. ZERO cuts. ZERO teleporting. Camera starts static — positioned outside, level with the window, facing it, framing her through the window pane. It does not move during the bedroom moment. When she falls, the camera holds on the empty window for half a beat, then dives straight down after her and becomes a physical drone-follow from behind for the rest of the shot — chasing her through space, tilting, banking, rolling severely with the action. Wide enough to always show her AND environment. Horizon severely canted 40–70 degrees during swings.

20-second continuous shot

0–3.5 sec: Static exterior camera, level with a high-floor NYC apartment window. Interior visible through the large window — messy teenage bedroom. Overcast NYC brick buildings reflected in the glass. She sits perched casually on the windowsill, back facing outside, feet dangling over the city. Headphones on shoulders. She glances back over her shoulder into the room. Easy smirk. Says it: "Alright. Let's do this one more time." Lazy one-finger spin.

3.5–5.5 sec: She pulls headphones over ears. No jump. No somersault. Simply leans back — falls back-first out the window. Completely calm. Arms slightly out. Eyes closed. Face peaceful. Body totally relaxed — hoodie flowing up, hair floating. She drops out of frame downward. Camera holds static on the empty window for half a beat — wind, silence.

5.5–7 sec: Camera suddenly plunges straight down after her, catching her mid-fall from behind. CLOSE on her white-gloved hand mid-air — fingers open, wrist snaps. Web shoots — fast, snapping taut, real vibration. The fall converts into a swing. Camera pulls back fast, locked behind her. Gone.

7–10.5 sec: Wide — residential NYC street from behind her. Brownstones, fire escapes, bodegas, pedestrians below. She swings LOW — 3 meters above street. Body: arms extended gripping web, legs together trailing, core tight, back straight. She whooshes past a fire escape close enough to almost touch it — body instinctively tucks slightly. At the bottom of the swing her feet skim the roof of a parked car — brief bounce, quick surprised laugh heard. Camera banks hard — horizon 40 degrees — following from behind.

10.5–13 sec: Releases at peak — body opens naturally, arms out, legs extend. Instead of immediately firing web — runs along brownstone wall, body 45 degrees toward surface, feet striking brick, arms pumping, 4–5 quick steps. Then both feet push off hard — knees explode into full extension — fires new web mid-air across wide intersection. Swing yanks her hard sideways. Camera rolls 60 degrees. She dips LOW again down a narrow street packed with yellow taxis.

13–15.5 sec: FAST and LOW between taxis — 2 meters off ground. Body streamlined — arms forward, legs trailing like an arrow. Web releases. Feet hit wet pavement — full sprint, arms driving, 3–4 steps between taxis. A taxi honks. Audible laugh. She plants one foot on the side of a taxi cab — explosive single-leg push off the car roof — fires web up at a tall building and rockets HIGH. Camera rises fast from directly behind. "WOOHOO!" — echoes between buildings.

15.5–18.5 sec: High above — wide aerial from behind. Regular NYC grid. Rooftops, water towers, fire escapes. She fires a third web, swings in a long wide arc. Camera banking severely — horizon past 70 degrees. At the peak — arms wide open, head back, body arched, completely still — one breathless moment of freedom. We see only her silhouette and the city below.

18.5–20 sec: Camera drifts behind her mid-swing. NYC stretches ahead. She is small against the city. Going somewhere.
