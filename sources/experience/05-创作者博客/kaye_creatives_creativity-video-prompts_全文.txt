BEYOND AND BELOW, more cloud tops recede into the distance to a soft horizon of cloud, and above them THE SKY FILLS THE UPPER PART OF THE FRAME — clean pale blue high up, warming to soft cream and the faintest blush pink lower down near the light.

=== IT MUST TOUCH THE CLOUD PROPERLY ===

WHERE SHE SITS, THE CLOUD COMPRESSES. The vapour packs and dents under the chair and under her weight, and small puffs of it are pressed out around the chair's base and around her shoes.

THE SYNTHESISER'S FOUR LEGS SINK SLIGHTLY INTO THE CLOUD TOP, each one standing in a small hollow of its own with vapour curling around it.

THE HEADPHONE CABLE AND THE PATCH CABLES HANG DOWN OFF THE INSTRUMENT AND DISAPPEAR INTO THE CLOUD, going nowhere. Nothing is plugged into anything.

=== THE ACTION, ALL SIX SECONDS ===

SHE IS PLAYING, absorbed and calm, entirely to herself. Her head nods very slightly, unhurried, to something we cannot hear. Her shoulders are relaxed.

SHE NEVER LOOKS AT THE CAMERA and never turns fully toward us. Her hair lifts a little in the moving air.

THE CAMERA drifts in very slowly and steadily across the whole shot — barely perceptible, just enough to feel alive — with the faintest live unsteadiness. No pan, no tilt, no zoom, no orbit.




=== THE LOOK ===

Photoreal, cinematic. Bright, airy and high-key, with real detail held in the white cloud and no blown-out flat patches. Natural film colour, gentle contrast, soft filmic highlight roll-off, a gentle bloom around the brightest cloud edges only, fine grain. Shallow depth with a soft falloff into the distance. No HDR, no digital sharpening, no oversaturation, no heavy glow filter over the whole frame.
