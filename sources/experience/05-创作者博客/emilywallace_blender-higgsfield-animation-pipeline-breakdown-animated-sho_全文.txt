Passport Rush is our five-minute animated action comedy in production. This breakdown shares the asset library, Blender blocking and full prompts behind its teaser sequence. The goal was a consistent film world: expressive 3D characters, a readable yellow taxi and a watercolor environment that could survive fast camera moves.

Before You Start

Build the recurring assets before generating scenes. A character sheet, a taxi car sheet and a clear location reference give every shot the same visual starting point. Fix the designs once, then reuse those corrected references throughout the sequence.

We used SOUL 2.0 for base image assets, hand work in Photoshop to refine them, Seedream 5.0 Pro for the roofless taxi car, Blender for camera and action blocking, and Seedance 2.5 for video generation. 




The  prompts used here are included below.

Stage 2 — The Prompting Framework

A usable scene prompt needs more than an action description. Give it a reference map, a clear first frame, the camera’s position, the sequence of physical actions and the details that must remain unchanged.




Our working order was:

Establish the scene with a storyboard or simple Blender previs.
Attach the approved characters, props, interior and location references.
Describe the action in timed beats, including how the shot ends.
Lock continuity: seats, accessories, vehicle damage and lighting.
Check the result against the references, then revise the specific source of the error.

Reference numbering is local to each prompt. The numbered image and video tokens below are input labels, not reusable element names. Attach the described media to the matching slots. Different scene prompts use different seat layouts and reference orders; keep each prompt together with its own map.

Framework — Shared Lighting

What it does: This reusable lighting fragment keeps warm highlights, cool shadows and a consistent light direction across the sequence. It is a component of a scene prompt, not a standalone shot request.

LIGHTING (cinematic). a golden-hour low-angle warm key light cuts diagonally across the whole frame, pulling long hard-edged shadows across the car body, the characters and the road; shadows lean cool, highlights lean warm — a cold/warm contrast that builds depth. Framework Example — A Controlled Two-Shot Sequence

What it does: This complete example specifies two timed shots, a single hard cut and separate reference responsibilities for a roofless-taxi encounter.

Reference setup: the prompt calls for the highway, roofless taxi, two police-character references, the motorcycle, the driver, the heroine’s crossed-seatbelt pose, a composition keyframe and a heroine video reference. The police, motorcycle and composition references are not included in this page’s asset set; supply them before recreating this example.

STYLIZED 3D ANIMATED-FILM SEQUENCE, slapstick action comedy from "PASSPORT RUSH" (taxi chase). Controlled 2-shot sequence with one HARD CUT, each shot one continuous take, real-time speed, NO slow motion, animation ON TWOS (every key pose held exactly 2 frames, stepped pose-to-pose, no mushy interpolation), FULL 12 PRINCIPLES applied (squash & stretch, anticipation, staging, pose-to-pose, follow-through & overlap, ease in-out, arcs, secondary action, timing, exaggeration, solid drawing, appeal). 16:9 cinematic. Total 6 s: Shot 1 = 3 s, Shot 2 = 3 s.

SCENE CONTEXT

A battered roofless yellow taxi races down a mountain highway with a traffic cop standing on its hood, glaring at the driver; his partner rides far ahead on a motorcycle. The taxi brakes hard, skids a short way forward on locked wheels, and stops with its nose pressed down; the cop is flung off the hood in a violent instant — deep into the frame toward his partner — and lands, back-first, on the rear seat of the partner's motorcycle. The driver and the girl in the back seat stay exactly where they are.

TAXI GEOMETRY LOCK: front seat — the driver @图片 5 at the wheel (left-hand drive), the passenger seat empty; rear bench — the heroine @视频 1 · 46 秒 alone in the centre, belted in the @图片 6 X-harness; Cop 1 @图片 3 standing upright on the HOOD next to the empty windshield frame, boots planted on the yellow metal, knees bent, arms out for balance, body turned to FACE THE DRIVER, glaring at him. Far ahead on the same highway, small in the distance at lower-right, Cop 2 @图片 4 rides his motorcycle away from the taxi in the same direction, helmet beacon a tiny red dot.

SEAT LOCK (HARD LOCK, Shot 1): the DRIVER and the HEROINE STAY IN THEIR SEATS for the entire shot — the driver's hands on the wheel, backside on the front bench, cap on his head; the heroine belted in the centre of the rear bench by her X-harness, fists on the belts. The brake throws their hair, clothes and upper bodies forward and back, but neither of them leaves the seat, stands up, climbs out or is launched — ONLY Cop 1 flies off the car.

CAMERA RIG LOCK (HARD LOCK, Shot 1): the camera is HARD-MOUNTED TO THE TAXI'S BODY — it is part of the car. It travels at the car's full speed, so the taxi stays locked in the same place in frame while the world streams past; when the taxi brakes, the CAMERA BRAKES WITH IT — same instant, same violence: the frame lurches with the nose-dive, jolts through the short forward skid, and comes to the same abrupt dead halt with the same nose-press and rebound, the streaming background snapping to a sharp standstill. The camera never floats free of the car, never continues forward after the stop, never drifts, pans or eases on its own.

BRAKE PHYSICS LOCK (HARD LOCK, Shot 1): the stop happens in three readable stages. (1) BRAKE BITE — the driver stamps the pedal, all four wheels LOCK instantly, the nose dives, the tail lifts, tire smoke bursts. (2) INERTIA SKID — the taxi does NOT stop on the spot: it keeps SLIDING FORWARD on locked, smoking, non-rotating tires for a short distance — about one car length, roughly 0.4 s — the road still streaming past but decelerating hard, the car slewing a few degrees, skid marks unrolling behind. (3) THE DEAD STOP — the slide ends with a hard jolt: the FRONT END PRESSES DOWN visibly on its suspension, the front bumper dipping toward the asphalt, the front tires flattening, the whole body pitching nose-down — then rebounds up once and settles level. Cop 1 is thrown at stage (1), the very first frame of brake bite, not later.

LAUNCH LOCK (HARD LOCK, Shot 1): Cop 1's departure from the hood is VIOLENT AND INSTANT — a catapult, not a slide. From standing to fully airborne takes 2–3 frames: one frame of his boots losing the hood, the next frame his whole body is already stretched flat in the air a full body-length ahead of the car, the next he is a shrinking streak down the road. His body snaps from upright to a rigid horizontal line with a whip-crack, arms and legs locking straight, motion-blurred and trailing speed lines for the first frames; no wind-up, no gentle tipping, no hang time.

SHOT 1 — THE BRAKE AND THE LAUNCH, LOW AT THE WHEEL (3 s)

Shot job: speed, the hard stop, the violent launch into depth.

CAMERA: per CAMERA RIG LOCK, exactly as in @图片 7 — a very low camera about 25 cm above the asphalt just behind the left rear wheel, the tire and fender looming large in the left foreground, looking forward and slightly up along the car's flank in left-rear three-quarter; rigid to the car; tiny road vibration only.

FIRST FRAME: the @图片 7 composition — the left rear tire spinning into a blur in the lower-left foreground, yellow fender, tail light and chrome bumper above it, the "T" door badge along the flank, the heroine's orange-red hair streaming above the rear seat at top-left, the driver's flat cap ahead of her; Cop 1 standing tall on the hood beside the windshield frame, turned toward the driver, GLARING — brows slammed down, eyes narrowed, mouth a hard angry line, one gloved fist clenched, arms out for balance; the @图片 1 highway curving away right, guardrail posts a blur, the blue gantry sign and green hillside sweeping by, hazy mountains beyond; Cop 2 a small figure on his white motorcycle far down the road at lower-right, riding away, red beacon dot glowing; thin tire smoke and dust along the bottom edge.

ACTION TIMING: Beat 1 (~1.1 s) — full speed: the taxi hurtles down the highway, the camera riding with it, the tire a spinning smear beside the lens, body rocking on its suspension, the road a streak beneath, guardrail and hillside streaming; Cop 1 leans into his glare at the driver, lanyard and ascot whipping, helmet beacon flashing; the heroine sits belted in the back, hair streaming, the driver steady at the wheel; Cop 2 a steady small figure ahead at lower-right. Beat 2 (~0.15 s) — BRAKE BITE and LAUNCH, together: the driver stamps the pedal — the tire beside the camera LOCKS dead, stops spinning, a burst of white smoke and dust erupting past the lens, the nose DIVES, the tail LIFTS, the camera lurching with the body — and in that SAME INSTANT Cop 1 is FLUNG off the hood per LAUNCH LOCK: his glare has time for one frame of wide-eyed SURPRISE (brows up, mouth a small "o") and then he is GONE — snapped from upright into a rigid horizontal streak, arms and legs locked straight, helmet first, motion-blurred, speed lines trailing, already a body-length ahead of the car, face flipping to SHOCK — eyes bulging, mouth stretched wide. Beat 3 (~0.4 s) — INERTIA SKID: the taxi keeps SLIDING FORWARD on four locked, smoking tires — the road still streaming past but braking hard, the car slewing a few degrees, skid marks unrolling, the locked tire beside the lens shuddering and smoking, the camera jolting with every judder; the heroine is pinned forward against her X-harness, hair over her face, fists on the belts; the driver braces, cap tipped forward, hands locked on the wheel; ahead, Cop 1 is already a shrinking flailing figure racing down the road INTO THE DEPTH of the frame toward Cop 2. Beat 4 (~0.25 s) — THE DEAD STOP: the slide ends with a hard jolt — the FRONT END PRESSES DOWN, bumper dipping toward the asphalt, front tires squashing, the body pitching nose-down and the camera pitching with it — then the car rebounds up once and settles level; the streaming road snaps into a sharp standstill; the heroine flops back into her seat, hair falling back; the driver's cap bounces back onto his brow. Beat 5 (remaining ~1.1 s) — the aftermath: the taxi sits dead still, smoke drifting forward past the car and thinning; far down the road Cop 1 is a tiny flailing dot closing on Cop 2's dot; the heroine, still belted in her seat, lifts her head above the seat back and turns it to track him; the driver, still at the wheel, tilts his cap a calm degree. Hold on the stopped taxi and the two tiny figures far down the road.

MICRO-ACTING: COP 1 — glare (brows down, slit eyes, hard mouth, clenched fist) → one frame of surprise (brows up, round eyes, small "o") → shock in flight (bulging eyes, stretched mouth, rigid limbs). HEROINE (seated, glimpsed from behind) — pinned forward, hair over face, flops back, head lifts and turns to track the cop, never leaving the seat. DRIVER (seated) — cap tips forward, shoulders brace, cap bounces back, one calm cap tilt, hands never leaving the wheel.

OPTICS: 84° classic wide per @图片 7, road-level lens, strong low-angle stretch on the tire and fender, deep focus so the flying cop and distant motorcycle stay sharp. Lens locked.

LIGHTING: soft overcast mountain daylight per @图片 1/@图片 7, cool grey-blue sky, muted greens, the yellow taxi the brightest thing in frame, tire smoke catching soft light.

PHYSICS: per BRAKE PHYSICS LOCK — bite, forward inertia skid on locked tires, dead stop with a deep nose-press and one rebound; camera lurching identically with the body it is bolted to; the belted heroine and the seated driver thrown forward and back but held by harness and seat; Cop 1, unrestrained on the hood, catapulted per LAUNCH LOCK with real momentum — no float — shrinking with correct perspective down the road.

AUDIO: roaring engine and rushing wind, the tire humming loud right beside the lens, then a sharp SCREECH as the tires lock, a whip-crack WHOOSH as Cop 1 is flung, a long grinding skid with hissing smoke, a heavy THUNK and creak of the front suspension bottoming out, one rebound squeak, then silence but for the idling engine, a startled squeak from the heroine, the far-off buzz of the motorcycle ahead. No dialogue. No music.

HARD CUT — abrupt, instant, no fade.

in two to three frames, then holds still while only hair, jacket fabric and belt

webbing keep settling. The opening beat is a genuine hold — she is motionless and

looking ahead before anything happens, and the reach that follows starts with a

sharp two-frame anticipation. The belt actions are quick and unforced — no

straining, no heaving. Each motion is followed by a soft overshoot that cushions

momentum in Seg 1 flows into the reach at the start of Seg 2 without a reset —

the seam between segments is invisible in the movement, even though the camera

and framing change. The final 1.5 seconds are a long deliberate hold: the pose

is completely still, carried only by breathing, blinks, drifting hair and the

moving landscape behind her. One clear readable silhouette per pose, one main

action at a time.

She is quietly frightened and on edge for the entire four seconds, in both

segments. She never smiles at any point — her mouth is never turned up at the

corners, in any frame, in either segment. Her expression moves only between

degrees of worry. One feature is pushed per beat, the rest of the face

restrained, and the face is always asymmetrical: brows at slightly different

heights, mouth pulled a little to one side.

0.0–0.3s — staring ahead at the road: eyes wide and fixed with white showing

The taxi is moving fast and swaying. The seatbelt webbing is stiff and holds a

Experimental — unfinished shot. This prompt is included as a work-in-progress example. The attempts did not reliably produce the intended cause and effect, so they should not be treated as a finished result.

INPUTS

Interior of a taxi at high speed, rear compartment, one passenger. The taxi races

violent, comedic escalation.

Camera is inside the @图片 2 cabin, mounted low on the back of the front

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING

The @图片 1 passenger is settled and braced on the bench, not yet thrown.

One single continuous take, 5 seconds, no cuts, no transitions, no camera cuts of

Continuous tilt following the passenger. Camera bolted to the @图片 2 cabin,

The car twists through hard alternating turns at speed. Lateral inertia throws the
