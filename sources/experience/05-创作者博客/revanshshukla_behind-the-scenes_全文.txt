Behind the Scenes: Color Palette & Atmospheric Contrast
​For Echoes of Tomorrow, color serves as the core narrative indicator of the protagonist’s mental state and environmental consequence:
​The Industrial Cold: Desaturated slate-blue tones and harsh lighting during the factory and routine scenes.
​The False Horizon: Hyper-saturated amber sunset during the beach sequence, building a false sense of comfort before the disaster.
​The Restoration: Balanced, organic greens and natural daylight in the final act to signify conscious restitution.
