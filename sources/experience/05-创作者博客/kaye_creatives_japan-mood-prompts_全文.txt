THE LOOK — AGGRESSIVELY OLD VHS CAMCORDER TAPE, ON A BRIGHT NEON NIGHT:

— MOTION: strong horizontal INTERLACE COMBING on the run, on her spin, on her hair, on passing people.

— TAPE FAULTS: a thin faint BAND OF HEAD-SWITCHING NOISE along the very bottom edge of frame. Slight GEOMETRIC INSTABILITY at the top, the picture breathing a few pixels. Chroma noise everywhere.

— TONE: milky and low contrast, gamma lifted, NO TRUE BLACK — the night sky reads as murky grey-brown, never black. Faded shifted colour with a lilac-magenta lean, skin warm and pinkish where the signs reach it. AUTO WHITE BALANCE DRIFTS AND PUMPS wildly in all this mixed coloured light.

— Slightly rounded soft corners. Warm, faded, the feeling of something lost.

STILL NOT PIXELATED: all TAPE degradation. No visible pixels, no blocks, no macroblocking, no mosaic, no digital compression artefacts, no glitch stripes, no VHS filter overlay.
