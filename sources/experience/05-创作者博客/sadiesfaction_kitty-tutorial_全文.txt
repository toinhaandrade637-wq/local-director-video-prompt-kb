everything done on higgsfield.ai, prompts are below, just copy & paste ★

1 CREATE YOUR SOUL ID ★
go to → cinema studio ← on higgsfield.ai
choose image, and choose SOUL 2.0
tap "Characters"
tap "Create character"
tap "Upload images" → 25+ photos of yourself
name it, model = Soul 2.0, tap "Create"

photo tips: one person, clear face, multiple angles, a few full-body shots, good lighting. no filters, no sunglasses, no group shots.

2 GENERATE THE STILL ★
open → Soul 2.0 ← → select your character
paste a still prompt from below
generate ★
3 ANIMATE IT ★
open Seedance 2.5
attach your still as the image input
paste the matching animation prompt from below
generate ★




STILLS
CHARACTER

Full-body studio lookbook fashion photo, straight-on eye-level shot of a young woman in her early twenties standing upright against a seamless white cyclorama, looking directly at the camera with a neutral expression. Choppy reddish-brown bob with blunt bangs, softly lit face. Single vertical 4:5 frame.




PROPORTIONS: average height and natural, realistic everyday body proportions — roughly 7 heads tall, NOT an elongated fashion-model or runway physique. Normal leg-to-torso ratio, relaxed natural stance. The camera is at true chest height and level (not low, not tilted up), shot on a normal ~50mm-equivalent lens so her legs are not stretched or lengthened.




OUTFIT: a crisp, fitted short-sleeve T-shirt with horizontal stripes in blue, yellow, and white, the knit stretching subtly across the arms and torso. A brown A-line miniskirt in brushed matte cotton/twill with a gentle flare. Glossy blue knee-high boots in smooth leather with a fine grain and soft wrinkling at the ankles. Both hands empty, arms slightly bent in a relaxed, casual pose.




TEXTURE NOTES: the tee reads as smooth cotton jersey with clean printed stripes; the skirt as soft brushed matte twill with no sheen; the boots as glossy supple leather with subtle creasing.




SETTING: seamless white cyclorama studio backdrop — the white floor sweeps up into the white wall with no visible corner or horizon line, pure clean white all around.




LIGHTING: flat, even, bright studio lighting — no directional or editorial light, evenly illuminated with minimal shadow, only a soft contact shadow at her feet.




AESTHETIC: crisp and clean, sharp focus throughout, moderate depth of field, no film grain and no digital artifacts, neutral observational mood.




HEX — garments: blue stripe/boots #51a1bf, white #f2f2eb, skirt brown #ad6955 #b39283 #d8bb99, muted red-brown #774e52 (hair/accents). Yellow stripe: a warm mid yellow.

INPUTS

(your photo)

SCENE CONTEXT

Live-action video with a soft dreamy mood: a relaxed girl lies on her back in a green meadow dotted with small white daisies, a cat lounging beside her, strong wind blowing through the whole scene from the first frame to the last. In the final seconds, flowers grow out of her body in dense uneven patches until they cover her completely, face included — her whole figure becoming a flower-covered silhouette. Surreal but gentle and beautiful, not horror. Until the growth overtakes her, she must read as a real, alive, breathing person.




ACTIVE REFERENCES

@Image 1 — the girl from the attached photo: shaggy copper-red hair with full bangs, soft calm face, slim build. Face, hair color and hair texture 100% match the reference. She wears the same blue-and-yellow striped tee and plum miniskirt as in the photo, barefoot.




FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING

First frame: a wide overhead top shot — the camera high above the meadow, looking almost straight down (a near-vertical bird's-eye view, tilted just a few degrees off true vertical), so the green field fills the entire frame edge to edge: no sky, no horizon, only the flat plane of grass seen from above. The girl lies diagonally across the middle of the frame, small in the composition — her full figure from hair to bare toes taking up barely a third of the frame's diagonal — with wide grass on all sides. The meadow is lush green scattered with small white daisies and clover — natural, not overgrown. Seen from above, her pose is loose and careless: flat on her back, face up to the camera, one arm stretched straight out into the grass, the other on her stomach, knees bent and dropped to one side, red hair fanned out around her head like a sunburst, streaming in the wind. The grey tabby cat lies curled against her side at hip level, a round ball of fur from above, rippling in the gusts.




FORMAT MODE

Horizontal 16:9 (change to 9:16 if needed — the diagonal pose works in both), single continuous shot, 10–12 s.




CAMERA

Completely static: locked-off fixed overhead camera, as if mounted high on an invisible rig, framing never changes — no descent, no rise, no drift, no rotation, no zoom, no sway. Plain deep focus across the whole frame. All movement in the shot comes from the wind, the girl, the cat and the growing flowers.




ACTION TIMING

0–4 s: pure chill in hard wind — seen from above, the grass moves in huge traveling waves that sweep across the entire frame like ripples on water, daisy heads flickering, the girl's fanned-out hair streaming and twisting on the grass, her striped tee rippling against her body, the skirt hem trembling. She breathes visibly, blinks up at the camera against the gusts, a faint contented smile; her outstretched hand lazily strokes the grass. The cat squints, ears twitching, fur parting in the wind. 4–6 s: she closes her eyes, serene. 6–12 s: the growth begins unevenly — a first cluster of stems bursts from one side of her ribs, then a separate patch on her thigh, then her shoulder, her shin, her hair — asymmetric clumps in no pattern, each unfurling like time-lapse: dense thickets in some spots, single stray blossoms in others, tall stems leaning in the wind next to tiny ones. From above the patches read like colorful lichen spreading across her figure, multiplying and merging over torso, arms, legs and feet, then creeping up her neck and across her upturned face — cheeks, forehead, finally covering her features — until her whole body is one continuous ragged carpet of flowers in the exact shape of a lying figure, uneven in height, small glimpses of striped fabric and red hair in the gaps. Petals tear off and streak across the frame in the wind. Final frame: the flat green field from above, a flower-covered human silhouette lying diagonally across it, gently rising and falling with breath, the cat still curled beside it, untouched.




PHYSICS

The wind is strong and constant, never pausing: grass waves sweep the frame continuously, hair and fabric never rest while visible, flower heads bob and shudder, the cat's fur parts and ripples. The girl is fully alive — visible breathing, micro-movements of fingers and eyelids — and even after full coverage the flower silhouette keeps rising and falling gently with her breath. The growing flowers behave like real plants under wind load: stems flex at different rates by height, taller clumps sway wider, petals flutter, nothing stiff or CG-static. The growth never breaks skin and never looks painful — flowers emerge from fabric, hairline and skin as if rooted there naturally, the face covered softly, blossoms layering over it like a blanket, no distortion of her features beneath. The growth stays strictly on her body: the meadow around her keeps its normal scattered daisies and never thickens.




LIGHTING

Sunrise: the sun just risen, very low and far out of frame, raking warm golden-pink light across the field — from the top-down view this reads as long soft shadows stretching sideways across the frame from the girl's body, the cat and every grass tuft, all pointing the same direction; dew glinting on blades, backlit grass tips glowing amber where waves lift them, cool lilac-blue in the shadowed troughs between waves. Warm light catching her red hair and the flying petals; thin morning haze in the far corners. Natural sunrise contrast, not stylized — just early light on a real field.




AUDIO

Strong wind rushing through grass — the dominant sound — with rustling, a soft cat purr fading in and out under the gusts, distant morning birdsong. No music, no dialogue.




STYLE

Natural photorealism with a quiet dreamy undertone: real skin, real fur, real botany, true-to-life color, plain digital clarity, subtle sensor noise. No film grain, no halation, no vignettes, no added grade, no cinematic look — the surreal event simply happens below a plain fixed camera at dawn. No plastic skin, no illustration look, no horror imagery.




POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS

Exactly one girl matching @Image 1 and one cat, both fully in frame for the entire shot, poses consistent, framing locked, wide overhead view with grass filling 100% of the frame — no sky or horizon at any point. Wind visibly affects every element in every frame. Flower growth starts around the midpoint, spreads in uneven asymmetric patches, and by the last two seconds covers her entire body including her face — a complete flower silhouette that still breathes. The surrounding meadow keeps its original sparse daisies. No text, no watermarks, no UI.
