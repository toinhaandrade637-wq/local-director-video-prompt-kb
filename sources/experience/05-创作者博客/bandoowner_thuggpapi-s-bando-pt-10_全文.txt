Generate two clips and join them in order. Use frames from the original video as references for faces, outfits, and locations.

Prompt 1 Money Everywhere

What it creates: the opening sequence, from the cash mattress to the sidewalk sale.

13-second photorealistic retro hip-hop montage. 4:3 frame,
35mm film grain, muted warm colors, handheld movement,
quick cuts, and close-ups of unusual details.

Show in order:
— A woman makes a bed whose mattress is built entirely
from neatly stacked bundles of dollar bills.
— A man in a white basketball jersey with a red number 7
and baggy jeans vaults a polished gold subway turnstile.
— A doctor examines a man in a dark fur coat. Push into
a chest X-ray showing dollar bills where his spine should be.
— A man in a fur vest pushes a vintage baby carriage.
Inside, a small combination safe rests under cream blankets
and a lace bonnet. He gently turns its dial.
— A street seller in a matching patterned outfit displays
numbered bags of clothing and watches to two police officers.
Close-up of a bagged gold watch being exchanged for cash.

Keep the textures realistic and the humor deadpan.
