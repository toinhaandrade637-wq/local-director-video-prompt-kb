CHARACTER SHEET PROMPT

USE THE UPLOADED FACE REFERENCE IMAGE AS THE ONLY IDENTITY REFERENCE.

Create a professional ultra-photorealistic CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET of the exact person shown in the uploaded face reference.

CRITICAL IDENTITY RULE:

The uploaded face reference is the absolute source of truth for the character’s identity. Preserve the person’s exact facial identity with maximum fidelity.

Preserve exactly:

- face shape

- facial geometry

- forehead

- eyebrows

- eyes and eyelids

- eye spacing

- nose shape, bridge, tip and nostrils

- lips and mouth shape

- cheeks and cheekbones

- jawline

- chin

- ears

- natural facial asymmetry

- skin tone

- hairline

- hairstyle and hair texture

- facial hair/stubble if present

- all distinctive facial characteristics

DO NOT beautify, idealize, redesign, masculinize, feminize, age-shift, or reinterpret the person.

CHARACTER SHEET:

Create ONE clean horizontal professional studio sheet containing THREE coordinated views of the SAME PERSON:

1. FULL-BODY FRONT VIEW

- standing naturally

- full body visible from head to feet

- neutral confident posture

- arms relaxed naturally

- straight-on camera angle

2. TRUE SIDE-PROFILE VIEW

- clean 90-degree side profile

- clearly visible facial silhouette

- exact forehead, nose, lips, chin and jaw proportions from the face reference

- neutral expression

3. FRONT-FACING CLOSE-UP

- head and upper shoulders

- straight-on view

- neutral natural expression

- eyes sharply focused

- highly detailed facial rendering

IDENTITY CONSISTENCY:

All three panels must depict the exact same person.

No facial drift.

No different person between panels.

No changes to facial proportions.

No changes to hairstyle.

No changes to age appearance.

No changes to skin tone.

No changes to body proportions between views.

OUTFIT:

Use a simple, timeless neutral outfit suitable for a professional character reference sheet:

- plain fitted black crew-neck T-shirt

- simple black straight-leg trousers

- clean minimal white sneakers

- no visible logos

- no jewelry unless naturally present in the face reference

- no unnecessary accessories

The outfit must remain identical across all three views.

SKIN REALISM:

Ultra-photorealistic human skin:

- visible natural pores

- realistic pore-size variation

- fine skin grain

- subtle peach fuzz/vellus hair

- tiny natural imperfections

- faint blemishes

- realistic pigmentation variation

- natural redness variation

- authentic under-eye texture

- realistic lips

- realistic nose and cheek texture

- individual facial hairs where applicable

- believable beard/stubble follicles if present

- physically realistic subsurface scattering

- natural light penetration through thinner facial areas

- realistic soft specular highlights

Skin must look like REAL photographed human skin.

Avoid:

- beauty filter

- airbrushing

- pore removal

- waxy skin

- plastic skin

- CGI skin

- excessive smoothness

- artificial HDR

- excessive sharpening

- unrealistic micro-contrast

- perfectly uniform complexion

PHOTOGRAPHY:

Premium professional studio photography.

High-end full-frame camera look.

Natural 50mm–85mm lens rendering.

Soft large-source studio lighting.

Clean neutral light-gray/white seamless background.

Natural exposure.

Accurate white balance.

Physically realistic shadows.

Natural depth and dimensionality.

Sharp eyes and facial features.

Organic photographic detail.

Subtle depth of field on the close-up.

No cinematic color grading.

COMPOSITION:

- clean horizontal triptych

- three evenly spaced panels

- consistent camera height

- consistent lighting

- consistent background

- consistent scale where appropriate

- professional actor/model casting-reference photography

- minimal uncluttered presentation

- no decorative graphics

- no text

- no labels

- no logos

- no watermark

- no collage distortion

The final image must look like a REAL professional character-reference sheet photographed in a high-end studio.

IMPORTANT:

ONLY the uploaded face reference controls the person’s identity.

Do not borrow identity, facial features, hairstyle, or facial structure from any other image.

Do not create a generic attractive face.

Do not reinterpret the reference.

The goal is to reproduce the SAME PERSON consistently across three photographic views.
