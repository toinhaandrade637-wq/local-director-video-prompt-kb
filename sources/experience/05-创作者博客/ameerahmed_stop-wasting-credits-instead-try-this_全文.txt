Stop Wasting Credits 💸

Ever spent a bunch of credits generating a video, only to realize the camera angle is wrong, the movement looks weird, or the prompt wasn't followed correctly?

Here's a simple trick to save your credits! 👇

Step 1: Start with Seedance 2.0 at 480p. Use it to test your prompt and check the camera movements, actions, timing, and overall scene.

Step 2: If something looks off, tweak your prompt and test again. It's much cheaper to experiment at lower quality.

Step 3: Once everything looks good, switch to Seedance 2.5 at 1080p for your final generation.

🔥 The golden rule: Test cheap. Perfect your prompt. Generate in high quality.

This won't guarantee identical results between models, but it can save you a ton of credits by reducing unnecessary high-resolution regenerations!
