Replace the one knight with that same recognizable selfie identity across every shot. Match the selfie's facial structure, skin tone, eyes, brows, nose, lips, distinctive marks, facial hair, hairline, hair color, texture and length. Do not inherit the original performer's messy black hair or add hair, makeup or facial hair absent from the selfie. How I made the original (optional)
Four reference images. A two-view character sheet of me in the armour, a sheet of the black horse, one sheet for the helmet and sword, and one wide plate of the plains at golden hour from an angle the film never uses.
One 30-second Seedance 2.5 prompt for the whole story, run twice. Keeping it in one generation keeps the smoke, the hills and the light consistent from shot to shot.
Direction written like a map. The smoke is always in the south and he rides north, away from it. Without that, the model had him riding into the fire.
The edit. The best moments from the two takes cut together, one shot mirrored so the horse stays on the same side, and the last frame held on the helmet.
