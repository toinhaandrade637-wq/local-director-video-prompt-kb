a latte floats in the middle of a real street. a girl walks in, gives it a suspicious look, takes it, sips, and walks away like nothing happened.

one prompt. two scenes. 8 seconds. no editing needed.

all you need is two photos: a place and a person. the prompt does the rest.




what you need

seedance, duration set to 8 seconds
@image 1: a photo of the location (a street corner works great)




@image 2: a photo of your character (full body, face clearly visible)







how to do it

upload the location as @image 1 and the character as @image 2, in that order.
set the duration to 8 seconds.
paste the prompt from the bottom of this post.
hit generate and watch her steal a latte.




why this one makes people stop scrolling

scene 1 (0 to 2s) is the hook: the mug floats alone and nobody is on screen. people stay to find out what happens.
scene 2 (2 to 8s) has one action per beat: she walks in and looks, she takes the mug and sips, she leaves. simple beats are easy for seedance to follow.
every beat has its own audio line: footsteps, "hmm", sip, chime. that is what makes it feel real.
the drink is described by shape and layers, no brand names. bottom to top: latte, foam, whipped cream, caramel.
"no people, no moving vehicles" is in there on purpose. if your location photo has people, seedance copies them into the scene.




if something goes wrong (i tested it, here are the fixes)

face looks distorted: big expressions like "eyes wide, mouth open" break the face. use "small pleased smile" instead.
face looks stretched: the ultra-wide lens does that when she is close. keep her a few steps back, full body in frame.
mug too small: change "about 10 cm tall" to "about 15 cm tall".
whipped cream melts: add "stiff whipped cream that keeps its shape".
outfit changes mid-video: add "there is no [the thing it keeps adding]" after the outfit line.
action feels rushed: remove the "hmm" line and keep only the curious look.




make it yours

want a different drink? replace only the mug paragraph in scene 1. everything else stays. want a different place or character? just swap the photos.




try it and tag @taiscfraga so i can see yours.
