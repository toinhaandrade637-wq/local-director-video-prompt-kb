Keep the person in the video exactly as filmed: face, hair, expressions, lip-sync, every hand movement and grip, pose, timing and the camera framing, frame for frame. The reference images have no people in them, so never take a face or hair from them.

Replace only these things, from the first frame to the last:

1. The room → @Image 1: this private jet cabin, oval windows behind the person with a golden sunset above the clouds drifting slowly past, cream leather walls and burl-walnut trim.

2. The outfit → @Image 2: a tailored navy blazer, white open-collar shirt and charcoal trousers. Remove any clip-on microphone.

3. The chair and table → @Image 3: the cream quilted-leather jet seat and glossy round walnut table. The things on the table become the wagyu steak and the caviar tin on ice, each in the same spot as what it replaces.

4. Whatever they drink from → @Image 4: a crystal champagne flute, following the grip exactly. When it's put down it stands still on the table.




One light for everything: warm sunset light through the windows and soft cabin light, the same exposure on the person and the cabin, soft contact shadows and matching grain. Keep the original audio. No text or logos.

How I made the multicam version (optional)
One take in my bedroom on a DJI: a water bottle, a tissue box and a small plate on a side table.
Genjutsu swapped the room, my outfit, the table and every prop in one pass (the card above). The bottle became the champagne, the tissue box the wagyu and the plate the caviar.
Then one Seedance 2.5 generation re-shot that clip from new angles: a side shot, the sip close-up, a top-down of the table and a table-level shot. I gave it the Genjutsu clip, a grid of my face and my voice, and cut on the pauses between my lines.
In the edit I kept my real voice and only used the new angles where my lips matched.
