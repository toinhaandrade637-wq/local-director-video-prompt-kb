Step 2: Character Sheet

Use Outfit Images in Prompt Reference

Create a high-end professional fashion character reference sheet using two visual references.




REFERENCE 1 — CHARACTER:

Use the first reference image strictly for the character's identity and physical appearance. Preserve the same face, facial structure, hairstyle, hairline, skin tone, skin texture, body proportions, height, physique, and overall recognizable appearance. The character must remain the exact same person throughout every view.




REFERENCE 2 — OUTFIT:

Use the second reference image strictly for the clothing design. Recreate the outfit accurately, including the exact garment silhouettes, proportions, colors, materials, textures, prints, graphics, embroidery, stitching, seams, pockets, hems, cuffs, logos/lettering, distressing, folds, and overall styling.




OUTFIT TRANSFER:

Dress the character from REFERENCE 1 in the complete outfit shown in REFERENCE 2. Do not redesign, simplify, reinterpret, or substitute the clothing. Preserve the visual identity and construction of the outfit while naturally adapting it to the character's body.




CHARACTER CONSISTENCY:

The same character must appear in every image/view. Maintain identical facial features, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, ears, hairstyle, hair texture, skin tone, body shape, height, proportions, and apparent age. No identity drift between angles.




MULTI-ANGLE PRESENTATION:

Create a professional multi-view fashion reference sheet showing the same character and outfit from multiple camera angles:




1. Full-body FRONT VIEW — directly facing camera

2. FRONT 3/4 VIEW — approximately 45 degrees

3. FULL SIDE PROFILE — 90 degrees

4. BACK 3/4 VIEW — approximately 135 degrees

5. FULL BACK VIEW — directly facing away from camera




Keep the character standing naturally with consistent height, proportions, and posture across all views.




COMPOSITION:

Arrange all views clearly in one clean professional fashion lookbook / character turnaround sheet. Each view should be full-body and completely visible from head to shoes. Maintain consistent scale between all views. Leave enough space between figures so garments are clearly visible.




CAMERA:

Professional studio fashion photography, eye-level camera, full-body framing, realistic perspective, 50mm photographic look, minimal lens distortion, straight vertical lines, consistent camera height and distance across all views.




LIGHTING:

Soft professional studio lighting, large diffused key light, subtle fill light, natural realistic shadows, even exposure across the entire character and clothing. Preserve accurate garment colors and material appearance.




BACKGROUND:

Clean seamless studio background, simple neutral environment, no furniture, no props, no distractions. Premium fashion catalog aesthetic.




REALISM:

Photorealistic human anatomy, realistic skin texture, natural pores, realistic hair, physically accurate fabric, natural garment folds and draping, realistic stitching and construction, accurate shadows and contact points, high-end editorial fashion photography.




IMPORTANT:

The character reference controls IDENTITY.

The outfit reference controls CLOTHING.

Do not merge or alter the character's facial identity.

Do not alter the outfit design.

Do not create different people for different angles.

Do not change body proportions between views.

Do not change hairstyle between views.

Do not add accessories that are not present in the outfit reference.

Do not remove any visible garment details from the outfit reference.




Output: an extremely clean, cohesive, photorealistic multi-angle fashion character reference sheet showing the exact same character wearing the exact referenced outfit.
