Seedance 2.5 prompt:

SCENE CONTEXT

A miniature handmade paper town where the residents are paper birds and origami unicorns. A young woman in a paper crown and lace wings lives here at the town's scale, like a doll in a dollhouse, inside the paper castle. Her whole day in fifteen seconds, photographed one pose at a time: she wakes on a tall stack of mattresses, looks out at the morning, climbs down, steps out of the castle, waters the rhinestone flowers, strolls through the town with the origami unicorns at her heels, and at the end hoists the small paper moon up on its thread while the paper sun comes down. A remembered dream. Handmade paper stop-motion, whimsical surreal living scrapbook, fashion editorial staged like a toy theater. Sound: diegetic paper foley only, no music.




ACTIVE REFERENCES

@Image 1 — set anchor: the paper town exterior — castle, houses, paper trees, fabric grass, paper flowers, toy animal figures, cotton-wool clouds, patterned pale wallpaper backdrop, pastel palette, cardboard and cut-paper materials. Use it for geography, scale, materials and atmosphere, not for framing.

@Image 2 — the woman, whole: face, hair, skin, body proportions, every garment, the paper crown, the layered wings, socks and shoes exactly from here, unchanged, early twenties, sleepy relaxed baseline expression. 100% matches the reference. She sleeps in this outfit. The crown is off her head only at the start of shot 3, lying beside her on the sheet, and is on her head from 4.9s to the end.




LOCATION MAP

Exterior, from @Image 1: patterned pale wallpaper with small printed motifs behind everything; the pale-pink paper castle with blue conical spires and tiny paper flags dead center, its pink arched door at ground level with a shallow paper doorstep; small blue-and-cream cottage with brown roof camera-left, a paper pine tree beside it; pink paper flowering bushes; tall green-and-white cardboard house with pink scalloped roof camera-right; an open lawn of green fabric grass between them, clean and empty. Beside the doorstep: a cluster of closed paper flowers with flat-back rhinestone centers. The set's toy animal figures stand still throughout. Foreground: cotton-wool clouds along the bottom edge. Above the lawn: flat paper clouds and pale-blue and pink satin ribbon bows on visible white threads. The hoist: a white thread runs from a small paper cleat on the cottage wall at her chest height, up over a small hook hanging just above her head height at the lawn's edge, and down again; at the start the palm-sized paper sun disc hangs from the hook end, and the palm-sized paper crescent moon, a tiny bulb inside, rests on the grass at the cottage wall tied to the other end. Both discs are the same size as the paper clouds beside them. Every paper window glows warm from a tiny bulb inside.

Interior, castle bedroom, built from the same paper as the exterior: a small pale-pink paper room, a tall arched paper window on the back wall glowing with soft morning light, a stack of six mismatched floral-print paper mattresses in the center of the room, taller than she is, firm and square, a wrinkled pink satin sheet draped over the top mattress and spilling down one side of the stack to pool on the floor.

Scale, constant through the whole film: she is an inhabitant — standing, her crown top reaches the top of the castle door arch; the cottage eave is at her head height; the green house stands twice her height; lying on the mattress stack she is close to the window's sill.




BACKGROUND EXTRAS (exterior wide shots)

Two kinds of extras, all hand-sized to her, all staying behind her or in the lower foreground, never covering her face.

Paper birds: born from the wallpaper — a printed motif lifts off the wall, folds into a bird in three held folds, descends on a visible white thread and lands on a roof, a fence or a branch. 8 to 12 birds by the end of shot 2. The bird in shot 1 is already a bird and simply flies past.

Origami unicorns: residents of the town — they hop out of the cottage door, the green house's door and from behind the flowering bushes onto the lawn as the town wakes. 6 to 8 unicorns, already folded, pearlescent white paper with visible creases. In shot 6 they walk with her.

Clouds turn over on their threads in one step now and then, showing their other pastel color.




FORMAT MODE

Controlled seven-shot sequence, HARD CUT at 1.5s, 3.5s, 6.0s, 8.0s, 9.5s and 12.5s. Real-time stop-motion timing. Shots 1 and 2 are intentionally without the woman; she first appears at 3.5s inside the castle.




STOP-MOTION RULE (every shot)

Everything moves like physical objects photographed one pose at a time: small displacement, short hold, next pose. Each held pose lasts 3 to 4 frames, then the object jumps to its next position. Every frame is a sharp still photograph with solid paper edges at full opacity; every change on screen is a physical displacement or a fold along a visible crease. The woman changes pose in small steps with short holds. Birds move in short flutters on their threads; unicorns move in 3 cm hops. Paper edges, wing ruffles, satin folds and threads carry a tiny frame-to-frame jitter of hand-placed objects. Shapes and faces stay identical between poses.




SURFACE OVERLAYS (every shot)

A sheet of glass sits in front of the lens; small holographic star sequins in silver and dusty green are placed on it one per held pose and stay — sparse at the start, a loose scatter by the end. Stars only. They live on the picture surface, sharp and flat over the scene.




OPTICS

Extreme close-up (shot 1): 18° diagonal field of view, classic telephoto lens character, camera far from subject, tight framing through lens reach, razor-thin focus on the subject, background melting into soft pastel bokeh.

Extreme wide (shot 2): 47° diagonal field of view, standard normal lens character, camera 8 m from the town at standing eye level, rectilinear, deep focus, the whole town small in frame.

Interior wide (shot 3): 47° diagonal field of view, camera 3 m from the mattress stack at the height of its top, rectilinear, the whole stack, the floor at its base and the window in frame.

Wide (shot 4): 47° diagonal field of view, camera 4 m from the doorstep at her standing eye level, straight on, rectilinear, deep focus with only the foreground cotton clouds soft.

Medium (shot 5): 29° diagonal field of view, short telephoto lens character, camera 4 m from her, from camera-left, lens at the height of her kneeling face, framing from just above her crown to the flowers at the bottom of frame, subject sharp, castle wall soft behind.

Wide from above (shot 6): 47° diagonal field of view, camera 5 m from the lawn, raised to a 35° downward angle from camera-right, rectilinear, deep focus.

Medium (shot 7): 29° diagonal field of view, short telephoto lens character, camera 4 m from her at her eye level from camera-left, framing from just above the hook to her knees, subject sharp, town soft behind.

Lens held constant within each shot.




CAMERA

Locked-off tripod in all seven shots. The camera stays perfectly still; all motion lives in the subjects and on the glass.




ACTING TASK — the woman (the work is in her eyes; the face stays sleepy, relaxed, slightly pensive)

GOAL: do the rounds that carry the day — wake, water, walk the town with its unicorns, hoist the moon when it is time. OBSTACLE: she is half asleep and everything around her is a little alive. TACTIC: one thing at a time, each checked with her eyes before she moves on — the window, crown on, flowers open, the unicorns at her heels, moon at the hook. She treats every strangeness as ordinary. Her last look is to us: day is done. (Safety: gaze always on a target — window, crown, flowers, unicorns, thread, moon, lens; natural blink cadence.)




ACTION TIMING

0.0s to 1.5s — SHOT 1, extreme close-up of the castle's tallest blue spire and its tiny paper flag, wallpaper soft behind.

0.0–0.3s: held frame.

0.3–1.3s: a paper bird on a visible thread crosses the frame from left to right in five short flutters, passing in front of the spire.

1.3–1.5s: the little flag turns one step in its wake. The first star sequin appears on the glass.

1.5s HARD CUT

1.5s to 3.5s — SHOT 2, extreme wide, straight on: the whole town fills the lower half of frame as an island of light, wallpaper dim in the upper half. Vertical frame. The town wakes: motifs peel off the wallpaper one after another and fold into birds that descend on threads and land on roofs and fences; the cottage door and the green house's door open in two steps each and unicorns hop out onto the lawn, more hop out from behind the flowering bushes; clouds and bows sway on their threads in 1 cm steps; the castle door stays closed; the palm-sized paper sun hangs from its hook above the lawn.

3.5s HARD CUT

3.5s to 6.0s — SHOT 3, interior wide: the castle bedroom, the mattress stack center frame, the glowing arched window behind it. She lies asleep on her stomach on top of the pink satin sheet, cheek on her folded arms, legs bent up at the knees with ankles crossed, wings resting on her back, the paper crown lying on the sheet beside her hand.

3.5–4.0s: held pose; the window light brightens one step.

4.0–4.5s: her eyes open; she lifts her head in one step and looks at the window, held.

4.5–4.9s: she pushes up to sitting in one step and sets the crown on her head with both hands in two steps.

4.9–5.6s: she swings her legs over the sheeted side of the stack in one step and slides down the satin sheet to the floor in three held steps, the sheet stretching under her, the stack standing exactly as it was.

5.6–6.0s: she stands up at the base of the stack in one step and walks toward the room's door, out of frame.

6.0s HARD CUT

6.0s to 8.0s — SHOT 4, wide, straight on, lens at her standing eye level: the castle's pink arched door and doorstep at center, the rhinestone flowers beside the step, birds on the door arch, unicorns on the lawn behind the foreground clouds.

6.0–6.6s: the door swings open on its hinge in two held steps.

6.6–7.4s: she steps out onto the doorstep in two steps, a small paper watering can folded from pearlescent paper in her right hand; the birds lift off the arch and settle on the nearest roof.

7.4–8.0s: she looks at the lawn, then down at the flowers, in two steps.

8.0s HARD CUT

8.0s to 9.5s — SHOT 5, medium from camera-left: she kneels on the doorstep beside the cluster of closed paper flowers, in three-quarter view, the watering can in her right hand held over the flowers, her face, crown and wings in the upper two thirds of frame, the flowers in the lower third, the castle wall soft behind her.

8.0–8.6s: she tilts the can in two steps, eyes on the flowers; four cut-paper teardrops fall one per held pose onto them.

8.6–9.3s: the flowers open in four held folds each, one crease at a time; their flat-back rhinestone centers catch the light as they turn up; her eyes follow them.

9.3–9.5s: a paper bird lands on the spout in one step and stays; she glances at it.

9.5s HARD CUT

9.5s to 12.5s — SHOT 6, wide from camera-right, 35° downward angle, looking into the town like into an open dollhouse: the lawn center frame, cottage at the left edge, green house at the right edge, doorstep at the top of frame.

9.5–11.6s: she strolls from the doorstep across the lawn toward the cottage in seven held steps, watering can in her left hand, wings shifting with each step; five unicorns walk with her in a loose procession — two hopping at her heels, two alongside, one hopping ahead and turning back to her in one step; birds circle above them on their threads; she looks down at the unicorns as she walks, then ahead.

11.6–12.1s: she stops at the cottage wall in one step; the lead unicorn hops up to her free hand and she touches its paper nose with one finger in one step, held.

12.1–12.5s: she sets the can down at the cottage wall in one step and lifts the hoist thread off its paper cleat in one step; the unicorns settle on the grass behind her.

12.5s HARD CUT

12.5s to 15.0s — SHOT 7, medium from camera-left, lens at her eye level: she stands at the cottage wall in three-quarter view, the hoist thread in both hands, the thread running up to the small hook just above her head at the top right of frame, the palm-sized paper sun hanging from the hook, the palm-sized paper crescent moon resting on the grass at her feet at the bottom edge, the town soft behind her. The wallpaper stays exactly as it is for the whole shot; the only things that move in the sky are the two small discs on their thread.

12.5–13.7s: she pulls the thread hand over hand in four held steps; with each pull the moon rises one step up past her hip, her shoulder, her crown, and the sun lowers one step toward the grass; both discs stay palm-sized, the same size as the clouds beside them.

13.7–14.2s: the moon reaches the hook and hangs there; the sun rests on the grass; she loops the thread over the cleat in one step; the moon's bulb lights in one step, a pale cool glow on her cheek, and the beam of daylight on the set dims two steps, so the warm window glow now carries the frame.

14.2–15.0s: her eyes come into the lens and hold, sleepy and calm. A loose scatter of star sequins on the glass. Final held frame.




PHYSICS AND MATERIALS

Cardboard houses with visible scored folds and cut edges; wallpaper motifs that lift like stickers and leave a faint pale patch on the wall; paper birds and origami unicorns with rigid creases; fabric grass mat that dents under her shoes; cotton-wool clouds; flat paper clouds, ribbon bows, the sun and the moon hanging under gravity on taut threads. The hoist behaves like a real thread over a hook: it goes taut as she pulls, the two discs move opposite ways by the same amount, each pull is a hand's length. The mattress stack is printed card, firm and square, and keeps its full height for the whole film; the satin sheet slides and stretches under her as she slides down, wrinkling and pooling at the base. The paper watering can creases slightly in her grip; the cut-paper teardrops fall straight down and lie flat where they land. Wings stay attached to her back and shift slightly with her shoulders; the crown stays seated once it is on.




LIGHTING

Theatrical daylight. Exterior: one soft wide beam from top-left, like light through a skylight, brightest on her and the castle door, falling off into soft lavender-grey dimness at the frame edges, so the town reads as an island of light on a stage; every paper window glows warm from a tiny bulb inside, with soft halation, in every exterior shot. The set's light stays the same while the discs move; in shot 7 the beam dims two steps once the moon is hung, and the moon's bulb adds only a small pale glow on her cheek. Interior: the arched window is the only source, a soft milky beam across the mattress stack and the satin sheet, the far walls falling into gentle pink shadow. Both: a faint frontal flash-like fill from the camera lifts her face and the pearlescent creatures with a gentle halo. Shadows short and delicate, falling camera-right. Exposure held for her face. Palette: milk white, dusty pink, pale blue, lavender, soft green, warm window glow, satin pink; the pink skirt is the single saturated accent.




LOOK — DREAM-MEMORY FILTER (every shot)

A lo-fi film snapshot of a dream remembered years later: soft halation around highlights and glowing windows, milky lifted blacks, low contrast, colors drifted toward pink and lavender, a faint warm light leak in the top-left corner, fine grain with a few dust specks, gentle softness at the frame edges, a subtle exposure flicker between held poses like hand-shot photographs. The star sequins on the glass stay crisp and flat over this haze.




AUDIO

Diegetic foley only, recorded on the set, close and dry: a single paper bird's wingbeats on its thread, paper creases snapping, the rustle of small paper wings, hops of folded feet on the grass mat, satin sliding over card as she slides down, the hinge of a paper door, taps of paper teardrops, the thread running over the hook as she pulls, the tick of a small bulb switching on, quiet room tone. Silence between moves. Music: none. No piano, no score, no background music of any kind; the soundtrack is only the foley listed above.




POSITIVE LOCKS

Exactly one woman in frame in shots 3 to 7, the same in every one: face, hair, wardrobe, crown and wings 100% @Image 2, face sleepy and relaxed throughout, her size identical in every shot. Birds and unicorns are background extras, small, behind her or at her feet, never in front of her face. The lawn stays clean fabric grass with nothing lying on it except the moon at the cottage wall. The mattress stack keeps its full height in shot 3. The sun and the moon are palm-sized paper props hanging inside the set at her scale, the same size as the paper clouds, in front of a wallpaper that stays unchanged; the swap happens beside her at the cottage wall, by her hands, on a thread: sun down, moon up. The town keeps the layout of @Image 1 in every exterior shot. Camera still; only objects, creatures and stars move, in held steps. Style: handmade paper stop-motion, fashion editorial staged like a toy theater, whimsical surreal living scrapbook, lo-fi dream-memory texture.
