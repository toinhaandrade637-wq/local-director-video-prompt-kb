SCENE CONTEXT

The woman from the character photo walks briskly across a daytime street from screen-left to screen-right. Seven fully opaque, frozen impressions of her remain behind along her path, each separated by a clearly visible gap. The real woman keeps walking at normal speed while the impressions stay completely still.




ACTIVE REFERENCE

The character photo defines her exact face, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportions, clothing and accessories. Every frozen impression reproduces this same woman and outfit, captured at a different moment of her walk.




LOCATION AND SPATIAL BLOCKING

An urban pedestrian crossing bordered by slate-blue and charcoal-grey façades, glass windows and pale concrete sidewalks. White crossing stripes run across the grey asphalt.




Her walking path extends approximately 12 meters horizontally across the composition, parallel to the image plane. She moves from left to right at a constant distance from the camera.




All seven impressions occupy this same line and depth plane, with matching body scale. Their bodies face screen-right. The crossing remains clear around them; distant pedestrians stay on the background sidewalk.




FORMAT MODE

One continuous 10-second shot. Normal-speed, real-time walking throughout. No slow motion, speed ramps or pauses by the moving protagonist.




CAMERA AND COMPOSITION

A fixed, wide, full-body side view from across the street, camera approximately at waist height. Frame the complete walking route with space at both edges.




Keep her entire body, the pavement beneath her feet and every accumulated impression visible. The camera remains in place so the separation between figures reads clearly. Minimal natural handheld breathing.




FIRST FRAME

She is already mid-stride near the left edge, facing screen-right. Her chin is slightly raised, shoulders relaxed, posture upright. The rest of the crossing is open ahead of her.




ACTION TIMING

0.0s to 1.0s — WALK

She advances with brisk, confident everyday steps, approximately 1.2 meters per second. Her arms swing naturally close to her body. She looks ahead with a composed, slightly defiant expression.




1.0s to 8.0s — SEVEN FROZEN IMPRESSIONS

At approximately 1.2-second intervals, one frozen impression appears in a position she has already passed and fully cleared.




Each impression captures an earlier phase of her stride. It appears immediately at full opacity and remains fixed in that position. The live woman is already about one stride ahead when it appears, keeping their outlines visibly separated.




Seven impressions accumulate in order from left to right. Their centers are approximately 1.4 meters apart, with clear strips of visible street between their outer silhouettes. Hands, clothing and legs remain visually separate from neighboring impressions.




8.4s to 10.0s — COMPLETE TRAIL

All seven impressions remain in place. The real woman continues walking toward the right edge at the same normal pace, increasing her distance from the final impression.




She keeps looking ahead and maintains her confident stride. The finished composition shows seven stationary figures behind one moving woman.




FROZEN-IMAGE EFFECT

The impressions are full-color photographic freeze-frames with solid clothing, natural skin tones and fully opaque bodies. Background details are completely hidden wherever an impression covers them.




Each holds its exact captured pose: face, fingers, hair and fabric remain motionless. Its supporting foot stays anchored at the original ground-contact point.




The impressions neither drift nor animate. They remain equally opaque until the end, with distinct outlines and visible space between them. Only the original woman continues moving.




PHYSICS

Her live movement retains natural cadence, heel contact, weight transfer and toe push-off. Hair and clothing respond to her walking with subtle inertia. The freeze-frame effect applies only to the seven impressions; time continues normally throughout the street.




LIGHTING AND COLOR

Cool directional daylight from high behind and to camera-right. Bright daylight catches the upper edges of her hair and shoulders, while the camera sees the shaded side of her face.




Soft blue-grey environmental bounce reveals her facial structure without flattening the shadows. Preserve detail in the luminous hair edges and natural shaded skin.




Use slate blue, charcoal grey, cool silver highlights and restrained saturation. Every impression retains the same lighting direction and exposure as the live woman.




Apply mild recorded-image softness and restrained highlight bloom while keeping the separate silhouettes clearly defined.




AUDIO

Natural footsteps, subdued traffic and distant street ambience. The protagonist remains silent.

SCENE CONTEXT

The woman from the character photo stands behind a white table on a daytime city street. Holding a pastel-pink vacuum, she rapidly sucks everyday objects off the tabletop, then looks down into the low camera with a satisfied, defiant expression. The action starts immediately and feels brisk, confident and playful.




ACTIVE REFERENCES

Character photo: the sole source for her face, hairstyle, body proportions, clothing and accessories.




Vacuum reference image: controls the equipment design and low tabletop camera angle. Reproduce its broad wedge-shaped suction head, short rigid handgrip, cylindrical connector and flexible corrugated hose. Recolor the plastic housing and handle soft pastel pink, retaining subtle metallic connector details.




The reference controls equipment and composition only. The setting is outdoors, and the woman retains her own referenced appearance.




FIXED VACUUM DESIGN

Use ONE rigid pastel-pink floorhead throughout: broad, low and trapezoidal, with softly rounded corners and a single wide rectangular suction opening along its lower front edge.




Its solid shape, proportions, color and intake geometry remain identical throughout the shot. The cylindrical connector stays behind the floorhead and never replaces it. The opening remains flat and rectangular; it never becomes a round mouth, narrow tube or different attachment.




LOCATION AND FIRST FRAME

A white rectangular table stands on pale concrete pavement. Slate-blue façades, charcoal-grey walls and glass windows form the background.




The woman stands behind the far edge, upper body near the center. The large pink floorhead occupies the lower-left foreground.




Six objects lie irregularly across the center-right tabletop, with visible gaps: a crumpled receipt, an unmarked plastic card, a small keyring, a closed lip-balm tube, a slim miniature music player and a small earbud case. All fit through the rectangular intake.




She grips the rigid handle with her right hand, arm extended. Her free hand rests on her hip. The connected hose curves behind her wrist and exits behind the table.




The floorhead points diagonally toward the objects. Its front edge is slightly raised, keeping the actual intake visible.




FORMAT MODE

One continuous 6-second shot. Normal-speed, real-time action. Dynamic movement comes from decisive gestures and rapidly accelerating objects, without slow motion or speed ramps.




OPTICS AND CAMERA

Match the reference’s low, upward-looking tabletop view.




Camera approximately 5 centimeters above the front edge of the table, approximately 84° diagonal field of view. The floorhead looms large near the lens, while her face and upper body remain visible above it.




Keep the intake, object trajectories and expression readable together. The camera holds its position with subtle handheld breathing.




ACTION TIMING

0.0s to 0.5s — IMMEDIATE START

The vacuum is already running. She leans forward and makes a short, decisive sweep toward the objects.




The receipt immediately flutters, and the card begins sliding. Her posture is assured, chin slightly raised, expression cool and self-satisfied.




0.5s to 4.8s — RAPID SUCTION

She guides the same floorhead toward screen-right in one brisk, controlled sweep, making small wrist corrections to collect everything.




The receipt folds inward and disappears through the rectangular intake. The card skims across the tabletop. The keys scrape, accelerate and rattle inside. The lip-balm tube rolls and aligns with the opening. The music player and earbud case slide, tip slightly and follow.




The objects enter in rapid succession, with brief, readable separation between them. Show each crossing the visible intake edge before disappearing. Their paths lead toward the nozzle rather than away from it.




Her shoulder and torso follow her working arm naturally. The hose flexes with each adjustment. She remains relaxed and confidently in control.




4.8s to 6.0s — DEFIANT FINISH

The final object enters, leaving the tabletop visibly empty.




She stops the floorhead with one precise movement and releases the control. Keeping her free hand on her hip, she looks directly down into the lens, narrows her eyes slightly and lifts her chin.




One corner of her mouth rises into a small, smug half-smile. Hold this satisfied, challenging look through the final frame. The pink rectangular floorhead remains visible and unchanged below her.




PHYSICS

Suction visibly causes every movement: objects slide or roll, accelerate and enter the opening. They retain their recognizable shapes until they pass inside.




The table stays stationary. Her grip remains secure. The rigid floorhead retains its shape while the flexible hose responds with natural weight and resistance.




LIGHTING AND COLOR

Cool directional daylight from high behind and to camera-right. Bright edges define her hair, shoulders and the pink housing.




Her face remains naturally shaded, with reflected daylight from the white table revealing her eyes and facial structure. Preserve the contrast between luminous edges and shaded skin.




Slate blue, charcoal grey, cool silver highlights, neutral white and restrained saturation. The pastel-pink vacuum is the main color accent. Gentle highlight bloom, clear object edges and readable facial detail.




AUDIO

Vacuum motor from the first frame, quick paper flutter, keys rattling and short object clacks. Quiet street ambience underneath. The motor winds down as she holds the camera’s gaze.

SCENE CONTEXT

The woman from the character photo walks confidently through a crowded daytime street. The camera searches across passing pedestrians, discovers her, then rapidly zooms in. She notices the lens, gives it a brief, defiant look, turns her gaze forward and keeps walking.




ACTIVE REFERENCES

Character photo: the sole source for the protagonist’s face, hair, body proportions, outfit and accessories. Her appearance matches this reference throughout.




First attached image: camera angle, layered crowd composition, three-quarter side view of the protagonist, compressed depth and blurred pedestrians crossing close to the lens.




Second attached image: daylight direction, exposure and color treatment only — cool blue-grey surroundings, bright backlit hair, shaded facial planes and restrained natural skin tones.




LOCATION MAP

An urban pedestrian street bordered by grey-blue façades and glass windows. The camera observes from the side of the pedestrian flow, approximately 8 meters from the protagonist’s walking path.




Anonymous pedestrians occupy three depth layers: large blurred figures close to the lens, the protagonist in the middle distance, and softer pedestrians against the buildings.




Her route runs from screen-right toward screen-left, parallel to the façades.




FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING

The opening frame is already filled with moving pedestrians. The protagonist initially remains beyond the right edge, allowing the camera to genuinely search before finding her.




Once discovered, she appears between foreground shoulders near the center of the composition. Her body remains oriented along her walking path. She turns only her head and eyes toward the camera during the brief exchange of looks.




FORMAT MODE

One continuous 10-second take. Real-time movement.




OPTICS

Begin with approximately 29° diagonal field of view, observing the crowd from a distance. During the discovery beat, rapidly narrow toward approximately 6° for a tight head-and-shoulders portrait.




The close framing comes from zooming while the camera remains physically across the street. Foreground pedestrians become large soft shapes; the background compresses behind her. Facial proportions stay natural.




CAMERA

Camera at the protagonist’s eye height, viewing her from a three-quarter side angle as in the first reference.




The operator initially pans right across the crowd. After finding her, the camera reverses into a controlled leftward follow-pan that matches her walking pace. Subtle operator breathing and small framing corrections give the shot human weight.




ACTION TIMING

0.0s to 2.8s — SEARCH

The camera pans right across passing faces, shoulders and gaps in the crowd. Nearby pedestrians briefly obscure parts of the frame with soft directional motion blur.




The protagonist enters the middle-distance view from the right, walking left with relaxed shoulders, a slightly lifted chin and decisive, even strides. Her eyes remain on the path ahead. A foreground pedestrian clears the lens, revealing her face.




2.8s to 4.2s — DISCOVERY AND ZOOM

The camera picks her out and begins following her leftward. A rapid zoom closes from the layered crowd view to her head and shoulders.




The operator briefly overshoots the framing, placing her face slightly off-center, then makes one small correction. Focus softens during the zoom and settles on her eyes. The zoom is a continuous visible lens movement.




4.2s to 6.3s — SHE NOTICES

Still walking, she senses the camera. Her eyes move toward the lens first, followed by a small turn of her head.




She holds direct eye contact, slightly narrows her eyes and lifts her chin a fraction. Her lips remain composed, one corner barely rising into a challenging half-smirk. Her expression reads as bold and fully aware of being watched.




The camera maintains the close portrait while following her. Her face remains unobstructed during this exchange.




6.3s to 10.0s — MOVING ON

She deliberately breaks eye contact, turns her head back toward screen-left and looks along her route.




Her stride continues at the same confident pace. The camera follows her three-quarter profile for the remaining seconds. Near the end, a blurred pedestrian crosses the lower foreground while her head stays visible above the moving shape.




PHYSICS

Every step has clear ground contact, weight transfer and toe push-off. Her shoulders move subtly with her stride. Hair and clothing respond to walking and a light street breeze with a slight delay. Foreground blur follows pedestrian movement; the tracked protagonist remains readable.




LIGHTING AND COLOR

Match the second attached reference. Directional daylight comes from high behind and to camera-right, catching the upper edges of her hair and shoulders. The camera views the shaded side of her face.




Cool environmental bounce gently reveals her eyes and facial structure while preserving the contrast between luminous hair edges and shaded skin. Expose to retain the bright rim light; her face stays naturally shadowed rather than evenly front-lit.




Use slate blue, charcoal grey, cool silver highlights and restrained saturation. Preserve natural skin color. Apply mild recorded-image softness and subtle glow around bright hair strands. The first reference controls composition only; its warm brown grading does not carry into this shot.




AUDIO

Natural footsteps, subdued traffic and indistinct crowd ambience. The protagonist remains silent.

Create a 7-second vertical fashion-art video, 9:16, one continuous static wide shot, on a quiet Lower East Side sidewalk in front of a white-painted storefront with a glass entrance door and a weathered wooden street bench.




CHARACTER (identical in every appearance)

A slim young adult woman with fair skin and long straight platinum blonde hair falling past her shoulders, with a long side-swept fringe sweeping across her forehead to one side. Soft 2000s makeup: lightly bronzed skin, subtle brown eyeliner, nude glossy lips, dark nail polish. She wears a fitted black short-sleeve cropped T-shirt with a large cross made of silver pyramid studs on the front, low-rise leopard print denim micro shorts with frayed hems, a wide black leather belt covered in silver pyramid studs, gray knit over-the-knee socks with folded cream tops, and knee-high black suede boots covered in long swinging fringe with a high hidden heel.




CAMERA

A static wide shot at eye level, locked on a tripod, framing the bench, the storefront door and the sidewalk. The framing stays completely fixed for all 7 seconds, with no zoom and no camera movement.




FLASH EFFECT

Each time she vanishes, a burst of bright white light like a giant camera flash erupts from her body. Her whole silhouette glows white from within for a split second, then disappears in the flash, leaving a faint glowing afterimage of her outline that fades within half a second. The street around her stays unchanged.




TIMING

0.0–2.0s — She sits on the bench, legs crossed, one fringe boot forward, leaning back. White wired earbuds are in her ears, the cable running to a small silver music player in her hand. She nods softly to the music, eyes half-closed.

2.0–2.3s — FLASH: her silhouette glows white and she vanishes. The bench is empty.

2.3–4.3s — She reappears mid-stride on the sidewalk, walking from screen-left to screen-right with a confident runway walk: hips swaying, boot fringe swinging, hair moving with each step, chin slightly raised.

4.3–4.6s — FLASH: she vanishes mid-step, leaving her glowing outline for a moment.

4.6–6.6s — The glass door of the building swings open and she steps out, talking on a phone held to her ear, a quick laugh, then breaks into a run along the sidewalk toward screen-left, hair and fringe flying.

6.6–6.9s — FLASH: she vanishes mid-run in a final white burst.

6.9–7.0s — The empty sidewalk, the bench and the slowly closing door. The final frame shows the quiet street with no one in it.




LIGHT AND AUDIO

Soft natural daylight bouncing off the white storefront. Each flash briefly lights the storefront and pavement in cold white light. Muffled indie music from the earbuds, a sharp electric flash pop with a soft whoosh for each disappearance, confident heel clicks, the door creak, her voice faintly on the phone, running footsteps, quiet city ambience.




Maintain identical face, hair and outfit in all three appearances. The camera stays perfectly static for the whole shot. Photorealistic, high detail, natural movement, 4K.
