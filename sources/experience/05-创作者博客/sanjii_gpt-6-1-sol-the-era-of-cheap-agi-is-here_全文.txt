Open-World Level, Part 2 — Bring It to Life

What it does: Layers a day/night cycle, NPCs with daily routines that react to the player, and real physics on top of the city from Part 1.

Now make the city feel alive. Keep the current look, districts and missions.




1. Day/night cycle: one full cycle every 20 minutes. Hazy golden mornings, bright afternoons, neon nights. Street lights and shop signs switch on at dusk, and the market closes at night.




2. NPCs: fill the streets with pedestrians who follow daily routines: walking to work, shopping at the market, drinking at the bar. They react to the player: they step aside when you push through, panic and run during shootouts, and call out when you draw a weapon. Give key NPCs dialogue and side tasks, like "Help the vendor" in the plaza and "Make him talk" at the docks.




3. Physics: crates, barrels and market stalls can be knocked over and pile up realistically. Ragdoll on knockdowns. Props react to bullets and punches.
