90s multi-camera sitcom scene, continuous, 9:16 vertical.

Shot on 35mm film, bright evenly lit cosy apartment living room, high-key warm

tungsten lighting, soft fill from every side, no harsh shadows, flat theatrical

staging, characters face camera, subtle film grain, sitcom set brightness.

EVENING: deep blue dusk outside the windows, warm lamps lit inside, soft

flickering bluish TV light on their faces. All dialogue in English.




CHARACTERS —

KAYE: [tag your character sheet here] woman in her early 20s, long straight dark-brown hair with centre parting,

thick natural eyebrows, 90s brown lipstick. Oversized chunky ribbed knit sweater

with horizontal stripes in purple, mustard, orange, dark green and burgundy,

dropped shoulders, hem below the hips, midriff fully covered, sleeves over her

hands. High-waisted baggy light-wash mom jeans, black high-top canvas sneakers,

beaded bracelets on her left wrist, thin gold choker. Speaks American English.

BOB: @Image 2 @Image 3  photorealistic real chubby grey-blue Scottish Fold cat, small folded ears

pressed flat, large round copper-orange eyes, round grumpy face, plush dense fur.

Burgundy red baseball cap worn backwards, oversized red and black plaid flannel

shirt open over its fur, rectangular blue-light computer glasses. Real cat

anatomy and proportions, four legs, sits on its haunches, NOT anthropomorphic,

no human arms, no human hands.

BOB'S VOICE: @audio_1 a refined, well-spoken older British gentleman — warm timbre, dry

wit, weary aristocratic irritation. Natural expressive intonation with drawn-out

vowels, pauses mid-sentence, sighs and emphasis. Never flat, never monotone,

never robotic or synthetic.




PROP — ICED MATCHA LATTE in a clear plastic coffee-shop cup with a round domed

lid and a thick green straw: ice cubes, a bright vivid green matcha layer sitting

on top of creamy white milk, condensation beading on the cup. Hers is a large cup,

the cat's is a small one. No other drinks anywhere in the scene.




SET — @Image 4 cosy lived-in apartment living room. Deep red velvet sofa facing camera,

mustard and cream pillows, knitted blanket, low mid-century coffee table on a

geometric striped rug, cream walls, white painted brick, houseplants, warm fairy

lights, open cluttered shelves. A boxy CRT television on a low stand opposite the

sofa, mostly off-frame, its glow falling on the sofa. A wooden tray with a

sandwich on a plate.




0:00–0:12 — MEDIUM SHOT, then SLOW ZOOM OUT. Kaye happily flops onto the red sofa

with the wooden tray — a sandwich and a large iced matcha with a straw. Her face

is blissful and delighted, finally settling in for a film and a good meal. She

sets the tray down, tucks her legs under herself, pulls the blanket over, wriggles

her shoulders with contentment, clicks the remote, wraps her lips around the straw

and takes a long satisfied sip, eyes closing in pleasure.

The camera slowly zooms out and reveals, on the LEFT side of the frame, the cat

sitting perfectly still, ears flat, locked in an unbroken stare at her, radiating

contempt. Without breaking eye contact for a second, it speaks immediately.

BOB (dry, irritated, drawling): "And where, exactly, is MY... whatever that is?"

[LAUGH TRACK: big laugh, over the zoom out and the line]




0:12–0:21 — CLOSE-UP on Kaye, smirking, straw still in her mouth.

KAYE (teasing): "You don't even know what that is."

CLOSE-UP on the cat, answering INSTANTLY, no pause, sharp and clipped:

BOB: "What is it?"

The cat holds her gaze, unblinking. Hold on its face.




0:21–0:31 — TWO-SHOT, the cat on the LEFT, Kaye on the RIGHT. Rapid-fire exchange,

no pauses between lines.

KAYE: "It's matcha."

BOB (clipping every word, mounting impatience): "I. Want. To. Try. It."

Kaye silently slides her cup toward the cat, straw first. It leans in, sniffs the

straw, recoils sharply, ears flattening in offence.

BOB (appalled, indignant): "Ewww! Madam! Not YOURS!"




0:31–0:43 — MEDIUM SHOT, slow pull back to show the whole sofa.

KAYE (shrugging): "Suit yourself."

She turns back to the TV and bites her sandwich. Pause. The cat slowly raises one

front paw, extends its claws, and — staring dead at her the entire time, never

breaking eye contact — begins slowly and deliberately clawing the sofa upholstery.

She freezes with the sandwich at her mouth and slowly turns her head. The cat keeps

clawing, looking straight into her eyes.




0:43–0:48 — CLOSE-UP on Kaye, giving in, exasperated.

KAYE: "Okay! Okay! You'll get your maaatcha!"

The cat instantly retracts its paw, sits up straight, front paws neatly together,

the picture of nobility.




0:48–0:56 — FINAL WIDE SHOT. Hard cut. The two of them now sit side by side on the

sofa watching TV, the cat on the LEFT, Kaye on the RIGHT. Both have matching clear

iced matcha cups with domed lids and straws — hers large in her hands, the cat's

small, clamped between its two front paws. They turn their heads to the screen in

unison and settle. TV light flickering on both faces. Perfect harmony. Hold the

shot, nobody speaks. FREEZE FRAME ending.

[LAUGH TRACK: warm laugh + applause, lands in the silence, into a final sitcom sting]




AUDIO: 90s sitcom laugh track ONLY at the two marked beats, nothing in between —

the middle of the scene plays dry with no audience reaction. No background music

except the final sting. Room tone, muffled television dialogue, tray on table,

remote click, gurgling slurp through a straw, slow claws dragging on upholstery.

cat, bipedal cat, human arms on cat, human hands, fingers, paws gripping like

fingers, cat standing on two legs, distorted anatomy, extra limbs, crop top, bare

midriff, milk, bowl of milk, hot drink, ceramic mug, latte art, daylight outside,

sunny, text, watermark, logo, subtitles, russian language, non-english dialogue.
