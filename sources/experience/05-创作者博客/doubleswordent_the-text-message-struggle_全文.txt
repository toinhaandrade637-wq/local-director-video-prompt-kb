Creating this particular scene was a real challenge. In the first generation, the words were spelled incorrectly. I returned to ChatGPT, refined the prompt, and tried again—but the same thing happened. After several more unsuccessful attempts, I decided to take a break and watch some of the other festival submissions for inspiration.

While watching The Trigger, I noticed that its creator had generated a separate image with the text spelled correctly and then used that image as the starting frame. That was my lightbulb moment. I tried the same technique, and it finally worked—I was able to generate the scene exactly as I intended.
