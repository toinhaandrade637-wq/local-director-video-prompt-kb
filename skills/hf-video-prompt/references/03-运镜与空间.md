# 03 · 运镜与空间（视频）


**官方设计哲学**：运动与场景分离。每条提示词独立可用——方括号 `[ ]` 为场景占位符，运动描述不变，换场景只换括号内容。46 条覆盖全部六大类。

## 一、核心共性规则（全部 46 条共享）[官方 S09]

每条运镜提示词都包含以下**四层结构**：

1. **运动立法**：精确描述相机做什么（travel/rotate/zoom），含速度立法（constant/decelerating/accelerating）
2. **否定排除**：精确列出**不做什么**（no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no handheld drift...）
3. **视差行为**：物理移动 vs 光学变焦的视差差异显式声明
4. **终态锁定**：最终构图精确描述 + hold/静态结束

**视差法则**：
- 物理移动（dolly/truck/track）→ 有视差（近物快远物慢）
- 光学变焦（zoom）→ 无视差（背景与主体等比缩放）
- 每条提示词显式声明属于哪种

## 二、各类代表 verbatim [官方 S09 逐字]

### Static（绝对静止）
> `The camera stays planted in one immovable position from the first frame to the last. Zero motion — no drift, no shake, no breathing, no stabilization float, no micro-drift; absolute rock-solid stillness throughout. Angle, elevation, distance to the scene and the overall composition are frozen and never altered; every ounce of motion comes from the scene itself, never from the camera. The clip closes on precisely the framing it began with, identical down to the pixel.`

### Pan right（水平摇右）
> `Rotate the camera horizontally from left to right from one fixed point, like a standing head-turn, starting on [composition A] and sweeping across [the environment]; no sideways travel, no dolly, no truck, no arc, no slide, no zoom, no tilt. Speed: smooth constant rotation, decelerating gently at the end. Framing: keep the horizon level while new space enters from the right side of the frame only through rotation; [the landing subject] stays beyond the right edge until the rotation brings them in. End: settle on a clear final composition on [the landing subject] and hold.`

### Tilt up（垂直仰升）
> `Fixed camera position at [lens height]. Pure TILT UP: the camera rotates vertically upward at one constant smooth speed from [the lower anchor] to [the upper framing], no crane rise, no pedestal, no dolly, no zoom, no horizontal drift; the camera's position never changes.`

### Dolly in（推轨进）[案例 ANERNEQ 同类]
> `One continuous decisive dolly in on straight ground rails — pure forward travel at constant speed along the axis toward the subject, at a locked constant lens height of [1.5] meters, decelerating smoothly into a static hold at the end framing. The field of view never changes — no zoom: the perspective shifts because the camera physically approaches, near objects sweeping out past the frame edges with strong parallax. No pan, no tilt, no crane, no handheld drift; dead-level travel from first frame to last.`

### Tracking（侧跟）
> `One continuous lateral tracking move — the camera glides parallel to the subject at exactly the speed that keeps their framing constant, strict 90-degree side view at all times. Three clean parallax layers: nearest — [posts / mullions / table edges] sweeping fast past the lens, briefly occluding the subject; middle — the subject, constant size at frame center; farthest — [the background row] sliding by slower behind. No zoom, no pan drift, no push-in.`

### Dolly zoom（滑动变焦）[案例 ANERNEQ 同类]
> `The camera physically dollies forward from [6] meters to [2.5] meters while the lens simultaneously zooms out from [18] to [84] degrees field of view, in one continuous, perfectly synchronized ramp. The subject's head size stays EXACTLY constant in frame the entire time; the background behind them visibly stretches, elongates and recedes into depth, [the repeating background elements] pulling away.`

### Crane up（升降）[案例 ANERNEQ 同类]
> `A smooth jib rise from [1.2] meters to [9] meters altitude over the shot, with a gentle continuous downward tilt that keeps the subject anchored in [the lower third] of frame as the world opens above and around them. No lateral orbit, no truck, no zoom, no speed ramps; one continuous vertical reveal easing into a high hold.`

### Drone orbit（环绕）[案例 ANERNEQ 同类]
> `A smooth constant-speed circular drone flight around the subject — [8]-meter radius, [4]-meter altitude, traveling screen-[right] (clockwise seen from above), covering roughly a [200]-degree arc across the shot. The horizon rotates continuously behind the subject; no altitude change, no radius drift, no zoom, no speed ramps.`

### Handheld（手持）[案例 ANERNEQ 同类]
> `Raw handheld shoulder-rig throughout — visible shake, operator breathing, rolling horizon, imperfect reframes that always recover. Path: [opening angle] → [rough half-circle drift around the subject] → [lean-in to close-up] → [loose pull-back and hold]. Never stabilized, no gimbal float, no rails feel.`

### POV（主观视角）
> `The camera is a person's eyes at [1.7]-meter height walking forward from [the start point] to [the end mark]. Natural human walking cadence — gentle vertical bob synced to the steps, slight lateral sway, small organic head micro-rotations glancing around the space. The person's hands appear in frame only for [the interaction], then drop out of frame.`

### Bullet time（子弹时间）
> `Bullet time orbit: time inside the scene slams to a near-freeze — the subject hangs suspended mid-action, with droplets, debris and particles locked motionless in mid-air... while the camera alone keeps moving at normal speed, sweeping along a smooth circular arc around the frozen moment.`

### Robot arm（机械臂）
> `The camera flies one fast, perfectly smooth stabilized motion-control path through four positions — [front eye-level medium close-up] → [side arc at eye level] → [sinking into a low angle with a slight dutch tilt] → [craning up and over into a top-down 3/4 view]. Each glide takes about [1] second with soft ease-in/out.`

## 三、跨条共性模板语法

### 括号占位符系统
每条模板用 `[ ]` 标记需要按场景填充的位置：
- `[composition A]` = 起始构图
- `[the environment]` = 环境描述
- `[the landing subject]` = 落幅主体
- `[1.5] meters` = 相机高度/距离
- `[84] degrees / [18] degrees` = FOV 角度
- `[the terrain]` = 地形/障碍

### 速度立法词汇表
| 词汇 | 含义 | 常见组合 |
|---|---|---|
| constant speed | 匀速 | smooth constant rotation |
| decelerating | 减速 | decelerating gently at the end |
| accelerating | 加速 | one continuous accelerating move |
| ease-in/out | 软入软出 | soft ease-in and ease-out at both ends |
| speed ramps | 变速 | no speed ramps（多数模板禁变速） |
| held breath | 屏息感 | The approach should feel like held breath |

### 否定排除词汇表
每条运镜的否定排除列出所有**不该混入**的运动：
- 无横向移动：no sideways travel, no dolly, no truck
- 无变焦：no zoom
- 无旋转混入：no pan, no tilt
- 无稳定器：no stabilization, no gimbal float
- 无手持混乱：no handheld chaos, no rails feel
- 无升降：no crane, no jib, no pedestal
- 无变速：no speed changes, no speed ramps
- 无轨道感：no rails feel

### 视差声明模板
| 运动类型 | 视差描述 |
|---|---|
| Dolly/Push/Track | strong parallax: near objects sweeping past fast, far layer slow |
| Zoom | no parallax: background keeps exact same apparent size |
| Dolly zoom | background stretches, elongates, recedes into depth |
| Truck | near objects sweeping past fast, far layer drifting slow |
| Arc | background sweeps behind them |

## 四、运镜选择速查

| 要什么效果 | 用哪种 | 关键参数 |
|---|---|---|
| 画面完全静止让场景自己动 | Static | zero motion, identical to pixel |
| 水平扫描环境 | Pan right/left | rotation only, horizon level |
| 从下往上揭示 | Tilt up / Crane up | vertical rotation or jib rise |
| 接近主体 | Dolly in / Push in | physical travel, strong parallax |
| 离开主体 | Dolly out / Aerial pullback | rearward travel, never rises |
| 并排跟随 | Tracking / Side tracking | 90-degree side view, constant speed |
| 从背后跟随 | Follow shot / Reverse tracking | 0.8m behind shoulder / matching walking speed |
| 环绕展示 | Arc right/left / Drone orbit / 360 orbit | constant radius + height |
| 压迫感接近 | Push in | gimbal-smooth, held breath feel |
| 震撼揭示 | Crane up / Aerial pullback | vertical reveal, world opens |
| 紧张混乱 | Handheld / Chase shot | visible shake, never stabilized |
| 主观代入 | POV | person's eyes, walking cadence |
| 时间冻结 | Bullet time | world frozen, camera alone moves |
| 精确多位置 | Robot arm | motion-control, 4 positions |
| 主体压迫感 | Snorricam | chest-mounted, locked to frame center |
| 时间流逝 | Timelapse | two speeds of time in one frame |
| 焦点转移 | Rack focus | one plane sharp at a time |
| 尺寸不变背景变 | Dolly zoom | synchronized dolly + zoom |

## 常见错误

1. 混合运动类型——同时 dolly + zoom（除非刻意做 Dolly zoom）
2. 忘写否定排除——模型自由发挥混入多余运动
3. 忘记声明视差类型——物理移动与光学变焦的视差不同
4. 没给相机高度——空间关系不清楚
5. 速度没有立法——模型自由变速
6. 终态没有锁定——模型不知道结束画面是什么
7. 忘写"运动与场景分离"——把场景描述混进运镜块

## 待验证

- 46 条在 seedance_2_5 / seedance_2_0 / kling 等各模型上的执行保真度对比
- 低速跟踪（~1000fps 慢动作）在各模型的可用性
- Robot arm 多位置路径在非运动控制系统中的可实现程度

### 官方错误学补充 [官方 S21]

- 让模型决定（pick the best composition）→ 替换为精确 FOV/高度/距离/速度
- 感觉代替规格（cinematic dolly zoom feel）→ 替换为 camera height + lens + FOV + direction
- 质量填充词（4k/insane visuals/epic）→ 替换为光学参数（shutter 270-360 degrees, 47 degree FOV）

## 2026-11-04 增补：手持呼吸律与遮挡转场 [官方 S25 SEED_AUDIO 发布片 5,426c 全文逐字]

### 1. 呼吸手持立法 [官方 CAMERA BEHAVIOR 块]
> `handheld throughout, rising and falling with the operator's breathing` + `subtle handheld with operator body weight shift, camera rises and falls gently with operator breathing, intimate observation distance, no aggressive handheld, no crane, no dolly`

手持不是"shake"而是**操作员身体行为**：呼吸起伏 + 重心移动 + 观察距离；同时否定排除 aggressive handheld/crane/dolly。CAMERA BEHAVIOR 独立块（×90）——比 CAMERA 块更专注"相机行为学"而非参数。

### 2. 遮挡转场（wipe-cover）[官方 TIMELINE 块]
> `The operator steps and reframes — a broad banana leaf sweeps full-frame across the lens, covering the move — and clears to find the girl`

用前景遮挡（叶子扫过全帧）**隐藏 reframe 运镜**——一镜到底内部换主体的合法手法。配套禁令：遮挡物可遮镜头但**不能遮说话者的嘴**（`leaf occluding the mouth during dialogue` 列入 negative）。

### 3. 单次主运动律 [官方]
> `One primary movement at a time, no spinning.` + 否定栈 `simultaneous complex movements, camera spinning`

一镜内同时只允许一个主运动——多运动并发禁令成文。

### 4. 活体微动律 [官方]
> `never fully locks off — a faint breathing micro-motion stays alive on the face so it never freezes into a still`

静态持镜 ≠ 完全锁死：面部保留呼吸微动防"冻结成静帧"。锁定（lock-off）与活体（alive）成对立法。

## 2026-11-04 增补：相机人格化与粒子景深 [案例 S30 nadia-7-of-9 5,455c 逐字]

### 1. CAMERA CHARACTER 相机人格化 [案例 GLOBAL OPTICS / CAMERA CHARACTER 合体块]
```
The camera is operated by a skilled human operator responding physically to Nadia and Sophie in
real time. Do not use mechanically perfect subject tracking, stabiliz[ation]...
```
相机=熟练人类操作者（实时物理响应被摄者），**禁机械完美跟踪/稳定**——相机行为有"人格"（65-85mm anamorphic 近全开光圈+透视压缩自然亲密）。

### 2. LAYER SEPARATION 粒子景深分级 [案例]
```
LAYER SEPARATION
Maintain a subtle atmospheric haze throughout the room to create depth and separation between
foreground, midground, and background planes.
The air should contain dry suspended particles, like fine dust, not smoke, fog, steam...
The distribution of particles should vary by depth:
Foreground: very few particles, small and only occasionally visible near lens
Midground: ...
```
空气粒子作为景深工具：干悬浮颗粒（fine dust）+ 三重排除（not smoke/fog/steam）+ **粒子按景深分级分布**（前景少而小、中景递增）——用大气颗粒做前中背景分离的可执行配方。

## DST 运镜定律蒸馏（2026-11-05 · 短摘录→好在哪→迁移法）

### 四相连续运镜 [案例 DST #96 逐字]
> ONE CONTINUOUS OPERATED MOVE — it meets them at a low barricade, walks backwards ahead of them, holds while they unmask, orbits round behind them to reveal what they are looking at, and then turns and leaves the ground. It is one camera doing one journey.
**好在哪**：相=动词+事件动机；four linked phases that never overlap and never stall；单焦距双用途论证（28mm why-one-lens）。
**迁移**：长镜先列相表（LEAD-HOLD-ORBIT-LIFT），每相写起点/动机/终点；高度收益集中末相（human height 前三/四分之三）。

### 甩摇解剖学 [案例 DST #119 逐字]
> EACH WHIP PAN LASTS ABOUT 0.7 SECONDS AND IS BUILT FROM: a short accelerating start, a fast middle where the world is a HORIZONTAL DIRECTIONAL MOTION BLUR SMEAR of real ground, rock, sky and haze, and a decelerating settle that overshoots a few degrees past the target and swings gently back to it.
**好在哪**：预判最易错点（被剪成切）——中段必须露出真实地景拖影；六伪装全禁（whip cuts/cross-dissolve hidden in the blur/match cut 等）；blurs to abstraction is correct but readable as ONE camera turning its head。
**迁移**：快动一律三段式（加速/拖影/过冲回摆）；声音只许麦克风风声（no designed transition sound）。

### 静框放行与横向通过 [案例 DST #110 逐字]
> THE CAMERA DOES NOT PAN RIGHT TO FOLLOW THE TWO WALKING FIGURES. They travel across a stationary frame and the camera lets them go.
**好在哪**：不追随族的放行形态——人物横穿静帧、镜头放手；排比强调制式（THEY WALK CAMERA RIGHT 连写两遍）。
**迁移**：告别/撤离段=静框+横向通过；反向即景深通道来人法（grows into the frame, camera never widens，#95）。
